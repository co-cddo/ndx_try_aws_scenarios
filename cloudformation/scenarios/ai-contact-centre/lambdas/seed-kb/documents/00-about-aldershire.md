# About Aldershire District Council

Aldershire District Council is a fictional UK local authority serving roughly 145,000 residents across the urban borough of Aldermarket and the rural hinterland of the Aldershire Vale. The council provides statutory services typical of a UK district / unitary authority: waste collection, environmental health, planning, council tax billing, housing benefits administration, parks and open spaces, leisure, electoral services, and contact-centre intake for all of the above.

Aldershire is used in this NDX:Try scenario as a demonstration council. Any resemblance to a real council is coincidental. Service descriptions, schedules, and team names in this knowledge base are illustrative; do not use them in any real-world advice context.

## Council reception opening hours

- Monday to Friday: 08:00 to 18:00
- Saturday: 09:00 to 13:00
- Sunday and bank holidays: closed (24/7 emergency line for safeguarding, environmental hazards, and out-of-hours housing repairs)

## How to get in touch

- Phone: the +44 800 toll-free number provisioned in this scenario's CloudFormation outputs
- WhatsApp: the simulator panel in the companion SPA. In production, a Bring Your Own WhatsApp Business Account (BYO-WABA) wiring is documented in walkthrough Step 6.
- In person: the Council Reception, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, Monday to Friday only

The council does not currently offer a live web chat. The chatbot you are interacting with handles voice and WhatsApp messaging.

## What this council does

Aldershire is a single-tier district council. It is responsible for: refuse, recycling, fly-tipping, environmental health, food hygiene, planning, building control, council tax billing, housing benefits, parks, leisure, public toilets, electoral services, council elections, licensing, anti-social behaviour, and homelessness prevention.

It is not responsible for: highways and roads (Aldershire County Council), schools (County Council), social care (County Council), police (Aldershire Constabulary), NHS services. Calls about those services should be transferred or signposted; the Lex bot recognises these out-of-scope intents and offers the appropriate phone number.
