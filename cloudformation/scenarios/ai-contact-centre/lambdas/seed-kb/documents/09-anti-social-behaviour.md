# Anti-social behaviour and community safety

Aldershire's Community Safety team works in partnership with Aldershire Constabulary, social housing providers, and youth services to address anti-social behaviour (ASB) in the district.

## What counts as ASB

ASB covers a broad range of conduct: persistent noise nuisance, drunkenness, intimidating behaviour, harassment, vandalism, fly-tipping (sustained), abandoned vehicles, drug dealing on residential streets, neighbour disputes that have escalated.

ASB is **not**: a single noisy party, a one-off argument, or two parties having a civil dispute. The Community Safety team's intervention threshold is patterns of behaviour, not isolated incidents.

## How to report

- Phone the council reception number for non-urgent ASB.
- For ASB involving threats, violence, or hate crime: 999 (emergency) or 101 (non-emergency police).
- Anonymous reporting via Crimestoppers: 0800 555 111.
- The council's ASB online form for written submissions with attached evidence.

## What the council does

For first reports, the Community Safety officer logs the report and watches for escalation. For repeat reports against the same address or perpetrator, the officer opens a case and may pursue:

- Acceptable Behaviour Contract (ABC), a voluntary agreement with the perpetrator.
- Community Protection Notice (CPN), a statutory notice with conditions.
- Premises Closure Order, for premises associated with serious ASB.
- Civil Injunction, court-ordered.

## Working with police

For cases involving criminality, the council coordinates with Aldershire Constabulary. The shared safeguarding board meets monthly. Confidential information about a case is not shared with neighbours, complainants, or local councillors except where statutory processes require it.

## Anti-social behaviour from rented housing

If the perpetrator is a tenant, the landlord (private or social) has a contractual interest in addressing the conduct. The council can liaise with the landlord. Social housing providers in Aldershire have signed up to the joint ASB protocol; private landlords are encouraged to engage.

## Hate crime

Hate crime (motivated by race, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, or disability) is reported to Aldershire Constabulary. The council's Community Safety team is informed and can support the victim with practical and emotional resources. The bot recognises hate crime disclosures and routes to a human agent immediately.
