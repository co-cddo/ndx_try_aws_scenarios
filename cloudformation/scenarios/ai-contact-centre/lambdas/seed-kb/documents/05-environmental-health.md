# Environmental health, damp, mould, pest control, food hygiene

Aldershire's Environmental Health team handles statutory environmental health matters. Common topics: damp and mould in private rented housing, pest infestations, food hygiene complaints, noise nuisance, and air quality complaints.

## Damp and mould

If you are a tenant of a private landlord and you have damp or mould in your home that the landlord has not addressed within a reasonable time, Environmental Health can intervene. Awaab's Law (effective for social housing from October 2025; private rented sector regulations to follow) sets statutory timescales for landlords to investigate hazards.

To report:

- Provide the address of the property.
- Describe the affected rooms and the duration of the issue.
- Note whether you have reported it to the landlord and what response you received.
- A photo (via WhatsApp) significantly speeds triage.

Environmental Health will inspect within 10 working days and serve an Improvement Notice on the landlord if statutory thresholds are met.

## Pest control

Aldershire's pest control service covers domestic premises within the district. Charges apply (subsidised for residents on Council Tax Reduction). Urgent infestations (rats in occupied homes, wasp nests near schools) are prioritised.

Common requests:

- Rats: domestic visit within 5 working days.
- Mice: same, plus advice on entry-point sealing.
- Wasp nests: visit within 3 working days during summer peak.
- Bed bugs: referred to a private pest contractor list; local-authority subsidy not currently available.

## Food hygiene

If you have a food hygiene concern about a registered food business in Aldershire (restaurant, takeaway, school kitchen, market stall), report it to Environmental Health. Investigations are confidential, your contact details will not be shared with the business.

## Noise and anti-social behaviour

Persistent noise nuisance (loud music, barking dogs, building work outside permitted hours) is investigated by Environmental Health. Anti-social behaviour involving harassment or violence is referred to the Community Safety team and Aldershire Constabulary.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot creates a case, takes initial details (with PII redaction at the Connect instance level), and routes to the right team. It does not give legal advice on enforcement, schedule the inspection visit, or estimate likely outcomes. Bedrock Guardrails block any attempted hallucinated advice on tenancy law, environmental enforcement powers, or food safety classifications.
