# Housing benefits and Council Tax Reduction

Aldershire administers Housing Benefit (legacy claims; transitioning to Universal Credit Housing Element for working-age applicants) and Council Tax Reduction (CTR) on behalf of central government.

## Who can claim

Working-age residents not claiming Universal Credit may still be entitled to Housing Benefit if they are in supported, temporary, or specified accommodation. Pension-age residents claim Housing Benefit through Aldershire if they rent their home and are on a low income.

CTR is a separate scheme and is open to working-age and pension-age applicants. It is means-tested and pays up to 100% of the council tax bill depending on circumstances.

## How to apply

- Online application form (linked from the council website) is the fastest route.
- Phone application: the bot can take initial details and create a case. A benefits officer will call back within 3 working days for the full assessment.
- In-person: the Civic Centre offers walk-in benefits surgeries on Tuesday afternoons and Friday mornings.

## Required information

- Proof of identity (passport, driving licence, or birth certificate plus a recent utility bill).
- Proof of all household income (payslips, pension statements, benefit award letters).
- Proof of all household savings and capital (bank statements, ISA statements).
- Proof of rent (tenancy agreement, landlord's last 12 months of rent receipts).
- National Insurance numbers for all household adults.

## Service standard

New claims are decided within 28 days of all evidence being received. Change-of-circumstance updates are processed within 14 days. Discretionary Housing Payments are decided within 21 days.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not assess entitlement, give advice on Universal Credit transitions, or negotiate on behalf of an applicant. Bedrock Guardrails block hallucinated benefits advice. The bot creates a case, captures redacted contact details (Contact Lens redacts the National Insurance number, address, and phone before persistence), and routes to a benefits officer.
