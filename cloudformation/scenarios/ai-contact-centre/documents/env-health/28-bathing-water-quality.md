# Bathing water quality: beaches and lakes

Aldershire is inland, so the Council does not have any designated coastal bathing waters. However, two inland sites in the district are designated under the Bathing Water Regulations 2013, and several others are unofficial swimming spots that the Council monitors informally.

## Designated bathing waters in Aldershire

Two sites have official Bathing Water status:

### Mereford Lake (Aldershire Country Park)

- Designation since: 2022 (lobbied for by Aldershire Wild Swimmers)
- Bathing season: 15 May to 30 September
- Sampling: weekly during the season by Environment Agency
- 2025 classification: Excellent
- 5-year mean: Excellent
- Facilities: changing village (£3.50 day pass), lifeguards weekends + August daily, on-site cafe

### Stoneford Reservoir (managed by Severn Valley Water)

- Designation since: 2024
- Bathing season: 1 June to 15 September
- Sampling: weekly during the season
- 2025 classification: Good
- 5-year mean: Good
- Facilities: Stoneford Open Water Swimming Centre (membership or day visit), changing rooms, hot showers

## Classification

Bathing Water classifications, based on bacterial sampling over 4 years:

- Excellent: less than 5% chance of mild illness from a swim
- Good: less than 10% chance
- Sufficient: less than 14% chance (still allowed for designated swimming)
- Poor: above 14% chance; advisory against bathing

Both Aldershire sites are classified annually by the Environment Agency. Results are at environment.data.gov.uk/bwq.

## Live information

During the season:

- A traffic-light board at each site shows current advisory status (green: safe; amber: recent rain may have raised risk; red: advisory against swimming)
- Live water quality predictions from the EA's Swimfo app (www.gov.uk/check-bathing-water)
- Twitter/X alerts: @AldershireWild for community-led updates
- Aldershire Council's website www.aldershire.gov.uk/bathing-water during the season

## Pollution events

If you see signs of pollution while at a bathing water (oil sheen, foam, dead fish, sewage, unusual odour or colour), do NOT enter the water.

Report immediately:

- Environment Agency 0800 80 70 60 (24/7 hotline)
- Council 01632 960140 (office hours)

Photos help. Note the time, exact location (what3words is ideal), wind direction, and any recent heavy rain.

## Storm overflows

Both Stoneford Reservoir and Mereford Lake have storm overflows from the public sewer system within their catchment. Severn Valley Water publishes near-real-time storm overflow data at www.svw.co.uk/storm-overflows. After a spill, water quality may be temporarily affected and an "amber" advisory will be displayed. Avoid swimming for 48 hours after a spill.

## Unofficial swimming spots

The Council does not actively manage these sites but they are known and informally monitored:

- The Cobbles, Mereford River (by the road bridge)
- Eastfield Weir Pool
- Brookside Lakes (private fishing lake; access negotiated with landowner)
- Aldershire Quarry Pool (closed since 2019 fatality; do not enter)

We strongly discourage swimming at the Aldershire Quarry Pool; the water is cold (4-6°C even in summer) and access is via fencing in disrepair. Recovery of bodies in 2017 and 2019.

## Open water swimming safety

If you swim outdoors, please:

- Acclimatise gradually; cold water shock is the main cause of drownings in the first 30 seconds
- Swim with at least one other person; tell someone where you are going
- Wear a tow float (high visibility, also helps you rest)
- Avoid alcohol before swimming
- Know how to get out (especially in lakes with steep banks)
- Keep your face out of the water if there is any sign of pollution
- Shower after swimming and don't swim with open cuts or wounds

## Lifeguards

Mereford Lake has Royal Life Saving Society UK qualified lifeguards every weekend May-September and daily through August. Stoneford Reservoir does not provide lifeguards; swimmers must use their judgement.

## Statutory background

Bathing Water Regulations 2013. Bathing Water (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2024 (revised classification approach). Water Resources Act 1991 (pollution offences). Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984.
