# Burials and memorial gardens: Aldershire Crematorium

Aldershire Crematorium and the connected memorial gardens are operated directly by the Council's Bereavement Services team. The site is at Crematorium Drive, Mereford, AL2 8RT, telephone 01632 960340.

## Facilities

The site comprises:

- Two cremators (modern, mercury-abated, target 90% emission reduction)
- One large chapel (200 seats, accessible, with audio-visual webcasting)
- One small chapel (80 seats, for smaller services)
- Two waiting rooms with refreshment facilities
- Memorial garden with cremated remains burial section, scattering area, and book of remembrance
- Cemetery section (full burial; reopened for limited new plots in 2024 after extension)
- Reception, parking for 80 cars (electric chargers being installed 2026)

## Booking a service

Funeral directors do most bookings. Direct family bookings (without a funeral director) are also possible: call Bereavement Services 9am-5pm Monday to Friday on 01632 960340.

Service slots: 30 minutes (chapel), 60 minutes (chapel double slot), 45 minutes (small chapel). The chapel is in use Monday to Saturday; Sunday services on request with surcharge.

Children's funerals are free under the Children's Funeral Fund for England, which Aldershire has fully implemented since 2019.

## Memorial gardens schedule

The memorial gardens have specific sections:

- The Rose Garden: scattering of ashes, simple plaque (south-facing, popular)
- The Cherry Walk: cremated remains burial in a 23cm × 23cm plot, with stone tablet
- The Reflection Pool: cremated remains burial in caskets, with raised stone marker
- The Garden of Memories (children): no charge, individual stone, butterfly themes
- The Cemetery Extension: full burial, granite or natural-stone headstones following the agreed schedule

The schedule for memorial design (size, material, lettering, vase rules) is at www.aldershire.gov.uk/memorial-schedule. New memorials must be approved by Bereavement Services before installation.

## Book of Remembrance

The Aldershire Book of Remembrance is on permanent display in the chapel foyer, open at the page for that day's date. Inscriptions are entered for a flat fee:

- 2 lines: £85
- 5 lines: £155
- Family crest or insignia: £45 additional

Inscriptions are typically scribed within 12 weeks. Order through Bereavement Services.

## Webcasting

All services in the main chapel are webcast (private, password-protected). The link is sent to family up to 4 hours before the service. Recordings are available on demand for 28 days. Extended access (1 year) is £45.

## Annual remembrance events

- Workers' Memorial Day: 28 April
- Baby Loss Remembrance Service: October (usually first Sunday)
- All Souls' Service: November
- Christmas Tree of Remembrance: December

All are open events. Families of those whose funerals took place at Aldershire Crematorium in the past 12 months are invited by post to the All Souls' service.

## Maintenance and access

The gardens are open daily from 8am until dusk. Vehicle access is closed at 7pm in winter. Litter, faded flowers, and balloons are removed by the grounds team. Personal items left at memorials beyond the agreed schedule may be removed (notice will be left first).

## Statutory background

Cremation (England and Wales) Regulations 2008. Local Authorities' Cemeteries Order 1977. Burial Act 1857 (still relevant for plot exclusive rights).
