# Stray dogs: capture, holding, and reclaim fees

Aldershire holds the statutory function for stray dogs under section 149 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990. The service is delivered by an in-house Dog Warden team, supported by the Mereford Dogs' Home for kennelling.

## Reporting a stray dog

If you find a dog that appears to be stray:

- During office hours (8am-5pm Mon-Fri): 01632 960140
- Out of hours (5pm-8am, weekends, bank holidays): 01632 960199 or 01632 960140 (calls divert)

Tell us:

- Where you saw the dog
- A description (size, colour, breed if known, collar/tag/microchip not yet checked)
- Whether the dog is friendly, anxious, or aggressive
- Whether it appears injured

If you can do so safely, you can keep the dog with you while we attend, or take it to one of the agreed handover points (the Mereford Civic Centre reception desk or any of the council depots during office hours). Do not approach a dog showing signs of fear or aggression.

## What happens next

A Dog Warden attends within 2 hours during office hours, or 4 hours out of hours. The warden will:

1. Check the dog for a microchip using a universal scanner
2. Try to contact the registered keeper
3. If the keeper can be contacted, return the dog directly (with a fee, see below)
4. If not, take the dog to Mereford Dogs' Home (Stoneford Lane, Mereford, AL3 7QF, telephone 01632 960480)
5. Take a photograph and post to the Council's lost-and-found dogs page (www.aldershire.gov.uk/stray-dogs)

## Statutory holding period

We must hold a dog for 7 clear days before any decision on rehoming or euthanasia. During this period, the dog's owner can reclaim it on payment of fees.

After 7 days, if no owner is found, the dog passes to the Mereford Dogs' Home, which works with rescue partners (Dogs Trust, RSPCA Aldershire branch) to find a new home where the dog is suitable.

## Fees to reclaim a dog (2026/27)

| Item | Fee |
|------|-----|
| Statutory release fee | £25 (set by national legislation) |
| Council administration | £20 |
| Each day in council care | £15 |
| Veterinary treatment (if needed) | At cost |
| Microchipping (if not chipped) | £15 mandatory |

Example: a dog held for 3 days, with no vet treatment, microchipped on entry: £25 + £20 + (3 × £15) + £15 = £105.

Fees are payable by debit card or cash at Mereford Dogs' Home before release. We do not waive fees on hardship grounds; the statutory release fee is fixed by law.

## Microchip law

Since 6 April 2016, all dogs over 8 weeks old must be microchipped, with the keeper's details up to date. From 10 June 2024, the same applies to cats.

Failure to microchip is an offence with a fixed penalty of £500 (Microchipping of Dogs (England) Regulations 2015). We will issue a 21-day notice first, allowing you to comply. The £500 penalty applies if you do not.

## Lost dogs

If your dog is missing:

1. Check the Council's lost-and-found page within 24 hours: www.aldershire.gov.uk/stray-dogs
2. Call us on 01632 960140
3. Contact local vets (we share photos with the 14 Aldershire vet practices)
4. Update your microchip database to flag the dog as missing
5. Use community Facebook groups: "Aldershire Dogs Reunited" and "Lost & Found Pets Mereford"

## Dogs killed on the road

Sadly, some dogs are killed in road traffic accidents. The Highways team collects deceased pets where possible; we will scan the microchip and contact the keeper if scannable. Call 01632 960100.

## Statutory background

Environmental Protection Act 1990 sections 149-151. Animal Welfare Act 2006. Microchipping of Dogs (England) Regulations 2015.
