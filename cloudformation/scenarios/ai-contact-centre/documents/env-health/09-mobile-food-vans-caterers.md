# Mobile food vans, caterers, and event food: H&S checks

Mobile food traders and event caterers are a growing part of the Aldershire food economy. The team carries out around 240 inspections of mobile units each year, in addition to event-specific visits at festivals, sports events, and Christmas markets.

## Where you must register

Mobile traders must register their food business with the local authority where the unit is normally kept (the "home depot"). For traders based in Aldershire, register at www.aldershire.gov.uk/food-business-registration.

You do not need to register again with each council where you trade, but you should let the host council know if you operate at one of their events or pitches.

## What we inspect on a mobile unit

Mobile inspection priorities are:

- Water supply: minimum 25 litres of potable water for hand washing and cleaning, with a separate waste tank
- Hot water on demand at the wash sinks
- Refrigeration: working at or below 8°C (8°C is legal maximum, target 5°C)
- Hot holding: above 63°C
- Generator and gas safety: Gas Safe certificate displayed (LPG safety record valid in date)
- Food storage: protected from contamination, off the floor
- Hand washing: dedicated hand basin separate from food prep sinks
- HACCP: documentation appropriate to the operation
- Allergen information available to customers

## Pitch licences

Pitches on Aldershire-owned land (town centre squares, Mereford Park, the Riverside) require a separate Street Trading Consent. Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/street-trading. Annual fees range from £450 (occasional) to £2,800 (5-day-a-week premium pitch).

Pitches on private land (a pub car park, an industrial estate) do not require Street Trading Consent, but the landowner's permission and full food registration are still needed.

## Events: temporary food provision

For events with multiple food traders (festivals, food fairs, summer fetes), the event organiser must:

- Notify Environmental Health 28 days before the event using the Event Notification Form (www.aldershire.gov.uk/event-food)
- Provide a list of food traders and their FHRS ratings
- Supply an event safety plan including waste, water, electricity, and welfare facilities

We may visit the event during set-up and trading hours. Traders rated 0, 1, or 2 may be refused permission to trade or required to demonstrate improvements before opening.

## Charity and community events

Charity barbecues, school summer fetes, and one-off community events selling food do still require food registration if they happen more than 5 times per year. A church Christmas fair selling cakes once a year falls below the threshold but still benefits from registration. Registration is free.

## Common issues we find

- Inadequate water supply, especially in busy units running multiple service hours
- Refrigeration in transit (a 4-hour drive from supplier to event with a fridge powered only at the event)
- Cross-contamination from raw to cooked meat in single-skillet operations (kebab vans, burger vans)
- Allergen information missing for "specials" added on the day
- Gas certificate expired (must be re-tested annually for commercial mobile units)

## Out-of-hours support

Event traders often work weekends and evenings. The food team has a duty officer reachable on 01632 960199 between 6pm and 11pm Friday and Saturday during the festival season (April to September) and at major events year-round.

## Statutory background

Food Safety and Hygiene (England) Regulations 2013, schedule 4 (mobile food premises). Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974 (gas, electricity, manual handling). Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 schedule 4 (street trading).
