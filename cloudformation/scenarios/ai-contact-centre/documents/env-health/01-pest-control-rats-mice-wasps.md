# Pest control: rats, mice, wasps

Aldershire District Council operates a domestic pest control service for residents within the district boundary. The service is delivered by in-house officers based at the Mereford depot. Charges apply for most treatments; concessions are available for low-income households.

## What we treat

- Rats (Rattus norvegicus) inside or in the curtilage of domestic property
- Mice (house mouse, field mouse) inside domestic property
- Wasps and hornets where the nest is on or in domestic property
- Squirrels in lofts (advice and referral only; specialist contractor required)

We do not treat commercial premises, food businesses, schools, or houses in multiple occupation under this service. Commercial customers should contact the British Pest Control Association list or our Commercial Pest Service on 01632 960123.

## Fees (2026/27)

| Treatment | Standard | Concession |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| Rats (up to 3 visits) | £92 | Free |
| Mice (up to 3 visits) | £78 | Free |
| Wasp nest (single visit) | £58 | £15 |

The concession rate applies to households in receipt of Council Tax Reduction, Universal Credit (with no earned income), Pension Credit (Guarantee element), or Housing Benefit. Proof of entitlement is requested at booking. The free rat and mice treatment is funded by the Public Health grant under section 2 of the Prevention of Damage by Pests Act 1949.

## Response times

- Rats inside an occupied dwelling: visit within 5 working days
- Rats in garden only: visit within 10 working days
- Mice indoors: visit within 5 working days
- Wasp nest within 3m of a children's play area, school, care home, or main entrance: visit within 2 working days during May-September
- Other wasp nests: visit within 5 working days

## Booking

- Online: www.aldershire.gov.uk/pest-control
- Phone: 01632 960100 (Customer Services, 8am-5pm Monday to Friday)
- Email: pest.control@aldershire.gov.uk

We will agree an appointment window of 4 hours. An adult aged 18 or over must be present. Pets and children must be kept clear of bait points and treated areas for at least 24 hours after treatment.

## Statutory background

Section 4 of the Prevention of Damage by Pests Act 1949 places a duty on local authorities to keep their district as free as practicable from rats and mice. Where a property owner does not act, the council can serve a notice under section 4 requiring treatment, and recover costs.
