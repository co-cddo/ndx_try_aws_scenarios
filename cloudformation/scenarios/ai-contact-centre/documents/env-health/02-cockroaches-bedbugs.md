# Cockroach and bedbug treatments

Cockroach and bedbug infestations are not covered by the standard council pest control service. They require specialist treatment regimes that the in-house team is not currently equipped to deliver. Aldershire maintains an approved contractor list and can offer a hardship grant in limited circumstances.

## Why we don't treat in-house

Cockroaches (typically German cockroach, Blattella germanica, in UK domestic premises) and bedbugs (Cimex lectularius) are highly resistant to over-the-counter products and require:

- Multiple inspection and treatment visits over 4-8 weeks
- Use of professional-grade insecticides under COSHH controls
- Heat treatment (for bedbugs) or insect growth regulators
- Specialist canine detection in some cases

A single failed treatment can entrench resistance and make later eradication harder. We refer all cases to BPCA-registered contractors.

## Approved contractor list

The list at www.aldershire.gov.uk/pest-contractors is maintained by Environmental Health and lists 14 BPCA-member firms operating in the district. We do not endorse any individual firm, but listed contractors must:

- Hold British Pest Control Association membership in good standing
- Carry £5m public liability insurance
- Use only HSE-approved products
- Provide written treatment plans and evidence

Typical private treatment costs (2026):

- Cockroaches: £180-£350 for a 3-visit course
- Bedbugs: £250-£600 depending on property size and method (heat treatment is more expensive but faster)

## Hardship grant

Households on Council Tax Reduction with a confirmed cockroach or bedbug infestation in a single-family dwelling can apply for a hardship grant of up to £400 toward private treatment. Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/pest-hardship or call 01632 960100. The grant is paid directly to the contractor on production of a paid invoice.

The grant is not available for:

- Houses in multiple occupation (landlord responsibility under the Housing Act 2004)
- Commercial premises
- Repeat applications within 24 months for the same property

## Landlord properties

If you rent privately, the landlord is responsible for treating cockroaches and bedbugs where the infestation existed before your tenancy began, or where it results from a defect in the property (for example, gaps around pipework). Report to your landlord in writing first. If there is no response within 14 days, contact Environmental Health on 01632 960140.

In social housing, contact your housing provider directly. Aldershire Housing tenants should call 01632 960300.

## Statutory background

Section 80 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 allows the council to serve an Abatement Notice on a property owner where pests amount to a statutory nuisance prejudicial to health. This is a last resort; we work cooperatively with landlords first.
