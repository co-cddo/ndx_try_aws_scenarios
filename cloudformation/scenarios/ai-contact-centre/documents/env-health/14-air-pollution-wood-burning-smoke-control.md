# Air pollution: wood burning and Smoke Control Areas

Aldershire has a mix of urban and rural air quality challenges. The town centre is a Smoke Control Area; surrounding villages are not. Domestic solid fuel burning is the largest single source of fine particulate matter (PM2.5) in the district.

## Smoke Control Areas in Aldershire

The whole of Mereford urban area, plus Clay Park, Ridgeway Village, and Eastfield Garden Suburb, are designated Smoke Control Areas under the Clean Air Act 1993. The map is at www.aldershire.gov.uk/smoke-control-map.

In a Smoke Control Area:

- You may NOT emit smoke from a chimney of a building
- You may only burn "authorised fuels" (a list at www.gov.uk/smoke-control-area-rules) in a normal stove or fireplace
- Or you must use an "exempt appliance" (DEFRA-approved stove) which can burn other fuels including dry wood

A list of exempt appliances is at smokecontrol.defra.gov.uk.

## Penalties

Emitting smoke from a chimney in a Smoke Control Area is a civil penalty offence under section 19A of the Clean Air Act 1993:

- Standard penalty: £175
- Reduced for early payment to £100
- Increased for repeat offence to £300

Penalties are issued by the Council. Right of appeal to the Council's Penalty Review Panel within 21 days.

Selling unauthorised solid fuel for delivery to a Smoke Control Area is also an offence (penalty up to £1,000).

## Wood burning best practice

Wood burning produces high levels of PM2.5 even in modern stoves. To minimise pollution:

- Only burn dry wood (moisture content under 20%; "Ready to Burn" certified logs)
- Do not burn wet wood, treated wood, painted wood, or household waste
- Use a DEFRA-exempt stove if you live in a Smoke Control Area
- Have your chimney swept at least annually (twice yearly for hardwood logs)
- Make sure your stove is the right size for the room (oversized stoves are run "low and slow" and produce more smoke)

The Council does not currently restrict wood burning outside Smoke Control Areas, but PM2.5 is the largest indoor air pollutant in many homes; consider reducing use even where legal.

## Reporting smoke

To report smoke nuisance from a chimney:

- Online: www.aldershire.gov.uk/chimney-smoke
- Phone: 01632 960140

We will write to the property owner. If the smoke continues, we may visit and witness, then issue a civil penalty notice.

## Wider air quality

Aldershire monitors NO2 (nitrogen dioxide, mainly from vehicle exhaust) and PM2.5 (mainly from domestic combustion and brake/tyre wear) at:

- Mereford High Street (continuous diffusion tube and reference monitor)
- A329 Northgate roundabout (continuous monitor)
- Aldershire Library (urban background)
- Ridgeway School (school exposure)

Latest readings are at www.aldershire.gov.uk/air-quality. Annual reports are published every June.

## Air Quality Management Areas

Aldershire has one declared Air Quality Management Area (AQMA), covering the High Street and Northgate roundabout for NO2. The Council's Air Quality Action Plan 2024-2029 sets out measures including bus electrification, anti-idling enforcement, and traffic calming.
