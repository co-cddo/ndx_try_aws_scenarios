# Asbestos disposal advice

Asbestos was widely used in UK construction up to 1999 and is found in many Aldershire homes built before 2000. It is dangerous when fibres become airborne, but stable, intact asbestos is generally safer left in place.

## Where asbestos is found

Common locations in Aldershire homes:

- Asbestos cement roof sheets on garages, sheds, and outbuildings
- Soffit and fascia boards on properties built 1960-1985
- Artex ceiling and wall coatings (low percentage chrysotile, mostly pre-1985)
- Floor tiles and bitumen mastic adhesive
- Older boiler flues and gaskets
- Pipe lagging (rare in domestic but found in 1950s-70s prefabs)
- Storage tanks in airing cupboards (1960s-70s)
- Some older partitions in council houses (now mostly removed)

If you live in a council house and suspect asbestos, contact Aldershire Housing on 01632 960300; we hold an asbestos register for our stock.

## When does it matter

Asbestos in good condition, painted or sealed, that is not being disturbed, generally does not need removing. The risk is when it is:

- Damaged (broken, cracked, weathered, crumbling)
- Drilled, sanded, cut, or sawn
- In a place where it will be disturbed by maintenance or alteration

If you are doing DIY in a pre-2000 property, assume any unidentified material may contain asbestos until tested.

## Testing

You can take a small sample yourself if you take precautions, or hire a UKAS-accredited surveyor. A surveyor visit costs £180-£350 typically. A single sample lab test is £35-£60.

UKAS-accredited labs that serve Aldershire:

- Mereford Asbestos Testing (Eastside Industrial Estate): 01632 960770
- ACAS Labs (countywide): 01632 950600

DIY sampling: wear an FFP3 mask and disposable gloves; dampen the sample with water before cutting; double-bag in clear bags labelled "asbestos sample for analysis"; deliver same day; do not breathe in dust.

## Removal

Most asbestos work can be done by trained but non-licensed contractors. The exceptions (high-risk asbestos, requiring HSE-licensed contractors) include:

- Sprayed asbestos coatings
- Asbestos lagging on pipes or boilers
- Most asbestos insulating board (AIB) work

For asbestos cement roof sheets, garage demolition, and Artex removal, you can use a non-licensed specialist (still trained and insured).

We do not recommend a DIY removal of asbestos cement larger than 1 square metre; for larger removals, use a contractor.

## Disposal at the Household Waste Recycling Centre

The Mereford HWRC accepts small amounts of asbestos cement (cement-based, not boards or sheets containing more dangerous types) from Aldershire households free of charge, subject to:

- Maximum: 5 sheets or equivalent (around 50kg)
- Booking required: www.aldershire.gov.uk/asbestos-booking
- Double-wrapped in 1000-gauge polythene
- Labelled with "Asbestos waste"
- Sealed and dampened
- Taken in a covered vehicle
- 14 days advance booking

The Eastfield and Oakford HWRCs do NOT accept asbestos. Mereford only.

For larger amounts, the Council does not provide a free service. Use a licensed waste carrier; typical disposal cost is £60-£90 per square metre for asbestos cement, more for AIB.

## Council tax discount and grants

We do NOT offer a Council Tax discount for asbestos works. We do operate a Disabled Facilities Grant for adaptation works, which can include incidental asbestos removal where works disturb it.

## Statutory background

Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012. Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005. Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974. Environmental Permitting Regulations 2016 for waste carriers.
