# Food business registration

If you are starting a food business in Aldershire, you must register with the Council at least 28 days before you start trading. Registration is free, statutory, and cannot be refused.

## Who must register

Anyone who:

- Sells food to the public, regardless of profit (charity, school PTA, sports club included)
- Cooks, prepares, stores, distributes, or sells food
- Operates from a fixed premises, mobile unit, market stall, home kitchen, or vehicle

This includes:

- Restaurants, takeaways, cafes, pubs serving food
- Mobile catering vans and ice cream vans
- Home bakers selling cakes online (Etsy, Instagram, Facebook)
- Childminders preparing food for children in their care
- Sandwich vans and lunch delivery rounds
- B&Bs and Airbnb hosts who serve food beyond a continental breakfast
- Wholesale and distribution from a unit in the district

Registration is required even if you only operate occasionally (for example, a once-a-month farmers market stall).

## How to register

Online at www.aldershire.gov.uk/food-business-registration. The form takes around 15 minutes. You will need:

- Your business name and any trading name
- Address of every premises where food is handled (including a home kitchen)
- Type of business and the food activities you carry out
- Estimated start date

After submission you receive an automated acknowledgement. A food officer will contact you within 14 days to schedule an initial inspection.

## What happens at the initial inspection

A food safety officer (a member of the Environmental Health team) will visit, usually in the first 6 weeks of trading. The inspection covers:

- Cleanliness and structure of the premises
- Hot and cold storage temperatures and food handling
- Pest control measures
- Personal hygiene and training of staff
- Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points (HACCP) documentation
- Allergen management

The first inspection produces your initial Food Hygiene Rating Scheme rating. We give written advice on any issues; only serious risks lead to enforcement action.

## Home kitchens

Home-based food businesses (cake makers, jam producers, online bakeries) must register the home kitchen. You will need to demonstrate:

- A separate area for food preparation (not used for other domestic activities at the same time)
- Adequate hand washing facilities (a kitchen tap is acceptable)
- Pets kept out of the food preparation area during preparation, storage, and serving

You do not need a separate commercial kitchen unless your local volumes exceed about 200 servings per week, or you produce high-risk foods (raw meat preparations, high-protein chilled foods).

## Changes to your business

Tell us within 28 days if you:

- Change ownership
- Move premises
- Add a new premises
- Change the type of food activity (for example, add a kitchen to a previously dry-stock-only business)

Update at www.aldershire.gov.uk/food-business-update.

## Statutory background

Regulation (EC) 852/2004 Article 6, retained in UK law, requires every food business operator to notify the appropriate competent authority of each establishment under their control. Local authorities are the competent authority for retail and catering. The Food Standards Agency oversees primary production and approval-only premises.
