# Smoking in public places: enforcement

Smoking has been banned indoors in workplaces and public buildings in England since 1 July 2007. Aldershire's Environmental Health team enforces the law jointly with police and Trading Standards.

## Where smoking is banned

The Health Act 2006 (and supporting regulations) bans smoking:

- In all enclosed and substantially enclosed workplaces
- In all enclosed and substantially enclosed public places (pubs, restaurants, cinemas, museums, libraries, shops)
- In public transport (buses, trains, taxis)
- In private vehicles when carrying any person under 18 (Smoke-free (Private Vehicles) Regulations 2015)
- In NHS hospital grounds (most NHS Trusts apply this in their grounds beyond the legal requirement)

"Substantially enclosed" means a structure with a roof and walls/sides covering more than 50% of the perimeter. A smoking shelter must have a maximum of 50% solid wall surrounding it to be lawful.

## Where smoking is not banned

- Private homes (except shared parts of HMOs and shared corridors)
- Most outdoor spaces, including pavement seating outside pubs
- Private cars when no under-18 is present
- Designated rooms in some long-stay residential settings (care homes, prisons, mental health units)
- Smoking shelters that meet the 50% rule

## Smoke-free signs

Premises must display "no smoking" signs at entrances. The standard sign is required to be:

- A4 size (210mm × 297mm) or larger at main entrances
- A5 size at lesser entrances and inside the premises
- Clear and legible

We provide free signs at the Civic Centre reception (Mereford AL1 4QQ) and PDFs at www.aldershire.gov.uk/smoke-free-signs.

## E-cigarettes and vapes

E-cigarettes and vapes are NOT covered by the Health Act 2006 ban. Each business sets its own rules. Many premises voluntarily restrict vaping to outdoor areas. The Council's own buildings prohibit vaping inside (employees and visitors).

For Aldershire's local rules on vaping in public areas, see the separate article.

## Reporting a breach

If a business permits smoking inside, or fails to display signs, you can report:

- Online: www.aldershire.gov.uk/smoking-complaint
- Phone: 01632 960140
- Anonymous reports accepted, but please give the address and approximate time

We will:

1. Visit the premises (often outside complaint hours) to check signage and any direct evidence
2. Speak to the operator
3. Issue a Fixed Penalty Notice if appropriate

## Penalties

Three offences and three penalties:

| Offence | Who | Penalty |
|---------|-----|---------|
| Smoking in a smoke-free place | The smoker | £50 (£30 early payment) |
| Failing to prevent smoking on premises | Person managing the premises | £200 (£150 early payment) |
| Failing to display no-smoking signs | Person managing the premises | £200 (£150 early payment) |

For repeat or serious failures, prosecution in the magistrates' court can lead to fines up to £2,500 (signage), £1,000 (smoker), or £2,500 (failing to prevent).

## Smoking in vehicles with under-18s

The driver and any other adult in the vehicle is committing an offence if any person under 18 is present and smoking happens. Joint enforcement with Aldershire Constabulary; £50 fixed penalty.

This applies to all enclosed vehicles, including taxis. Open-top cars with the roof down do not count as enclosed.

## Hookah and shisha

Shisha (smoking flavoured tobacco through a water pipe) is treated identically to cigarettes. Indoor "shisha lounges" and "hookah cafes" must comply with the smoke-free legislation. Aldershire has had a long-running enforcement case at one premises in Mereford; check www.aldershire.gov.uk/enforcement-cases for current public records.

## Help to quit

Smoking is the largest preventable cause of death in Aldershire (estimated 280 deaths per year from smoking-related causes). Free support to quit is available through:

- Aldershire Wellbeing Service: 01632 960660 (free advice, nicotine replacement, behavioural support)
- Pharmacies offering Stop Smoking service across the district (list at www.aldershire.gov.uk/stop-smoking)
- NHS Smoking Helpline: 0300 123 1044
- Better Health "Quit Smoking" app: download from app stores

## Statutory background

Health Act 2006 part 1. Smoke-free (Premises and Enforcement) Regulations 2006. Smoke-free (Signs) Regulations 2012. Smoke-free (Private Vehicles) Regulations 2015. Children and Families Act 2014.
