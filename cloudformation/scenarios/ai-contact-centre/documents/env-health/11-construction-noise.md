# Construction noise: permitted hours and breaches

Building work, demolition, and major repair generate noise. Aldershire's policy balances the need for development against the right of residents to peace at unsocial hours.

## Permitted hours

For all construction sites in Aldershire, including domestic extensions, commercial development, and infrastructure works, the agreed working hours are:

| Day | Permitted hours |
|-----|-----------------|
| Monday-Friday | 7:30am-6pm |
| Saturday | 8am-1pm |
| Sunday and bank holidays | No noisy work permitted |

Quiet activity (planning, internal painting with no machinery, deliveries off-site) outside these hours is normally acceptable. Site set-up and welfare activities (workers arriving, kettles boiling, vehicles parking) are not in themselves noisy work.

These hours can be varied by:

- A planning condition specifying tighter or looser hours for a specific site
- A section 61 prior consent under the Control of Pollution Act 1974 (typical for major sites)
- An agreement with Environmental Health for a specific works episode (concrete pours that need to run continuously, lift equipment that can only be craned in at night)

## Section 61 prior consent

For major construction projects, the developer can apply for prior consent under section 61 of the Control of Pollution Act 1974. This sets out:

- Allowed working hours (sometimes longer than the standard)
- Methods of working (selection of equipment, screening, monitoring)
- Noise limits at specified locations
- Notification arrangements with neighbours

Prior consent gives the developer protection against an Abatement Notice provided they comply with the agreed conditions. Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/section-61 with at least 28 days' lead time. Fee from £900 for small sites to £3,500 for large infrastructure works.

## Reporting a breach

If you believe a site is working outside permitted hours, or producing excessive noise within permitted hours:

- Online: www.aldershire.gov.uk/construction-noise
- Phone: 01632 960140 (office hours)
- Out of hours: 01632 960199

Tell us:

- Site address (a planning application reference if you know it)
- Time, day, and what activity is happening
- Equipment type if you can identify it (concrete breaker, piling rig, generator)
- Whether dust or vibration is also a problem
- A photo or short video helps if you can take one safely

## What we do

Within 24 working hours we will:

- Check planning conditions and any section 61 consent
- Contact the site manager and either ask for an explanation or require immediate cessation
- Visit the site if the noise is continuing

For repeat or serious breaches we serve an Abatement Notice (Environmental Protection Act 1990 section 80) prohibiting noisy work outside permitted hours. Breach is a criminal offence with an unlimited fine.

## Major works in 2026

The following sites currently have specific section 61 consents in force:

- A329 Mereford bypass widening (Balfour Beatty): some night working agreed for traffic management; resident notification 7 days before each episode
- Mereford Station footbridge replacement (Network Rail): Saturday afternoon and Sunday working agreed during full line possession weekends
- New Aldershire General Hospital (Multiplex): standard hours; concrete pours occasionally extended to 8pm with notice

Sign up to construction-noise email alerts at www.aldershire.gov.uk/construction-alerts.

## Vibration

Significant vibration (piling, breaking concrete) can damage neighbouring property. Developers should carry out a pre-construction condition survey of nearby properties and may need to demonstrate compliance with BS 7385 limits.

## Statutory background

Control of Pollution Act 1974 sections 60 (notice) and 61 (prior consent). Environmental Protection Act 1990 sections 79-82. BS 5228:2009+A1:2014 Code of practice for noise and vibration control on construction sites.
