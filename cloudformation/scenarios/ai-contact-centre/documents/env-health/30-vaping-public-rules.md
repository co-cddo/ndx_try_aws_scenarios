# Vaping in public places: local rules

E-cigarettes and vapes are not covered by the Health Act 2006 smoking ban. Aldershire has, however, introduced local rules on vaping in specific public spaces and Council premises.

## Council premises (no vaping)

Aldershire prohibits vaping inside any Council-managed building. This includes:

- Aldershire Civic Centre and all area offices
- Council-run leisure centres (Mereford Lido, Eastfield Pool, Aldershire Sports Hall)
- Council libraries
- Council museums and the Aldershire Heritage Centre
- Children's Centres and youth clubs
- Council depots and meeting rooms

Visitors who wish to vape are asked to step outside the building, ideally beyond any covered entrance area. The rule applies in the same way as the smoking law.

## Public Spaces Protection Order 2023

Aldershire's PSPO (renewed 2026 to 2029) restricts vaping in:

- Children's play areas (the 7 fenced play areas across the district)
- Mereford Town Centre Pedestrian Zone (10am-6pm, the busiest hours)
- The Riverside Walk between Mereford Bridge and the Aldershire Country Park

Breach is a Fixed Penalty Notice of £100 (reduced to £75 if paid within 14 days). Persistent breaches are prosecutable.

The PSPO does NOT cover:

- General public pavements outside the town centre zone
- Open-air markets and squares outside the zone
- Vehicles on the public highway

## NHS sites and schools

NHS Aldershire and Mereford Hospitals operate a "smoke-free and vape-free" estate covering the building and grounds. This is the NHS Trust's local rule, not a council-imposed one, but signs are displayed and breaches can lead to security being called.

State schools in Aldershire all prohibit student vaping under their own behaviour policies. Trading Standards (administered by Aldershire County Council, not the District Council) enforces age-of-sale restrictions and the disposable vape ban.

## Disposable single-use vape ban

Since 1 June 2025, single-use disposable vapes have been banned from sale across the UK under the Environmental Protection (Disposable Electronic Cigarettes) (England) Regulations 2024. Aldershire Trading Standards and Environmental Health enforce. Any retailer displaying or selling disposable vapes faces a Fixed Penalty up to £200 and prosecution.

If you see disposable vapes for sale, report to:

- Aldershire Trading Standards: 0808 223 1133
- Online: www.aldershire.gov.uk/disposable-vapes

The ban applies to all single-use vapes regardless of nicotine content (so 0mg "fruit-only" disposables are also banned).

## Vape disposal

Refillable and rechargeable vapes contain lithium-ion batteries. They must NOT go in:

- General waste bin
- Any kerbside recycling bin

Battery fires from improperly disposed vapes have caused refuse vehicle fires across the country. Dispose of vapes at:

- The Mereford, Eastfield, or Oakford Household Waste Recycling Centre (small electricals area)
- Larger Aldershire shops with a vape disposal point (most supermarkets, some pharmacies)
- Battery collection points in Council buildings (Civic Centre reception)

## Smoking in shisha cafes (vape variant)

A few Aldershire venues offer "vape lounges" or e-shisha experiences. These follow the same rules as any indoor public space: customers may vape if the operator allows it, and the operator decides their own rules. The smoking ban does NOT cover vapes.

## Vaping on public transport

Bus operators and taxi firms set their own rules. Most prohibit vaping the same as smoking. Train operators (Severn Valley Trains) prohibit vaping on all trains and at stations.

## Help to quit

Vaping is widely accepted by NHS as a less harmful alternative to smoking and a helpful tool for quitting. The Council's Wellbeing Service can advise on a structured quit plan, including managing the eventual move away from vaping. Call 01632 960660.

For young people who have started vaping, the Aldershire Healthy Young People Service offers confidential 1:1 support: 01632 960665.

## Statutory background

Health Act 2006 (does NOT cover vapes). Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (PSPO powers). Environmental Protection (Disposable Electronic Cigarettes) (England) Regulations 2024. Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016 (vape standards).
