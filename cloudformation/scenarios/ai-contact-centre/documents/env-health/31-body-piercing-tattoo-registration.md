# Body piercing and tattoo registration

Anyone carrying out tattooing, body piercing (including ear piercing), semi-permanent skin colouring (e.g. micro-pigmentation), and similar activities in Aldershire must be registered with the Council under the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982. The premises must also be registered.

## What is regulated

Registration is required for:

- Tattooing
- Body piercing (ear lobes, cartilage, nose, navel, nipple, genital, dermal anchors)
- Semi-permanent skin colouring (cosmetic tattooing of eyebrows, lip liner, scalp micro-pigmentation)
- Acupuncture (covered by a separate but parallel scheme; see related article)
- Electrolysis

It is NOT required for:

- Henna painting
- Decorative temporary transfers
- Sunbed shops (separate Sunbed Regulation 2010 rules)
- Microblading by a registered nurse working in a clinical setting (covered by CQC; though council registration may still be relevant if not CQC)

## How to register

Two separate registrations are needed:

1. **Practitioner registration**: each person carrying out the activity. Fee £165, lasts for life unless lapsed by inactivity.
2. **Premises registration**: each location where activities are carried out. Fee £230 first time; £75 annual renewal.

Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/skin-piercing-registration. Provide:

- Practitioner's full name, date of birth, address, and ID
- Premises address with details of the rooms used
- Photos of the work area, sterilisation equipment, hand wash facilities
- Evidence of training (course certificates from BACP, Tattoo Cleanse, Micro-Pigmentation Academy etc.)
- Public liability insurance certificate (£2 million minimum)
- Details of the consent forms used and aftercare leaflets given

A registration officer visits before issuing the registration. Inspections cover:

- Hand wash facilities (hot and cold running water, separate from work sinks)
- Disposal of sharps (yellow sharps bins, licensed collection)
- Sterilisation (autoclaves, instrument cleaning, single-use needles only)
- Surface materials (impervious, easy to clean)
- Storage of clean and dirty equipment
- First aid arrangements

## Bylaws

Aldershire's Skin Piercing Bylaws 2018 set local minimum standards including:

- Minimum age 16 for any piercing without parental consent (some piercings require 18)
- No piercing of intoxicated clients
- No piercing of pregnant clients without GP letter
- All instruments single-use disposable or autoclaved between clients
- Aftercare information given in writing
- Daily logs of clients piercings and reactions

Breach of bylaws is a criminal offence; fines up to £1,000.

## Scratch parties and home piercing

Tattooing or piercing at a private home or party (sometimes called "scratch parties") generally needs registration. The premises is the home. The Council recognises that this can be a route to lower-cost services but the same hygiene standards apply.

There is a public health concern with unregistered home tattooing: blood-borne virus transmission (Hepatitis B, C, HIV) is well-documented. We do not prosecute for unregistered tattooing without prior warning except where there is direct evidence of harm.

## Children

Tattooing of anyone under 18 is illegal under the Tattooing of Minors Act 1969 (an absolute offence; consent of parent or child is no defence). Body piercing of children is not absolutely prohibited but Aldershire's bylaws set minimum ages and require parental presence.

## Consumer complaints

If you have had a poor experience (infection, injury, unsanitary conditions):

- Contact the practitioner first
- If unresolved, contact us on 01632 960140
- For NHS treatment of an infected piercing or tattoo, contact your GP or 111
- For civil claims, see a personal injury solicitor

## Mobile and pop-up

Mobile tattooists working at festivals or short-term pop-up venues need premises registration for the venue (or the operator does). The Council can issue a temporary registration for events at our discretion; ring 01632 960140 well in advance of the event.

## Cancellation

If you stop trading or move premises, tell us within 28 days. Premises registration does not transfer with the building; new operators must re-register.

## Statutory background

Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 sections 14-17. Public Health (Aircraft) Regulations 1979. Tattooing of Minors Act 1969. Aldershire Skin Piercing Bylaws 2018.
