# Council tax, billing, bands, exemptions, and what to do if you can't pay

Council tax pays for council services. Aldershire District Council collects council tax on behalf of the precepting authorities (Aldershire County Council, Aldershire Constabulary, parish councils where applicable).

## Bands

Properties are assigned to a band A through H based on their April 1991 capital value. Bands and 2024-25 indicative annual amounts (for reference; actuals vary by parish):

- Band A: under £40,000, £1,250
- Band B: £40,001 to £52,000, £1,460
- Band C: £52,001 to £68,000, £1,667
- Band D: £68,001 to £88,000, £1,876
- Band E: £88,001 to £120,000, £2,295
- Band F: £120,001 to £160,000, £2,710
- Band G: £160,001 to £320,000, £3,127
- Band H: over £320,000, £3,752

## Exemptions and reductions

- Single occupancy: 25% discount.
- Full-time student-only households: full exemption.
- Severely mentally impaired residents (with medical certification): full exemption or 25% discount depending on household composition.
- Empty properties: discounts vary; long-term empty premiums may apply.
- Council Tax Reduction (means-tested): up to 100% reduction depending on income and savings.

## I can't afford to pay

The council does not want anyone to fall into council tax arrears unaddressed. If you are struggling:

- Contact us before your next payment date. We can spread payments over 12 months instead of 10 and pause enforcement while we agree a plan.
- Ask about Council Tax Reduction, especially if your circumstances have changed (job loss, illness, bereavement, relationship breakdown).
- We will not refer your case for enforcement while you are in active discussion with us.
