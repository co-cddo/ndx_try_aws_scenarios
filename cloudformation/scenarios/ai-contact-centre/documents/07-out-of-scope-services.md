# Services that are not Aldershire District Council's responsibility

Many resident calls come to the district council that should go to a different authority or service. The bot recognises these out-of-scope intents and signposts.

## Highways and roads

Aldershire **County** Council (different authority) handles: potholes, road resurfacing, traffic signals, road signs, gritting, drains and gulleys on adopted roads, school crossings, parking enforcement on yellow lines.

Phone: 0300 555 1234 (County Council Highways line, 24/7)

The bot can transfer the call (where the contact flow supports a warm transfer) or signpost.

## Schools

Aldershire **County** Council Children's Services handles: school admissions, school transport, school closures, special educational needs, school complaints.

Phone: 0300 555 1235 (County Council Children's Services)

## Adult social care

Aldershire **County** Council Adult Social Care handles: care needs assessments, care home placements, blue badge applications, occupational therapy referrals, safeguarding adults referrals.

Phone: 0300 555 1236 (24/7, safeguarding adults line is staffed out of hours)

## Police

Aldershire Constabulary handles: crime, anti-social behaviour involving threats or violence, hate crime, fraud, online abuse.

- 999 in an emergency.
- 101 for non-emergency police matters.

The bot does not call 999 on a caller's behalf. If the caller is reporting an immediate threat to life, the bot urgently signposts to 999 and offers a warm transfer to a human agent who can stay on the line.

## NHS

NHS services (GP appointments, hospital appointments, mental health crises) are not the council's responsibility.

- 111 for urgent non-emergency NHS advice.
- 999 for life-threatening medical emergencies.
- The Samaritans on 116 123 for mental health crisis.

## When in doubt

If the bot is uncertain whether an issue is in or out of scope, it asks clarifying questions. If it cannot resolve, it routes to a human agent. Bedrock Guardrails ensure the bot does not give wrong-authority signposting that could delay an urgent matter.
