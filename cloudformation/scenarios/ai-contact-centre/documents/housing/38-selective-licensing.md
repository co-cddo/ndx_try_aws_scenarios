# Selective licensing of private landlords

Selective licensing is a tool under Pt 3 Housing Act 2004 that requires landlords of private rented homes in designated areas to hold a licence, meet conditions, and be a fit and proper person.

## Where in Aldershire

Aldershire has 2 selective licensing designations as of April 2026:

- Riverbank Wards (Riverbank North, Riverbank South, Riverside), designated 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2029
- Aldershire Town Central, designated 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2030

A third designation (Westmoor East and Westmoor Central) is in consultation; check aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/selective-licensing for updates.

## Why we designate

Designations are based on evidence of:

- High levels of private renting (typically 19%+)
- Low housing demand
- Significant or persistent ASB linked to PRS conditions
- Poor property conditions

Cabinet approves a designation after a 10-week consultation under s.80 HA 2004. Designations under 20% of stock or 20% of geography are made locally; over 20% require Secretary of State confirmation.

## Who needs a licence

Every private rented dwelling in a designated area must have a licence, even single-tenant properties. Houses in Multiple Occupation (HMOs) need an HMO licence (see HMO Licensing knowledge document); selective licensing is the regime for non-HMO PRS.

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/selective-licence-apply
- Postal Form SL1
- Phone: 01624 460 246. Private Sector Housing Team

Each licence is normally for 5 years. Fee (April 2026): £805 for the standard licence, with discounts for accredited landlords (£605) and early-bird applications (£705).

## Licence conditions

Mandatory conditions include:

- Gas safety certificate (annual)
- Smoke and CO alarms (Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015)
- Electrical Installation Condition Report (5-yearly)
- Tenancy agreement provided in writing
- Deposit protection (one of TDS, DPS, MyDeposits)
- Fit and proper person
- Reasonable management of ASB

Aldershire-specific conditions include:

- Annual visit log of property condition
- Energy efficiency to band E or above
- Maximum occupancy specified

## Failure to license

A criminal offence under s.95 HA 2004, unlimited fine, or financial penalty up to £30,000. Rent Repayment Orders may be made for up to 12 months' rent.
