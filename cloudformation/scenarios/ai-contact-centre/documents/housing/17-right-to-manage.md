# Right to manage and tenant participation

Council tenants have rights to be involved in decisions about their homes. The framework includes the statutory Right to Manage and Aldershire's tenant participation arrangements.

## Right to Manage (RTM)

Under the Tenant Participation Compact and the Housing Act 1985 s.27, secure tenants can form a Tenant Management Organisation (TMO) to take over the day-to-day management of their estate or block, including repairs, allocations, and tenancy management, while Aldershire remains the landlord.

### Forming a TMO

1. Notice of intent, to Aldershire and to all affected tenants
2. Feasibility study (funded by Aldershire), typically 6 months
3. Development phase, training, business plan, governance
4. Continuous tenant ballot, over 50% must vote, and over 50% of voters in favour
5. Modular Management Agreement (MMA) signed
6. Go-live within 12 months of signing

### Aldershire support

Aldershire pays for the feasibility study, ballot administration, and an independent advisor (chosen by tenants from the TPAS register).

## Tenant Voice forums

You do not need to form a TMO to be heard:

- Aldershire Tenant Voice, meets quarterly, elected representatives from each ward
- Sheltered Tenants' Forum, for over-55s schemes
- Repairs and Improvements Panel, scrutinises contractor performance
- Online tenant survey, twice a year (TSM compliant)

## Scrutiny panel

A tenant-led Scrutiny Panel reviews services in depth, with full access to data. Reports are presented to Cabinet.

## Expenses

Tenant volunteers can claim travel, childcare, and care costs for attending meetings. Training is fully funded.
