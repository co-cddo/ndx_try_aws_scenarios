# Homelessness application

If you are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless, Aldershire has duties under Pt VII Housing Act 1996 (as amended by the Homelessness Reduction Act 2017).

## When to apply

You should apply if you:

- Have nowhere to sleep tonight
- Are sleeping rough
- Are sofa-surfing without permission to stay
- Are at risk of being asked to leave (within 56 days)
- Are fleeing domestic abuse
- Have been served notice by your landlord
- Are leaving care, prison, hospital, or the armed forces with no home to go to

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/homelessness-apply (covers s.184 application)
- Phone: 01624 460 222 (8:30 to 17:00 Mon-Fri)
- In person: Civic Centre, Market Square, AL1 2QP, emergency triage 8:30 to 16:00
- Out of hours: 01624 460 199 option 4

## What we do: Section 184 duty

Aldershire must:

1. Take the application (we cannot refuse to take it)
2. Make inquiries to decide the duty owed
3. Issue a written s.184 decision letter within 56 days

The letter sets out:

- Whether you are eligible (immigration status check)
- Whether you are homeless or threatened with homelessness
- Whether you are in priority need (see Priority Need knowledge document)
- Whether you are intentionally homeless
- Whether you have a local connection (see Local Connection knowledge document)

## Prevention duty (s.195)

If you are threatened with homelessness in 56 days, Aldershire works with you on a Personalised Housing Plan (PHP) for 56 days to keep you housed. Tools include:

- Mediation with landlord/family
- Discretionary Housing Payment
- Bond and deposit guarantee for private rented (see Bond Scheme knowledge document)
- Tenancy training, pre-tenancy MOT

## Relief duty (s.189B)

If you are homeless, Aldershire works with you for 56 days to help you find accommodation. Same toolkit plus emergency interim accommodation if you are in priority need (s.188).

## Main housing duty (s.193)

If after relief you remain homeless, eligible, in priority need, and not intentionally homeless, the main duty arises. Aldershire owes you suitable accommodation until that duty ends (e.g. by acceptance of a Pt VI offer, see CBL knowledge document).

## Right of review

You can request a review of any s.184 decision within 21 days. Reviews are conducted by an officer not involved in the original decision.

## Out-of-hours emergency

Call 01624 460 199 option 4, interim accommodation for that night will be arranged where the duty is owed.
