# Asbestos in council homes

Asbestos was widely used in UK building materials before 1999. Many Aldershire council homes built between 1945 and 1985 may contain asbestos-containing materials (ACMs). Aldershire has a duty under the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 to manage these materials.

## Where might asbestos be found?

- Artex or textured ceilings/walls (pre-2000)
- Vinyl floor tiles and bituminous backings
- Soffit boards and rainwater goods
- Insulation board (AIB) on heating cupboards or service ducts
- Sheet boarding behind boilers or fireboxes

Asbestos is only dangerous when fibres become airborne. Undamaged ACMs in good condition are safe and should be left in place under the management plan.

## Aldershire asbestos management

- Every home built before 2000 has a management survey on file (typically completed 2018–2023)
- Surveys are reviewed every 5 years or before any planned works
- A refurbishment or demolition (R&D) survey is commissioned before invasive works (kitchen replacement, rewires, new heating)

## Suspecting asbestos

Do not drill, sand, or scrape suspect materials. Contact us first:

- Phone: 01624 460 199
- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/asbestos
- Bot: takes the location and a photo, dispatches an asbestos surveyor within 5 working days

## Removal

Where removal is necessary it is carried out by a HSE licensed contractor (UKAS-accredited). You will be:

- Notified at least 14 days in advance
- Offered temporary accommodation if the works require you to vacate (rare; most removals are short and on-site)
- Given the air-test certificate after re-occupation

## Health concerns

If you are worried about exposure, speak to your GP. Aldershire can provide a property history extract for medical records on request.
