# Sheltered housing

Sheltered housing in Aldershire offers self-contained flats or bungalows for older people who want to live independently with the security of a scheme manager and on-site support. Aldershire manages 14 sheltered schemes across the district.

## Who can apply

You can apply if you are:

- Aged 60 or over (some schemes 55+; verify per scheme)
- Aged 50+ with a recognised disability or housing need
- Couples: at least one applicant meets the age criterion

You must also meet the general housing register eligibility (see Council Housing Application knowledge document).

## What you get

- A self-contained 1 or 2-bedroom flat or bungalow
- Communal lounge, garden, and laundry
- Scheme manager on-site Monday to Friday (typically 09:00 to 13:00)
- 24/7 emergency call system (CareLink) connecting to a regional response centre
- Adapted features (level access, walk-in shower, rails, low-level sockets)

## What it costs

- Rent (sub-market, set by Aldershire's Rent Policy)
- Service charge for communal areas, garden, scheme manager, and CareLink, typically £18 to £32/week
- Council tax (often Band A or B; you may qualify for CTR, see the Council Tax Reduction document)

## Schemes

Major schemes include:

- Hawthorn Court (Aldershire Town), 28 flats
- Bramble Lodge (East Aldershire), 24 flats
- Rosebay Place (Westmoor), 30 flats including 6 bungalows
- Linden House (Riverbank), 22 flats with extra-care wing

## How to apply

1. Join the housing register (if not already), aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/register-apply
2. Tick "sheltered housing interested" on Form HR1
3. Bid on sheltered properties via Aldershire Homefinder (Wednesday cycle)
4. Visit your shortlisted scheme, viewing within 7 days

## Couples and visitors

Partners, family, and friends can stay overnight. Long-term visitors (over 14 days) require notification to the scheme manager.

## When sheltered isn't enough: extra care

If you need on-site personal care, see the Extra Care Housing knowledge document.
