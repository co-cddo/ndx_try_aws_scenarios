# Intentional homelessness

Even if you are eligible, homeless, and in priority need, Aldershire's main housing duty is reduced if you are found intentionally homeless under s.191 Housing Act 1996.

## What "intentional" means

You are intentionally homeless if:

1. You did or failed to do something
2. As a result, you ceased to occupy accommodation
3. The accommodation was available for you
4. The accommodation was reasonable to continue to occupy
5. The act/omission was deliberate

## Common intentional-homelessness scenarios

- Eviction for arrears where you could have paid (and the rent was affordable)
- Eviction for ASB perpetrated by you or a household member
- Leaving suitable accommodation voluntarily without good reason
- Refusing to take up suitable accommodation offered to you
- Selling or relinquishing a tenancy without good reason

## What is NOT intentional

- Eviction for arrears where the rent was unaffordable
- Eviction caused by changes in benefits beyond your control
- Fleeing domestic abuse, or violence/threats from anyone
- Acting in good faith in ignorance of a relevant fact (e.g. unknowingly losing a tenancy)
- Where mental capacity affects the act (Aldershire applies the MCA 2005 test)

## Burden of proof

Aldershire must show, on the balance of probabilities, that all five tests are met. We cannot find you intentionally homeless on suspicion or on a vague history.

## What if I am found intentional

You are owed:

- A reasonable period of temporary accommodation to give you time to find your own home, typically 28 to 56 days (s.190 HA 1996)
- Advice and assistance to find your own accommodation (Personalised Housing Plan)
- Continuing relief duty for 56 days

You can still apply to the housing register but at Band C or D.

## Right of review

You have 21 days to request a review under s.202. The review takes up to 56 days. You can extend this with the reviewing officer.
