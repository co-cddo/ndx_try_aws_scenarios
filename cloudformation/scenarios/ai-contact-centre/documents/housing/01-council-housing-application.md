# Council housing application

To apply for a council or housing-association home in Aldershire you must join the Aldershire Housing Register. The register is the single waiting list shared between Aldershire District Council and our partner registered providers (Aldershire Homes, Westmoor Housing, and Riverbank HA).

## Eligibility

Applicants must normally:

- Be aged 16 or over (under-18s require a guarantor)
- Have a local connection to Aldershire (see the local connection knowledge document)
- Have eligible immigration status; persons subject to immigration control are excluded under the Allocation of Housing Regulations 2006
- Not have unspent housing-related debt over £500 (subject to the Reasonable Preference exemptions in s.166A Housing Act 1996)

## Points and banding

Aldershire uses a banding scheme (A, B, C, D) underpinned by a points calculation. Points reflect medical need, overcrowding, time on the register, and welfare grounds. The full scheme is published as the Aldershire Allocations Policy v7.2 (March 2026).

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/register-apply (preferred; takes about 25 minutes)
- Phone: the Housing Options team on 01624 460 200, Monday to Thursday 09:00 to 17:00, Friday 09:00 to 16:30
- In person: Civic Centre, Market Square, Aldershire AL1 2QP. Tuesday and Thursday 10:00 to 15:00 by appointment

## Required evidence

- Proof of identity (passport, biometric residence permit, or driving licence) for every adult on the application
- Proof of address for the last 5 years
- Proof of household income and benefits
- Medical evidence (if claiming medical priority)
- Pregnancy confirmation (MAT B1) where relevant

## Service standard

Applications are assessed within 28 working days. You receive a written banding decision and a unique applicant reference. You can review or appeal the band within 21 days of the decision.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not assess banding, override eligibility decisions, or guarantee a home. It captures contact details (Contact Lens redacts National Insurance numbers and date of birth), creates a case, and routes to the Housing Options team.
