# Bond and deposit scheme

Aldershire's Rent Deposit and Bond Scheme helps eligible households access the private rented sector when they cannot afford a deposit and rent in advance. The scheme is a tool of homelessness prevention and relief.

## Who can apply

You can apply if:

- You are owed the prevention or relief duty (or main duty) under Pt VII HA 1996, OR
- You are at imminent risk of homelessness (within 56 days), OR
- You are leaving Aldershire's TA, hostel, or refuge with a positive move-on plan

You must:

- Have eligible immigration status
- Pass an affordability check (rent + bills must be sustainable on your income)
- Agree to a pre-tenancy MOT (1 hour with a Housing Options officer)

## What the scheme provides

- A written bond (guarantee) up to the equivalent of 5 weeks' rent (capped at £1,500), covering the deposit
- One month's rent in advance as a recoverable loan, repayable at £20/week or 25% of any DHP awarded
- Tenancy training before moving in
- 6 months of in-tenancy support

## How it works for the landlord

- Aldershire's bond is a written guarantee, no cash deposit is held by the Tenancy Deposit Scheme during the bond period
- If the landlord makes a verified claim at the end of the tenancy, Aldershire pays direct to the landlord
- Aldershire then pursues recovery from the tenant (pre-arranged via the agreement)
- After 12 months, the bond can be converted to a cash deposit funded by Aldershire's revolving fund

## How to apply

1. Speak to a Housing Options officer first, call 01624 460 222
2. Complete Form BD1, aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/bond-scheme
3. Affordability and ID check (1 to 5 working days)
4. Once approved, you are matched with willing landlords on our register
5. Sign tenancy with a Tenancy Officer present

## Landlord register

Landlords sign a Code of Conduct and provide:

- Gas safety certificate (current)
- EPC of E or above
- Electrical Installation Condition Report (5-yearly)
- Smoke and CO alarms compliant with the 2015 regs

## Limits

- Rent must be at or below the Local Housing Allowance rate
- 12-month minimum tenancy
- Maximum 5 households per landlord per year
