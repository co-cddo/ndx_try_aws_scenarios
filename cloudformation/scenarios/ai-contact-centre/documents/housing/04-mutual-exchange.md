# Mutual exchange

Mutual exchange lets council and housing-association tenants swap homes with another social tenant, locally or nationally. Exchanges are governed by the Housing Act 1985 (secure tenants) and Housing Act 1988 (assured tenants).

## Who can exchange

- Aldershire secure or flexible tenants whose tenancy has not been served notice
- Tenants whose rent account is not in arrears (or in arrears below £100 and on a sustained payment plan)
- Tenants who have not received a formal warning for anti-social behaviour in the last 12 months
- Joint tenants: both must agree and sign

## How to find a swap

Aldershire is a member of HomeSwapper (free for tenants). Register at homeswapper.example.co.uk using your tenancy reference. The bot can issue a HomeSwapper invitation to your phone or email.

## Application process

1. Find a willing match
2. Each tenant submits a Mutual Exchange Application (Form ME1), online at aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/mutual-exchange
3. Aldershire has 42 days from receipt of all applications to decide (s.92 Housing Act 1985)
4. Inspections of both homes are arranged within 21 days
5. A gas safety check, electrical certificate, and EPC are completed before sign-off

## Grounds for refusal

Refusal must be on a statutory ground in Schedule 3 of the Housing Act 1985, including:

- Possession order made or served
- Property substantially larger than reasonably required
- Property designed for a person with a disability and the incoming tenant has no such need
- Property is part of supported accommodation

## Costs

There is no fee. Tenants pay their own removal costs. Outstanding rechargeable repairs must be settled before exchange.

## Service standard

Decisions in 42 days. Sign-up appointments offered within 14 days of approval.
