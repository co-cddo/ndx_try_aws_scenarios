# Damp and mould

Aldershire has a duty to keep homes free from category 1 hazards under the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (Housing Act 2004) and to act on damp and mould under s.10A Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (Awaab's Law). We treat reports as a priority.

## Reporting damp or mould

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/damp-mould (please attach photos)
- Phone: 01624 460 199, 08:00 to 20:00
- Bot: takes the report, triages severity, and books an inspection

## Response standards (Awaab's Law-aligned)

- Initial inspection: within 14 days of report
- Significant hazard: works begin within 7 days of inspection
- Emergency hazard (risk to health): action within 24 hours

## What the inspection covers

A surveyor visits to check:

- Building fabric (roof, gutters, pointing, render, windows)
- Heating and ventilation (extractor fans, trickle vents, boiler operation)
- Insulation (loft, cavity walls)
- Internal moisture sources (washing drying, leaks, blocked extractors)

## Likely works

- Mould wash and stain-block treatment (within 7 days of inspection)
- Replacement extractor fans (humidity-tracking type)
- External pointing or render repairs
- Loft insulation top-up to 270mm
- Positive Input Ventilation (PIV) unit where appropriate

## Escalation

If you are unhappy with progress:

1. Stage 1 complaint to Aldershire Housing, 10 working days
2. Stage 2 complaint to the Director of Housing, 20 working days
3. Housing Ombudsman (housing-ombudsman.org.uk), independent review
4. Environmental Health (Aldershire Public Protection) can serve an Improvement Notice under s.11 Housing Act 2004 in serious cases, 01624 460 305

## Vulnerable households

If anyone in the home has asthma, COPD, or a respiratory condition, tell us at the time of report, we prioritise the inspection within 7 days.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not diagnose damp causes or commit to remedial works. It logs the report, triggers Awaab's Law clock, and escalates to a damp-and-mould officer for any case where a child under 5 lives at the property.
