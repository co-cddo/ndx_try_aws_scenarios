# Housing register banding

Aldershire allocates social housing using a four-band scheme set out in the Aldershire Allocations Policy. Banding determines how often you can bid and how quickly you are likely to be rehoused.

## Band A: emergency/critical need

- Statutorily homeless households owed the main duty under s.193 Housing Act 1996
- Households at imminent risk of significant harm (domestic abuse, witness protection)
- Severe medical or disability need where the current home directly threatens life or limb
- Tenants required to move under a management transfer

Band A applicants are usually rehoused within 12 weeks. Direct lets may apply (no bidding required).

## Band B: high need

- Significant overcrowding (lacking 2 or more bedrooms by the bedroom standard)
- Serious disrepair where the landlord has failed to act
- Care leavers aged 18 to 21 with a local connection
- Households moving on from supported accommodation with a positive move-on plan

## Band C: moderate need

- Lacking 1 bedroom (overcrowded by one room)
- Sharing kitchen or bathroom with another household
- Moderate medical need (some adaptation possible in current home)
- Under-occupation by 1 bedroom (downsizers from social rented homes)

## Band D: low or no priority

- Adequately housed with no identified housing need
- Persons owed lower duties under Pt VII Housing Act 1996 (prevention/relief discharged)
- Discretionary applicants without preference grounds

## Effective date

Each band has an effective date, the date you first qualified for that band. Where two applicants share a band, the earlier effective date wins the bid.

## Reviews

You can request a review of your band within 21 days under s.166A(9) Housing Act 1996. Reviews are conducted by an officer not involved in the original decision.

Phone: 01624 460 201 (allocations).  Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/banding-review.
