# Local connection

If you apply as homeless to Aldershire but your local connection is to another council, Aldershire may refer you to that council under s.198 Housing Act 1996. Local connection also affects allocation of social housing under the Aldershire Allocations Policy.

## What counts as a local connection

Section 199 Housing Act 1996 sets out the tests:

1. Residence, you have lived in Aldershire by your own choice for:
   - 6 of the last 12 months, OR
   - 3 of the last 5 years
2. Employment, you (or a household member) have settled employment in Aldershire (typically 16+ hours/week, sustained 6+ months)
3. Family association, close relatives (parents, adult children, siblings) have lived in Aldershire for 5+ years
4. Special circumstances, care leaver placed in Aldershire, fleeing domestic abuse to a safe area, armed forces deployment

Time in prison, hospital, or armed forces accommodation does not count as voluntary residence.

## What if you have no local connection anywhere

You can still apply to Aldershire. If we accept the s.193 main duty we discharge it locally, we cannot refer to "no council".

## When Aldershire will refer

Aldershire may refer your application to the council where you have a local connection if:

- You have a local connection to that other council, AND
- You do not have a local connection to Aldershire, AND
- You will not be at risk of violence in the receiving area

## Domestic abuse exception

Aldershire will not refer you back to an area where you fled abuse. Section 198(2A) HA 1996 explicitly protects you. We can also accept your application even where your only connection is somewhere unsafe.

## Allocations and local connection

For Aldershire's housing register (CBL):

- Bands A–C generally require local connection
- Band D may include applicants with no local connection
- Reasonable Preference categories under s.166A(3) HA 1996 are not refused on local-connection grounds alone

## Reviewing a local-connection decision

You have 21 days under s.202 to request a review of a referral decision.

## Evidence we accept

- Tenancy agreements, council tax bills, utility bills
- Payslips or letter from employer
- Birth certificates, marriage certificates for family ties
- Medical and social-care records for vulnerable cases
