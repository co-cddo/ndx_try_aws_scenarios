# Gas safety checks

Aldershire has a statutory duty under the Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998, regulation 36, to check every gas appliance and flue in council homes annually and to provide tenants with a Landlord Gas Safety Record (LGSR, often called the CP12).

## What is checked

- All gas appliances owned by Aldershire (boiler, gas fire, cooker if landlord-supplied)
- Flues and ventilation
- Gas pipework integrity
- Carbon monoxide alarm operation (mandatory in every room with a fixed combustion appliance, Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015)

## When does the check happen?

- 10 to 12 months after the last check, unless an earlier visit is needed (e.g. void inspection)
- You receive 28 days' written notice with an appointment slot
- You can rebook once via aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/gas-check or by calling 01624 460 199

## Tenant obligation to give access

Refusal to give access is a breach of the tenancy agreement (clause 9.2). The escalation path is:

1. First missed appointment: rebooking letter
2. Second missed appointment: a "no access" card and a 7-day warning letter
3. Continued refusal: an injunction application under s.3 Housing Act 1985, costs may be recharged
4. Persistent refusal can ultimately lead to possession proceedings on Ground 12

## Tenant-owned appliances

If you own a gas cooker or gas fire, Aldershire still checks that the connection to the gas pipework is safe but does not service the appliance. You must ensure your own appliance is maintained by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

## Carbon monoxide

If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999, open windows, and leave the property. If your CO alarm sounds, leave the property and call 01624 460 199.
