# Boiler repairs

Aldershire is the responsible landlord for the heating system in council homes. Most homes have a gas combi boiler; some sheltered schemes use district heating.

## Reporting a boiler fault

- 24/7 emergency line: 01624 460 199
- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/boiler-repair
- Bot: triage by symptom (no heating, no hot water, leaking, error code) and dispatch a Gas Safe engineer

## Response times

- Total loss of heating or hot water in winter (1 October to 30 April): attended within 24 hours
- Total loss of heating or hot water outside winter: attended within 3 working days
- Partial loss (e.g. one radiator cold): within 5 working days
- Boiler making unusual noise but functional: within 10 working days

If the engineer cannot fix on first visit, temporary heaters (oil-filled radiators) are provided to vulnerable households (over-65s, under-5s, pregnant tenants, disabled tenants) within 2 hours.

## What you can do first

- Check the pressure gauge: it should read 1.0 to 1.5 bar when the system is cold. The bot can talk you through repressurisation.
- Check the thermostat batteries
- Check the boiler is plugged in and the gas supply is on
- Note any error code on the boiler display

## Out of hours

The 24/7 line is staffed by Aldershire Property Services. Out-of-hours engineers are dispatched for genuine emergencies only. Non-emergency calls are logged for next-working-day triage.

## Annual service

Every boiler is serviced annually as part of the Landlord Gas Safety Record (LGSR/CP12). See the Gas Safety Checks knowledge document.

## Recharges

If a fault is caused by tenant misuse (e.g. tampering with the gas supply, draining the system) the cost may be recharged. The engineer notes this on the works order; you have 21 days to dispute under the Tenancy Agreement (clause 14.3).

Contact: 01624 460 199 (heating helpline).
