# Mortgage rescue: last-resort scheme

Aldershire's Mortgage Rescue Scheme is a last-resort tool to prevent homelessness for owner-occupiers facing repossession. It is a discretionary scheme funded from the Homelessness Prevention Grant.

## Who it is for

Owner-occupiers (and shared-ownership homeowners) at risk of repossession by their mortgage lender. You are likely eligible if:

- You have received an arrears letter or pre-action protocol letter
- A possession claim has been issued or a hearing date set
- You are in priority need (or your household is) under HA 1996, see Priority Need knowledge document
- You have actively engaged with your lender (e.g. payment plan, term extension, interest-only switch)
- You have explored Support for Mortgage Interest (SMI) and Discretionary Housing Payment is not appropriate (DHP doesn't cover mortgages)

## Two mortgage rescue routes

### Route 1: assistance toward arrears

A small interest-bearing loan (max £8,000) used to clear arrears, secured against the property. Repayable on sale or refinance. Aldershire's loan ranks behind the mortgage.

### Route 2: mortgage-to-rent (sale to a partner registered provider)

Aldershire matches you with a partner registered provider (Westmoor HA or Riverbank HA) who:

- Buys your property at independent market valuation
- Grants you an assured tenancy at the same address
- You become a social tenant, paying social rent

You lose ownership but stay in the home. Equity (sale price minus mortgage) is paid to you (less Aldershire's costs).

## What you must do first

Before applying:

1. Get free debt advice. Aldershire Citizens Advice (0808 800 900) or StepChange (0800 138 1111)
2. Talk to your lender about all forbearance options (FCA MCOB 13)
3. Explore SMI (loan from DWP), gov.uk/support-for-mortgage-interest
4. Explore selling on the open market (with our Empty Homes Officer if you're stuck)

## How to apply

- Phone: 01624 460 240. Mortgage Rescue Officer, Tuesday and Thursday 10:00 to 16:00
- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/mortgage-rescue
- Letter from a debt adviser usually speeds up triage

## Service standard

- Initial assessment within 10 working days
- Full panel decision within 56 days (urgent cases, court date within 28 days, within 14 days)
- Completion within 12 weeks of approval

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not approve loans or buy your home. It books a callback, sends the debt-advice signposts, and confirms court dates if you've shared them.
