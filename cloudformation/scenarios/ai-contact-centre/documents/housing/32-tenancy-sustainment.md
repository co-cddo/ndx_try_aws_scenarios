# Tenancy sustainment

Aldershire's Tenancy Sustainment Service helps tenants stay in their homes when life gets difficult. Support is voluntary, free, and time-limited (typically 12 weeks, extendable to 26).

## Who we work with

We support tenants who are:

- New to a tenancy and need help to settle
- In rent arrears or facing arrears
- At risk of eviction
- Leaving hostel, refuge, prison, or care
- Affected by mental health, substance use, or learning disability
- Hoarders or struggling with home conditions
- Bereaved or recently separated
- Domestic abuse survivors moving on
- Overwhelmed by paperwork, benefits, or debt

## What support workers do

- 1-to-1 home visits (weekly to start, then fortnightly)
- Help with benefits, including UC, PIP, ESA, Carer's Allowance
- Budgeting and debt support, referral to FCA-regulated advice (Aldershire Citizens Advice)
- Liaison with creditors, landlord, utilities
- Setting up direct debits, paying bills, changing tariffs
- Help with letters and forms
- Support to attend court, GP, mental-health appointments
- Connection to community groups and peer support

## Typical outcomes

In 2025/26:

- 87% of tenants kept their tenancy at 6 months
- 78% reduced their arrears
- 64% improved their wellbeing score (DIALOG)
- 54% increased their income through correct benefit claims

## How to refer

- Self-referral: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/sustainment
- Phone: 01624 460 227, 09:00 to 17:00
- Bot: takes a referral with consent and sends to the Tenancy Sustainment Coordinator
- Aldershire staff (rents, ASB, allocations) refer directly via internal CRM

## Eligibility

You must:

- Hold an Aldershire tenancy (or be moving into one), partner-housing-association tenants supported via cross-funding
- Consent to the work
- Have at least one identified housing-related need

## Confidentiality

We follow GDPR and information-sharing protocols. Information is shared with rent and ASB teams unless you opt out (with documented reasons).

## Couples and families

We work with the household as a unit where everyone consents.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not deliver sustainment work directly. It places a referral, books the first visit, and confirms by SMS within 1 working day.
