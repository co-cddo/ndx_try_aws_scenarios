# Domestic abuse: housing safety

If you are experiencing domestic abuse, Aldershire's housing service can help, whether you want to stay safely in your home or leave.

## Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, call 999. If you cannot speak, dial 999 and press 55.

## Aldershire Domestic Abuse Service (24/7)

- Phone: 0808 200 0247 (free from any phone, doesn't appear on most bills)
- Online: aldershire-da.example.gov.uk
- Text: 80800

## National 24/7 lines

- National Domestic Abuse Helpline (Refuge): 0808 2000 247
- Men's Advice Line: 0808 801 0327
- Galop (LGBT+): 0800 999 5428

## Sanctuary Scheme: staying safely at home

Aldershire's Sanctuary Scheme adapts your home to make it safer where you choose to remain. Free works include:

- Reinforced doors and locks (anti-snap, anti-bump)
- Window restrictors and shock alarms
- External lighting and CCTV
- A "sanctuary room", secure room you can retreat to with a phone
- Letterbox guards (against accelerant attacks)
- Personal alarms

Eligibility: any tenure, where the police or an IDVA assess the threat. Apply via the DA Service or call 01624 460 250.

## Leaving safely

We can help you:

- Find emergency refuge, 24/7 placement support nationally
- Apply as homeless under Pt VII HA 1996, automatic priority need under Domestic Abuse Act 2021 s.78
- Bypass local-connection rules, s.198(2A) HA 1996
- Use the Bond and Deposit Scheme to access private rented in a safe area
- Make a sole-tenancy application even if your name is not on the original tenancy
- Get a bond and a deposit covered (see Bond Scheme document)

## In-situ tenancy support

If your perpetrator is your tenant or partner, we can:

- Apply for a Domestic Abuse Protection Notice/Order (DAPN/DAPO)
- Pursue tenancy action against the perpetrator (Ground 14A, HA 1985/1988)
- Evict the perpetrator under absolute possession (s.84A HA 1985)
- Reassign the tenancy to the survivor

## Children and pets

- Children's Services join the team around your case
- Pets can be fostered free via the Cinnamon Trust or Dogs Trust Hope Project
- Refuges that accept pets are listed on Aldershire's local map (limited but growing)

## Confidentiality

We do not contact the perpetrator without your consent. Information is shared with police, IDVA, and (where children are at risk) Children's Services under the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 information-sharing duty.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot will not call the perpetrator, leave voicemails on shared phones, or send unredacted email confirmations to a household address. It can call the DA Service on your behalf, book a safe call back, and create a flagged case.
