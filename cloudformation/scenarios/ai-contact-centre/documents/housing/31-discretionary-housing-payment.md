# Discretionary Housing Payment (DHP)

DHP is a discretionary, time-limited top-up to help with housing costs when Housing Benefit or the Universal Credit Housing Element does not cover your rent and you have a shortfall.

## Who can apply

You can apply if you receive Housing Benefit or the Universal Credit Housing Element and have a shortfall caused by:

- The bedroom tax (under-occupation reduction)
- The Benefit Cap
- Local Housing Allowance below market rent (private renters)
- Non-dependent deductions
- A temporary financial crisis affecting your ability to pay

## Who cannot use DHP

DHP cannot pay for:

- Council Tax (use Council Tax Reduction or Council Tax discretionary discount)
- Service charges that are not eligible for HB/UC housing element
- Rent arrears that pre-date the DHP claim (in most cases)

## How much

DHP awards are need-based. Aldershire's discretionary policy considers:

- Severity of shortfall
- Health and disability factors
- Children's circumstances and schooling
- Domestic abuse history
- Whether you are actively looking for cheaper accommodation, work, or both

The maximum award typically does not exceed 26 weeks at a time, though severe-disability and domestic-abuse cases may be funded longer.

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/dhp
- Phone: 01624 460 226 (Benefits team)
- Postal Form DHP1 from the Civic Centre

You will need:

- Your HB or UC reference
- Bank statements (last 2 months)
- Tenancy agreement
- Evidence of any special circumstances (medical, abuse, debt, school admissions)

## Decision

Aldershire decides within 21 days of all evidence being received. The decision is sent in writing.

## Reviews and appeals

DHP is discretionary, so there is no statutory right of appeal. Aldershire offers an internal review by a senior officer, request within 28 days. Beyond that, judicial review is the only route.

## Annual budget

DHP is funded by a fixed government grant. When the budget is exhausted (usually by January) only emergency awards continue.
