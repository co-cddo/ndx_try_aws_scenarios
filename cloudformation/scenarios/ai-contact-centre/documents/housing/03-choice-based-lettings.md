# Choice-based lettings

Aldershire operates a choice-based lettings (CBL) scheme called Aldershire Homefinder. Applicants in Bands A, B, and C bid for homes that match their household need.

## Bidding cycle

- The cycle opens every Wednesday at 00:01 and closes the following Tuesday at 23:59
- New properties are advertised on Wednesday morning
- Shortlists are produced Wednesday afternoon and viewings offered the following week

## How to bid

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/homefinder (login with your applicant reference and date of birth)
- SMS: text BID followed by the property reference to 80018 (standard message rate)
- Phone (assisted bidding): 01624 460 202, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 16:00

You may place up to 3 bids per cycle. Auto-bids can be set for matching property types if you have not bid in 3 consecutive cycles.

## Eligibility filters

Each advert lists eligibility filters such as:

- Minimum and maximum household size
- Bedroom need (matched to the bedroom standard in the Allocations Policy)
- Age criteria (e.g. over-55s for sheltered schemes)
- Local connection or community connection requirements

If you do not meet a filter, you cannot bid.

## Shortlisting

Properties are shortlisted in band order, then by effective date. The top-ranked applicant is offered the home, subject to verification (rent account check, identity check, references).

## What if I refuse an offer?

A reasonable offer can only be refused once. A second refusal of a reasonable offer leads to a 6-month suspension of your application under the Allocations Policy.

## Need help bidding?

Tenancy support workers and the bot can take a verbal bid up to 17:00 on the closing day. Bids placed by phone are confirmed by SMS.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not change your band, override eligibility filters, or jump you up the shortlist. It can read out the current adverts, place an assisted bid against your applicant reference, and book a viewing.
