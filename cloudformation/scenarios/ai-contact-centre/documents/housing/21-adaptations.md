# Adaptations and Disabled Facilities Grant

If you or someone in your household is disabled, you may be entitled to adaptations to your home. The route depends on your tenure.

## Council tenants: Aldershire-funded adaptations

Aldershire pays for adaptations in council homes following an occupational therapist (OT) assessment. There is no means test for council tenants.

Common adaptations:

- Grab rails, lever taps, half-steps
- Stair lifts
- Level-access showers (wet rooms)
- Ramps and external handrails
- Door-widening
- Ceiling track hoists
- Through-floor lifts (exceptional cases)

## Owner-occupiers and private tenants: Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG)

DFG is a means-tested mandatory grant under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996. The maximum mandatory grant in England is £30,000 (Aldershire tops this up to £45,000 for severe cases under our Discretionary Top-Up Policy).

DFG funds:

- Access into and around the home
- Bathroom adaptations (level-access shower, accessible WC)
- Kitchen adaptations (height-adjustable units)
- Heating where current system is unsuitable for the disabled person's health
- Suitable means of access between rooms

## How to apply

1. Contact Aldershire Adult Social Care for an OT assessment: 01624 460 600
2. The OT assesses needs and recommends adaptations
3. For DFG: Aldershire's Home Improvement Agency at aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/dfg processes the means test and contracts the works
4. For council tenant adaptations: Aldershire Property Services contracts the works directly

## Service standard

- OT initial visit: within 28 days of referral (urgent: within 5 days)
- DFG approval: within 12 weeks of full application
- Works start: within 6 weeks of approval (small works) or 12 weeks (larger works)

## Children

Children's adaptations follow the same route. The means test ignores parental income for adaptations to support a disabled child (passported under the 2018 Regulations).

## Children's Equipment Service

Smaller equipment (e.g. bath seats, raised toilet seats) is loaned by Aldershire's Equipment Service free of charge, call 01624 460 601.
