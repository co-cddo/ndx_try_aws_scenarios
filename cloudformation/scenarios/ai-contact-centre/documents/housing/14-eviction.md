# Eviction

Eviction is always a last resort and only happens through the courts. Aldershire follows the protocol set by the Civil Procedure Rules and the Pre-Action Protocol for Possession Claims by Social Landlords.

## Grounds for possession

Possession actions are brought on a statutory ground:

- Ground 1, rent arrears (mandatory if 8+ weeks at hearing)
- Ground 2, anti-social behaviour (mandatory ground 2A under absolute possession)
- Ground 12, breach of tenancy (e.g. refusing gas access)
- Ground 14, domestic abuse (perpetrator only, post-conviction)

## Court process: typical timeline

1. Pre-action letter and last opportunity to pay/comply
2. Notice of Seeking Possession (NoSP), at least 4 weeks
3. Claim issued at the County Court, defence period 14 days
4. Hearing listed, typically 6 to 8 weeks after issue
5. Possession order, outright or suspended
6. Application for warrant of possession (if breached)
7. Notice of Eviction (N54), 14 days minimum
8. Bailiff attendance, locks changed, eviction complete

## Suspended orders

A suspended possession order means you can stay if you keep to the conditions (usually rent + arrears amount). One missed payment can trigger an application for a warrant, get advice immediately.

## Your rights

- You have the right to legal aid for possession defence (income-tested), call Civil Legal Advice 0345 345 4 345
- The court duty desk at Aldershire County Court (Holland Street, AL1 5HJ) is open every Tuesday 09:30 to 12:30, no appointment needed
- Aldershire Citizens Advice runs eviction prevention surgeries: 0808 800 900

## What Aldershire offers

- An Income Officer assigned to your case
- Tenancy Sustainment referral within 24 hours of hearing date
- DHP application support (see the DHP knowledge document)
- Mortgage Rescue or downsizing options where relevant

## Illegal eviction

It is a criminal offence under s.1 Protection from Eviction Act 1977 for any landlord (including private) to evict without a court order. If you are being threatened with illegal eviction call Aldershire Tenancy Relations on 01624 460 209.
