# Pet policy in council homes

Aldershire allows tenants to keep pets in most council homes, subject to written permission and reasonable conditions. This policy implements clause 8 of the Tenancy Agreement.

## Houses and ground-floor flats with private gardens

You may keep:

- One dog (with restrictions, see below)
- Up to two cats
- Small caged pets (rabbits, guinea pigs, rats, reptiles, birds, fish)

Permission is granted by default, please notify us within 14 days of getting the pet via aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/pet-notification.

## Flats above ground floor

You may keep:

- Up to two cats
- Small caged pets

Dogs are not permitted in flats above ground floor unless:

- It is a registered assistance dog (Equality Act 2010)
- Aldershire has granted express permission for an exceptional case (e.g. mental-health support)

## Dog restrictions

The following are prohibited under the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991, as amended:

- Pit Bull Terrier
- Japanese Tosa
- Dogo Argentino
- Fila Brasileiro
- XL Bully (banned 2024 unless on the Index of Exempted Dogs and microchipped, neutered, muzzled in public)

You must:

- Microchip your dog (Microchipping of Dogs Regulations 2015)
- Pick up after your dog in shared areas (Public Spaces Protection Order, fixed penalty £100)
- Keep your dog under control at all times

Persistent fouling, barking, or aggression is treated as ASB (see ASB knowledge document).

## Sheltered and supported housing

Pets in sheltered or extra-care schemes are decided case by case, considering the welfare of all residents. Apply at the time of viewing.

## Number limits

A maximum of two pets per household (excluding fish and small caged animals). Exceptions for fosterers registered with a recognised charity (RSPCA, Dogs Trust), apply for a written variation.

## Insurance

We strongly recommend public liability insurance for dog owners. Many home contents insurance policies include this.
