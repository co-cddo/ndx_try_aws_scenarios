# Decoration allowance

Aldershire provides a decoration allowance to help new tenants and tenants whose homes have been disrupted by major works to decorate internally.

## Who qualifies

You may receive a decoration allowance if:

- You have just signed a new tenancy on a void property where Aldershire has not redecorated (most "Aldershire Standard" voids are decorated as part of the void; allowance applies to "Aldershire Basic" lets where the tenant has chosen to decorate themselves in exchange for an allowance and an extended start date)
- Your home has been substantially disrupted by major works (kitchen replacement, rewire, structural repairs, fire damage repair) and rooms need redecorating
- You are a successor whose tenancy has just transferred and the property requires immediate redecoration

## How much

Allowance is set per room (April 2026 rates):

- Living room or kitchen: £140
- Bedroom: £120
- Bathroom: £80
- Hallway: £100

Maximum allowance per property: £600. Cash is not paid; allowance is given as:

- B&Q Aldershire vouchers (collected at sign-up); or
- Credit applied to your rent account (your choice at sign-up)

## How to claim

1. The allowance is offered automatically at sign-up for qualifying lets, sign Form DA1
2. Mid-tenancy claims (after major works): apply within 28 days of works completion via aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/decoration-allowance
3. A Tenancy Officer inspects within 14 days
4. Vouchers issued within 21 days

## Conditions

- Decoration must be completed within 6 months
- A post-decoration inspection is offered (not mandatory)
- Wallpaper and flooring patterns must be reasonable (the bot can show the policy guidance images)
- No textured coatings on ceilings (asbestos risk in older homes)

## What is not covered

- New furniture, white goods, or curtains
- Skim plastering (this is a landlord repair)
- Damage caused by the tenant
- External decoration (Aldershire does this on a 7-year cycle)

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not assess the amount or expedite payment. It records a claim, books the inspection, and issues a reference number.
