# Hostels for people who are homeless

Aldershire commissions hostel and supported housing for people who are homeless or at risk. The largest direct-access hostel is Aldershire House (Riverbank).

## Aldershire House

- 24 single en-suite rooms for adults aged 18+
- 24/7 staffing, keyworker on every shift
- Direct access (no appointment) for rough sleepers between 19:30 and 09:00
- Day-time referrals via the Housing Options service
- Average stay 3 to 6 months
- Address: 14 Riverside Lane, Riverbank, AL3 7QF
- Phone: 01624 460 220

Aldershire House is run by Riverbank Housing Group on behalf of Aldershire DC.

## Other commissioned services

- The Bridge (East Aldershire), 20 beds, 18 to 25 year olds, foyer model
- Magnolia House (Westmoor), 12 beds, women fleeing domestic abuse (referral only)
- Step Up (Aldershire Town), 8 self-contained move-on flats for hostel leavers

## How to access

- Direct presentation at Aldershire House between 19:30 and 09:00
- Aldershire Housing Options: 01624 460 222 (8:30 to 17:00 Mon-Fri)
- Out-of-hours: 01624 460 199, choose option 4
- Police, hospital A&E, or an outreach worker can refer 24/7
- Online self-referral: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/hostel

## What is offered

- A safe, lockable room
- 3 meals/day (Aldershire House) or self-catering kitchens (others)
- Keyworker support every 7 days
- Help with benefits, ID, GP registration, mental health, addiction
- Move-on planning from week 1

## Charges

You pay rent (covered by Universal Credit Housing Element or Housing Benefit if entitled). A small personal contribution may apply if you are working.

## Pets

Aldershire House welcomes pets (registered, well-behaved). Other schemes vary, call to check.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot reserve a hostel bed in advance. Aldershire House operates direct-access only after 19:30. The bot can place a same-day Housing Options referral and provide directions to the building.
