# Housing complaints

Aldershire's housing complaints process applies to council tenants, leaseholders, applicants, and people we owe a homelessness duty. It complies with the Housing Ombudsman Complaint Handling Code 2024 and the Social Housing (Regulation) Act 2023.

## What you can complain about

- Poor or delayed repairs (including damp and mould)
- Behaviour of an Aldershire officer or contractor
- A decision on your housing register banding or homelessness application (note: many decisions also have statutory review rights, see relevant document)
- Anti-social behaviour case handling
- Service failure (missed appointments, lost paperwork)
- Communication failures
- Any neighbour-related dispute we have failed to address

## Stage 1: local resolution

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/complaints
- Phone: 01624 460 100 (general complaints line, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00)
- Email: complaints@aldershire.example.gov.uk
- In writing: Customer Resolution, Aldershire Civic Centre, AL1 2QP

We acknowledge within 5 working days and respond fully within 10 working days. We can extend by 10 days with reasons.

## Stage 2: internal review

If you're not happy with the Stage 1 outcome you can escalate within 28 days. A senior officer (Director of Housing or delegate) reviews. We respond within 20 working days, extendable by 20 days with reasons.

## Housing Ombudsman

After Stage 2, complaints about your tenancy or housing service can go to the Housing Ombudsman:

- Web: housing-ombudsman.example.org.uk
- Phone: 0300 111 3000
- Post: Housing Ombudsman Service, PO Box 152, Liverpool L33 7WQ

The Ombudsman can issue determinations and orders for redress, including financial compensation. Aldershire reports on Ombudsman cases publicly (Tenancy Standard 2024).

## Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman

For homelessness, council-tax, and benefits complaints (rather than tenancy):

- Web: lgo.example.org.uk
- Phone: 0300 061 0614

## Compensation

We may offer compensation for service failure following the Housing Ombudsman Remedies Guidance:

- Time and trouble (up to £150)
- Distress (up to £600)
- Loss of amenity (case-by-case for repairs)
- Specific financial loss (recoverable on evidence)

## Designated Persons

You can also raise concerns with a Designated Person (an MP, councillor, or recognised tenant panel) who can help mediate or refer to the Ombudsman.

## Vexatious complaints

We treat all complaints seriously. We have an Unreasonable Behaviour Policy for the small number of cases involving abuse of staff or repeated raising of resolved issues, escalation is decided by the Director.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not decide complaints. It logs the complaint, captures the outcome you want, redacts personal data from public-facing fields, and sends an SMS confirmation. Statutory review decisions (homelessness, banding) also remain available regardless of any complaint.
