# Right to buy

Right to Buy (RTB) lets eligible secure tenants of Aldershire District Council purchase their home at a discount under Pt V Housing Act 1985.

## Eligibility

You can normally apply if:

- You are a secure tenant of a council home (not a flexible or starter tenancy)
- You have spent at least 3 years as a public-sector tenant (not necessarily continuous, not necessarily with Aldershire)
- The home is your only or principal home
- Your home is not a sheltered or specially adapted property excluded by Schedule 5 HA 1985

You cannot apply if you have an outstanding possession order, are bankrupt, or have unspent housing-related debt above £1,000.

## Discount

As of April 2026 the maximum discount in England (outside London) is £102,400. The discount accrues at:

- 35% after 3 years, plus 1% for each additional year, up to 70% (houses)
- 50% after 3 years, plus 2% for each additional year, up to 70% (flats)

The cost-floor rule may reduce the discount if Aldershire has spent on the property in the last 15 years.

## How to apply

1. Submit Form RTB1, paper or via aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/right-to-buy
2. Aldershire has 4 weeks to send Form RTB2 (admit or deny the right)
3. Within 8 weeks (house) or 12 weeks (flat) of admission, Aldershire serves a Section 125 notice with the offer price
4. You have 12 weeks from the s.125 notice to accept, dispute the valuation, or withdraw

## Repayment of discount

If you sell within 5 years you must repay some or all of the discount on a sliding scale.

## Help and advice

Free, independent advice is available from the Right to Buy Agent Service (gov.uk/right-to-buy-buying-your-council-home). Aldershire cannot provide financial advice.
