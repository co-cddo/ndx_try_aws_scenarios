# Housing repair reporting

Aldershire repairs and maintains around 6,800 council homes. Repairs are categorised by urgency.

## Emergency repairs: within 24 hours

Reported on 01624 460 199 (24/7) or via aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/repair-emergency:

- Total loss of heating or hot water (October to April)
- Total loss of electricity or water
- Burst pipe, severe leak, or flooding
- Blocked main drain or only toilet in the home
- Insecure property after break-in (boarding-up only; police report required)
- Unsafe gas appliance or smell of gas (call National Gas on 0800 111 999 first)
- Lift entrapment in sheltered or extra-care schemes

## Urgent repairs: within 5 working days

- Partial loss of heating
- Leaking gutter causing damage
- Defective extractor fan in bathroom or kitchen
- Faulty entry phone in flats
- Lock change after lost keys (rechargeable)

## Routine repairs: within 28 working days

- Minor plaster repairs
- Door or window adjustments
- Kitchen unit replacement (single)
- Floor tile replacement
- Internal joinery

## Planned works

Larger works (kitchen replacement, bathroom replacement, full external paint) are scheduled through the Asset Management Programme. You receive 28 days' notice in writing.

## How to report

- Online (preferred): aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/report-a-repair, accepts photos
- Phone: 01624 460 199 (24/7 emergency line; routine triage 08:00 to 20:00)
- Bot: collects address, repair description, access arrangements; returns the job reference

## Access

You must give access on the agreed appointment date. Two missed appointments may lead to recharge of the failed-visit fee (£42 as of April 2026).

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not classify repair priority, that is a trade-officer decision after diagnostic. It books the slot and creates the works order.
