# Empty Homes Officer

There are around 1,200 long-term empty homes in Aldershire. Empty homes are wasted homes; bringing them back into use is one of our most cost-effective housing interventions.

## What "empty" means

For Aldershire's Empty Homes Strategy 2024-2029:

- Empty: unoccupied for more than 6 months
- Long-term empty: unoccupied for 2+ years
- Premium-charged: 3+ years (council tax 200%, see Council Tax knowledge document)
- Severely premium-charged: 5+ years (council tax 300%); 10+ years (400%)

## How the Empty Homes Officer can help

If you own an empty home, we can:

- Match you with builders, agents, and finance from our partner panel
- Offer the Aldershire Empty Property Loan, interest-free up to £15,000, repayable in 5 years on sale, refurb, or rent
- Arrange a discounted property valuation
- Connect you with the Bond and Deposit Scheme so a tenant can move in immediately
- Help with probate-related complications (where the owner has died and the property is in estate)
- Offer leasing direct to Aldershire (5-year private-sector lease, full management, guaranteed rent)

## Reporting an empty home

If you are concerned about an empty property, anti-social behaviour, dangerous structure, or just wasted housing, let us know.

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/empty-homes-report
- Phone: 01624 460 245. Empty Homes Officer, Wednesday and Friday 09:00 to 16:00
- Bot: takes the address, photo, and concern and routes to the EHO

## Enforcement

Where empty owners are unwilling to act, Aldershire can use:

- Empty Dwelling Management Order (EDMO). Pt 4 Housing Act 2004 (rare; 4+ years empty, with prospect of return)
- Compulsory Purchase Order (CPO). Housing Act 1985, s.17 (last resort, with regeneration value)
- Council tax premium escalation
- Enforced Sale (Law of Property Act 1925, s.103) where Aldershire is a creditor

We always prefer collaboration; enforcement is genuinely last resort.

## Why act?

- Bring a home back into use within 12 weeks of agreement
- Earn rental income and capital appreciation
- Avoid council tax premiums
- Help your neighbours and the wider housing market

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not approve loans or commit Aldershire to leasing. It books the EHO meeting, sends a starter pack, and follows up by SMS.
