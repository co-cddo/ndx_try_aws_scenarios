# Severe Weather Emergency Provision (SWEP)

Aldershire activates SWEP whenever the forecast meets the trigger, to ensure that no one needs to sleep rough in conditions that risk their life.

## When SWEP is activated

SWEP is activated when the Met Office forecast for Aldershire shows:

- Three consecutive nights at or below 0°C; OR
- One night at or below -2°C; OR
- Severe weather warning (yellow or above) for snow, ice, wind, or storm
- High temperatures in summer at or above 30°C for 3 consecutive nights (Heat-SWEP)

## What SWEP provides

- Open-access emergency beds at Aldershire House and 2 commissioned partners (additional 18 beds during cold SWEP)
- Outreach extended to 24/7 during activation
- Free transport to a SWEP bed (taxi or minibus)
- Hot meal and drink on arrival
- No referral or eligibility test for SWEP, every rough sleeper is offered a bed
- Pets accepted at all SWEP venues

## How to access SWEP

If you are sleeping rough:

- Walk in to Aldershire House, 14 Riverside Lane, Riverbank AL3 7QF (24/7 during SWEP)
- Call 01624 460 199 option 4, out-of-hours response
- Approach any outreach worker (extended hours)

If you see someone:

- Call 01624 460 230 (08:00 to midnight) or 01624 460 199 option 4
- StreetLink: streetlink.example.org.uk

## What happens after SWEP

When SWEP ends Aldershire offers:

- Continuing accommodation if you are in priority need (s.188 HA 1996 interim duty)
- Move-on hostel placement for non-priority cases
- Housing First referral for repeat rough sleepers with multiple needs
- Reconnection where it is your wish and safe

## How we tell people SWEP is on

- aldershire.example.gov.uk/swep, daily status from 14:00 each day during winter
- SMS alert subscription (text SWEP to 80018)
- Posters at Aldershire House, the bus station, libraries, and food banks
- Social media (council X and Facebook accounts)
- Faith and food-bank partners notified

## Volunteers

SWEP welcomes trained volunteers (DBS-checked) for transport, kitchen, and welcome roles. Apply at aldershire.example.gov.uk/volunteer.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not activate SWEP, that is a Service Manager decision. It can read out the current activation status, find the nearest SWEP bed, and arrange transport.
