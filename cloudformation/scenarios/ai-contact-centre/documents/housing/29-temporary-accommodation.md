# Temporary accommodation

If Aldershire owes you the interim duty (s.188) or the main housing duty (s.193) under Pt VII Housing Act 1996, we provide temporary accommodation until that duty ends.

## Types of temporary accommodation

Aldershire uses, in order of preference:

1. Aldershire-owned temporary accommodation (TA flats), 80 self-contained units
2. Housing-association leased schemes (HALS), 60 units across the district
3. Aldershire House and other hostels (see Hostels knowledge document)
4. Spot-purchased B&B, used only as a last resort, never for families with children for more than 6 weeks (Homelessness (Suitability of Accommodation) (England) Order 2003)
5. Out-of-area placements, only with welfare considerations and a written suitability assessment

## What you pay

- TA rent, set at the Local Housing Allowance rate; usually covered by Housing Benefit or Universal Credit Housing Element
- Service charge, typically £15 to £35/week, also benefit-eligible in TA
- Personal contribution if you are working, calculated on a sliding scale

If you are unable to pay, contact the Income Team, we will not evict from TA for arrears alone where the duty is still owed.

## Pets in TA

We try to accept pets but cannot guarantee. If your pet cannot come, Aldershire's Pet Foster Scheme (in partnership with the Cinnamon Trust and Dogs Trust Hope Project) places pets temporarily for free.

## How long?

Average TA stays in 2025/26 were 78 days. Stays vary widely:

- Short stay: B&B for 1 to 2 weeks while we finalise placement
- Medium: 3 to 9 months in a self-contained unit while you bid on Aldershire Homefinder
- Long: 12+ months for larger families (4-bed need) due to property scarcity

## Suitability and reviews

The TA must be suitable for your household. Suitability considers:

- Size of household
- Distance from schools, work, support, health
- Disability and accessibility
- Domestic abuse safety

You have 21 days to request a suitability review under s.202.

## Moving from TA

The duty normally ends when:

- You accept a Part VI offer (CBL or direct let)
- You secure your own private rented accommodation (with our help)
- A successful review removes the duty

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not allocate TA, that is a placement-officer decision. It can confirm your TA reference, raise repair requests, and refer to the Income Team.
