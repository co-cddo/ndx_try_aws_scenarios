# Anti-social behaviour at home

Aldershire takes anti-social behaviour (ASB) by tenants and visitors seriously. Action is governed by the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 and tenancy clauses 5–7.

## What is tenancy ASB

- Persistent noise (loud music after 23:00, frequent parties)
- Verbal abuse, harassment, intimidation
- Drug dealing or production
- Domestic abuse
- Damage to property or communal areas
- Hate-motivated incidents
- Fly-tipping in communal areas

## How to report

- Emergency or crime in progress: 999
- Aldershire ASB team: 01624 460 213, 09:00 to 19:00 weekdays, 10:00 to 16:00 weekends
- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/asb-report (anonymous reporting available)
- Bot: takes a structured report (incident type, dates, perpetrator, impact) and dispatches to ASB officers within 1 working day

For cross-tenure ASB (private rented or owner-occupier perpetrators) see also the general ASB knowledge document.

## What we do: escalation

1. Acknowledge the report within 1 working day
2. Open a case and assign an ASB officer within 5 working days
3. Initial action plan within 10 working days (interviews, noise app, mediation referral)
4. Use of tools as appropriate:
   - Acceptable Behaviour Contract (ABC)
   - Mediation (via Aldershire Community Mediation, free)
   - Community Protection Warning, then Notice (CPW/CPN)
   - Civil injunction under Pt 1 of the 2014 Act
   - Closure Order under Pt 4 of the 2014 Act
   - Tenancy action. NoSP, possession, demoted tenancy, absolute possession (Ground 7A)

## ASB Case Review (Community Trigger)

If three qualifying complaints have been made about the same problem in 6 months, you can ask for an ASB Case Review. This brings police, Aldershire, and other agencies together. Apply at aldershire.example.gov.uk/asb-case-review.

## Support for victims

- Victim Support: 0808 168 9111
- Aldershire Domestic Abuse Service: 0808 200 0247 (24/7), see Domestic Abuse Housing knowledge document
- Sanctuary Scheme (target hardening): contact ASB team

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not replace 999 or 101. It does not advise on whether evidence is enough for an injunction. It logs the case, redacts perpetrator addresses from the audit log, and refers to a human officer.
