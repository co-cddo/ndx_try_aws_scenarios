# Rent payments

Council and housing-association tenants pay rent weekly. Aldershire's rent week starts on Monday and ends on Sunday. There are 52 charging weeks and 2 rent-free weeks (typically the last week of March and the second week of December).

## How to pay

- Direct debit (preferred): aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/rent-direct-debit, choose any day of the month, weekly, fortnightly, or four-weekly
- Online card payment: aldershire.example.gov.uk/pay
- Phone (24/7 automated): 0300 200 460
- Standing order: ask the bot to text the bank details and your tenancy reference
- PayPoint or Post Office: with the rent payment card (replacement card £4)
- In person: Civic Centre cashier desk, Monday and Wednesday 09:30 to 14:00

## Universal Credit

If you receive Universal Credit, the housing element is paid to you. You must arrange to pass it on to Aldershire, direct debit is the simplest. You can request an Alternative Payment Arrangement (APA) so DWP pays Aldershire directly if you are vulnerable or in arrears 8+ weeks.

## What if I can't pay this week?

Contact us before the rent is due:

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/rent-arrears
- Phone: 01624 460 207. Income Team, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00
- Bot: can record an inability-to-pay note, set up a payment plan up to £20/week extra, and refer you to tenancy sustainment

## Rent statements

Statements are produced quarterly. You can view your live balance at any time on the tenant portal at aldershire.example.gov.uk/myhome.

## Drop-in surgeries

The Civic Centre runs a rent drop-in every Wednesday 10:00 to 13:00, no appointment needed. The Income Team and a debt adviser from Aldershire Citizens Advice attend.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not negotiate write-offs or vary the rent charged. It can confirm balance, take a card payment, set up a direct debit, and book a callback with the Income Team.
