# Rough sleeping: outreach and contact

Aldershire's Rough Sleeping Outreach Team works on the streets every night, helping people sleeping rough into safer accommodation. The service is funded by the Rough Sleeping Initiative (DLUHC/MHCLG).

## How to contact us

- Aldershire Outreach Team: 01624 460 230 (08:00 to midnight)
- Email: outreach@aldershire.example.gov.uk
- StreetLink (national alert): streetlink.example.org.uk or 0300 500 0914
- Out of hours emergencies: 01624 460 199 option 4
- Severe weather alerts: see SWEP knowledge document

## Telling us about someone sleeping rough

When you call or report via StreetLink please give:

- Time, date, and exact location
- Description (gender, age, clothing)
- Any vulnerability (visibly unwell, known to services)
- Any pets

We attend within 24 hours during normal operations. SWEP and severe-risk cases get a same-shift visit.

## What outreach offers

- A safe space conversation, no obligation
- Hot drink, food, blanket, sleeping bag
- Walk to Aldershire House or other commissioned hostel
- Same-day Housing Options application (s.184)
- GP and substance-use referrals via Aldershire NHS Outreach
- Mental-health rapid response (ARMHA service)
- Identity recovery (replacement birth certificate, NI number, bank ID)

## Repeat rough sleepers

Aldershire runs Housing First (15 self-contained units) for rough sleepers with high needs. Tenancies are unconditional; intensive support is offered indefinitely. Referral via Outreach.

## Persons from abroad

Persons subject to immigration control may not be eligible for housing duties under HA 1996. We still offer:

- Reconnection (assisted return to country of origin)
- EEA Citizen Settlement support
- Modern Slavery NRM referral
- Asylum-seeker referral to Migrant Help (0808 8010 503)
- Section 4(2) IAA 1999 support applications

## Visitors and concerned residents

If you regularly buy food/drink for a rough sleeper, please also call us, gentle support paired with practical help is the strongest pathway off the streets.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not dispatch outreach directly. It logs the report (location and welfare details) and notifies the Outreach Team within 5 minutes. For an active emergency, the bot routes to 999.
