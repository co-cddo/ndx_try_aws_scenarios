# Extra-care housing

Extra-care housing is for people aged 55 and over who need on-site personal care to live independently. Care is delivered by a CQC-registered provider 24/7, on the same site as the housing.

## How it differs from sheltered

| Feature | Sheltered | Extra-care |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 55–60+ | 55+ |
| Care | None on site | 24/7 on site |
| Meals | Self-catering | Optional restaurant |
| Bathroom | Adapted | Wet room + ceiling hoist |
| Tenure | Rent / shared ownership | Rent / shared ownership |

## Aldershire's extra-care schemes

- Linden House (Riverbank), 30 extra-care flats; care provider Aldershire Care Group
- Cherrywood Place (Aldershire Town), 50 extra-care flats; care provider Caremark Aldershire
- Heathside Gardens (East Aldershire, due 2027), 60 flats and 12 cottages

Each scheme has communal facilities: restaurant, hairdresser, lounge, gym, garden, and scheme manager.

## Eligibility

You can apply if:

- You are 55 or over (couples: at least one of you)
- You have an assessed care need (10+ hours/week or more)
- You have a local connection to Aldershire (see Local Connection knowledge document)

## How to apply

1. Adult Social Care assessment: 01624 460 600, books an OT/social-work joint assessment within 28 days
2. Housing register application: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/register-apply
3. Allocations panel (held monthly): cross-references housing and care needs
4. Bid on extra-care lets via Aldershire Homefinder

## What it costs

- Rent set at "affordable rent" (up to 80% of market). Aldershire benchmark £165 to £210/week
- Service charge £40 to £65/week (covers laundry, communal cleaning, garden, scheme manager)
- Care charges through Adult Social Care, means-tested under the Care Act 2014

Most residents qualify for Housing Benefit or the Universal Credit Housing Element to cover rent and most service charge.

## Couples: same flat

Couples are housed together. If only one partner has the care need, the other partner is recognised as the household carer with formal care-plan input.

## Visiting and leaving

Family and friends can stay overnight. If care needs increase to nursing-home level, the social work team supports a planned move to a nursing home.

Contact: 01624 460 217. Extra-Care Lettings Officer.
