# Ending your tenancy (notice to quit)

If you want to end an Aldershire tenancy you must give written notice. The notice period depends on the tenancy type.

## Notice periods

- Secure or flexible tenancy (council): 4 weeks ending on a Monday (s.5 Protection from Eviction Act 1977)
- Assured tenancy (housing association partner): 4 weeks ending on a Monday or as your tenancy agreement states
- Joint tenancy: any one tenant can serve notice and end the tenancy for everyone (Hammersmith and Fulham LBC v Monk [1992])
- Introductory tenancy (first 12 months): 4 weeks ending on a Monday

## How to give notice

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/housing/end-tenancy, generates the right form for your tenancy type
- Email: housing.endings@aldershire.example.gov.uk
- Post or in person: Aldershire Civic Centre, Market Square, Aldershire AL1 2QP
- Phone (followed by written confirmation): 01624 460 208

## What happens next

1. We confirm the end date in writing within 2 working days
2. A pre-termination inspection is offered within 7 days
3. You receive a list of tenant obligations (clear and clean, return keys, settle rent and rechargeables)
4. On the end date you return all keys to the Civic Centre by 12:00, late return = 1 extra week's rent
5. We carry out a final inspection within 5 working days; you may be present

## Bereavement

If a tenant has died, do not serve a notice to quit until you have read the Succession of Tenancy knowledge document. A successor may have rights you would lose by serving a notice.

## Forfeiting fixtures

You may take fixtures you bought (e.g. a freestanding cooker). Built-in items become Aldershire's property. Decoration, flooring, and built-in wardrobes must be left in good order.

## Recharges

You may be recharged for:

- Damage beyond fair wear and tear
- Items left in the property
- Cleaning beyond reasonable

The bill is sent within 28 days. You have 21 days to dispute.
