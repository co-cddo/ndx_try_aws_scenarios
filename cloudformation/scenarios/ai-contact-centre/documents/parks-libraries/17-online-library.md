# Online library: eBooks, eAudiobooks, and eMagazines

Members of Aldershire Libraries get free access to a wide range of digital content. Sign in at aldershire.gov.uk/elibrary using your library card number and PIN. For password resets, call 01632 960170.

## Platforms

| Platform | Content type | Concurrent loans | Notes |
| -------- | ------------ | ---------------- | ----- |
| OverDrive / Libby | eBooks, eAudiobooks | 8 at a time | iOS, Android, Kindle compatible |
| BorrowBox | eBooks, eAudiobooks | 5 at a time | Strong fiction list |
| PressReader | Newspapers and magazines | Unlimited (3-day cache) | 7,000 publications globally |
| Naxos Music Library | Classical, jazz | 1 stream | 30,000 albums |
| Theory Test Pro | Driving theory | Unlimited | UK car, motorbike, LGV, PCV |
| Ancestry Library Edition | Family history | In-branch only | Genealogy database |
| Britannica School | Reference | Unlimited | Filtered for school use |

All platforms are free at point of use for Aldershire library members.

## Loan periods

Default loan periods:

- OverDrive eBooks: 21 days
- OverDrive eAudiobooks: 21 days
- BorrowBox eBooks and eAudiobooks: 21 days
- PressReader: cached 3 days for offline; online use unrestricted
- Naxos: streaming session, no download
- Ancestry: 30 minutes per session, in-branch use only

Items return automatically at the end of the loan; there are no late fees on digital content.

## Holds

OverDrive and BorrowBox both support holds. Average waits for popular titles vary widely:

- New literary fiction releases: 4 to 6 weeks
- Bestsellers (Lee Child, Richard Osman, etc.): up to 12 weeks
- Backlist and award shortlist titles: usually under 2 weeks
- Children's picture books: typically same day

Skip-the-line copies (high-demand 7-day-only loans) are offered on rotation through OverDrive.

## Kindle compatibility

OverDrive titles can be sent to a Kindle device or Kindle app via a registered Amazon account. The Aldershire library does not see any data exchanged with Amazon during the transfer. BorrowBox does not support Kindle.

## Help and training

The Digital Library Coach scheme runs free 30-minute one-to-one slots in branch on weekday afternoons. Book on 01632 960170 or at aldershire.gov.uk/digital-coach.

The chatbot can answer general questions about the eLibrary platforms but cannot reset library PINs; that needs a phone call or an in-branch visit.

## Accessibility

OverDrive Libby supports Voice Over and TalkBack. BorrowBox supports Voice Over. PressReader has a high-contrast and dyslexia-friendly view. Larger text alternatives are available for any digital service.
