# Park bookings: events, sports pitches, and fees

Aldershire District Council accepts bookings for events, ceremonies, fairs, fitness classes, and sports use across its parks. Bookings are coordinated by the Park Bookings Office on 01632 960142 or parkbookings@aldershire.gov.uk.

## Lead times

| Booking type | Minimum notice | Maximum lead time |
| ------------ | -------------- | ----------------- |
| Small private event (under 50 guests, no amplified sound) | 14 days | 12 months |
| Medium event (50 to 499 guests, or any amplified sound) | 8 weeks | 18 months |
| Large event (500+ guests, or any ticketed entry) | 16 weeks | 24 months |
| Sports pitch (one-off match) | 7 days | 90 days |
| Recurring fitness class | 4 weeks | Annual licence |

Large events are referred to the Aldershire Safety Advisory Group (SAG), which sits monthly. Applicants should expect to attend at least one SAG session.

## Standard fees

Fees are reviewed every April. Indicative 2026/27 charges:

- Birthday party gazebo pitch (up to 4 hours): GBP 35
- Wedding photo permit (90 minutes): GBP 60
- Fitness class licence (annual, weekly slot): GBP 480
- Funfair pitch (per day): GBP 750 plus a refundable GBP 1,500 reinstatement bond
- Filming and TV (per day, residential street or park): GBP 1,200 to GBP 4,500 depending on scale
- Charity fun run (under 200 entrants): GBP 0 (concessionary)

Sports pitch fees are listed in the Sports Pitches knowledge article.

## What you must provide

For any event over 50 guests, the council requires:

1. Public liability insurance of at least GBP 5 million (GBP 10 million for events over 1,000 guests).
2. A risk assessment, in any reasonable format.
3. An event management plan if amplified sound, vehicles, or temporary structures over 4 metres tall are involved.
4. Premises licence or Temporary Event Notice (TEN) if alcohol will be sold; TENs are processed by the Licensing team, not Park Bookings.

The chatbot can take initial booking enquiries and pass these to a human agent. It does not currently issue confirmations directly.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancellations more than 28 days before the event are refunded in full minus a GBP 25 admin fee. Cancellations between 28 and 7 days before the event are refunded at 50%. Cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable except in cases of severe weather or council-initiated cancellation (for example, a flood warning or ground condition closure).
