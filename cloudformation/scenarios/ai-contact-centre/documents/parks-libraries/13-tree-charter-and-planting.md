# Tree planting and the Aldershire Tree Charter

Aldershire District Council adopted the Aldershire Tree Charter in March 2023. The Charter commits the council to a net increase of 12,500 trees by 2030 and protects the existing canopy through a Tree Strategy. Tree Officer: 01632 960158, trees@aldershire.gov.uk.

## What the Charter says

The five Aldershire Tree Charter principles:

1. The right tree in the right place. Species selection considers climate resilience, pest exposure, and soil suitability.
2. Treat trees as long-term infrastructure. Planting must be matched by a 10-year aftercare plan.
3. Protect what we have. No net loss of canopy on council land; replacement at 2:1 for any tree removed for safety reasons.
4. Make tree benefits visible. Annual canopy-cover dashboard published on aldershire.gov.uk/canopy.
5. Support community ownership. Volunteer planting, sponsorship, and street tree request schemes are available year-round.

## Free street tree request

Residents can request a tree for the verge or footway outside their home. Apply online at aldershire.gov.uk/free-street-tree from 1 August to 31 October. Plantings happen between November and March each year.

Eligibility criteria:

- Verge at least 1.5 m wide, or paved area where a tree pit can be cut
- No underground utilities within the planting zone (checked by the council)
- Householder agrees to water for the first 2 summers (the council provides a watering bag)

The council picks species from the approved Aldershire Street Tree Palette of 38 species. Residents may express a preference but the final choice is the Tree Officer's.

## Memorial trees and sponsored planting

Memorial planting is offered at Aldermarket Memorial Park, Vale Gardens, and Aldershire Vale Country Park. Costs:

- Standard memorial tree: GBP 220 (includes 5-year care, no plaque)
- Memorial tree with plaque: GBP 440 (includes a small flush-mounted granite plaque)
- Sponsored woodland tree (Country Park): GBP 90

Memorials are scheduled for the planting season. The bot can take an enquiry but cannot complete the order; sales are handled by the Tree Officer.

## Volunteer planting days

Three large community planting days each year:

- Vale Common edge planting: late November
- Strawford coastal hedgerow: early February
- Aldermarket Heath gap-up: mid-March

Booking via aldershire.gov.uk/treevolunteer. Tools, gloves, and refreshments provided. Minimum age 8 (with a guardian).

## Tree Preservation Orders (TPOs)

There are 312 TPOs in force across Aldershire. Works to a TPO tree, or any tree in a Conservation Area, require notification or consent. The application form is on aldershire.gov.uk/tpo. Decisions are made within 8 weeks for TPO consents and 6 weeks for Conservation Area notifications. Unlawful works carry a maximum fine of GBP 20,000 per tree.

## Pests and diseases

Aldershire monitors for ash dieback (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus), oak processionary moth, and elm yellows. The current Ash Dieback Action Plan (2024-2034) sets a removal-and-replacement programme at 12% of severely affected trees per year.
