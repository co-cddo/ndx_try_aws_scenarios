# Tennis courts: pay-as-you-play and membership

Aldershire District Council operates 14 outdoor tennis courts at four sites under a partnership with the LTA Parks Tennis programme. Booking is online at aldershire.gov.uk/tennis or via the LTA ClubSpark portal. Phone bookings: 01632 960148.

## Court sites

| Site | Courts | Surface | Floodlights |
| ---- | ------ | ------- | ----------- |
| Memorial Park | 6 | Acrylic (macadam) | Yes (4 of 6) |
| Vale Gardens | 4 | Acrylic | No |
| Heath Road Recreation Ground | 2 | Acrylic | No |
| Aldershire Vale Country Park | 2 | Tarmac | No |

All courts are open every day from 07:00 until dusk, or 22:00 where floodlights are fitted. Floodlight tokens are issued via the booking confirmation email; there is no on-court coin slot.

## Pay-as-you-play

Anyone with a free LTA / ClubSpark account can book pay-as-you-play. Costs:

- Adult, off-peak (weekday before 17:00): GBP 5.00 per hour
- Adult, peak (evenings, weekends, school holidays): GBP 8.00 per hour
- Junior (under 18): GBP 3.50 per hour any time
- Floodlight surcharge: GBP 4 per hour
- Concessionary (Aldershire Council Tax Support recipients): 50% off

Free hours: every site offers two free public hours per week, currently Sunday 09:00 to 11:00. These slots are released at midnight on the Monday before.

## Annual membership

Aldershire Annual Tennis Pass: GBP 75 adult / GBP 35 junior / GBP 110 family (2 adults + up to 4 juniors). Includes:

- Unlimited bookings up to 14 days in advance (non-members can book 7 days)
- Free floodlights at Memorial Park
- 20% off Aldershire Tennis Coaching Programme courses
- Members-only Sunday morning club session at Memorial Park (10:00 to 12:00)

Membership runs 1 April to 31 March. Pro-rata pricing applies if joining after 1 October.

## Coaching

Coaching is delivered by Aldermarket Tennis Coaching CIC under contract. Adult group lessons: GBP 12 per session in blocks of 6. Junior squad: GBP 8 per session. Cardio Tennis runs Saturday mornings at Vale Gardens. Bookings via tennisaldermarket.org.uk; the council bot will redirect coaching enquiries.

## Etiquette and rules

Bookings are limited to one slot per person per day during peak times. Whites are not required. Spike-soled footwear and bicycles are not allowed on the courts. Dogs must remain outside the fence line. Anti-social behaviour can be reported via the chatbot or 01632 960148.
