# Postal vote applications and deadlines

Aldershire District Council issues postal votes to electors who prefer to vote from home. Postal voting is a long-standing alternative to polling-station voting in Great Britain. Phone: Electoral Services 01632 960191. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/postal-vote.

## How to apply

Two ways:

1. Online via gov.uk/apply-postal-vote (the preferred route since the Elections Act 2022 enabled the digital service in late 2023).
2. Paper application, downloaded from aldershire.gov.uk/postal-vote-paper or requested by phone.

Both routes require the elector's National Insurance number and signature. The signature is captured as a live image in the online service or in pen on paper.

## Deadlines for the next election

For any UK election or referendum, the deadline to apply for a new postal vote, or change your existing postal vote details, is:

- 5pm on the 11th working day before polling day

For example, a polling day of Thursday 7 May 2026 has a postal vote application deadline of 5pm on Wednesday 22 April 2026 (working days exclude weekends and bank holidays).

If you are already a postal voter, your ballot is dispatched automatically; no fresh application is needed unless your details change.

## How long can you have a postal vote for?

Under the Elections Act 2022, postal vote registrations now last a maximum of 3 years before they must be renewed. The council writes to you in advance of the expiry date. If you do not renew, your postal vote stops and you go back to polling-station voting (you can still register again later).

This 3-year rule applies to applications received from 31 October 2023 onwards. Long-standing postal vote registrations from before that date were transitioned and have been written to in line with the staggered-renewal schedule.

## When ballots are sent out

Postal ballot packs are dispatched in two waves:

- First wave: around 18 to 19 days before polling day (for early-applied postal votes)
- Second wave: around 9 to 10 days before polling day (for late applicants)

Packs are sent by Royal Mail. Aldershire uses a Royal Mail Track and Trace barcode for outbound and a freepost return envelope.

## Returning your postal vote

Three options:

1. Royal Mail freepost (recommended; allow at least 4 working days)
2. Drop at any Aldershire library or council reception during opening hours
3. Hand in at the count venue or at any Aldershire polling station on polling day

Postal votes handed in at a polling station now require a completed Postal Vote Return Form (Postal Vote Return Form A) to identify who handed it in and what household it relates to. This came in with the Elections Act 2022.

A single person can hand in postal votes for up to a maximum of 5 other electors plus their own household, the "postal vote handling limit." Beyond that, the votes are rejected.

## Spoiled postal votes

If you make a mistake, return the spoiled pack with a note and Electoral Services will issue a replacement, provided you ask before 5pm on polling day.

## Help and accessibility

The chatbot can answer common questions and confirm whether you are registered for a postal vote. It cannot apply for you (gov.uk requires a one-time identity step). For sight-impaired voters, the council provides a tactile postal voting overlay on request.
