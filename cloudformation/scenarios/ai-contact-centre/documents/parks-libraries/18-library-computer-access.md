# Computer and internet access at libraries

Every Aldershire library branch provides free public computers and free WiFi. Booking is recommended at the busier sites, particularly Aldermarket Central. Phone: 01632 960170. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/library-pcs.

## How to book

Three options:

1. Online via aldershire.gov.uke/pc-bookings, up to 7 days in advance
2. By phone on 01632 960170 during opening hours
3. At the branch enquiry desk

You can hold up to 2 hours of booked time per day per member, in 30-minute blocks. Express PCs are walk-up only and limited to one 15-minute session per visit.

## What you can do

- Web browsing (filtered to remove illegal content; categories blocked include gambling, pornography, file-sharing, and known phishing)
- Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook web)
- LibreOffice
- Adobe Reader
- Print, scan, and copy (charges below)
- Universal Credit and HMRC self-assessment work (the council has a confidentiality understanding with DWP and HMRC)
- Video calling (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet); please use a headset, and staff have headsets available to lend

## Universal Credit and HMRC support

Aldermarket Central and Cooper's Hill both run a weekly Digital Help drop-in for Universal Credit, HMRC, NHS App, and gov.uk forms. Slots can be booked on 01632 960170 or via aldershire.gov.uk/digital-help. The council provides a private booth for sensitive sessions.

## Accessibility software

All public PCs run Read & Write (Texthelp) for text-to-speech and screen masking. Five PCs across the network have ZoomText and JAWS for visually impaired users; book in advance.
