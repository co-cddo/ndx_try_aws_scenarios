# Aldershire Vale Country Park

Aldershire Vale Country Park is the council's flagship outdoor destination. At 92 hectares it is one of the largest publicly accessible green spaces in the county, including ancient semi-natural woodland, restored grassland, three ponds, and 11 kilometres of waymarked trails. Phone: 01632 960154. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/vale-country-park.

## Location and access

Address: Vale Park Way, off the A1140, AL7 8WQ. Postcode for satnav goes to the main car park; secondary access is from Strawford Lane (no parking).

Open daily, dawn to dusk. The visitor centre and toilets open 09:00 to 17:00 (16:00 in winter). The site is closed only on Christmas Day.

By bus: route 17 from Aldermarket bus station, hourly Monday to Saturday; reduced service Sundays. By bike: National Cycle Route 41 passes the south boundary; secure bike racks at the visitor centre.

## Parking

The main car park has 240 spaces including 12 Blue Badge bays and 8 EV charging bays (Pod Point, contactless). Charging:

- Up to 1 hour: free
- 1 to 4 hours: GBP 2.50
- All day: GBP 5.00
- Aldershire Annual Park Permit holders: free

Pay by RingGo (location code 56120) or by card at the meter. Coins are not accepted.

## Trails and accessibility

Five waymarked trails, all colour-coded:

- Red trail (1.2 km): all-ability surface, suitable for buggies and wheelchairs
- Blue trail (3.0 km): firm gravel, gentle gradients
- Green trail (4.5 km): mixed surface, includes steps in places
- Yellow trail (6.0 km): woodland and pasture, can be muddy
- Black trail (11.0 km): full perimeter, includes a steep ascent at Beacon Hill

A free Tramper mobility scooter is available to book on 01632 960154 with 48 hours notice. Two are kept at the visitor centre.

## Wildlife

The park is a Local Nature Reserve and a Local Wildlife Site. Notable species include lapwing (winter visitors on the floodplain), great crested newt (the western pond), white-letter hairstreak butterfly (elm hedges), and hazel dormouse (woodland). See the Local Nature Reserves article for survey detail.

## Events

The Country Park hosts a year-round programme:

- Family Easter trail (April school holidays, GBP 3.50 per child)
- Summer evening walks led by rangers (June to August, free)
- Bat walks (May to September, GBP 5)
- Bonfire and fireworks (5 November, GBP 8 per ticket)
- Christmas wreath-making (December, GBP 25)

Bookings via aldershire.gov.uk/vale-country-park or 01632 960154.

## Dogs, cycling, and horse-riding

Dogs welcome on lead in spring grazing areas and around the floodplain (1 April to 30 June). Cycling allowed on the red, blue, and black trails. Horse-riding by permit only via the Estates team.
