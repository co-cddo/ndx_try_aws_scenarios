# Outdoor gym equipment: locations and maintenance

Aldershire District Council has installed outdoor (free-to-use) gym equipment at six sites across the district. Equipment is supplied by Proludic and Fresh Air Fitness under the council's 2023 leisure framework. For faults, call Parks on 01632 960140.

## Locations

| Site | Number of stations | Wheelchair-accessible station |
| ---- | ------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| Memorial Park (south circuit) | 8 | Yes (cross-trainer) |
| Riverside Park (riverside trail) | 6 | No |
| Aldermarket Heath | 5 | Yes (arm bike) |
| Vale Common | 7 | No |
| Heath Road Rec | 4 | No |
| Goose Bottom Park | 6 | Yes (chest press) |

Each site has signage in plain English with QR codes linking to a short video demonstration on aldershire.gov.uk/outdoor-gym. The videos include British Sign Language interpretation, captions, and audio description.

## Equipment types

Typical stations include:

- Air walker / cross-trainer
- Pull-up bars (three heights)
- Sit-up bench
- Chest press
- Leg press
- Hand cycle (one accessible station per site)
- Pendulum / waist twister
- Push-pull bike

Resistance is bodyweight only; there are no plate-loaded weights.

## Inspection and maintenance

Outdoor gym kit follows the same RPII inspection cycle as playgrounds (see the Children's Playgrounds article). Annual inspections include:

- Bearing and bushing wear checks
- Bolt torque and powder-coat condition
- Surfacing rebound testing
- Anti-vandal sign condition

Lubrication is on an 8-week cycle. Worn parts are replaced from a stock of common spares held by Parks Operations.

## Damage and faults

Common faults reported through the chatbot or the FixIt portal:

- Squeaking bearings (replaced within 5 working days)
- Vandalised QR-code signs (replaced within 10 working days)
- Surfacing scuffs (rebatched into the quarterly programme)
- Missing footrests on steppers (replaced within 3 working days as a safety priority)

If a piece of equipment is unsafe to use, Parks Patrol will fence or zip-tie it out of use within 24 hours of report and replace within 15 working days.

## Health partnerships

The equipment is part of the Aldershire Active partnership with the County NHS team. Free instructor-led "Park Workout" sessions run at Memorial Park (Tuesday 18:00) and Riverside (Saturday 09:30). Sessions are bookable via Aldershire Active on 01632 960188; they are not handled by the council booking team.
