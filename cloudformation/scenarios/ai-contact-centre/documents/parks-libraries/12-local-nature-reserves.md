# Local nature reserves: hedgehog and bat surveys

Aldershire District Council manages seven Local Nature Reserves (LNRs) under the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949. The Biodiversity Officer can be contacted on 01632 960156 or biodiversity@aldershire.gov.uk.

## LNR sites

| Reserve | Hectares | Notable habitat |
| ------- | -------- | --------------- |
| Aldershire Vale | 92 | Floodplain meadow, ancient woodland |
| Mill Pond | 14 | Reed bed, shallow standing water |
| Strawford Cliffs | 38 | Maritime grassland, soft cliff |
| Goose Bottom | 22 | Wet woodland, alder carr |
| Aldermarket Heath | 18 | Lowland heath, acid grassland |
| Old Brickworks | 9 | Brownfield mosaic, reptile habitat |
| Vale Common | 26 | Wood-pasture, veteran oaks |

Each reserve has a published management plan, on a 10-year cycle, available at aldershire.gov.uk/lnrs.

## Volunteer surveys

The council runs four annual citizen-science surveys:

1. Spring hedgehog footprint tunnel survey (April to May). 60 tunnels deployed across all seven reserves; volunteers check ink and paper inserts every 3 nights for 2 weeks.
2. Bat acoustic survey (June to August). Static detectors placed at 12 fixed points; volunteers help retrieve and process audio. Free training session in late May.
3. Autumn fungi walk (October). Recording walks led by the Aldershire Mycology Group.
4. Winter bird census (December to February). Linked to the BTO Wetland Bird Survey at Mill Pond.

Volunteer recruitment is via aldershire.gov.uk/biodiversity-volunteers. No experience required for hedgehog and bird counts; bat survey volunteers must complete the half-day training.

## Recent results (2025 season)

- Hedgehog activity index: 17.4% of tunnels recorded prints (up from 11.8% in 2023)
- Bat species detected: 9 (including a confirmed barbastelle at Goose Bottom, the first county record since 2019)
- Bird census: 1,240 wildfowl across the count period at Mill Pond, including pintail and goosander

Full results are published in the Aldershire State of Nature report, released each May.

## Habitat works

Recent and planned interventions:

- Aldermarket Heath: scrub reduction and gorse coppicing on a 5-year rotation
- Mill Pond: reed bed re-profiling 2026 to improve open-water margins
- Strawford Cliffs: invasive Himalayan balsam removal annually in June
- Old Brickworks: south-facing slope refresh for sand lizards (proposed 2027)

Works are advertised at the reserve gates and on the council biodiversity blog at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Reporting wildlife crime

Disturbance to nesting birds, badger sett interference, and unlawful tree felling should be reported to Aldershire Constabulary's Rural Crime team on 101 quoting "Operation Heron". The chatbot will signpost these calls and can also log a parallel report with the council's Biodiversity Officer.
