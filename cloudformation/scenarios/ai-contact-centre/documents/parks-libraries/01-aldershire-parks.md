# Aldershire parks: list, opening hours, and facilities

Aldershire District Council manages 27 parks and open spaces totalling around 340 hectares. The Parks and Open Spaces team can be reached on 01632 960140 or via aldershire.gov.uk/parks.

## Park opening hours

Most parks are open from dawn to dusk. The five gated parks (Aldermarket Memorial Park, Vale Gardens, Riverside Park, Town Hall Gardens, and Aldermarket Wheels Park) are locked overnight by the Parks Patrol team:

- Summer (April to September): 07:00 to 21:30
- Winter (October to March): 07:30 to 16:30
- Christmas Day and Boxing Day: closed all day

The unfenced parks (Vale Common, Mill Pond Field, Aldermarket Heath, etc.) have no gates and are accessible 24/7, although lighting is limited.

## Cafes and concessions

Cafes operate at Memorial Park (Pavilion Cafe), Vale Gardens (The Tea Room), and Riverside Park (Boatyard Kiosk). The council does not run these directly; they are leased to local SMEs through a five-year concession agreement managed by the Estates team. Lost property and accessibility complaints about cafes still go to Parks on 01632 960140.

## Reporting park issues

Damaged equipment, fly-tipping in parks, dog fouling, and broken benches can be reported via the Aldershire FixIt portal at aldershire.gov.uk/fixit, by phone, or through the chatbot. Photographs help Parks Patrol triage, but they are not required.

For safeguarding concerns or anti-social behaviour in progress, the bot will signpost to 999 (emergency) or 101 (non-emergency police) rather than a council route.

## Parks team structure

The Parks team has 34 staff, split between Parks Patrol (litter, locking up, visitor liaison), Grounds Maintenance (mowing, planting, hedging), and Tree Care (highway and park trees, including the Tree Charter scheme). All three sit under the Head of Open Spaces, currently Surinder Atwal.
