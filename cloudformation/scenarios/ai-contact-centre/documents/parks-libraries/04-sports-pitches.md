# Sports pitches: football, rugby, and cricket bookings

Aldershire District Council provides 22 sports pitches across nine sites. Bookings are managed by the Sports Bookings team on 01632 960146 or sports@aldershire.gov.uk. Online self-service booking is available at aldershire.gov.uk/sports-pitches.

## Pitch inventory

| Sport | Adult pitches | Junior / mini pitches | Sites |
| ----- | ------------- | --------------------- | ----- |
| Football | 8 | 6 | Memorial Park, Riverside Park, Heath Road Recreation Ground, Vale Common |
| Rugby (union) | 2 | 1 | Vale Common, Strawford Fields |
| Cricket | 3 squares | 1 colts square | Riverside Park, Aldershire Vale Country Park, Heath Road |
| Multi-use grass (5-a-side) | 4 | n/a | Aldermarket Heath, Goose Bottom, Mill Pond |

## Football fees (2026/27 season)

- Adult 11-a-side, single match: GBP 92
- Adult 11-a-side, league block (16 home matches): GBP 1,280
- Junior 11-a-side, single match: GBP 38
- Mini soccer (5/7-a-side), per session: GBP 18

All football pitches include changing rooms. Match officials' rooms are provided at Memorial Park and Heath Road only.

## Rugby fees

- Senior pitch, single match: GBP 110
- Junior pitch, single match: GBP 42
- Floodlit training (Strawford only, Wednesday and Thursday evenings): GBP 35 per hour

Rugby pitches are renovated each May; bookings are not accepted between 1 May and 31 July.

## Cricket fees

Cricket squares are leased seasonally rather than per match. The full season fee (1 April to 30 September) is:

- Senior square, league use: GBP 2,400
- Junior square: GBP 950
- Casual single-game booking (where availability allows): GBP 130

Cricket clubs are responsible for the square itself in line with ECB groundsmanship guidance; the council maintains the outfield.

## Cancellations and weather

The council's Pitch Inspector publishes a daily playability decision by 09:00 on match days during the football and rugby seasons. Updates are posted at aldershire.gov.uk/pitch-status, on the Aldershire Sports X account, and via the chatbot. Cancellations due to council closure are refunded or rebooked.

User-led cancellations get a 50% refund if notified at least 7 days in advance, and 0% refund within 7 days. Walking off mid-match for non-weather reasons does not trigger a refund.

## Lining and goalposts

Pitches are lined every Friday. Posts are up from the second Saturday of August until the last Saturday of April. Cricket nets at Riverside are bookable separately on 01632 960146.
