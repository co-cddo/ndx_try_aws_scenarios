# Beach hut hire on the Aldershire coast

Aldershire's eastern parishes include a 9-mile stretch of coastline managed by the council, including the resort village of Strawford-on-Sea. The council owns 142 beach huts at three sites and offers them for annual seasonal lease and short stays. The Beach Hut Office can be reached on 01632 960152 or beachhuts@aldershire.gov.uk.

## Sites and stock

| Site | Total huts | Short-stay huts | Annual-lease huts |
| ---- | ---------- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| Strawford-on-Sea promenade | 84 | 18 | 66 |
| North Sands | 36 | 6 | 30 |
| Cliff Top Crescent | 22 | 0 | 22 |

Beach huts are timber-clad with a corrugated metal roof, painted in the council's heritage palette (six approved shades). Each hut is around 2.4 m by 2.4 m with a small front veranda. There is no piped water and no electricity in the huts; communal water taps and toilets are nearby.

## Annual lease

The lease season runs 1 April to 31 March. Annual rents for 2026/27:

- Strawford promenade (front row, sea view): GBP 1,180
- Strawford promenade (second row): GBP 720
- North Sands: GBP 880
- Cliff Top Crescent: GBP 660

Annual lease holders may keep belongings inside (at their own risk) and apply external decoration in any of the six heritage colours, painted by 1 May each year. Subletting is not permitted, although day-loaning to friends and family is fine.

The annual-lease waiting list is currently around 9 years for Strawford front row, 4 years for second row, and under 18 months for Cliff Top Crescent.

## Short-stay hire

Short-stay huts are released through the council booking system at aldershire.gov.uk/beach-huts.

- Day rate (10:00 to 18:00): GBP 22
- Weekly rate (Saturday to Friday): GBP 110
- Bank Holiday weekend (3 days): GBP 75

Bookings can be made up to 6 months in advance. A GBP 50 refundable cleaning bond applies.

## Insurance and liability

The council insures the structure of the hut. Tenants and short-stay hirers are responsible for their own contents and for public liability while the hut is in use. The council recommends contents insurance with a beach hut specialist.

## Storm closures

The coastal team applies a storm closure protocol when the Met Office issues an amber wind warning. Closures are announced on aldershire.gov.uk/coastal-status and via the bot. Annual lease holders are advised to remove or secure loose items inside their hut. Refunds for short-stay closures are processed within 10 working days.

## Maintenance

The council inspects huts every spring and autumn. Tenants must report broken doors, leaking roofs, or graffiti within 7 days. Repairs costing under GBP 250 are absorbed by the council; tenant-caused damage is recharged.
