# Marriage and civil partnership: 28-day notice

To marry or form a civil partnership in England and Wales, both parties must give Notice of Marriage / Civil Partnership in person at their local register office. Aldershire District Council's Registration Service handles notice for residents living in the district. Phone: 01632 960184. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/giving-notice.

## The 28-day rule

After giving notice:

- A standard notice is held at the register office for 28 clear days.
- If either party is subject to immigration control (not a British or Irish citizen and not holding settled status), the notice period extends to 70 days under the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Minimum Age) Act 2022 framework. Home Office referral rules apply.
- After the notice period elapses, you can marry or form a partnership at any time within the next 12 months.

You cannot marry or form a partnership before the notice period ends, even in an emergency, except via the Registrar General's Licence, which has narrow grounds (typically a deathbed marriage where one party cannot be moved).

## Booking your notice appointment

Each party must give notice individually, in person, at the register office of the district they have lived in for at least 7 days immediately before the appointment. If both parties live in Aldershire, both notice appointments can be booked at Aldermarket Register Office on the same day.

Book via aldershire.gov.uk/giving-notice or 01632 960184. Current lead time is 4 to 6 weeks. Appointments take around 45 minutes per person.

## Documents required

- Photo ID (passport or driving licence preferred; biometric residence permit if applicable)
- Proof of address dated within the last 1 month (utility bill, council tax, bank statement)
- If divorced or widowed: the decree absolute / final order or the deceased spouse's death certificate (originals only, no photocopies)
- If a name has changed by deed poll: the deed poll
- For non-British or non-Irish citizens: visa or residence status proof; the registrar runs a Home Office check

## Fees

- Notice of Marriage / Civil Partnership (per person, standard): GBP 35
- Notice subject to immigration control (per person, where the longer 70-day notice applies): GBP 47
- Foreign-language interpreter if needed (independent, booked by the couple): variable; the registrar maintains a list of approved interpreters

## After notice

A notice is "kept" at the register office and openly available for inspection on the public notice board (in person, in the office foyer, during opening hours). This satisfies the long-standing public-notice requirement.

Once the notice period passes, you collect the Marriage Schedule from the register office on the day of (or in the few working days before) the ceremony if the ceremony is at a religious building or other approved venue; the registrar holds it directly for civil ceremonies at council venues.

## Civil partnerships

Civil partnerships work the same way as marriage notices. Same-sex couples and opposite-sex couples both have the option to choose marriage or civil partnership. The fees and notice periods are identical.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancelled notices are refunded at 50% if cancelled more than 14 days before the notice appointment, and 0% inside that window. The bot will signpost cancellations to the Registration Service.
