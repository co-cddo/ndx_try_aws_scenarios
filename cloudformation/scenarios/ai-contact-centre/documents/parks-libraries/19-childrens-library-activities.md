# Children's library activities: Bounce & Rhyme, Storytime, Summer Reading

Aldershire Libraries run a regular children's events programme aimed at under-fives, primary-age, and secondary-age readers. Most events are free and walk-in. Phone: 01632 960170 or events@aldershire.gov.uk. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/library-kids.

## Under-fives (0 to 4)

Bounce & Rhyme: drop-in baby and toddler sessions of songs, rhymes, and instruments. 30 minutes long.

Storytime: under-fives storytelling, 20 minutes plus a craft. Most branches run a Saturday morning Storytime; check the branch page.

Sensory Storytime: a quieter, dimmable-lighting session for autistic children and children with additional needs, on a monthly cycle at Aldermarket Central (first Saturday) and Cooper's Hill (third Saturday).

## Primary-age (5 to 11)

Code Club: Wednesday after-school 16:00 to 17:00 at Aldermarket Central. Scratch and micro:bit projects. Free, term time only.

LEGO Club: Saturday morning 10:30 at Aldermarket East and Heath Road. Tubs of bricks provided, no purchase required.

Chess Club: Sunday afternoon 14:00 at Aldermarket Central, term time only. Coaching from the Aldermarket Chess Society.

## Secondary-age (12 to 18)

Aldershire Teen Reads: monthly, last Thursday at Aldermarket Central, 18:00. Pizza provided. No charge.

Manga Club: fortnightly Saturday at Aldermarket East. Group buys voted on by members and bought from the Friends fund.

## Summer Reading Challenge

The annual Summer Reading Challenge is delivered as a national partnership with the Reading Agency. Children read 6 books over the summer holidays and collect medals, stickers, and a certificate. Free to join; sign up from mid-July at any branch.

Aldershire's local Summer Reading add-ons:

- Bonus medal for any child who reads a book by an Aldershire-resident author (list at aldershire.gov.uk/local-authors)
- "Aldermobile Adventures": children visiting the mobile library during the holiday get a special sticker
- Final celebration party in late August at Aldermarket Central, with a craft tent and visiting illustrator

In 2025, 4,820 Aldershire children completed the challenge, up from 4,510 in 2024.

## School class visits

Schools can book a free class visit for any year group. Sessions include a tour, book recommendations, library card sign-up if needed, and an age-appropriate themed activity. Bookings via aldershire.gov.uk/school-visits with at least 4 weeks notice.
