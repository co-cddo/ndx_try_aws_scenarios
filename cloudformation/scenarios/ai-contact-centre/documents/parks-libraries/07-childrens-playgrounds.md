# Children's playgrounds: locations and safety

Aldershire District Council operates 38 children's play areas across the district. Each is inspected regularly to RPII standards (Register of Play Inspectors International). For reports of damage or vandalism, call Parks on 01632 960140 or use the FixIt portal at aldershire.gov.uk/fixit.

## Locations and age ranges

The 38 sites split into:

- 9 destination playgrounds (full age range 0 to 14, accessible equipment, toilets nearby)
- 17 neighbourhood playgrounds (age 2 to 11)
- 12 toddler-only sites (age 1 to 5)

Destination playgrounds: Memorial Park, Vale Gardens, Riverside Park, Heath Road Rec, Aldermarket Heath, Mill Pond Field, Strawford Fields, Aldershire Vale Country Park, and Goose Bottom Park.

Vale Gardens and Memorial Park are wheelchair-accessible throughout, with a Nielsen wheelchair-accessible roundabout, accessible swing, and resin-bound surfacing.

## Inspection regime

| Inspection type | Frequency | Carried out by |
| --------------- | --------- | -------------- |
| Visual inspection (litter, broken parts, sharps) | Daily at the 9 destination sites; weekly at all others | Parks Patrol |
| Operational inspection | Every 6 weeks | Parks Operations team |
| Annual independent inspection | Once per year | RPII-certified external contractor |

Inspection records are kept for 21 years (the council's standard period for child-related risk records). They are available on request through the Information Governance team under the Environmental Information Regulations 2004.

## Reporting damage or hazards

Specific items that should be reported immediately on 01632 960140 (24-hour line for parks safety):

- Sharps (needles, broken glass) on the surfacing
- Exposed bolts or splinters on wooden equipment
- A swing chain that has unhooked
- Significant fire damage (bins, equipment, gates)

Lower-priority items (graffiti without offensive content, faded signage, loose wood chip) are handled in routine maintenance batches.

## Surfacing and accessibility

Aldershire is moving from rubber crumb to resin-bound and wet-pour surfacing as part of the 2025-2030 Playground Strategy, in response to environmental concerns about microplastic shedding. Five sites have been converted; the rest are scheduled for 2026 to 2029 subject to budget.

## Closed or under-construction sites

When a playground is closed for refurbishment, it is fenced off, signed in plain English, and a temporary sign in Welsh, Polish, Bengali, and Urdu at the higher-footfall sites. The bot can give the current re-opening estimate when asked about a specific site.

Comments about playground design and equipment selection go to the Parks Strategy team on 01632 960141.
