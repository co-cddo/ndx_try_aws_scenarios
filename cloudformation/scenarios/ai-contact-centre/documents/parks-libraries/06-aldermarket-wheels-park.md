# Aldermarket Wheels Park: skate park and BMX track

Aldermarket Wheels Park is a free, unstaffed, fully fenced skate and BMX facility on the eastern edge of Riverside Park. It opened in 2019 following a successful community fundraising campaign and a section 106 contribution from the Aldermarket East housing development.

Address: Riverside Park East entrance, Aldermarket, AL4 5KP. Bus: routes 12, 14, and 17. Cycle parking: 24 Sheffield stands inside the gate.

Phone for enquiries: Parks team on 01632 960140. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/wheels-park.

## What's there

- A 1,200 m squared concrete street plaza with banks, ledges, and a 1.6 m mini-ramp
- A separate 360 m squared bowl, depths between 1.5 m and 2.4 m
- A pump track (asphalt, 220 m loop) suitable for BMX, skateboards, scooters, and small wheels
- A junior skills area with kerbs and rolls (under 8s)
- Floodlights, on a free push-button timer until 21:30 in summer / 19:30 in winter

## Opening hours

The park is open 07:00 to 22:00 daily, with floodlights timed as above. After 22:00 the park is locked and any use is treated as trespass.

## Rules

Posted at the gate and on the council website:

1. Helmets are strongly recommended; full pads recommended for the bowl. Use is at the user's own risk.
2. No motorised vehicles (no e-bikes, e-scooters, motor-assisted boards, mopeds).
3. No alcohol, no glass bottles, no smoking inside the fence line.
4. No dogs on the concrete features.
5. No graffiti or sticker bombing on the ramps or signage. Tagged-out areas are repainted on the third Monday of each month by Parks Patrol.

Under-12s should be supervised by an adult during peak periods. There is no minimum age but the junior skills area is best suited to ages 4 to 8.

## Booking for events, jams, and competitions

The street plaza or bowl can be reserved for organised jams, demos, or filming for a flat fee of GBP 75 per half day. Apply through the Park Bookings team on 01632 960142 with at least 4 weeks notice. The pump track cannot be booked exclusively.

## Damage and repairs

Cracks, broken coping, and graffiti can be reported via aldershire.gov.uk/fixit. Critical damage (exposed rebar, large potholes on the pump track) is repaired within 5 working days. Cosmetic items are batched into a quarterly maintenance window.

A community group, Friends of Aldermarket Wheels Park (chair: Devon Atherton), holds quarterly meetings open to all users. Meetings are advertised on the Friends' Instagram and the council's events page.
