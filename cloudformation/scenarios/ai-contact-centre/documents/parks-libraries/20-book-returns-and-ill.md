# Book returns, drop boxes, and inter-library loans

Aldershire Library Service provides multiple ways to return library items, plus access to inter-library loans (ILL) through the East Mercia Libraries Consortium and the British Library Document Supply Service. Phone: 01632 960170.

## Returning items

Books and audiobooks can be returned to:

1. Any Aldershire library branch during opening hours, at the self-service kiosk or staffed desk.
2. The 24/7 external book drop boxes at:
   - Aldermarket Central (Town Hall Square wall, sheltered)
   - Aldermarket East (front porch)
   - Vale Library (side wall)
   - Cooper's Hill (under the canopy)
   - Strawford-on-Sea (promenade-side)
3. The Aldermobile mobile library, when it visits your village.
4. Any partner library in the East Mercia Libraries Consortium (12 council areas; full list at consortium.eastmercia-libraries.org.uk).

DVDs cannot go in the external book drops because of weather damage risk; they must be returned during opening hours to a member of staff.

## Receipts and confirmation

Self-service kiosks print a receipt of returned items; the kiosks also email a return confirmation to members who have opted in. Items returned via the drop box are checked in the next working morning. If the system says you still have an item out for up to 24 hours after a drop-box return, no action is needed; if it's still on the account after that, contact the branch.

## Damaged items

Spilled-on or torn items should be returned with a brief note. Staff assess minor damage at no cost. Major damage (water damage that destroys the book, animal damage, missing pages) is recharged at the replacement cost. Recharges can be paid in branch or by contactless card.

## Inter-library loans (ILL)

If an item is not held in Aldershire stock, members can request an inter-library loan via two routes:

- East Mercia Consortium ILL: free to the member. Typical wait 5 to 10 working days.
- British Library Document Supply: GBP 6 administration fee per item. Wait 7 to 21 working days. Best for academic articles, theses, and out-of-print books.

Request via aldershire.gov.uk/ill or by talking to staff. Members can have up to 8 active ILL holds at a time.

The bot can take an outline ILL enquiry but cannot place the request itself; members are transferred to a librarian.

## ILL collection and return

ILL items are collected from the requested branch. They cannot be returned to a different consortium council, only back to Aldershire Library Service. ILL loan periods vary depending on the lending library; the ILL ticket attached to each book gives the due date.

## Holds and reservations within Aldershire

Reservations within Aldershire stock are free and can be sent to any branch. Standard wait depends on demand; recent fiction can take 4 to 12 weeks. Members are notified by email or SMS when an item is ready and have 7 calendar days to collect.
