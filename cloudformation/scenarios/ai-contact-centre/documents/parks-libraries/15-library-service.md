# Aldershire Library Service: branches and opening hours

Aldershire District Council operates 9 libraries plus a mobile library serving outlying villages. The service holds around 320,000 physical items and is part of the East Mercia Libraries Consortium for inter-library loans. Phone: 01632 960170. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/libraries.

## Mobile library

The mobile library (the "Aldermobile") covers 31 villages on a 4-week rota. The current timetable is at aldershire.gov.uk/aldermobile. Stops are typically 30 to 45 minutes. The Aldermobile is wheelchair-accessible (rear lift) and has a small computer terminal.

## Bank holidays and Christmas

All libraries close on bank holidays. Christmas closure is 24 December (close at 13:00) through 1 January inclusive, reopening on 2 January.

## Strikes, snow days, and short-notice closures

Short-notice closures are posted on:

- The library service X account (@AldershireLibs)
- aldershire.gov.uk/libraries
- Each branch's outside noticeboard
- The chatbot's branch-specific opening-hours response

If you have items due during a closure, the loan auto-renews to the next opening day, no fines incurred.

## Friends groups and trustees

Each branch has a Friends group; the Aldermarket Central group is the largest (520 members). Friends groups support events, summer reading, and local history collections. Membership is free.
