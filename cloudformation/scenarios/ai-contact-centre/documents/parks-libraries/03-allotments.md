## Plot sizes and rents (2026/27)

- Full plot (250 m squared): GBP 56 per year
- Half plot (125 m squared): GBP 30 per year
- Quarter plot (62 m squared, beginner sites only): GBP 18 per year

Concessionary rents (50% off) are available for tenants in receipt of Council Tax Support, Universal Credit with no earned-income element, or Pension Credit guarantee element.

## Waiting list

The combined district waiting list has approximately 380 people. Waits vary widely:

- Old Brickworks: typical wait under 4 months
- North Aldermarket: typical wait 14 to 18 months
- Vale Lane: typical wait 28 to 36 months
- Mill Pond: closed to new applicants until 2027

Apply online at aldershire.gov.uk/allotments or by phone. You may name up to three preferred sites, in priority order.

## Tenancy rules

Standard Aldershire tenancy conditions apply, including: no permanent structures over 2.4 metres, no livestock other than bees (with separate apiary licence), no bonfires between April and September, and a requirement to cultivate at least 75% of the plot within the first growing season.

Annual site inspections are carried out each June by the Allotments Officer with the Site Secretary. Tenants who repeatedly fail inspection receive a 28-day improvement notice; a second failed inspection triggers a Notice to Quit.
