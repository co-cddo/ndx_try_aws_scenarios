# Wedding and civil partnership ceremonies: venues and fees

Aldershire District Council Registration Service performs civil marriage and civil partnership ceremonies at the register office and at over 30 approved venues across the district. Phone: 01632 960186. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/wedding-ceremonies.

## Where you can be married

You can have a civil marriage or civil partnership ceremony at:

1. The Aldermarket Register Office (Town Hall Annex)
2. Any "approved premises" licensed by the council, for example:
   - Aldershire Vale House (a Grade II listed manor on the Country Park edge)
   - The Strawford Pavilion (seaside Edwardian venue)
   - The Aldermarket Town Hall State Room
   - Vale Gardens Marquee (May to September only)
   - Heath Road Cricket Club Pavilion
   - 28 other licensed hotels, country houses, and registered venues
3. A religious building, if the religious organisation can authorise marriages and a registrar is invited (for example, Aldermarket Methodist Hall has its own authorised person and does not need a council registrar)

The full list of approved premises is at aldershire.gov.uk/approved-premises and is updated each January.

## Ceremony types and fees (2026/27)

Register Office ceremonies (statutory and short):

- Statutory ceremony, Mon to Thu, max 6 attendees including witnesses: GBP 57
- Short ceremony, weekday, register office, max 12 attendees: GBP 95
- Saturday short register office ceremony: GBP 175

Standard register office ceremonies (full ceremony, music, readings, full guest list up to 35):

- Mon to Thu: GBP 280
- Friday: GBP 320
- Saturday: GBP 380
- Sunday and bank holiday: GBP 460

Approved premises ceremonies (registrar fee, on top of the venue's own fees):

- Mon to Thu: GBP 460
- Friday: GBP 540
- Saturday: GBP 620
- Sunday and bank holiday: GBP 720

These fees do not include the venue hire (paid to the venue), the marriage notice (GBP 35 each at the appointment stage), or the marriage certificate (GBP 12.50).

## Booking lead time

Couples typically book ceremonies 6 to 18 months in advance for Saturdays in summer; weekday and winter weekends are usually available within 12 to 16 weeks.

The bot can check availability for register office ceremony slots but cannot reserve a slot. A confirmed reservation requires a deposit (50% of the ceremony fee) which is paid by card to the booking team.

## Personalisation

Couples can:

- Choose readings (any non-religious content; the registrar reviews readings 7 days before the ceremony)
- Choose music (any non-religious content; live or recorded)
- Write their own non-religious vows (in addition to the statutory declarations)
- Use ring exchanges, sand-pouring, hand-fasting, etc.

Religious content (hymns, prayers, scriptural readings) is not permitted in a civil ceremony.

## Witnesses

Two witnesses are required for a marriage. They must be physically present and able to understand the ceremony in English (the registrar can accept witnesses who use British Sign Language with a registered interpreter). The witnesses are named on the Marriage Schedule and sign it after the ceremony.

## Cancellations and weather

Cancellation refunds: 75% more than 90 days out, 50% between 90 and 30 days, 0% inside 30 days.

For outdoor approved premises ceremonies (e.g. Vale Gardens Marquee), the registrar follows the venue's wet-weather plan. There is no refund for weather-driven indoor moves.
