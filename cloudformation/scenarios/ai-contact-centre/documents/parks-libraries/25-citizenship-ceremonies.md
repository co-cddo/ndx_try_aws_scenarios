# Citizenship ceremonies at Aldershire District Council

Aldershire District Council's Registration Service holds citizenship ceremonies on behalf of the Home Office for new British citizens. Phone: 01632 960188. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/citizenship-ceremony.

## When a ceremony is needed

After the Home Office approves an application for British citizenship for an applicant aged 18 or over, the Home Office issues an invitation letter asking the applicant to book a ceremony. The ceremony must be held within 90 days of the invitation date. After 90 days the invitation lapses and the applicant has to write to the Home Office to request a fresh one.

Children under 18 do not have a ceremony; they receive their certificate by post.

Citizenship ceremonies are run by district / unitary councils, not by the Home Office directly. Applicants choose the council that holds the ceremony, normally one near where they live or work.

## Booking with Aldershire

To book a ceremony with Aldershire:

1. Receive the Home Office invitation letter (it has a unique reference).
2. Phone 01632 960188 or use aldershire.gov.uk/citizenship-ceremony with the invitation reference and your full name as it appears on the letter.
3. Pay any guest fees in advance.

Aldershire holds:

- A monthly group ceremony at Aldermarket Town Hall State Room (around 30 to 50 new citizens at once, plus families). Saturday mornings.
- Smaller weekday ceremonies at Aldermarket Register Office (max 12 new citizens). For applicants who prefer a smaller setting or who need a quicker date.

Lead time is currently 4 to 6 weeks for a slot.

## Fees and what is included

The standard Home Office citizenship fee already covers one ceremony place for the applicant. There is no additional fee to attend.

Aldershire charges:

- Each accompanying guest: GBP 5 (children under 12 are free)
- Recorded music or photographer service: free
- A commemorative photograph with the Mayor: GBP 12 (optional)

Each new citizen receives:

- The British citizenship certificate
- A welcome pack (Aldershire-branded)
- A small UK flag
- A commemorative card signed by the Mayor

## What happens at the ceremony

The full ceremony lasts around 45 minutes. The order:

1. Welcome by the Aldershire Registrar
2. National anthem (recorded; standing optional)
3. Oath of Allegiance (or affirmation, a non-religious version)
4. Pledge of Loyalty to the United Kingdom
5. Presentation of the certificate by the Mayor or Deputy Mayor
6. Photograph with the Mayor (optional, for those who paid for it)
7. Light refreshments

Applicants choose either the religious oath ("I swear by Almighty God...") or the non-religious affirmation ("I do solemnly, sincerely and truly declare and affirm..."). Both have the same legal weight.

## Dress code

There is no dress code, but most attendees dress smartly. Cultural dress is welcome. The Mayor wears the Mayoral chain of office.

## Photography and filming

Aldershire takes a single official photograph of each new citizen receiving their certificate. Personal photography by family is welcome. Filming is permitted by family with the new citizen's consent, but flash photography is asked to be limited during the oath itself.

## Accessibility

The State Room is wheelchair-accessible. Hearing loop available. BSL interpreter on request with at least 14 days' notice. The registrar can also arrange a private "small ceremony" alternative for applicants with sensory or anxiety needs.
