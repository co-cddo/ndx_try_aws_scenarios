# Parish council elections: candidate nominations

Aldershire District Council acts as the Returning Authority for parish council elections held inside the district. Phone: Electoral Services 01632 960193. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/parish-elections.

## Parish councils in the district

There are 22 parish councils in Aldershire and 1 town council (Strawford-on-Sea). Together they form the lowest tier of UK local government, with powers over local amenities, parish open spaces, footpaths, neighbourhood plans, and small grants. Each parish typically has between 5 and 15 councillor seats.

## Election cycle

Most parish councils hold ordinary elections every 4 years, on the first Thursday in May. The next ordinary election cycle for Aldershire parishes is May 2027. By-elections to fill casual vacancies occur as and when needed.

A casual vacancy can be:

- Filled by election if 10 electors of the parish call for one, within 14 days of the vacancy being publicly advertised
- Filled by co-option if no election is called

## Standing as a candidate

You can stand for parish councillor if you are aged 18 or over and:

- A British, Irish, qualifying Commonwealth, or qualifying EU citizen, AND
- Resident in the parish, OR
- Have worked in the parish for at least 12 months (as your principal or only place of work), OR
- Own or rent property in the parish, OR
- Have lived within 4.8 km (3 miles) of the parish boundary for the last 12 months

You cannot stand if you are bankrupt, are a paid officer of the parish council, or have been sentenced to a prison term of 3 months or more in the last 5 years (whether or not the sentence was suspended), among other disqualifications.

## Nomination paperwork

Each candidate must submit:

- A nomination paper, signed by 2 electors registered in the parish (proposer and seconder); not 10 as for principal authority elections
- A consent to nomination, signed by the candidate
- A home address form (the candidate can choose to have their address withheld from public papers under the Localism Act 2011)

There is no deposit for parish council elections. Forms are at aldershire.gov.uk/parish-elections-forms or available by post.

## Nomination deadline

For an ordinary election on the first Thursday in May, nominations must be delivered, in person, to the Returning Officer at the Aldermarket Town Hall before 4pm on the 19th working day before polling day. For a 6 May 2027 election, that is approximately 8 April 2027 (subject to confirmation in the formal Notice of Election).

Late or incomplete nominations are not accepted. Aldershire's Electoral Services team offers a free informal pre-check up to 2 working days before the formal deadline; book on 01632 960193.

## After nomination closes

If the number of valid candidates is less than or equal to the number of seats, the candidates are returned uncontested and no poll takes place. Smaller parish councils often run uncontested elections.

If the number of candidates exceeds the number of seats, a poll is held on polling day. The count is held at the Aldermarket Town Hall the morning after polling day.

## Casual vacancies and co-options

Casual vacancies are advertised on the parish noticeboard and the council website for 14 days. Anyone meeting the candidate eligibility above can write to the parish clerk expressing interest in co-option. The parish council then chooses by simple majority vote.

## Help and training

Aldershire runs a free "Standing for the Parish" briefing each January, and a "Newly Elected Parish Councillor" induction in May after the election. Both are 2 hours and held at Aldermarket Town Hall and online. Book via aldershire.gov.uk/parish-elections.
