# Birth registration: the 42-day rule

In England, a baby's birth must be registered within 42 days. Aldershire District Council's Registration Service registers all births that take place inside the Aldershire registration district, regardless of where the parents live. Phone: 01632 960180. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/registrar.

## Where to register

The main register office is at:

Aldermarket Register Office
Town Hall Annex, 16 Town Hall Square, AL1 1JT

Satellite appointments are available weekly at Strawford-on-Sea and Cooper's Hill. The full schedule is published at aldershire.gov.uk/register-office-locations.

If your baby was born in Aldershire but you live elsewhere, you can register at your local register office under the "declaration" process; the certificate is then issued by Aldershire by post within 7 days. There is no extra fee.

## Booking

Appointments are bookable up to 6 weeks in advance and required (no walk-ins). Book via aldershire.gov.uk/register-birth or by calling 01632 960180. Average lead time is currently 12 to 18 working days.

The chatbot can take a booking slot enquiry but cannot complete the booking; the registrar requires confirmed information.

## Who can register

Married parents: either parent can register and the marriage details apply automatically.

Unmarried parents:

- The mother can register alone (the father's name will not appear)
- Both parents together can register and both can be named
- The father can register alone only if he attends with a Statutory Declaration of Parentage signed by the mother, or with a parental responsibility agreement

Same-sex female parents: under section 42 / 43 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008, the second parent can be registered if conception was through a licensed UK clinic; otherwise an alternative legal route applies.

## What to bring

- Hospital "Notification of Birth" form (sent automatically to the registrar by the hospital, usually no need to bring a copy)
- Both parents' photo ID (passport, driving licence, biometric residence permit)
- Both parents' proof of address (council tax, utility bill, tenancy)
- Marriage or civil partnership certificate, if applicable
- Both parents' dates and places of birth
- The mother's maiden name (if she has changed it)

Appointments take around 30 minutes.

## Fees

Registering a birth is free. Birth certificates cost GBP 12.50 each (standard or short form). Order at the appointment, or later online via the General Register Office.

## What if you miss the 42 days?

The council still completes the registration. There is no fine in England for late registration of a birth, but the process is more involved: the registrar issues a Late Registration form which must be signed by the informant. Allow extra time and bring all the documents above.

## Stillbirth

Stillbirths must be registered within 42 days. The Aldermarket Register Office offers a quiet, separate appointment with a senior registrar. Phone 01632 960181 (direct line, bypasses general queue) or ask the bot to transfer.
