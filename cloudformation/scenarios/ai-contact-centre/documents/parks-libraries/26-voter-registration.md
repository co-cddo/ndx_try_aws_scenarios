# Voter registration: annual canvass and rolling registration

Aldershire District Council's Electoral Services team maintains the electoral register for the district. Phone: 01632 960190. Web: aldershire.gov.uk/voter-registration.

## Two ways to register

1. Online via gov.uk/register-to-vote (preferred). Takes around 5 minutes if you have your National Insurance number and date of birth.
2. By paper form, requested from Electoral Services on 01632 960190 or downloaded from aldershire.gov.uk/register-paper.

Online and paper applications both go into the council's electoral management system; processing typically takes 2 to 3 working days.

The chatbot can confirm what details are needed and walk you through gov.uk/register-to-vote, but it cannot register you directly; the gov.uk service has its own identity step.

## The annual canvass

Every summer, Aldershire writes to every household in the district to confirm who is eligible to vote. This is the annual canvass, mandated by law. The 2026 canvass schedule is:

- 1 July to 14 July 2026: data-matching phase (the council compares national records to confirm most households automatically)
- 15 July to 31 August 2026: forms and emails sent to households where a change is suspected
- 1 September to 15 October 2026: reminder forms to non-responders
- 16 October to 30 November 2026: doorstep canvass to remaining non-responders

Returning the form (or replying by SMS, email, or online) is a legal duty. Failure to respond can attract a fine of up to GBP 1,000, although in practice Aldershire prosecutes only persistent non-responders after multiple reminders.

The published electoral register is republished on 1 December each year using canvass results.

## Rolling registration

You do not have to wait for the canvass. You can register at any time via gov.uk/register-to-vote. New entries are added monthly to the register (the "monthly update notice"); the only exception is the run-up to an election, when there is a registration deadline (typically 12 working days before polling day).

## Who can register

You can register to vote in Aldershire if you are:

- A British, Irish, or qualifying Commonwealth citizen, OR
- An EU citizen (see the EU voting rights article for the post-Brexit rules)
- Aged 16 or over (16 and 17 year olds can register; they vote when they turn 18, except in some elections such as Scottish Parliament where 16 is the voting age)
- Resident at an address in Aldershire (or with a connection; see "special category" below)

## Special category voters

People without a permanent address (homeless, narrow-boat dwellers, travellers) can register using a Declaration of Local Connection, available from Electoral Services. Service personnel can register as a service voter. British citizens overseas can register at their last UK address; under the Elections Act 2022 the 15-year limit was abolished, so any British citizen who once lived in the UK can register.

## Open and edited registers

Aldershire holds two registers:

- The full register: used only for elections, jury selection, credit checks, and a small number of other lawful purposes.
- The open (edited) register: sold to a limited list of marketing companies.

You can opt out of the open register at any time by writing to electoral@aldershire.gov.uk or by ticking the box on your canvass form.
