# Dog walking and on-leash zones

Aldershire District Council welcomes dogs in all parks and open spaces, with specific on-leash and exclusion zones for safety, wildlife, and shared use. Dog control rules are set out in the Aldershire Public Spaces Protection Order (PSPO) 2024-2027. Phone: Environmental Enforcement on 01632 960160.

## Where dogs are welcome

Dogs are welcome off-lead, under close control, in:

- Aldermarket Heath (open access)
- Vale Common (off-lead zone, marked by green-tipped posts)
- Strawford Fields recreation ground
- Goose Bottom Park (off-lead zone, except the formal gardens)
- Most areas of Aldershire Vale Country Park (see country park article for grazing season exceptions)

"Under close control" means the dog responds reliably to recall and stays in sight of the handler.

## On-lead-only areas

Dogs must be on a lead no longer than 2 metres in:

- All formal parks (Memorial Park, Vale Gardens, Town Hall Gardens)
- All children's playgrounds (dogs are excluded from the play surface itself; lead-only on the surrounding paths)
- All sports pitch surfaces during fixtures
- All cemetery grounds
- Shared-use paths along the Riverside Park promenade
- Aldermarket bus station forecourt

## Dog exclusion zones

Dogs are not permitted at all in:

- Children's play surfaces (inside the fence line)
- The formal rose garden at Vale Gardens
- The new pollinator meadow at Memorial Park
- Outdoor gym surfacing
- The Strawford Cliffs LNR cliff edge between 1 March and 31 July (ground-nesting bird protection)

Assistance dogs are exempt from exclusion zones.

## Fouling

Picking up after your dog is required everywhere. Failure to do so is a fixed penalty notice of GBP 100 (reduced to GBP 75 if paid within 14 days). The council provides 240 dog waste bins, emptied to the same schedule as litter bins. Bagged waste can also go in any general litter bin.

The "no bag, no excuse" rule applies: an enforcement officer may issue a fixed penalty notice purely for not carrying the means to pick up, even if the dog has not fouled.

## Number of dogs

In a public place under the Aldershire PSPO, no person may walk more than 6 dogs at one time. Professional dog walkers operating commercially must register with the council on 01632 960160 and accept a code of conduct including liability insurance evidence.

## Dog wardens and lost dogs

The council does not run a separate dog warden out-of-hours service. Stray dogs found during office hours can be reported on 01632 960160 and are collected to the contracted boarding kennel at Mill End. Out of hours, the bot will direct callers to the kennel direct line. Reclaim fees: GBP 65 collection fee plus GBP 12 per night kennelled.

## Dangerous dogs

Aggressive dog incidents are a police matter (Aldershire Constabulary, 999 in progress, 101 otherwise). The council can act on PSPO breaches but does not investigate Dangerous Dogs Act offences.
