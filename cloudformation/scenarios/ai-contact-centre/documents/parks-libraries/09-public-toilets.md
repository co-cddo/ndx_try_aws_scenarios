# Public toilets: locations and opening times

Aldershire District Council operates 14 public toilet sites. Five are staffed (full attendant cover), eight are unstaffed-with-CCTV, and one is a Changing Places facility. For cleanliness or maintenance issues, call 01632 960150 or report via aldershire.gov.uk/fixit.

## Changing Places

The Changing Places facility at Aldermarket Library has:

- An adult-sized height-adjustable changing bench
- A ceiling track hoist (Molift)
- Space for a wheelchair plus two carers
- A peninsular toilet
- A privacy screen

Access is by RADAR key (held by reception) or by booking ahead on 01632 960150.

## Free vs charged

All Aldershire public toilets are free to use. There are no 20p turnstiles. Donation boxes at three sites support the Friends of Aldermarket Toilets community group, which co-funds extra hand-dryer maintenance.

## Cleaning and inspections

Staffed sites are cleaned at least three times per day. Unstaffed sites are cleaned daily by the contractor (Aldermarket Cleaning Services Ltd). A QR code in each cubicle goes to a one-tap rating and incident report form. Fly-tipping or sharps in toilets are escalated within 4 hours.

## Out-of-hours access

The Town Hall Square and bus station sites are the only Aldershire toilets open outside of typical park hours. There is no 24-hour facility in the district. The bot will signpost users requiring late-night facilities to the bus station and provide its postcode.
