# Library membership: joining and ID

Joining Aldershire Libraries is free for anyone who lives, works, or studies in the East Mercia Libraries Consortium area. The membership entitles users to borrow books, audiobooks, DVDs, eMagazines, eBooks, and to use library computers and PressReader.

## How to join

Three ways to join:

1. Online at aldershire.gov.uk/library-join: instant digital-only membership; physical card collected from any branch within 30 days.
2. In branch: bring proof of address (see below); a card is issued on the spot.
3. By post: for housebound or remote users; a paper application can be requested by phone on 01632 960170.

Children under 16 are signed up by a parent or guardian who countersigns the form.

## ID requirements

Bring one item of photo ID and one item of address evidence:

Photo ID: passport, photocard driving licence, council-issued elector ID, NHS card with photo, MOD photo card, or PASS-accredited proof-of-age card.

Address evidence (dated within the last 3 months): utility bill, council tax demand, bank statement, NHS letter, HMRC letter, tenancy agreement signed within the last 12 months, or a letter from a homelessness or supported-living service.

If the user has no fixed address, the staff member can accept a c/o address through a known charity or council service. Contact the Library Manager on 01632 960171.

## Card replacement

Lost or damaged cards are replaced for free for under-16s and over-65s. For other adults, the replacement fee is GBP 2.50 per card. The first card is always free.

## Borrowing limits

Each member can have up to:

- 30 items on loan at once (15 if under 11)
- 5 DVDs at once
- 8 inter-library loan reservations active

Loan periods are 3 weeks for books and audiobooks, and 1 week for DVDs. Items auto-renew up to 4 times unless reserved by another reader.

## Late items and fines

Aldershire abolished overdue fines on under-19 children's items in 2018, and on all stock in 2022. Items are simply blocked from further loans until returned. Lost items are charged at the replacement cost plus a GBP 1 admin fee.

## Privacy and data

Library data is held under a published Privacy Notice at aldershire.gov.uk/library-privacy. Borrowing history is retained for 60 days for operational reasons (renewals, holds, and stock decisions) and is then anonymised. Borrowing activity is not shared with any third party except where the law requires (for example, a court order).

## Visitor and reciprocal cards

Visitors from outside the East Mercia Consortium area can join with proof of UK address. Proof of overseas address is accepted only for a non-borrowing reading-room and PC card.
