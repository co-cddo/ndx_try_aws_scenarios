# Glass recycling banks

Although glass is collected at the kerb in the blue bin, many residents prefer to use bottle banks for bulk loads (after parties, weddings, or seasonal events). Aldershire operates 14 public bottle banks across the district.

## Rules of use

- Remove all lids and corks before depositing.
- Rinse bottles and jars to keep banks hygienic.
- Do not leave bags or cardboard boxes around the bank; this is fly tipping and may be prosecuted under the Environmental Protection Act 1990.
- No window glass, drinking glasses, ovenware, or light bulbs (these have different melting points).

## Reporting overfull banks

If a bank is full, locked, or damaged, report it on 01432 556 250 with the site name and a photo if possible. Standard service is to collect within 48 hours of report. The contractor for glass collections is Verdant Logistics under contract WS24-007.
