# New build properties: bins and first collection

## Bin entitlement at handover

Each new residential property in Aldershire is entitled to a starter pack of waste containers, supplied free of charge once on completion of the property. The standard pack contains:

- One 240-litre brown bin (general refuse)
- One 240-litre blue bin (mixed recycling)
- One 23-litre outdoor food caddy and one 5-litre kitchen caddy
- One starter roll of 50 compostable caddy liners

A green garden waste bin is not included; this is an opt-in subscription service (see 02-garden-waste-subscription.md).

## How to order

Developers should provide bin packs on handover where the Section 106 agreement requires it. If a pack has not been supplied:

1. The new occupier registers the move-in at aldershire.example.gov.uk/new-build.
2. Provide proof of completion (solicitor letter or completion certificate) and council tax registration reference.
3. Bins are delivered within 10 working days. The first collection follows the established round schedule for the property's postcode.

## Round assignment

New estates may not appear on the published round map immediately. The Waste Routing team adds new addresses each Friday to the following Monday's round. If your property is not yet routed, the council provides up to four free black sacks of refuse collection support (lifted from outside the property) until the round is updated. Phone 01432 556 200 to arrange.

## Houses in multiple occupation (HMO)

HMOs of 6 or more units have a separate bin assessment, often involving 1100-litre Eurobins or sets of 360-litre wheelie bins. Contact the Waste Operations team to schedule a site survey. The landlord or managing agent is responsible for storage, presentation, and bin maintenance.

## Multi-occupancy / flats above shops

Where the only available curtilage is shared with a commercial unit, a Joint Storage Plan must be agreed before bins are issued. Contact 01432 556 230.
