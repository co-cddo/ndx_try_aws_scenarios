# River and waterway cleaning

## About the scheme

Aldershire is an inland district crossed by the River Alder and a network of canalised drainage ditches. Although Aldershire has no coast, the council operates a riverway cleaning programme equivalent to a coastal beach-clean operation in scale and ambition.

## Planned cleans

The Streetscene team conducts six scheduled river cleans per year, in partnership with the Environment Agency and the Aldershire Wildlife Trust:

| Month | Stretch | Length |
| ----- | ------- | ------ |
| March | Vale Bridge to Aldermarket Mill | 2.4 km |
| May | Aldermarket Mill to Riverside Walk | 1.8 km |
| June | Pendle Road industrial section | 0.9 km |
| August | Wendlewood Lock to Brackton Weir | 3.1 km |
| October | River Alder upper reach (rural) | 5.2 km |
| November | All canalised drainage ditches | varies |

Volunteers join via the Tidy Towns scheme (see 27-litter-picking-volunteer.md).

## Equipment and safety

River cleans use:

- Inflatable safety vessels (council-owned)
- Throwlines and life rings
- Long-handled litter pickers
- Wetsuits where wading is required (volunteers must be 18+ and competent swimmers)

Each clean is led by a qualified Inland Waterways Coordinator. Volunteers must attend a 90-minute river-safety briefing before participating.

## Reporting waterway pollution

If you see oil sheens, dead fish, or chemical discolouration on the River Alder, report immediately:

- Environment Agency hotline: 0800 80 70 60 (24/7)
- Aldershire Environmental Health: 01432 556 280

## Common items recovered

In 2025, 38 tonnes of debris were recovered, including:

- 412 shopping trolleys (see 29-abandoned-shopping-trolleys.md)
- 8,200 plastic bottles
- 612 traffic cones (returned to highways)
- 14 supermarket baskets
- 6 mattresses
- Numerous tyres, fishing lines, and golf balls

## Wildlife considerations

Cleans avoid the kingfisher nesting season (late March to mid-July) on the upper reach. Sections marked as Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSIs) are cleared by Environment Agency licensed contractors only.
