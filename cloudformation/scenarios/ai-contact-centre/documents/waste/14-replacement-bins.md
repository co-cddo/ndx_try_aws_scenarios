# Replacement bins

## Damaged bins

If your bin is cracked, has a broken lid, faulty wheel, or damaged hinge, request a repair on aldershire.example.gov.uk/bin-repair or by phone on 01432 556 200. Most repairs are made on-site within 10 working days. If the bin is beyond economic repair, a free replacement is issued.

## Stolen bins

Bins are council property and remain so even when at your address. To report a stolen bin:

1. File a report with the police if you believe a theft offence has occurred. Get a crime reference number.
2. Contact Aldershire on 01432 556 200 with the crime reference and the date you last saw the bin.
3. The council will issue a replacement free of charge for one stolen bin per property in any 24-month period. A second stolen-bin replacement within that window is charged at £40.

## Lost or removed bins

If a previous occupant took the bin, or the bin has gone missing without evidence of theft, a replacement is issued for an administrative charge:

| Bin | Charge |
| --- | ------ |
| 140-litre brown or blue | £30 |
| 240-litre brown or blue | £35 |
| 360-litre brown or blue (assisted larger capacity) | £45 |
| 23-litre food caddy | £15 |
| 5-litre kitchen caddy | £8 |
| 240-litre green garden bin (subscriber) | £30 |

## Council-managed transfers

If you have moved into a property where the bin should have been left by the previous occupant under the council's transfer scheme, replacement is free. You will need to provide the date of move-in and the property's previous occupier name (where known).

## Marking bins

Each bin is fitted with an RFID chip linked to its property address. Residents must not move bins between properties. Exchanging bins voids the warranty and may delay future collections.
