# Blue bin schedule (mixed recycling, fortnightly alternating)

## Frequency

Blue bins are emptied fortnightly, alternating with brown bin (refuse) weeks. This means every household receives one bin lift per week, alternating streams.

A and B alternate. Look up your week at aldershire.example.gov.uk/my-bin.

## Co-mingled stream

Aldershire collects all blue-bin recyclables together in a fully co-mingled stream. Sorting happens at the Pendle Road Materials Recovery Facility (MRF), where optical sorters, eddy currents, and manual pickers separate paper, card, plastic, glass, and metal.

## Acceptance summary

Yes: plastic bottles, pots, tubs, trays (clear or coloured), tins, cans, aerosols, paper, card, glass jars and bottles, foil scrunched into a ball, cartons.

No: black plastic, film and bags, food-soiled card, polystyrene, electrical items, batteries (use kerbside bag scheme), nappies.

## Why glass goes in the blue bin

Aldershire moved glass into the blue bin in 2022 after a £4.2m upgrade to the MRF that added a glass cleaning line. Glass kerbside captures rose 31% in the first year of the new arrangement. Bottle banks remain available for bulk loads (see 06-glass-recycling-banks.md).

## Contamination

Bins with significant non-recyclable content are tagged and not emptied. The 2025 contamination rate was 6.4%. Repeated contamination triggers Waste Education visits and, in extreme cases, removal of the recycling bin under Section 46.

## Recycling rate

The district achieved 49.7% household recycling in 2025, ranked 4th in the regional league table. Target for 2027 is 55%.
