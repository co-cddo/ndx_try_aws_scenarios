# Rural collection: narrow lanes and alternative pickup points

## Why rural areas are different

Around 18% of Aldershire's housing is in rural settlements, hamlets, and isolated farms. Standard 26-tonne refuse collection vehicles (RCVs) cannot safely access many narrow lanes, single-track roads, ungritted hills, and unmade tracks. Aldershire operates a tiered service to ensure rural residents are not disadvantaged.

## Tier 1: standard service

Where lanes are passable for an RCV, collection follows the published schedule (see 21, 22, 23, 24). This applies to most of AL5, AL6, AL7, and the village cores of AL8.

## Tier 2: small RCV

A 7.5-tonne small RCV serves lanes too narrow for the main fleet but accessible to a smaller vehicle. Routes covered include:

- Mill Lane, AL5
- Vale Cobbles area, AL5
- Brackton Hill, AL7
- Wendlewood Common Road, AL6

The collection day is the same as the main round, but small RCVs typically arrive between 10:00 and 14:00.

## Tier 3: alternative presentation point (APP)

For properties on tracks, drives, or single-track lanes that cannot be safely accessed even by the small RCV, residents present bins at an Alternative Presentation Point (APP) at the nearest accessible junction. The APP is a council-agreed gathering place, often a lay-by or junction widening.

| APP | Properties served | Postcodes |
| --- | ----------------- | --------- |
| Brackton Cross lay-by | 18 properties (Brackton End Lane) | AL7 |
| Wendlewood Common gateway | 22 properties (Common Cottages, Long Track) | AL6 |
| Vale Top junction | 14 properties (Vale Top, The Spinney) | AL5 |
| Aldershire Edge layby | 38 properties (Edge Farm row) | AL8 |

The council provides 4-wheel rural-spec bins for properties using APPs (where storage allows). Crews unload at the APP and the residents return bins to the property.

## Tier 4: bag-only

For very isolated farms (typically 1-2 properties at the end of a track), a bag-only service operates. Residents place clear sacks at the APP. Bag size and quantity limits apply; phone 01432 556 200 to register.

## Winter weather

Rural rounds are most affected by snow, ice, and floods. The council's Winter Operations Plan deploys 4x4 vehicles to maintain services where possible. Severe weather may force a stand-down; affected residents can present extra capacity at the next collection (see 12-holiday-schedule-changes.md).

## Reviewing your access

If your property's access conditions change (new gate, lane widening, lane narrowing, surface change), email rural@aldershire.example.gov.uk and a Routing Officer will reassess.
