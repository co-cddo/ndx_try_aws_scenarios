# Battery recycling

## Why batteries cannot go in your bin

Lithium-ion and alkaline batteries cause around 700 fires a year in UK waste vehicles and material recovery facilities. Aldershire experienced two confirmed battery-fire incidents at the Pendle Road MRF in 2024, both traced to vape devices in the residual waste stream.

## Kerbside scheme

Place small household batteries (AA, AAA, button cells, 9V) in a clear sandwich bag, knot it, and balance it on top of the blue recycling bin. Crews collect the bag separately.

Accepted: dry-cell batteries, button cells (taped over), small lithium cells under 100Wh.

Not accepted at kerbside: car batteries, e-bike batteries, power tool packs, vape devices intact. These must be taken to the Pendle Road centre.

## Vapes (single-use e-cigarettes)

Since the producer-responsibility regulations of 2024, all retailers selling vapes must accept used devices for recycling. The council additionally accepts vapes at Pendle Road. Do not place vapes in any kerbside bin under any circumstances.

## Lead-acid (vehicle) batteries

Free disposal at Pendle Road. Bring proof of address. Maximum two batteries per visit; trade volumes need a commercial waste contract (see 18-commercial-waste).
