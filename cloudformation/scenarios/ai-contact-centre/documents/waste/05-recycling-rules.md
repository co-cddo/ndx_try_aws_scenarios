# Recycling: what goes in which bin

## Blue bin (mixed recycling, kerbside)

Yes please:

- Plastic bottles, pots, tubs, and trays (rinsed, lids on)
- Tins, cans, and aerosols (empty)
- Paper and card (clean and dry)
- Glass jars and bottles (rinsed, lids removed)
- Aluminium foil and clean foil trays (scrunched into a ball larger than a golf ball)
- Cartons (Tetra Pak style)

No thank you:

- Black plastic trays (cannot be detected by optical sorters)
- Crisp packets, sweet wrappers, or film lids (return to supermarkets)
- Nappies, sanitary products, or food-soiled card
- Textiles, electrical items, or batteries
- Polystyrene packaging or bubble wrap

## Brown bin (general refuse)

Anything that cannot be recycled, composted, or returned to a take-back scheme. Wrap broken glass, sharp metal, or pet litter in newspaper before bagging.

## Contamination

If a blue bin contains more than 10% non-recyclable material by volume, the crew tags the bin, leaves it at the kerb, and the resident is asked to remove the offending items. Persistent contamination (three tagged bins in a 12-month period) triggers a Waste Education visit and, in extreme cases, removal of the recycling bin. Aldershire's contamination rate in 2025 was 6.4%, below the regional average of 9.1%.

## Doubtful items

When in doubt, "rinse and wonder, leave out and ponder, throw in brown bin if no answer found." The Aldershire Recycling Hotline is 01432 556 240, Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00, or use the A-Z waste guide at aldershire.example.gov.uk/recycle-az.
