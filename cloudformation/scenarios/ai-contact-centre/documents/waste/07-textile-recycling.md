# Textile recycling and donation

## What can be donated

Clean, dry, and reusable items including:

- Adult and children's clothing
- Shoes (paired and tied together)
- Bedding (clean sheets, duvet covers, blankets)
- Belts, bags, and clean accessories
- Curtains and tablecloths

Damaged textiles can still be recycled into industrial cleaning cloths or insulation. Even single shoes, ripped jeans, or stained T-shirts have value if presented dry and free of mould.

## Charity partners

The council works with three preferred partners under a textile reuse memorandum of understanding (MoU). Around £42,000 a year is returned to local charities through the textile bank network. The lead partner for 2025-2027 is Aldershire Hospice, with secondary partners British Heart Foundation and TRAID.

## Kerbside textile bag scheme

Twice a year (mid-March and mid-September) every household receives a clear textile sack. Fill it, label it, and place it on top of your blue bin on collection day. Crews tip the textile sacks into a separate compartment of the recycling vehicle. Roughly 38 tonnes were diverted via this scheme in 2025.

## Concerns

If you suspect a textile bank is being misused as fly-tipping cover, report it on 01432 556 250.
