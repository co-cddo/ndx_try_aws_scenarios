# Extra capacity and assisted collection

## When extra capacity is allowed

Households of six or more permanent residents may apply for a 360-litre brown refuse bin in place of the standard 240-litre. Applications are assessed against:

- Number of permanent residents (council tax and electoral roll cross-reference)
- Whether children under three are in the household (nappy allowance)
- Medical conditions producing extra waste (continence, dialysis, stoma care)

The application form is at aldershire.example.gov.uk/extra-capacity. Decisions take up to 15 working days.

## Nappy and incontinence allowance

Households with children under three or with a household member with continence needs receive an additional 240-litre brown bin (or, where space is limited, weekly small clear-sack collection). No fee. Annual review of eligibility.

## Assisted collection

If no one in the household is able to move the bin to the kerb (due to age, disability, or temporary illness), apply for assisted collection. Crews collect the bin from a designated point on the property (e.g. side gate, garage entrance) and return it after emptying.

Eligibility:

- All adult residents are aged 75+ with no able-bodied helper, or
- A resident has a long-term health condition or disability that prevents bin movement, or
- Temporary assistance for up to 12 weeks following surgery or hospital discharge

Apply on 01432 556 200. Crews are notified the same day. The household reference is flagged on the in-cab system used by collection crews.

## Recycling capacity

Households with high recycling volume can request a second 240-litre blue bin free of charge if they evidence regular full bins for 8 consecutive weeks. The Waste Education team may visit to advise on segregation before authorising.

## Reviews

All extra capacity entitlements are reviewed every two years. Residents are written to in advance and asked to confirm circumstances have not changed.
