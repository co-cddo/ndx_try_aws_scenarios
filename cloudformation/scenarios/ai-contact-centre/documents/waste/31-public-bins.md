# Public street and park bins

## Coverage

Aldershire maintains 1,210 public bins across the district:

- 412 dual-aperture bins (general waste + recycling) in town centres
- 462 single-aperture general waste bins on residential streets
- 86 dog waste bins (red lid)
- 142 park bins (including 28 picnic-zone enlarged bins)
- 38 cigarette stubber bins outside pubs and offices
- 28 nappy bins at children's playgrounds
- 18 sanitary bins in public toilets
- 24 large 1100L Eurobins at outdoor markets and event spaces

## Collection frequency

| Location type | Frequency |
| ------------- | --------- |
| Aldermarket town centre | Twice daily (07:00 and 14:00) |
| Vale High Street | Daily (08:00) |
| Residential streets | 2 times per week |
| Country parks | Daily during peak season; 3 times per week off-season |
| Picnic zones | Daily, plus on-demand response in heatwaves |

## Reporting overflowing bins

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/public-bin
- Phone: 01432 556 250

Tell us the bin location (postcode and street name or park area). Photographs help. Standard response is to clear within 24 hours.

## Bin maintenance

Damaged bins are reported and replaced within 10 working days. The current public bin specification is the "Townscene 90L Galvanised", finished in Aldershire dark green.

## Bin sponsorship

Local businesses and community groups can sponsor a public bin. Sponsorship costs £450 per year, includes a discreet plaque, and funds an additional emptying frequency for that bin. Apply at aldershire.example.gov.uk/sponsor-bin.

## Why we don't add more bins

Adding bins in residential areas often increases littering by attracting domestic side waste from households. Each bin location is reviewed against the district's Street Furniture Strategy 2024 before approval. New bin requests are evaluated quarterly by the Streetscene team.
