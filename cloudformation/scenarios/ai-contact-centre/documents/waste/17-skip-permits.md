# Skip permits

## When you need a permit

A skip permit is required whenever a skip is placed on the public highway, including the carriageway, footway, verge, or shared parking bay. Skips wholly on private property (driveways, private courtyards) do not need a permit but should be reported to the supplier.

## How to apply

Skip hire companies usually arrange the permit on your behalf, but the council also accepts direct applications:

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/skip-permit
- Phone: 01432 556 260 (Highways Licensing, Mon-Fri 09:00-16:00)
- Email: skips@aldershire.example.gov.uk

Allow 5 working days for processing. Same-day permits are charged at double the standard fee.

## Fees

| Duration | Fee |
| -------- | --- |
| 1-7 days | £45 |
| 8-14 days | £75 |
| 15-28 days | £125 |

Maximum permit duration is 28 days. Extensions are not permitted; a fresh application is required.

## Conditions

- Skip must display the supplier's name, address, and 24-hour phone number.
- Lights or reflective markers are required from dusk until dawn (cones at each corner).
- The skip must not block visibility at junctions or pedestrian crossings.
- No flammable, asbestos, or clinical waste.
- The skip must be covered or netted when unattended.

Failure to comply may result in the skip being removed by the council, with all costs recovered from the permit holder.

## Prohibited streets

Permits are not granted on the A1432 Aldermarket bypass, on Vale Bridge, or within 30m of any junction with the A1432. Where street parking is residents-permit only, the bay must be temporarily suspended at additional cost (£35 per bay per week).

## Insurance

The skip operator must hold valid public liability insurance to a minimum of £5 million. Proof can be requested.
