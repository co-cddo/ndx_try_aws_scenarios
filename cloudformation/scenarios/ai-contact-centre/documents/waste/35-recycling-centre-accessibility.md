# Pendle Road Recycling Centre: accessibility

## Overview

Pendle Road Recycling Centre, AL2 5GH, is the council's flagship Household Waste Recycling Centre (HWRC). The site is fully wheelchair accessible and supports a range of mobility, sensory, and cognitive needs.

## Site features

- Step-free access throughout the public concourse
- Accessible blue-badge parking (12 bays) with extra-width ramps directly to the lower-level skips
- Lowered "step-down" recycling bays for wheelchair and pushchair users
- Hearing loop at the kiosk
- Tactile paving on all walkway edges
- High-contrast signage in 24-point sans-serif throughout

## Assistance staff

The site has at least one trained Customer Service Operative on shift at all times. Operatives can:

- Help unload vehicles
- Carry items to the appropriate skip
- Read and explain signage
- Operate the hazardous-waste store on your behalf

To request operative help, drive to the kiosk and use the buzzer. Average wait time for assistance: under 4 minutes (2025 data).

## Sensory considerations

The site can be noisy, especially during commercial vehicle compactor cycles. Quieter visiting periods:

| Day | Quieter window |
| --- | -------------- |
| Tue-Thu | 14:00-16:00 |
| Sun | 16:00-17:30 (winter close 16:30) |

Ear defenders can be borrowed from the kiosk, free of charge. The council can arrange a "quiet visit" by appointment, where compactors are paused for the duration of your visit. Book on 01432 556 270 (48 hours' notice).

## Vehicle restrictions

Cars, small vans (under 2.0t), and trailers under 1.83m length are unrestricted. Larger vans need a free permit from aldershire.example.gov.uk/van-permit. Permit holders limited to 12 visits per year. Commercial vehicles must use the trade waste route at the rear of the site (separate gate, separate fees).
