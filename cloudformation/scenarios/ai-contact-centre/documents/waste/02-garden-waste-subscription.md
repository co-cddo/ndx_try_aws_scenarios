# Garden waste subscription service

## How the scheme works

Aldershire District Council operates an opt-in garden waste collection service. Householders pay an annual fee of £45 per 240-litre green wheelie bin. The subscription runs from 1 April to 31 March each year and is non-refundable once collections have started. Additional bins for the same property can be added at £35 each.

## What is collected

- Grass cuttings and small hedge trimmings
- Leaves, weeds, dead plants, and small twigs (under 5cm thick)
- Cut flowers and untreated cuttings

What is not accepted: soil, turf, rubble, plastic flowerpots, food waste, animal bedding, or branches over 5cm thick. Contaminated bins are tagged and left at the kerb with an information leaflet.

## Bin sticker

Every subscriber receives a yellow bin sticker showing their property reference and the year of validity (e.g. 25/26). Crews scan the sticker on collection. Bins without a current sticker are not emptied. Replacement stickers cost £3 and can be requested through aldershire.example.gov.uk/garden-waste.

## Schedule

| Period | Frequency |
| ------ | --------- |
| April to October | Fortnightly, on the same day as your blue recycling round |
| November | Final autumn lift in the second week |
| December to March | No service (subscription paused) |

The service resumes the first full week of April. The first lift after winter is often heavy; subscribers may present extra bagged garden waste alongside the bin on that one date only.

## Renewal

Renewal letters are sent in February. Direct debit subscribers are auto-renewed unless cancellation is logged before 15 March. Phone the customer service team on 01432 556 210 to cancel or amend.
