# Reducing waste at home

## The waste hierarchy

The most effective way to reduce environmental impact is to prevent waste in the first place. Aldershire's "Less in the Bin" campaign supports residents to reduce, reuse, repair, and recycle in that order of priority.

## Top tips

### Kitchen

- Plan meals weekly. UK households throw away around £700 of food per year.
- Use the freezer; many fresh foods (bread, cheese, milk, eggs out of shell) freeze well.
- Reuse glass jars for storage instead of plastic tubs.
- Keep a "use-up" shelf in the fridge for items near their date.

### Bathroom

- Switch to bar soap, shampoo bars, and refillable products. Aldershire has three refill stations (Vale Refill at AL5 1AA, Aldermarket Eco Hub at AL1 3LL, Wendlewood Larder at AL6 2NM).
- Use a safety razor instead of disposables.
- Reusable cotton pads and menstrual products reduce bathroom waste significantly.

### Around the home

- Repair before replacing. Aldershire's Repair Cafe runs the second Saturday of each month at the Vale Community Hub (10:00-13:00). Bring small electricals, clothing, bikes, or wooden furniture.
- Shop local charity shops first (see 07-textile-recycling.md for partners).
- Borrow rather than buy: Aldermarket Library of Things lends garden and DIY tools.

## Free composters

Subsidised composters are available (see 32-garden-waste-exemptions.md). The council also runs free Master Composter training every March; book on 01432 556 256.

## School engagement

The Less in the Bin team visits primary schools across Aldershire, delivering assemblies and classroom workshops. In 2025, 28 schools and 4,200 pupils took part. Schools can book at aldershire.example.gov.uk/schools-waste.

## Campaigns and events

The annual Less in the Bin Week runs the third week of October. Events include:

- Repair Cafe weekend special
- Clothes swap at Vale Community Hub
- Library of Things drop-in
- "Big Compost Day" at Pendle Road
- Recycling tours of the Materials Recovery Facility

Sign up to the campaign newsletter at aldershire.example.gov.uk/less-in-bin.

## Tracking your impact

Households can request a free home waste audit. A Waste Education Officer visits, reviews bin contents, and offers tailored advice. Most households who complete an audit reduce residual waste by 18% over the following six months.
