# Missed bin collection: reporting and escalation

## When to report

A missed collection is a bin that was correctly presented at the kerb by 06:30 on the scheduled day but was not emptied by the time the round closed (typically 17:00). Before reporting, please check:

- Was the bin lid fully closed? Overflowing bins are not emptied by Aldershire crews.
- Was the bin within one metre of the public footway?
- Did neighbours on the same side of the street have their bins emptied? If everyone on the street was missed, the round was likely cancelled and a recovery visit will already be scheduled.

## How to report

Reports must be lodged within 24 hours of the scheduled collection day, or they roll forward to the next cycle.

| Channel | Detail |
| ------- | ------ |
| Online | aldershire.example.gov.uk/missed-bin |
| Phone | 01432 556 200 (Mon-Fri 08:00-17:30, Sat 09:00-12:00) |
| Email | wasteservices@aldershire.example.gov.uk |

You will need:

1. House number and full postcode (e.g. AL3 7QF).
2. Bin colour and stream (brown refuse, blue recycling, green garden, food caddy).
3. Whether anything obstructed access on the day (parked cars, road works, building skip).

## Service standard

Crews aim to return within two working days for valid reports. The Waste Operations team at the Aldershire Depot, Pendle Road, AL2 5GH will text you a confirmation slot the day before. If the bin is still not emptied at the second visit, the case is escalated to the Waste Services Manager (currently Donna Marlow) for a chargeback against the contractor and a written response within five working days.

## Repeat misses

Three or more missed collections at the same property within a rolling 12-week period triggers an automatic case review. A round-supervisor inspection is added to the route to identify whether crew error, access difficulty, or a routing fault is the cause.
