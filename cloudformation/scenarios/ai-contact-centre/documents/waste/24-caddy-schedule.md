# Food caddy schedule (weekly, year-round)

## Frequency

Food waste caddies are emptied weekly, every week of the year, including bank holiday weeks (with the standard one-day slip; see 12-holiday-schedule-changes.md). Weekly food collection is the most consistent of all Aldershire's waste services and runs alongside whichever bin (brown or blue) is also collected that week.

## Day per area

Food caddies are collected on the same day as the rest of your bins; whichever main bin is out that week, the food caddy joins it.

## Presentation

- Place the outdoor caddy at the kerb by 06:30, lid closed and locked.
- Use compostable liners (see 04-food-waste-caddies.md). Plastic carrier bags are not accepted.
- Crews lift the caddy, empty it into the food compartment of the vehicle, and return the caddy to the kerb.
- Bring the caddy in promptly to deter foxes and rats.

## Where the food goes

Food waste is collected in dedicated insulated compartments and tipped at the Wendlewood Anaerobic Digestion plant. The biogas produced is upgraded and injected into the regional gas grid. Digestate is supplied to local farms as fertiliser. Each tonne of food waste produces approximately 130 cubic metres of biogas.

## Smell and pests

If your outdoor caddy attracts pests:

- Wrap food in compostable liner or newspaper before binning.
- Rinse the caddy weekly with mild detergent.
- Keep the lid locked between collections.
- Store the caddy in shade if possible.

The Environmental Health team can advise on pest issues; see 05-environmental-health.md or call 01432 556 280.

## 2025 capture

Aldershire households produced 8,420 tonnes of food waste in 2025, of which 96.1% was captured via the kerbside scheme. The remaining 3.9% appears in residual brown bin waste; reducing this is a key priority for the 2026 Waste Strategy.
