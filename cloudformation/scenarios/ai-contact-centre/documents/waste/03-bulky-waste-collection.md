# Bulky waste collection

## What counts as bulky waste

Items too large or heavy for a standard wheelie bin: sofas, armchairs, mattresses, fridges, freezers, washing machines, dishwashers, cookers, dining tables, wardrobes, garden furniture, BBQs (drained of fuel), and similar household furnishings.

Items not collected through this service:

- Builders' rubble, paving slabs, or bagged DIY waste
- Asbestos or asbestos-containing materials
- Oil tanks, gas bottles, or pressurised cylinders
- Vehicle parts including tyres
- Garden sheds (these need to be dismantled and arranged through a private contractor)

## Fees

| Volume | Charge |
| ------ | ------ |
| 1 to 3 items | £35 |
| 4 to 6 items | £55 |
| 7 to 10 items (maximum per booking) | £75 |

White goods (fridges, freezers) count as one item each but trigger a £15 surcharge per appliance to cover safe handling of refrigerant gases.

## Booking

Bookings are taken at aldershire.example.gov.uk/bulky-waste or by phone on 01432 556 230. Payment is required at booking; the AI assistant cannot process card payments and will warm-transfer to an officer for that step.

The next available slot is usually 7 to 10 working days. Items must be presented at the front kerb by 07:00 on collection day, with mattresses bagged in clear plastic sheeting (sold at most DIY stores).

## Concessions

Households in receipt of Council Tax Reduction or Universal Credit qualify for one free bulky waste collection (up to 6 items) per financial year. Proof of entitlement is required at booking.

## What happens to the items

Collected items are sorted at the Pendle Road depot. Reusable furniture is offered to Aldershire Reuse Network (ARN) and the local Furniture Friends charity. Around 38% of bulky items collected in 2025 were diverted from landfill via reuse or refurbishment.
