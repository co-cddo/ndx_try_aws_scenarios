# Holiday schedule changes

## Bank holiday slips

When a bank holiday falls Monday to Friday, collections from that day onwards in the same week shift forward by one weekday. Saturday recovery rounds catch up the missed Friday.

| Bank holiday | Effect |
| ------------ | ------ |
| Good Friday | Mon-Thu unchanged; Fri collections move to Sat |
| Easter Monday | Mon collections move to Tue; rest of week +1 day; Fri to Sat |
| Early May | All week +1 day; Fri to Sat |
| Spring Bank Holiday (late May) | All week +1 day; Fri to Sat |
| Summer Bank Holiday (late August) | All week +1 day; Fri to Sat |

## Christmas and New Year

The Christmas/New Year schedule is published every year in the first week of December. The detailed pattern depends on which day of the week 25 December falls. As a rule:

- 25 December and 1 January never see collections.
- Boxing Day collections are usually moved to the following Saturday.
- Rounds catch up by mid-January.

The 2026/27 dates will be on aldershire.example.gov.uk/christmas-bins by 1 December 2026.

## Communicating changes

Residents on the SMS reminder service receive a text at 18:00 the day before the revised collection day. Sign up at aldershire.example.gov.uk/bin-alerts. Subscribers receive around 26 reminder texts per year.

## Crew cancellations

Severe weather (heavy snow, ice, gale-force winds) can force operational stand-downs. The Waste Operations duty manager makes the call by 05:30 on the affected day. Residents are notified by SMS, the council website banner, and the @AldershireDC social media accounts.

## Catch-up rules

If a stand-down causes a missed collection, present your bin again on the next scheduled day with up to one extra clear sack of overflow waste alongside (refuse only). Recyclables must remain inside the blue bin to prevent contamination.
