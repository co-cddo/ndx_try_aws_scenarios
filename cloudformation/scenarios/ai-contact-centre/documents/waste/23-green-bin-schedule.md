# Green bin schedule (garden waste, weekly Apr-Oct)

## Frequency

Garden waste collections run weekly from the first full week of April to the last week of October, with a single closing lift in the second week of November. The service is paused from mid-November through March; subscribers do not receive a refund or credit for the winter pause as the £45 annual fee covers operational costs across the active period.

## Day per area

Garden waste is collected on the same day as your blue bin (recycling) but every week, not fortnightly. So if your blue bin day is Tuesday, your green bin is also Tuesday whenever the green service is operating.

## Subscription required

You must subscribe (£45/year) to receive collections (see 02-garden-waste-subscription.md). Bins without a current yellow sticker are not emptied.

## What is accepted

Grass, leaves, weeds, dead plants, hedge trimmings, twigs under 5cm, cut flowers, untreated cuttings.

Not accepted: soil, turf, rubble, plastic flowerpots, plastic bags, food waste, animal bedding, treated timber, fence panels, branches over 5cm.

## Composting destination

Garden waste is taken to Aldershire Compost Co-op, an in-vessel composting site at the Pendle Road industrial estate. The compost is used by Aldershire Parks & Greenspaces team and sold in 25kg bags at Pendle Road Recycling Centre for £3 each (Mon-Sun, while stocks last).

## Tips for clean collections

- Avoid filling beyond the rim (bins must close fully).
- Do not bag garden waste; loose into the bin.
- Lay bulky cuttings on the ground to dry overnight before binning, to reduce weight.
- Do not include Japanese knotweed or other invasive species; phone 01432 556 280 for guidance.
