# Garden waste fee: exemptions and discounts

## Standard fee

The garden waste service costs £45 per 240-litre bin per year (see 02-garden-waste-subscription.md). The fee covers operational costs including fuel, labour, and the contract with Aldershire Compost Co-op.

## Low-income discount

Households in receipt of any of the following qualify for a 50% discount (subscription £22.50):

- Council Tax Reduction
- Universal Credit (housing element or limited capability for work)
- Pension Credit (Guarantee Element)
- Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance
- Income-related Employment and Support Allowance

To claim, complete the discount form at aldershire.example.gov.uk/garden-discount and attach proof (entitlement letter dated within 3 months).

## Home composting alternative

Residents who prefer to compost at home are not required to subscribe. The council offers a subsidised compost bin scheme:

| Bin | Standard | Subsidised (Aldershire residents) |
| --- | -------- | --------------------------------- |
| 220L compost bin | £35 | £15 |
| 330L compost bin | £45 | £20 |
| Bokashi indoor kit (food) | £30 | £15 |

Order through aldershire.example.gov.uk/compost-bin. Delivery within 14 working days.

## What home composters can compost

Cooked food and meat should not be added to home compost bins as they attract pests; use the food caddy instead. Suitable items: garden waste, fruit and vegetable peelings, coffee grounds, tea bags (with non-plastic seal), eggshells, untreated paper.

## Bonfire alternative

Burning garden waste at home is discouraged. Smoke from bonfires can constitute a statutory nuisance under section 79 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990. The council will investigate complaints and may issue an Abatement Notice.

## Bulk seasonal discount (autumn)

In November, subscribers may purchase up to 4 extra bagged sacks of leaves for £10 the lot. Bags must be biodegradable and presented alongside the bin on collection day. This service is for the leaf-fall surge and runs only in November.
