# Holiday season extra waste advice

## Why volumes spike

Christmas, Eid, Diwali, Chinese New Year, and the late-summer holiday period all produce sharp spikes in household waste, especially packaging, food, and decorations. Aldershire processes approximately 38% more refuse in the last week of December than in an average week.

## Practical advice for big celebrations

### Before

- Plan portions to avoid leftovers (lovefoodhatewaste.com).
- Choose recyclable wrapping (kraft paper) over foiled wrap (foiled is not recyclable).
- Buy refillable cards or send digital greetings where possible.
- Order glass bottles for parties and use bottle banks afterwards (see 06).

### During

- Set up two clearly labelled bins at gatherings: one for recyclables, one for general waste.
- Pour cooking oils into a sealed jar; cool and bin in the brown bin (do not pour down the sink).
- Use compostable plates and cups only if your guests will dispose of them in the food caddy; otherwise paper plates in the brown bin or reusables are better.

### After

- Flatten cardboard before binning; bins close fully.
- Place foil wrap in the kitchen bin (not blue bin) unless it scrunches into a ball larger than a golf ball and stays scrunched.
- Christmas tree collection is in January; book free pickup (see 11-christmas-tree-collection.md).
- Donate unwanted gifts to a local charity shop (Aldershire Hospice, British Heart Foundation, Salvation Army; see 07).

## Extra capacity

The council allows up to two extra clear sacks of household refuse beside the brown bin in the week beginning 27 December (Aldermarket north and south rounds) or the equivalent first round after Christmas in your area. This extra-capacity allowance is automatic and does not need a booking.

For Eid, Diwali, or Chinese New Year, residents can request a one-off extra-capacity allowance for the relevant week by phoning 01432 556 200. This is not yet automated and depends on operational capacity.

## Food waste

The food caddy continues weekly throughout the holiday season (see 24-caddy-schedule.md). Plate scrapings, vegetable peelings, leftover stuffing, gravy (cooled), and meat fat all go in the food caddy.
