# Food waste caddies and liners

## Caddy types issued

Every household receives two caddies as standard:

- A 5-litre indoor kitchen caddy (cream-coloured, vented lid) for daily use
- A 23-litre outdoor caddy (brown, lockable lid) for weekly kerbside collection

Flats and houses in multiple occupation receive a single shared 23-litre caddy per six dwellings. Block managers are responsible for caddy hygiene and presentation.

## What is accepted

All cooked and uncooked food, including:

- Meat, fish, bones, and shellfish shells
- Dairy, bread, pasta, rice, and leftovers
- Vegetable peelings, fruit, tea bags, coffee grounds
- Plate scrapings and out-of-date food (removed from packaging)

Not accepted: liquids over half a cup, packaging, nappies, pet faeces, oil, or garden waste.

## Liners

The council provides starter rolls of compostable liners (50 per household per year, certified to EN13432). Refill rolls are available:

| Outlet | Cost | Notes |
| ------ | ---- | ----- |
| Aldermarket Library, AL1 2BB | £2 for 50 | Mon-Sat 09:00-17:00 |
| Vale Community Hub, AL5 1QR | £2 for 50 | Tue-Thu 10:00-16:00 |
| Online via aldershire.example.gov.uk/caddy-liners | £2.50 for 50 | Posted within 5 working days |

Newspaper, paper bags, or kitchen roll are also accepted as liners. Plastic carrier bags must not be used; loads contaminated with plastic are rejected at the anaerobic digestion plant in Wendlewood.

## Replacement caddies

Lost or broken caddies are replaced free of charge once per year. Subsequent replacements cost £8 (kitchen) or £18 (outdoor). Order through the website or call 01432 556 200.
