# Annual waste data report 2025

## Headline figures

| Indicator | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
| --------- | ---- | ---- | ------ |
| Total household waste arisings | 53,210 t | 51,840 t | -2.6% |
| Recycling rate (NI 192) | 47.3% | 49.7% | +2.4pp |
| Residual waste per household | 487 kg | 462 kg | -5.1% |
| Food waste captured | 7,840 t | 8,420 t | +7.4% |
| Garden waste collected | 6,210 t | 6,470 t | +4.2% |
| Fly tipping incidents | 1,360 | 1,210 | -11.0% |

## Recycling stream breakdown

- Paper and card: 4,820 t
- Plastic bottles, pots, trays: 2,140 t
- Metal cans and aerosols: 850 t
- Glass: 4,310 t
- Cartons: 320 t
- Garden waste: 6,470 t
- Food waste: 8,420 t
- Textiles (kerbside scheme): 38 t
- Small WEEE: 412 t

## Residual destination

Aldershire's residual waste goes to Wendlewood Energy from Waste, generating 412 GWh of electricity in 2025. Bottom ash is recovered as construction aggregate; only 1.8% of total waste arisings was sent to landfill.

## Methodology and verification

Figures are reported under WasteDataFlow conventions and audited by the Aldershire Internal Audit Service in May 2026. Independent verification of recycling tonnages is conducted by Verdant Audit Ltd. The full report is available on aldershire.example.gov.uk/waste-data-2025.

## Targets

- Recycling rate: 55% by March 2027
- Residual per household: below 425 kg by March 2027
- Food waste capture: 9,500 t by March 2027
- Fly tipping: under 1,000 incidents by March 2027

## Public engagement

The council ran 18 community recycling events in 2025, attended by 4,200 residents. The Waste Education team made 612 visits to households flagged for repeat contamination. The "Right Bin, Right Time" campaign reached 38,000 residents through social media impressions.
