# Litter picking volunteer scheme

## About the scheme

Aldershire's "Tidy Towns" volunteer scheme equips residents to organise litter picks in their neighbourhood. The council provides equipment, insurance cover, and waste collection of bagged litter.

## Joining

1. Register at aldershire.example.gov.uk/tidy-towns
2. Complete the 20-minute online safety briefing
3. Attend an in-person induction at Pendle Road depot (held first Saturday of every month, 10:00-11:00)
4. Receive your volunteer pack

Adults can register individually. Volunteers under 18 must be accompanied by a registered adult.

## Equipment loan

| Item | Quantity |
| ---- | -------- |
| Litter pickers | 1 per volunteer (returned at end of session) |
| Hi-vis vests | 1 per volunteer |
| Heavy-duty rubble sacks | Up to 30 per pick |
| Cable ties for sealing bags | Pack of 50 |
| Gloves (Kevlar lined) | 1 pair per volunteer |
| First aid kit | 1 per group |

Equipment is collected from Pendle Road or any of three satellite hubs (Aldermarket Library, Vale Community Hub, Wendlewood Village Hall).

## After the pick

Bag your collection and tie tightly. Place the bags at an agreed lay-by or kerbside point. Email tidytowns@aldershire.example.gov.uk with the location, your group reference, and the number of bags. Bags are collected within 48 hours by the dedicated litter pick van.

## Insurance

The council holds a public liability policy covering registered Tidy Towns volunteers up to £5 million. Cover applies during scheduled picks only and excludes work on motorways, dual carriageways above 50mph, and railway corridors.

## Annual awards

The Tidy Towns Award is presented at the Mayor's Civic Reception each November. In 2025 the award went to the Wendlewood Wombles for organising 32 picks across the year, collecting an estimated 1.8 tonnes of litter.
