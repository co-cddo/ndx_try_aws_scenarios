# Hazardous household waste

## What is classed as hazardous

Items containing chemicals, fuels, or substances that pose risk if mishandled:

- Paint (oil-based, gloss, undercoat) and paint thinners
- Engine oil, brake fluid, antifreeze
- Garden chemicals (weedkiller, slug pellets, fertilisers)
- Pool and spa chemicals
- Asbestos and asbestos-containing materials (cement sheets, garage roofs)
- Solvents, adhesives, and aerosol propellants
- Fluorescent tubes and energy-saving bulbs

## Disposal options

| Item | Where | Conditions |
| ---- | ----- | ---------- |
| Water-based paint (emulsion) | Solidify with sawdust or sand, then brown bin | Lid open |
| Oil-based paint, thinners | Pendle Road hazardous bay, AL2 5GH | Original tin if possible |
| Engine oil | Pendle Road oil tank | Maximum 5 litres per visit |
| Garden chemicals | Pendle Road hazardous bay | Original packaging |
| Fluorescent tubes | Pendle Road WEEE bay | Carry vertically |
| Asbestos | Specialist booking only, see below | Double-bagged in red asbestos sacks |

## Asbestos

Aldershire does not collect asbestos at the kerb under any circumstances. Residents may book an asbestos disposal slot at Pendle Road by calling 01432 556 270 (Tue and Thu mornings only). The fee is £55 per cubic metre. Items must arrive double-wrapped in heavy-gauge plastic and clearly labelled. Commercial asbestos must use a licensed carrier.

## Spills and unknown substances

Do not pour unknown chemicals down drains or into the ground. Phone the Aldershire Environmental Health team on 01432 556 280 for advice. Out-of-hours emergencies (chemical spills, large fuel leaks) should go to the Environment Agency hotline 0800 80 70 60.

## Safety

Always wear gloves and eye protection. Keep hazardous waste away from children and pets. Never mix chemicals (especially bleach with ammonia or acids).
