# Christmas tree collection

## Free January service

Aldershire District Council offers a free real Christmas tree collection in the first three weeks of January. The service is funded by the Aldershire Climate Action grant and is delivered in partnership with the Aldershire Hospice as a fundraising lift; an optional donation is suggested at booking.

## How to book

Book online at aldershire.example.gov.uk/xmas-tree between 27 December and 18 January. Bookings open on Boxing Day at midday. Slots are allocated by postcode group:

| Group | Postcodes | Collection week |
| ----- | --------- | --------------- |
| A | AL1, AL2 | 6-10 January |
| B | AL3, AL4 | 13-17 January |
| C | AL5, AL6, AL7, AL8 | 20-24 January |

You will receive a confirmation email with your collection day and a downloadable luggage-style tag. Tie the tag to a branch.

## Presentation

- Remove all decorations, lights, tinsel, and tree bases.
- Trees over 6 feet must be cut in half.
- Place at the kerb by 06:30 on collection day.
- Maximum one tree per household via the free service.

## Drop-off alternative

If you miss the kerbside slot, drop trees at Pendle Road Recycling Centre, AL2 5GH (Mon-Sun) until 31 January. Aldermarket Park hosts a tree-mulching event on the third Saturday of January, organised by the Friends of Aldermarket Park.

## Artificial trees

Artificial trees are not collected through this scheme. Donate intact artificial trees to charity shops or include them in a bulky waste collection booking.

## Where the trees go

Collected trees are chipped at Pendle Road and the resulting woodchip is used on parks footpaths and woodland trails across the district. In 2025, 2,140 trees were collected and produced 38 tonnes of woodchip, all returned to Aldershire green spaces.
