# Brown bin schedule (general refuse, fortnightly)

## Frequency

Brown bins (general non-recyclable waste) are emptied fortnightly across Aldershire. The fortnightly cycle alternates with blue (recycling) collections so that every household has one weekly lift between two streams.

## Day per area

| Area | Postcodes | Day | Weeks |
| ---- | --------- | --- | ----- |
| Aldermarket north | AL1, AL2 | Monday | A weeks |
| Aldermarket south | AL3, AL4 | Tuesday | B weeks |
| Vale villages | AL5, AL6, AL7 | Wednesday | A weeks |
| Aldershire fringe | AL8 | Thursday | B weeks |

A and B weeks alternate. Look up your week at aldershire.example.gov.uk/my-bin or use the "Aldershire Bins" SMS reminder service.

## Capacity

The standard brown bin is 240 litres. Households of six or more, or with continence needs, can apply for additional capacity (see 15-extra-capacity-and-assisted.md). Smaller 140L bins are issued where space is limited (terraced cottages, flats above shops).

## Side waste

Side waste in extra bags is not collected with brown bins. Excess refuse outside the bin is treated as side-waste contravention. Repeated breaches can attract a Section 46 notice and a £100 fixed penalty.

## What goes in

- Nappies, sanitary products, and hygiene waste
- Pet faeces and used cat litter (bagged)
- Polystyrene and foam packaging
- Crisp packets, sweet wrappers, film lids
- Hoover dust and dirty rags
- Broken crockery and ovenware

## What does NOT go in

- Recyclables (use blue bin)
- Food waste (use food caddy)
- Garden waste (use green bin if subscribed)
- Hazardous waste (see 10-hazardous-waste.md)
- Batteries or electrical items

## Final destination

Aldershire's residual waste is taken to Wendlewood Energy from Waste facility. The plant generated 412 GWh of electricity in 2025, equivalent to powering 95,000 homes.
