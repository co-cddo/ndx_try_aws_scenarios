# Dog fouling

## The law

Dog fouling on public land is an offence under the Public Spaces Protection Order (PSPO) made by Aldershire DC under the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014. The PSPO covers all public open spaces, footpaths, parks, school grounds, and the public highway. Offences attract a fixed penalty notice of £100 (reduced to £75 if paid within 14 days), or prosecution leading to a fine up to £1,000.

## Reporting incidents

To report dog fouling not cleared:

- Online: aldershire.example.gov.uk/dog-fouling
- Phone: 01432 556 250

Tell us:

- Location (postcode or what3words)
- When you saw it
- Description of dog and walker if you witnessed the offence
- Vehicle details if relevant

The Streetscene team aims to clear reported fouling within 1 working day in town centres and 3 working days elsewhere.

## Hotspots and enforcement

Aldershire deploys plain-clothes wardens to recurring hotspots. In 2025 the highest-issued areas were:

| Location | FPNs issued |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Aldermarket Riverside Walk | 32 |
| Vale Park southern field | 24 |
| Pendle Road footpath | 18 |
| Wendlewood Common | 15 |
| Brackton Allotments boundary | 12 |

Total fixed penalty notices issued: 142, of which 118 paid (83% recovery rate). Eight prosecutions in Aldershire Magistrates' Court secured fines averaging £380.

## Equipment provided

Free dog waste bags are available from:

- Aldermarket Library, AL1 2BB (Mon-Sat 09:00-17:00)
- Vale Community Hub, AL5 1QR (Tue-Thu 10:00-16:00)
- All council-owned dog bins (red lid; emptied 2-3 times per week)

There are 86 dog waste bins across the district. Report a full or damaged bin on 01432 556 200.

## Education

The Streetscene team runs a "Bag It, Bin It" campaign each spring. Posters and stencilled pavement reminders are placed at hotspots. Schools may request a class visit on 01432 556 256.
