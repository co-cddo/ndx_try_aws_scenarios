# Communal bins (flats and blocks)

## Where communal bins apply

Properties without individual curtilage for waste storage typically use shared communal bins. This includes:

- Purpose-built blocks of flats
- Houses converted to flats with no rear access
- Sheltered housing schemes
- Mixed-use buildings (residential above commercial)

Bins are usually 1100-litre Eurobins (refuse and recycling) and 240-litre food caddies. Some smaller blocks share standard wheelie bins.

## Collection windows

Communal bins are collected on dedicated rounds, often outside the standard kerbside schedule:

| Round | Day | Time window |
| ----- | --- | ----------- |
| Aldermarket town centre flats | Mon, Wed, Fri | 06:00-08:00 |
| Vale apartments | Tue, Thu | 06:30-09:00 |
| Sheltered housing schemes (district-wide) | Wed | 09:00-13:00 |
| Mixed-use buildings | Daily | Variable, usually before 10:00 |

Communal food caddies are collected three times a week to prevent odour issues.

## Block manager responsibilities

The freeholder, managing agent, or housing association is responsible for:

- Storing bins in the designated bin store between collections
- Presenting bins at the agreed collection point by 06:30 on collection days
- Returning bins to the store after collection
- Maintaining cleanliness of the bin store
- Reporting damage and arranging repairs through Aldershire DC

A bin presentation agreement is signed when communal bins are first installed. Failure to meet the agreement may result in fixed penalty notices (£300, fixed by the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014) for "litter authorities".

## Side waste

Side waste left beside communal bins is treated as fly tipping (see 16-fly-tipping-reporting.md). Block residents can be served Section 46 notices requiring better waste management.

## Reporting issues

For overflowing bins, blocked stores, or contamination, call 01432 556 200 with the block name, address, and bin description.
