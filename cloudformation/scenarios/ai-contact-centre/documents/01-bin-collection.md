# Bin collection schedule and missed-collection reporting

## Collection day

Bin collection day depends on the resident's address. The scheme is two-weekly alternating: one week refuse and food waste, the next week recycling and garden waste.

| Area | Day | Stream rotation |
| ---- | --- | --------------- |
| Aldermarket north (postcodes AL1, AL2) | Monday | Week 1 refuse + food, Week 2 recycling + garden |
| Aldermarket south (AL3, AL4) | Tuesday | Week 1 recycling + garden, Week 2 refuse + food |
| Vale villages (AL5, AL6, AL7) | Wednesday | Week 1 refuse + food, Week 2 recycling + garden |
| Aldershire fringe (AL8) | Thursday | Week 1 recycling + garden, Week 2 refuse + food |

Bins must be at the kerb by 06:30. Crews finish a round between 14:00 and 17:00 depending on traffic and volume.

## Missed collection

If a bin was not collected by 17:00 on the scheduled day:

1. Check whether neighbouring bins on the same side of the street have also been missed. If only your bin was skipped, the crew may have logged an access issue (for example, blocked by a parked car).
2. Report the missed collection within 24 hours. Reports older than 24 hours are queued for the next scheduled collection day instead of an immediate revisit.
3. Provide your address, the bin colour or stream (refuse / recycling / food / garden), and any access notes.

The council's service-level commitment is a return visit within two working days for missed-collection reports submitted within the 24-hour window.

## Bins that are damaged or missing

Damaged bins are repaired or replaced within 10 working days. Missing bins (stolen, lost, or removed by the previous occupant) are replaced for a small administrative charge unless the move was a council-managed transfer.

## Bulky waste, fridges, mattresses, and white goods

These are collected by appointment only. Charges apply. The bot can take the appointment booking but will need card details, which are taken on a secure agent transfer; the AI does not handle payment card data.

## Christmas and bank holidays

Collection days slip by one weekday over Christmas and New Year. The detailed schedule is published every December and the bot is updated each year. Bank holidays in May and August generally cause a one-day slip for that week's affected rounds.
