# Fly-tipping reports

Fly-tipping is the illegal dumping of waste on land that is not licensed to receive it. Aldershire District Council collects fly-tipped material from public land within its boundary and pursues enforcement against the offender where evidence supports it.

## How to report

- Phone the council reception number, or use the WhatsApp channel and attach a photo. A photo significantly increases the chance of successful enforcement.
- Provide the location: a street address, a what3words reference, or a description like "the lay-by 200 metres east of the Aldermarket-Newford junction".
- Provide a description: type of waste, approximate volume, any visible identifying material (envelopes, signage, tyre treads).
- If the fly-tip is on private land, the landowner is responsible for clearance. The council may still investigate the offender.

## Service standard

- Hazardous fly-tipping (asbestos, drums, sharps, suspected hazardous chemicals): cleared within 24 hours of report.
- Standard fly-tipping (household waste, building waste, garden waste): cleared within 5 working days.
- Roadside lay-bys and public footpaths: priority cleared within 48 hours where there is a hazard to traffic or pedestrians.

## Enforcement

If the fly-tipped material contains evidence of the offender (envelopes, named documents, vehicle registration plates), the case is referred to the Environmental Crime Team. Penalties range from a Fixed Penalty Notice (£400) to prosecution carrying a fine of up to £50,000 and 12 months imprisonment.

## Photos and PII

The companion SPA's WhatsApp simulator and any real WABA wiring both pass photos through Bedrock multimodal describe. The Lambda extracts a structured description (object class, severity, suggested action). It does not extract or store any text from envelopes or letterheads visible in the photo. Identifying information visible in a photo is the responsibility of the human enforcement officer to notice during follow-up.
