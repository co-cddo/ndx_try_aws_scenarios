# Direct Payments

A Direct Payment is a cash payment made by Aldershire to a person with eligible care needs (or their nominated representative) so they can arrange and purchase their own care, instead of receiving services arranged by the council. Direct Payments are governed by section 31 of the Care Act 2014 and the Care and Support (Direct Payments) Regulations 2014.

## Who can have a Direct Payment

- An adult who has a care and support plan with eligible needs under the Care Act.
- A carer with assessed needs of their own.
- A parent of a disabled child (under section 17 Children Act 1989 / section 17A 1989 / 26 of the Children and Families Act 2014).
- The person's authorised representative (deputy, attorney, or suitable person where capacity is lacking).

You can choose Direct Payments for some or all of your care, mixed with council-arranged services if you prefer.

## What you can spend it on

- Personal Assistant (PA) wages, NI, pension, and HMRC duties.
- Agency care.
- Day services.
- Short breaks (respite).
- Equipment and assistive technology.
- Activities and goods that meet outcomes in your support plan (e.g. gym membership for a person with a learning disability).

You cannot spend it on things prohibited by regulation 3 of the 2014 regulations, including:

- Local authority services (residential care over 4 weeks/year).
- Anything illegal.
- Gambling, alcohol, tobacco, debt repayment.
- Health services (these are commissioned by the ICB).

## How it works

- Personal Budget calculated from your support plan.
- Direct Payment Agreement signed.
- Money paid four-weekly into a dedicated bank account (we recommend a Direct Payment Card or pre-paid account).
- Receipts and timesheets kept.
- Quarterly monitoring statement returned to us.
- Annual support plan review.

## Support to manage

- Aldershire Direct Payment Support Service: 01432 555 560 (free help with payroll, PA recruitment, DBS checks, and HMRC filings).
- Skills for Care toolkit: skillsforcare.org.uk
- Pre-paid card support from Allpay (provided by Aldershire under contract).

## Misuse

Misused Direct Payments can be recovered under section 31(7) Care Act and may lead to the payment being suspended or withdrawn.

## Reference

Care Act 2014 sections 31-33; Care and Support (Direct Payments) Regulations 2014 (SI 2014/2871).
