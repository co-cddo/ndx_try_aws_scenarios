# Attendance Allowance and Personal Independence Payment

Attendance Allowance (AA) and Personal Independence Payment (PIP) are non-means-tested DWP benefits for people with long-term health conditions or disabilities. Aldershire does not decide entitlement but provides referral and application support, and uses both benefits as passporting evidence for council services (Blue Badge, Council Tax disability reduction, taxicard).

## Personal Independence Payment (PIP)

PIP is for people aged 16 to State Pension age (66). It has two components:

- **Daily living**: standard rate £73.90/week, enhanced rate £110.40/week.
- **Mobility**: standard rate £29.20/week, enhanced rate £77.05/week.

To claim, phone DWP on 0800 917 2222 (textphone 0800 917 7777). After the phone claim a long-form (PIP2) is sent; we recommend completing it with help from Citizens Advice Aldershire (0808 250 5050).

Adult Disability Payment (ADP) replaces PIP in Scotland, but Aldershire residents claim PIP.

## Attendance Allowance (AA)

AA is for people aged 66 and over with care needs.

- Lower rate £73.90/week (frequent help during the day OR at night).
- Higher rate £110.40/week (frequent help day AND night, OR terminal illness).

Claim form AA1 from gov.uk/attendance-allowance, or phone 0800 731 0122.

## Aldershire support

- **Form-filling appointments**: free 1-hour slot with a benefits adviser. Book on 01432 555 102.
- **Home visits**: available for housebound applicants. Phone 01432 555 102.
- **Library appointments**: every Wednesday 10am-3pm at Aldershire Library.
- **Hospital discharge**: the Aldershire Adult Social Care hospital team can complete the AA fast-track form (DS1500 / SR1) for residents with a life-limiting illness; same-day decision by DWP.
- **Tribunal support**: Citizens Advice Aldershire represent at PIP/AA appeal tribunals at Aldershire HMCTS.

## Passported entitlements

Once awarded, AA or PIP can unlock:

- Blue Badge.
- Council Tax disability reduction.
- Aldershire Taxicard (subsidised taxi rides).
- Free Aldershire bus pass for under-66 disabled residents.
- Carer's Allowance for someone caring for you.
- Severe Disability Premium in means-tested benefits (if you live alone and no one claims Carer's Allowance for you).

## Reference

Welfare Reform Act 2012, Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 sections 64-67 (AA), Welfare Reform Act 2012 Part 4 (PIP).
