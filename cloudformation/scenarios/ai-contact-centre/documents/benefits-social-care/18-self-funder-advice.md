# Self-funder information and advice

Under section 4 of the Care Act 2014 every council must provide information and advice on care and support to all adults in its area, regardless of income, savings, or whether the council pays for any care. Aldershire's information-and-advice duty applies equally to residents who fund their own care ("self-funders") as to council-funded users.

## Who is a self-funder

A self-funder is anyone who pays the full cost of their care because:

- Their capital is over the upper threshold (£23,250 — the lower threshold is £14,250).
- Their care is excluded from council means-testing (e.g. some types of supported living rent and service charges).
- They have chosen private arrangements without coming to the council.

## What we provide free

- Phone, online, and in-person advice on the care market in Aldershire.
- A directory of CQC-registered providers (aldershire.gov.uk/care-directory).
- Indicative weekly costs for residential care (£780-£1,250) and home care (£25-£35 per hour) in Aldershire.
- Information on Continuing Healthcare assessments through Aldershire Integrated Care Board.
- Signposting to financial advice (Society of Later Life Advisers, 0333 202 0454) for care-fee planning, equity release, and long-term care annuities.
- Help to challenge a refusal of NHS-funded nursing care or Continuing Healthcare.
- Lasting Power of Attorney signposting to the Office of the Public Guardian (0300 456 0300).

## Right to ask for an assessment

A self-funder can request a Care Act needs assessment at any time. Even if we will not pay, the assessment:

- Confirms your eligible needs.
- Lets you ask the council to arrange your care under section 18(3) Care Act (we charge an arrangement fee of £350 set-up plus £75 per quarter).
- Triggers a financial assessment so we can monitor when your capital approaches the threshold.

## Deferred payment agreement

If you go into residential care and your home is your main asset, you can defer your care fees against the equity in your home. Aldershire offers Deferred Payment Agreements (DPA) under section 34 Care Act and the Care and Support (Deferred Payment) Regulations 2014. Phone 01432 555 530 to request a DPA pack.

## Reference

Care Act 2014 sections 4, 18(3), 34; Care and Support Statutory Guidance (DHSC, latest update).
