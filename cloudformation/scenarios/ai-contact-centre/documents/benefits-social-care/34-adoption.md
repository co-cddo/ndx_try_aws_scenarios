# Aldershire DC Adoption Service

Aldershire is a registered Voluntary Adoption Agency partner of the regional adoption agency **Aldershire and Severn Adoption Partnership** (ASAP), formed in April 2018 under the Adoption and Children Act 2002 section 1A. Aldershire DC is the lead authority. ASAP recruits, assesses, prepares, and matches adopters across Aldershire, Severnshire, and Northwich Borough.

## Who can adopt

- Aged 21 or over.
- Single, in a couple (married, civil partnership, or unmarried), of any sexual orientation.
- Of any ethnicity, faith, or none.
- Renting or owning a home.
- In or out of work.
- With or without existing children.
- Without a disqualifying criminal record (Schedule 3 Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005).

## The journey

1. **Initial enquiry**: phone 01432 555 450 or asap-info@aldershire.gov.uk.
2. **Information event**: monthly at the Aldershire Family Hub.
3. **Registration of Interest**: Stage 1 (2 months) — DBS, medicals, references.
4. **Stage 2** (4 months) — preparation training, home study assessment, social worker visits, prospective adopter report (PAR).
5. **Adoption panel**: independent panel recommends approval.
6. **Matching**: with a child or sibling group whose needs match your assessment.
7. **Matching panel**: confirms the match.
8. **Introductions** over 1-3 weeks.
9. **Placement** with the family.
10. **Adoption order** by the family court (after at least 10 weeks).

Statutory timescales: assessment within 6 months of registration of interest (Adoption Agencies Regulations 2005).

## Financial support

- **Adoption Allowance** (section 71 Adoption and Children Act 2002): means-tested, paid where the child has additional needs that would otherwise prevent adoption.
- **Adoption Pay and Leave** from employer: 52 weeks' leave, with statutory adoption pay broadly equivalent to maternity pay.
- **Adoption Support Fund** (DfE-funded): up to £5,000 per child per year for therapeutic support, plus up to £2,500 for specialist assessment.

## Adoption support

- Therapeutic support (Theraplay, DDP, life-story work).
- Aldershire Adoption Support Group meets monthly at the Civic Centre.
- Letterbox contact service for birth-family contact.
- Support for adult adoptees searching for birth records (section 60 Adoption and Children Act 2002).
