# Personal Budgets and care and support plans

Where Aldershire has assessed eligible needs under the Care Act 2014, every person has the right to a Personal Budget — a clear statement of how much money the council has allocated to meet their needs. The Personal Budget is the foundation of the care and support plan.

## What goes in the Personal Budget

The Personal Budget includes:

- The cost of meeting all eligible needs (calculated using a Resource Allocation System).
- The amount the council will pay.
- The amount the person is required to contribute (after a financial assessment).

The Personal Budget must be sufficient to meet the assessed needs (R (Davey) v Oxfordshire CC [2017]).

## How we calculate it

Aldershire uses an indicative budget tool that maps assessed needs (toileting, mobility, communication, behaviour, cognition, social participation) to weekly hours of care, charged at our framework rates:

- Personal care (home care): £24.50/hour.
- Personal Assistant (Direct Payment): £14.20/hour gross plus 22% on-costs (NI, pension, holiday pay).
- Day service: £75/day.
- Short break (residential): £742/week.

The indicative budget is finalised after the support plan is co-produced.

## Care and support plan (section 25 Care Act)

The plan must include:

- The needs to be met.
- The outcomes the person wants to achieve.
- Whether and how they will be met.
- The Personal Budget.
- Information on direct payments.
- The named worker and review date.

Plans are signed off by the person (or their representative) before services start.

## Three ways to receive your Personal Budget

1. **Council-arranged services**: we commission the care directly.
2. **Direct Payment**: you receive the money and arrange your own care (see separate document).
3. **Mixed**: some services council-arranged, the rest as a Direct Payment.

You can change between these at any review.

## Reviews

Plans are reviewed at 6-8 weeks after starting, then at least once every 12 months, or sooner on request or change of circumstances. Reviews can be done over phone, video, or face-to-face.

## Charging

Adult social care is means-tested under the Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014. After the financial assessment we confirm what you contribute and what the council contributes.
