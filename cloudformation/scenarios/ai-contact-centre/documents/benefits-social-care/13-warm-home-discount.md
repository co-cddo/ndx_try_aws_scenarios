# Warm Home Discount

The Warm Home Discount (WHD) is a one-off £150 deduction from a domestic electricity bill (or fuel voucher for prepayment customers) administered by participating energy suppliers under the Warm Home Discount (England and Wales) Regulations 2022. Aldershire does not pay WHD but supports residents to claim and helps with the digital and evidence steps.

## Who qualifies (England and Wales)

There are two routes:

### Core Group 1 (automatic)

You receive the Guarantee Credit element of Pension Credit on the qualifying date (usually mid-August). The energy supplier and DWP cross-match data and the discount is applied automatically. A confirmation letter is sent each November.

### Core Group 2 (high-cost-to-heat)

You receive a means-tested benefit (UC, JSA, ESA, Income Support, Housing Benefit, Pension Credit Savings Credit, or Tax Credits below thresholds) AND your home has high estimated energy costs based on its property characteristics (age, type, floor area, fuel). DWP and BEIS use a property-cost score to decide automatically.

## How payments are made

- Credited to electricity account by 31 March each scheme year.
- Prepayment meter customers receive a £150 voucher by post or text from their supplier; redeem at Post Office or PayPoint.

## If you think you qualify but did not get it

- Check your supplier offers WHD (most large suppliers do; smaller suppliers may not — switching mid-year does not preserve eligibility).
- Phone the WHD helpline: 0800 030 9322 (Mon-Fri 8am-5pm).
- Confirm DWP holds your correct National Insurance and address.

## Aldershire support

- Energy advice drop-in sessions every Friday 10am-12pm at Aldershire Library, run jointly by Aldershire Council and Citizens Advice Aldershire.
- Free Energy Healthchecks for residents over 60 or with a disability. Phone 01432 555 230 to book.
- Aldershire Welfare Assistance can issue an emergency fuel voucher if WHD is delayed and you cannot top up the meter (see separate document).
- Boiler replacement grants up to £6,000 via the council's HUG2 scheme for low-income owner-occupiers (eligibility criteria apply).
