# Council Tax Hardship Reduction

Aldershire offers a discretionary Council Tax Hardship Reduction under section 13A(1)(c) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992. This is separate from the means-tested Council Tax Reduction scheme (section 13A(1)(a)) and from the £25 Hardship Fund payment for working-age CTR recipients (section 13A(1)(c) Hardship Fund). It is the safety-net for residents whose circumstances are not adequately addressed by the standard reduction.

## When to apply

- After CTR has been awarded but a council tax shortfall remains.
- During exceptional events (bereavement, fire, flood, severe illness, fleeing domestic abuse).
- After a benefit sanction or DWP overpayment recovery.
- When a sudden change of circumstances means you cannot pay this month's instalment.

You should apply as early as possible — do not wait for a summons or liability order.

## Eligibility

- Liable for Aldershire council tax.
- Demonstrably unable to pay due to exceptional circumstances.
- Already in receipt of CTR (or applying simultaneously).
- Not already in arrangement with the council for arrears (but the recovery team may refer you).

## What we can do

- Reduce or write off some or all of the current year's bill.
- Suspend recovery for a defined period while you stabilise.
- Cancel late-payment summons costs (£68) and liability order costs (£45).
- Convert annual instalments to weekly or fortnightly payments.

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/council-tax-hardship
- Phone: 01432 555 100 (council tax team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- A senior officer reviews each case and decides within 14 working days.

## Evidence

- Statement of circumstances (what has happened, what you have already tried).
- Last 2 months' bank statements for all household adults.
- Income and outgoings (Common Financial Statement format preferred).
- Evidence of the exceptional event (medical letter, fire brigade incident number, police crime reference).

## Decisions

There is no statutory right of appeal but you can ask for a senior review within 28 days, then escalate to the Valuation Tribunal under section 16 LGFA 1992.

## Reference

Local Government Finance Act 1992 section 13A; Aldershire Council Tax Discretionary Discount Policy 2024.
