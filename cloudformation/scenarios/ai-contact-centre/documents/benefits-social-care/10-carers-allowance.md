# Carer's Allowance

Carer's Allowance is a DWP benefit paid to people who care for someone with substantial caring needs. Aldershire does not administer the benefit but supports residents through the application and signposts to wider carer support.

## Eligibility (DWP rules)

You can claim Carer's Allowance if:

- You are aged 16 or over.
- You spend at least 35 hours a week caring for the person.
- The person you care for receives a qualifying disability benefit (DLA middle/high care, PIP daily living, Attendance Allowance, Armed Forces Independence Payment, or Constant Attendance Allowance at the basic full-day rate).
- You are not in full-time education (over 21 hours a week).
- You earn no more than £196 per week net (after tax, NI, pension, and 50% of childcare).

## Rate

£91.90 per week (2026/27).

## How to apply

- Online: gov.uk/carers-allowance
- Phone: Carer's Allowance Unit 0800 731 0297.
- Paper form DS700 from gov.uk.

Aldershire's Carers Hub (Aldershire Carers Trust, 01432 555 410) can complete the form with you.

## What we do

- Refer you for a Carer's Needs Assessment under the Care Act 2014 (see separate document).
- Issue an Aldershire Carers Card giving free leisure access, library reservations, and a local discounts directory.
- Provide free 1-day carer training (lifting, mental health first aid, safeguarding) at the Civic Centre.
- Run a monthly carers cafe at Aldershire Library, every second Thursday 10am-12pm.
- Connect you to Aldershire Crossroads for short respite breaks (referral required, free at point of use up to 12 hours per quarter).

## Important interactions

Carer's Allowance is taxable and counts as income for means-tested benefits. It can also reduce the disabled person's Severe Disability Premium by £81.50/week. Get a Better Off calculation before claiming. Citizens Advice Aldershire offers free benefit calculations on 0808 250 5050.

## Underlying entitlement

If your earnings or another benefit prevent payment, ask DWP to record an "underlying entitlement" — this can unlock a Carer Premium of £45.60/week in your means-tested benefits.
