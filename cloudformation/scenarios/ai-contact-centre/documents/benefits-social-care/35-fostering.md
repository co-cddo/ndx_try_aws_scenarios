# Fostering with Aldershire

Aldershire is the in-house fostering service for around 320 fostering households who care for our looked-after children and young people. The service is regulated by Ofsted under the Care Standards Act 2000 and the Fostering Services (England) Regulations 2011.

## Types of fostering we recruit for

- **Mainstream long-term fostering**: children needing stability up to 18 (and beyond, see Staying Put).
- **Short-term and emergency**: from one night to several months.
- **Sibling groups**: brothers and sisters placed together.
- **Parent and child placements**: one parent and their baby, with assessment.
- **Therapeutic fostering**: enhanced support and training for children with significant trauma.
- **Connected persons (kinship)**: friends or family who become approved foster carers for a known child.
- **Short breaks**: weekend or holiday fostering for children with disabilities.

## Who can foster

- Aged 21 or over.
- Single, married, civil partner, cohabiting, of any sexual orientation, any faith or none.
- A spare bedroom for the foster child.
- A safe and stable home in or near Aldershire.
- The right to live and work in the UK.
- Time, energy, and emotional resilience to parent a child who has experienced loss or trauma.

You do not need to own your home, be employed, have your own children, or have professional childcare experience.

## Allowances and fees (2026/27)

Aldershire follows the Fostering Network National Minimum Allowances and adds a skill-based fee. Total weekly payments per child:

| Age | Allowance | Mainstream fee | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | £176 | £140 | £316 |
| 5-10 | £196 | £140 | £336 |
| 11-15 | £244 | £140 | £384 |
| 16-17 | £286 | £140 | £426 |

Therapeutic carers receive an enhanced fee of £225/week. Sibling group, parent-and-child, and emergency placements attract additional payments.

Holidays, birthdays, festivals, school clothes, and a tax-free £18,140 fostering allowance per household (2025/26 HMRC rate, plus additional per-child amounts) are HMRC qualifying-care relief items.

## Recruitment process

1. Initial phone call: 01432 555 460.
2. Information evening (monthly).
3. Application form.
4. Stage 1 (DBS, references, medicals, local authority checks): 8 weeks.
5. Stage 2 (Form F home study assessment): 4 months.
6. Skills to Foster training (3 days).
7. Fostering panel.
8. Approval decision by the Agency Decision Maker.

## Staying Put

Young people aged 18-21 can remain with their former foster carer under section 23CZA Children Act 1989 (introduced by Children and Families Act 2014). Aldershire pays a Staying Put allowance to the carer.
