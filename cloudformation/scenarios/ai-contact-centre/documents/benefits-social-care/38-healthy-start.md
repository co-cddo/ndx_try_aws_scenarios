# Healthy Start

Healthy Start is a national NHS Business Services Authority scheme that provides a prepaid card for buying milk, plain fresh and frozen fruit and vegetables, plain dried or tinned pulses, and infant formula based on cow's milk. The scheme is governed by the Healthy Start Scheme and Welfare Food (Amendment) Regulations 2021. Aldershire does not pay Healthy Start but actively promotes uptake and supports applications.

## Who qualifies

You qualify if you are pregnant or have a child under 4 AND your family receives one of:

- Universal Credit, with no earned income or earned income of £408 or less per month for the family.
- Income Support.
- Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance.
- Pension Credit (with the child addition).
- Child Tax Credit (without Working Tax Credit) with annual family income under £16,190.
- Working Tax Credit run-on (the 4 weeks after stopping work).

Any pregnant woman aged under 18 qualifies regardless of family income, and continues to qualify until her baby is 4 weeks old (after which the standard means test applies).

Migrants with No Recourse to Public Funds **can** apply if a child is a British citizen or lawfully present.

## Card values (2026)

- £4.25 per week during pregnancy.
- £8.50 per week for each child aged 0-1.
- £4.25 per week for each child aged 1-4.

The card is topped up every 4 weeks. Unspent funds carry over but funds expire 16 weeks after the last top-up.

## Healthy Start vitamins

Free vitamins are available for:

- Pregnant women: vitamin D and folic acid.
- Breastfeeding women.
- Children from birth to 4: vitamins A, C, and D.

Pick up from any Aldershire Family Hub on production of the Healthy Start card or proof of pregnancy/registration.

## How to apply

- Online: healthystart.nhs.uk
- Phone: 0300 330 7010 (NHS BSA, Mon-Fri 8am-6pm).
- Aldershire Family Hubs and midwives can help complete applications.
- The bot can transfer to Aldershire Family Hub, 01432 555 350, for digital support.

## Aldershire support

- Free 1:1 application appointments at Brookfield, Northlea, and Stoneford Family Hubs.
- "Eat Well, Live Well" cooking sessions for Healthy Start families: free childcare provided.
- Aldershire Foodbank top-up of fresh produce on production of Healthy Start card.
