# Homelessness prevention support

Aldershire has duties to prevent and relieve homelessness under the Housing Act 1996 Part 7 (as amended by the Homelessness Reduction Act 2017). Anyone at risk of losing their home should contact us as early as possible — we can do more before the eviction than after.

## The 56-day prevention duty

If you are threatened with homelessness within 56 days, we owe a prevention duty under section 195 Housing Act 1996. We will:

- Take a homelessness application.
- Carry out a personalised housing assessment.
- Agree a Personalised Housing Plan with reasonable steps for both you and us.
- Try to keep your current home or help you move to a new one.

## The relief duty

If you are already homeless or become homeless during the prevention duty, the relief duty (section 189B) applies for 56 days. We must take reasonable steps to help you secure suitable accommodation.

## Main housing duty

Where prevention and relief have not succeeded and you meet:

- Eligibility (immigration status).
- Homelessness.
- Priority need (pregnant, dependent children, vulnerable, fleeing violence, 16/17, care leaver under 21).
- Not intentionally homeless.
- Local connection (or referred from another authority).

We owe the main housing duty under section 193 — temporary accommodation followed by an offer of suitable housing.

## What we can do for prevention

- Mediation with landlords or family.
- Discretionary Housing Payments (top-up rent shortfall).
- Rent deposit guarantee (Aldershire Rent Bond Scheme — up to £1,200).
- Direct payment to landlord if you are in arrears.
- Liaison with Universal Credit / Housing Benefit teams.
- Mortgage rescue advice.
- Sanctuary Scheme (additional security in your existing home if you are at risk of domestic abuse).
- Help to find a new private tenancy.

- Aldershire Night Shelter: 22 Mill Lane, AL2 1NN, drop in 6pm onward.
