# Adult Social Care needs assessment

Aldershire has a duty under section 9 of the Care Act 2014 to assess any adult who appears to have needs for care and support. The assessment is the gateway to council-funded care, free advice, and signposting. It is free, regardless of income or savings.

## Who can ask for an assessment

- An adult (18+) who has, or appears to have, care and support needs.
- A carer requesting an assessment of their own needs (covered separately under section 10 Care Act 2014).
- A friend, family member, GP, hospital, or anyone else with the person's consent.

## What we look at

The assessment is "appearance of need" not diagnosis-based. We consider whether you can:

- Manage and maintain nutrition.
- Maintain personal hygiene.
- Manage toilet needs.
- Be appropriately clothed.
- Make use of the home safely.
- Maintain a habitable home environment.
- Develop and maintain family and personal relationships.
- Access work, training, education, or volunteering.
- Make use of necessary facilities and services in the local community.
- Carry out caring responsibilities for a child.

If you cannot meet two or more of these outcomes, and the impact on your wellbeing is significant, you meet the national eligibility threshold (Care Act regulations 2015, regulation 2).

## How we do it

- Self-assessment supported online: aldershire.gov.uk/adult-social-care
- Phone: 01432 555 500 (adult social care contact team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- Out of hours: emergency duty team 01432 555 999.
- Face-to-face at home (the default for older or housebound residents).
- Joint with health where appropriate (Continuing Healthcare, mental health, hospital discharge).

## What happens after

If you meet eligibility, we co-produce a care and support plan, set a personal budget, and arrange services. If you do not meet eligibility, we still provide universal information and advice (section 4 Care Act).

## Service standards

Initial contact response within 5 working days. Routine assessment within 28 calendar days. Urgent assessment within 24 hours. Hospital discharge within 2 working days under the Hospital Discharge and Community Support Guidance (DHSC, 2024).

## Reference

Care Act 2014 sections 9-13, 18-20; Care and Support (Eligibility Criteria) Regulations 2015.
