# Local Housing Allowance rates

Local Housing Allowance (LHA) sets the maximum Housing Benefit or Universal Credit Housing Element payable for private rented tenancies. Rates are set annually by the Valuation Office Agency for each Broad Rental Market Area (BRMA). Aldershire falls within the Central Aldershire BRMA.

## 2026/27 weekly rates (Central Aldershire BRMA)

| Category | Weekly rate |
|---|---|
| Shared accommodation rate | £92.05 |
| 1 bedroom | £143.83 |
| 2 bedrooms | £178.36 |
| 3 bedrooms | £207.12 |
| 4 bedrooms | £276.16 |

The 4-bedroom rate is the maximum payable; LHA is not paid above this size regardless of family size.

## Bedroom-size rules

Under the size criteria you are entitled to one bedroom for:

- Each adult couple.
- Any other person aged 16 or over.
- Two children under 10 (regardless of sex).
- Two children of the same sex aged 10 to 15.
- Any child or qualifying young person not covered above.
- An overnight carer for a disabled person who cannot be left alone.

Single adults under 35 with no dependants are restricted to the shared accommodation rate, with limited exceptions for care leavers under 25, severe disability premium recipients, and survivors of domestic abuse.

## Direct payment to landlord

LHA is normally paid to the tenant. Direct payment to the landlord is allowed where the tenant is in 8 weeks of arrears, has a history of rent arrears, or is unlikely to manage their own finances (vulnerability test).

## Appeals and reviews

LHA rates themselves cannot be appealed (set by statute), but the bedroom-size assessment for your household can be challenged. Phone the benefits team on 01432 555 102.

## Reference

The Rent Officers (Housing Benefit and Universal Credit Functions) (Local Housing Allowance Amendments) Order 2024 governs rate-setting.
