# Mental Health social work

Aldershire's mental health social work service is delivered through three integrated Community Mental Health Teams (CMHTs) jointly with Aldershire NHS Trust. Social workers are employed by the council and seconded into the NHS-led teams under section 75 NHS Act 2006 partnership agreements.

## What we do

- Care Act needs assessment for adults with serious and enduring mental illness.
- Approved Mental Health Professional (AMHP) duty for assessments under the Mental Health Act 1983 (sections 2, 3, 4, 135, 136).
- Care planning and review under the Mental Health Act Code of Practice and the Care Programme Approach.
- Section 117 aftercare planning for people leaving detention.
- Best Interests Assessor (BIA) duty for Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) under the Mental Capacity Act 2005.
- Safeguarding adults enquiries (section 42 Care Act 2014).
- Carer support for relatives of people with mental illness.

## How to access

- **Routine**: GP referral to your CMHT. There are three teams: Aldershire North (01432 555 800), Aldershire South (01432 555 810), Aldershire Rural (01432 555 820).
- **Urgent**: Aldershire Mental Health Crisis Line, 0808 196 4000 (free, 24/7).
- **Out of hours emergencies**: 999 or A&E at Aldershire General Hospital.
- **AMHP service**: only contacted by professionals (police, paramedics, GPs, hospitals); members of the public should call 999 or the crisis line.

## Section 117 aftercare

Free aftercare for life or until needs no longer apply, jointly funded by the council and Aldershire and Severn ICB. Includes accommodation, day services, support workers, and Direct Payments. Aftercare is not means-tested.

## Mental Capacity Act decisions

Where a person lacks capacity for a decision, our practitioners follow the 5 statutory principles (section 1 Mental Capacity Act 2005), use the 2-stage test, and act in the person's best interests. Independent Mental Capacity Advocates (IMCAs) are commissioned from POhWER (0300 456 2370) for people with no friends or family.

## Confidentiality

Mental health records are held in NHS systems; council social work records are linked under information sharing protocols. Disclosure follows the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Caldicott Principles.

## Reference

Mental Health Act 1983 (revised by the Mental Health Act 2007 and Health and Care Act 2022); Care Act 2014 sections 9, 42, 75; Mental Capacity Act 2005.
