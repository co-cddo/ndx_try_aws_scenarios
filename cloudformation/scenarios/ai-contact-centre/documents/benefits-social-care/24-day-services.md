# Day services

Aldershire commissions and runs day services for older people, adults with learning disabilities, adults with mental health needs, and people with dementia. Day services give the person social contact, structured activities, and a hot meal, and give carers a regular break.

## Aldershire-run day services

- **Aldershire Hub (older people, dementia-friendly)**: Civic Centre annexe, AL1 2BC. Mon-Fri 9:30am-3:30pm.
- **Brookfield Resource Centre (adults with learning disabilities)**: 12 Brookfield Lane, AL3 4DG. Mon-Fri 9:00am-4:00pm.
- **Aldershire Recovery College (mental health)**: 4 St Mary's Road, AL1 5JJ. Various time-limited courses.

## Independent providers (commissioned by Aldershire)

- Age UK Aldershire Friendship Club (multiple sites).
- Aldershire Mencap (learning disability sessions).
- Aldershire Alzheimer's Society Singing for the Brain.
- Aldershire Rural Wheels (transport-included village outreach).

A full directory is at aldershire.gov.uk/day-services.

## Activities

- Arts and crafts.
- Reminiscence sessions and life-story work.
- Light exercise (chair yoga, tai chi).
- Music and singing.
- Cooking and gardening.
- Outings and trips.
- IT and life-skills classes.
- Hairdressing and chiropody (independent providers, additional charge).

A two-course hot lunch and morning/afternoon refreshments are included.

## Charges

Day services are means-tested under the Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014. The standard charge is:

- £42 per day (Aldershire Hub).
- £55 per day (Brookfield Resource Centre — higher staff ratio for complex needs).
- Subsidised charge after financial assessment, can be reduced to £0 for low-income users.
- Transport (Aldershire Dial-a-Ride) charged separately at £6 return.

The first 6 weeks are free if part of a reablement plan.

## How to access

- Care Act needs assessment first (see separate document).
- Contact Adult Social Care: 01432 555 500.
- Self-referrals welcome for universal sessions (Recovery College, Friendship Clubs).

## Trial visits

We arrange free trial visits before any placement is confirmed. Most providers welcome family or carers to attend the first session.

## Reference

Care Act 2014 sections 18-20, 25; Care and Support Statutory Guidance chapter 8.
