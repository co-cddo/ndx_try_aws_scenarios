# Telecare equipment

Aldershire Telecare provides equipment and 24/7 monitoring to help people stay safe and independent at home. The service is part of the council's Care Act 2014 prevention duty (section 2) and is delivered in partnership with Aldershire NHS Trust.

## Equipment available

- **Pendant alarm**: lightweight wrist or neck alarm linked to a base unit. Press the button to speak to the response centre.
- **Falls detector**: automatic alert if it detects a sudden fall.
- **Bed and chair sensors**: alert if a person does not return after a set time (useful for people with dementia who get up at night and may wander).
- **Smoke and heat sensors**: linked to the response centre and Aldershire Fire and Rescue.
- **Carbon monoxide alarm**: linked to the response centre.
- **Flood detector**: under the kitchen sink and bath, alerts if there is a leak.
- **Door sensors**: alert if a vulnerable person leaves the property.
- **GPS locator**: for people with dementia who are at risk of going missing (Mindme or Buddi devices).
- **Medication dispenser**: locked dispenser that releases the right pills at the right time.
- **Epilepsy sensor**: detects seizures during sleep.

## How the response works

When an alert triggers:

1. The Aldershire Telecare Centre answers within 60 seconds (target).
2. They speak to you via the base unit's loudspeaker.
3. They contact a nominated responder (relative or neighbour with a key safe code).
4. If no nominated responder is available, they contact Aldershire Mobile Response (24/7), arriving within 45 minutes.
5. If there is a medical emergency, they call 999.

## Who is eligible

- Adults at risk of falls, with a long-term condition, with dementia, or living alone after bereavement.
- Children with complex medical needs (referred by hospital paediatrics).
- Carers worried about the person they look after.

## Cost

- £6.85 per week for the standard package (pendant + base unit + monitoring).
- £1.20 per week per additional sensor (smoke, falls, etc.).
- Free for people on Pension Credit Guarantee Credit, full Housing Benefit, or full Council Tax Reduction.
- Free during the 6-week reablement period.
