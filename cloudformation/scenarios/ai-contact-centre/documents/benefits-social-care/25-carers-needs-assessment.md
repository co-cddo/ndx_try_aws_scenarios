# Carer's needs assessment

A carer is a person of any age who provides unpaid support to a family member or friend who could not manage without their help. Aldershire has a duty under section 10 of the Care Act 2014 to assess any carer who appears to have a need for support.

## Who can ask

- Anyone caring for an adult Aldershire resident, regardless of how many hours of care they provide.
- Young carers (under 18) — covered by section 17ZA of the Children Act 1989, but Aldershire treats young-carer assessments as a specialist case (referred to the Aldershire Young Carers Project, 01432 555 415).
- Parent carers of disabled children — section 17ZD Children Act 1989.

You can ask for an assessment whether or not the person you care for is known to social care.

## What we look at

- Whether the carer is willing and able to continue caring.
- The impact on the carer's wellbeing (emotional, physical, financial).
- The carer's own employment, education, training, or recreation needs.
- Whether the carer has eligible needs under the Care and Support (Eligibility Criteria) Regulations 2015 (regulation 3): inability to achieve relevant outcomes such as undertaking caring responsibilities for a child, providing care to others, maintaining a habitable home, managing nutrition, and developing relationships.

## Eligibility threshold

A carer meets eligibility where there is a significant impact on wellbeing because they cannot achieve one or more outcomes due to their caring role.

## Outcomes from an eligible assessment

- A carer's support plan with a personal budget.
- Direct payments for the carer (e.g. for short breaks, leisure activities, or replacement care).
- Carer's emergency card and 72-hour replacement-care plan via the Aldershire Carers Trust.
- Free flu vaccination signposting.
- Aldershire Carers Card (free leisure access, library priority, local discount directory).
- Counselling via Aldershire Mind Carer Support (01432 555 720).
- Free training (manual handling, mental health first aid, dementia awareness).

## How to ask

- Phone: 01432 555 410 (Aldershire Carers Hub, run by Aldershire Carers Trust under contract).
- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/carers-assessment
- The bot can take initial details and book a callback within 5 working days.

## Reviews

Plans are reviewed every 12 months, or sooner on request. Carers can also be reviewed jointly with the person they care for.

## Reference

Care Act 2014 sections 10-13; Care and Support (Eligibility Criteria) Regulations 2015 regulation 3.
