# Care home placements

Aldershire arranges and funds residential and nursing home placements where this is the assessed best way to meet a person's eligible needs under the Care Act 2014. There are several funding routes; the right one depends on the person's needs and means.

## Funding routes

### Council-funded (Care Act)

After a needs assessment confirms eligibility and a financial assessment confirms means, the council contributes to the placement cost up to the Aldershire usual cost for the assessed band:

- Residential standard: £742/week.
- Residential dementia: £812/week.
- Nursing standard: £900/week (inclusive of NHS Funded Nursing Care contribution of £253.55/week).
- Nursing dementia: £965/week.

Top-ups may be sought from a third party (typically a relative) where the resident chooses a more expensive home; top-ups must be sustainable over the lifetime of the placement.

### NHS Continuing Healthcare (CHC)

Where a person has a primary health need, NHS Aldershire and Severn ICB pays the full placement cost, including accommodation and care. We refer using the CHC Checklist under the National Framework 2022 (revised). Phone the CHC team at the ICB on 01432 999 200.

### NHS Funded Nursing Care (FNC)

A person in a nursing home who does not meet CHC but has assessed nursing needs receives £253.55/week towards nursing input.

### Self-funded

Capital above £23,250 means full cost. We can still arrange the placement under section 18(3) Care Act.

### Section 117 Mental Health Act 1983

Aftercare placements for people detained under sections 3, 37, 45A, 47, or 48 are jointly funded by the council and ICB at no cost to the person, for as long as needs persist.

## How we arrange a placement

- Care plan agreed.
- 3 quotes sought from CQC-rated Good or Outstanding homes within Aldershire's contracted framework.
- Trial visits arranged.
- Contract signed (council-funded) or self-arrangement supported (self-funded).
- Review at 6 weeks, then annually.

## Choice of accommodation

The Care and Support and Aftercare (Choice of Accommodation) Regulations 2014 give the person the right to choose any home that meets their needs and is within the council's usual cost. Top-ups are required for higher-cost homes.
