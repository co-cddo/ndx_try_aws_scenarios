# Universal Credit and what Aldershire still does

Universal Credit (UC) is administered by the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) under the Welfare Reform Act 2012. Aldershire District Council does not assess UC, decide UC awards, or pay UC. We do, however, support residents through the application and ongoing claim.

## How to claim Universal Credit

- Apply online at gov.uk/apply-universal-credit
- Help to Claim is delivered nationally by Citizens Advice on 0800 144 8444.
- DWP Aldershire Jobcentre Plus: 0800 169 0190.

## What Aldershire does

We continue to deliver:

- Council Tax Reduction (a separate council scheme; UC does not include help with council tax).
- Discretionary Housing Payments to top up UC Housing Element where there is a shortfall.
- Aldershire Welfare Assistance (food, fuel, white goods) for residents in crisis while waiting for a UC payment.
- Free school meal applications (we use UC earnings thresholds to assess).
- Healthy Start vouchers signposting.
- Digital and budgeting support at Civic Centre and Aldershire Library.

## Universal Credit Housing Element

The UC Housing Element replaces Housing Benefit for working-age private and social tenants. DWP pays this directly to claimants by default. Tenants in arrears, with vulnerability indicators, or where the landlord requests it, can apply for an Alternative Payment Arrangement (APA) so rent is paid direct to the landlord. Aldershire's Housing Solutions team can submit a Tier 1 APA request on a tenant's behalf; phone 01432 555 145.

## Managed migration to UC

DWP is migrating the remaining legacy benefit claimants (Tax Credits, Income Support, JSA, ESA, Housing Benefit) to UC. Migration Notices give 3 months to claim. Missing the deadline ends legacy benefits with no fallback. Aldershire's benefits team can help residents understand their Migration Notice on 01432 555 102.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot make a UC claim, view a UC journal, or speak to DWP on a resident's behalf. We can book a digital appointment at Aldershire Library to use a public PC with staff support, or transfer to Citizens Advice for Help to Claim.
