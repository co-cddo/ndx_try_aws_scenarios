# SEND — Education, Health and Care Plans

Aldershire is the lead authority for Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs) for children and young people aged 0-25 with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND). Duties are set out in the Children and Families Act 2014 Part 3 and the SEND Regulations 2014, supported by the *SEND Code of Practice 2015*.

## Who has an EHCP

A child or young person needs an EHCP when their special educational needs cannot be met by the school's ordinary "SEN Support" arrangements. EHCPs replace the previous Statement of SEN and Learning Difficulty Assessment.

About 4.7% of Aldershire pupils have an EHCP (above the England average of 4.4%).

## How to ask for an assessment

- Anyone can request an EHC needs assessment: parent, young person aged 16+, school, GP, social worker.
- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/send-assessment-request
- Phone: 01432 555 360 (SEND team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- Aldershire SENDIASS (independent advice) on 01432 555 365.

## Statutory timescale (20 weeks)

Day 0: request received.

- Decide whether to assess: 6 weeks.
- Carry out assessments and decide whether to issue: another 10 weeks.
- Draft plan and consult on placement: 4 weeks.
- Final EHCP issued: by week 20.

## What an EHCP covers

- Section A: views, interests, and aspirations.
- Sections B-K: educational needs and provision, health needs and provision, social care needs and provision, named placement, personal budget, and the advice and information used.

## Personal Budget

Parents and young people can ask for a Personal Budget for parts of the EHCP, taken as a Direct Payment, council-arranged, or notional budget.

## Annual reviews

Schools convene an annual review (or 6-monthly for under-5s). The local authority decides whether to maintain, amend, or cease the EHCP.

## Right of appeal

If we refuse to assess, refuse to issue, or you disagree with the content of the EHCP, you have a right of appeal to the First-tier Tribunal (SEND). Mediation must be considered first (free advisory service: KIDS, 0808 800 5256).

## Aldershire Local Offer

Statutory under section 30 Children and Families Act 2014. Lists local SEND services in education, health, and care: aldershire.gov.uk/local-offer.
