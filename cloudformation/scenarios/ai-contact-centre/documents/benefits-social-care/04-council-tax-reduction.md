# Council Tax Reduction (CTR)

Council Tax Reduction is Aldershire's local scheme to help residents on low incomes pay their council tax. It is set under the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (as amended by the Local Government Finance Act 2012) and reviewed annually by Full Council.

## Two schemes

- **Pension-age scheme**: prescribed nationally; can pay up to 100% of the council tax bill.
- **Working-age scheme**: Aldershire's local scheme. Maximum award is 80% of the bill (working-age applicants pay at least 20%, except for protected groups).

## Means-tested calculation

The award is calculated as:

1. Start with the eligible council tax (Band A-H, less any single person discount or other reductions).
2. Apply the maximum percentage cap for the scheme (100% pension-age, 80% working-age).
3. Compare household income to the applicable amount (a personal allowance reflecting household composition).
4. If income is at or below applicable amount, full award is paid.
5. If income exceeds applicable amount, the award is tapered by 20p in the £ for working-age, 20p in the £ for pension-age.
6. Capital between £6,000 and £16,000 generates tariff income of £1 per £250.
7. Capital over £16,000 disqualifies (except if claimant receives Pension Credit Guarantee Credit).

## Protected working-age groups (full 100%)

- Pensioners in mixed-age couples on legacy benefits.
- Households with a child under 5 (Aldershire local addition).
- Care leavers up to age 25.
- Recipients of Severe Disability Premium.

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/ctr
- Phone: 01432 555 102.
- A combined Housing Benefit / CTR form is available at the Civic Centre.

## Evidence

Same evidence list as Housing Benefit (ID, two months' bank statements, payslips, benefit awards, NI numbers).

## Backdating

Working-age: up to 1 month with good cause. Pension-age: up to 3 months automatic.

## Decision and review

Decisions are issued within 28 days. Annual review notifications are sent each March. Residents must report changes within 21 days.
