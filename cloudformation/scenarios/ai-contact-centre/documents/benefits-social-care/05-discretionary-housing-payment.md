# Discretionary Housing Payment (DHP)

A Discretionary Housing Payment is a top-up to Housing Benefit or the Universal Credit Housing Element where there is a shortfall between rent and benefit. DHPs are governed by the Discretionary Financial Assistance Regulations 2001 and funded from a fixed annual DWP allocation supplemented by Aldershire from general funds.

## When DHP can help

- Bedroom tax (under-occupancy) shortfall in social housing.
- Benefit cap shortfall.
- Local Housing Allowance below local market rent in the private sector.
- Rent arrears arising from a temporary loss of income.
- Costs to move to a more affordable home (rent in advance, deposit, removal costs).
- Short-term need following a relationship breakdown, fleeing domestic abuse, or hospital discharge.

## Eligibility

You must be entitled to Housing Benefit or the UC Housing Element on the date of application. DHP cannot be paid for service charges that are not eligible for Housing Benefit (water rates, food, personal care charges).

## How to apply

- Online form: aldershire.gov.uk/dhp
- Phone: 01432 555 102.
- A benefits officer can take details verbally, but supporting evidence must be submitted in writing within 14 days.

## Evidence

- Statement of need (what the shortfall is and what efforts you have made to manage it).
- Bank statements (last 2 months, all accounts).
- Income for all household adults.
- Essential outgoings (food, utilities, debt repayments).

## Decision

DHPs are decided within 21 working days. Awards are typically time-limited (4 weeks to 6 months) and reviewed if extended. There is no statutory right of appeal, but a request for review can be made within 1 month and considered by a senior officer.

## Aldershire DHP budget 2026/27

£612,000 (DWP £388,000 + council top-up £224,000). Awards are prioritised to households with children, fleeing domestic abuse, or with a disability.

## Reference

DHP Good Practice Guide (DWP, Manual GM5, latest edition) is followed by Aldershire decision-makers.
