# Children's services — early help to child protection

Aldershire's children's social care follows the four-tier framework set out in *Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023*: universal services, early help, child in need (section 17 Children Act 1989), and child protection (section 47 Children Act 1989).

## Tier 1 — universal services

Open to all children and families: schools, health visiting, GPs, libraries, family centres. No referral needed.

## Tier 2 — early help

For families starting to struggle. Voluntary, multi-agency support coordinated through an Early Help Assessment (EHA, formerly CAF) led by a lead practitioner.

- Free parenting programmes (Triple P, Solihull Approach).
- Family Support Workers from Aldershire Family Hubs.
- Brookfield, Northlea, and Stoneford Family Hubs (open Mon-Sat).
- Aldershire Children's Centre toddler groups, antenatal sessions.

Phone the Aldershire Family Information Service on 01432 555 350 to access early help.

## Tier 3 — child in need (section 17 Children Act 1989)

A child whose health or development would be significantly impaired without local authority support, or who is disabled. A child-in-need plan is co-produced. Statutory visits at least every 6 weeks. Reviewed every 3 months.

## Tier 4 — child protection (section 47 Children Act 1989)

Where there is reasonable cause to suspect that a child is suffering, or is likely to suffer, significant harm. We carry out a section 47 enquiry, jointly with police and health where appropriate, and convene an Initial Child Protection Conference within 15 working days. The child becomes "subject to a child protection plan" under one of four categories: physical abuse, sexual abuse, emotional abuse, or neglect.

## Looked after children

Where a child cannot remain at home (parental request under section 20 Children Act 1989, or court orders under section 31 Children Act 1989) we accommodate them with foster carers, in residential care, or with kinship carers.

## How to refer a worried child

- **Anyone (professional or member of public)**: Aldershire Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub (MASH), 01432 555 700, Mon-Fri 8am-6pm.
- **Out of hours**: Emergency Duty Team 01432 555 999.
- **Police, urgent danger**: 999.
- **NSPCC**: 0808 800 5000.
- **Childline**: 0800 1111.

## Information sharing

Sharing information to safeguard a child does not require consent if doing so would put the child at risk. See *Information Sharing Advice for Safeguarding Practitioners* (DfE, 2024).

## Reference

Children Act 1989 sections 17, 20, 31, 47; Children Act 2004 section 11; Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023.
