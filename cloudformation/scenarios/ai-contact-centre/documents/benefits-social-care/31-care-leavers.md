# Care leavers — Personal Allowance and Local Offer

A care leaver is a young person who was looked after by Aldershire (typically for at least 13 weeks since age 14, including a period after age 16). Aldershire's duties to care leavers run until age 25, regardless of whether they are in education or work. The duties are set out in the Children Act 1989 (sections 23A-24D) as amended by the Children and Social Work Act 2017 section 3.

## Categories

- **Eligible**: looked after, aged 16-17.
- **Relevant**: aged 16-17, no longer looked after but was after 14.
- **Former relevant**: aged 18-25, previously eligible or relevant.
- **Qualifying**: aged 16-24, in care for 13 weeks since age 14 (more limited duties, but still entitled to advice and support).

## Personal Adviser (PA)

Every care leaver up to age 25 is allocated a Personal Adviser. The PA:

- Maintains contact (phone, text, video, in person; minimum every 8 weeks).
- Develops and reviews the Pathway Plan (every 6 months).
- Coordinates education, employment, training, health, and accommodation support.

## Pathway Plan

A statutory plan that sets out what the council, the young person, and partners will do. Written from the young person's perspective.

## Setting Up Home Allowance

A one-off £3,000 grant to help a care leaver furnish their first home (formerly £2,000, increased by Aldershire from April 2025). Ring-fenced for white goods, beds, sofas, kitchen essentials.

## Personal allowance for care leavers (16-17)

If a relevant child is not in employment, education, or training and not in foster care, Aldershire pays a personal allowance of £91.40/week — equivalent to the under-25 Universal Credit rate, with no sanctions and no conditions.

## Council tax exemption for care leavers up to age 25

Aldershire grants 100% council tax exemption to care leavers aged 18-25 living alone or with other care leavers, under section 13A LGFA 1992 and the Aldershire Local Offer.

## The Local Offer

Published at aldershire.gov.uk/care-leavers-offer. Highlights:

- Free Aldershire bus pass until age 25.
- Free Aldershire Library full membership.
- Free leisure-centre membership.
- Apprenticeship guaranteed-interview scheme with Aldershire Council.
- £100 birthday and Christmas vouchers each year up to age 21.
- £1,500 contribution toward driving lessons (subject to eligibility and a learning plan).
