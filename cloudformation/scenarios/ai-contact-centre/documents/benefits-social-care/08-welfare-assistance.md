# Aldershire Welfare Assistance Scheme

The Aldershire Welfare Assistance Scheme (AWAS) is the council's local replacement for the abolished DWP Social Fund Crisis Loan and Community Care Grant. It provides emergency support to residents in financial crisis. The scheme is funded from the Household Support Fund and authorised under section 1 Localism Act 2011.

## What we can provide

- **Food**: a 3-day or 7-day food parcel via Aldershire Foodbank (referred by the council).
- **Energy vouchers**: prepayment meter top-ups of £49 (gas) or £49 (electricity), issued via PayPoint or Post Office.
- **White goods**: refurbished cooker, fridge, freezer, washing machine, or bed (for households being resettled or fleeing domestic abuse).
- **Travel costs**: bus or train ticket for essential journeys (medical, child contact, fleeing harm).
- **Baby essentials**: starter pack of nappies, formula (where breastfeeding is not possible), and clothing.

We do not provide cash. We do not pay rent (use Discretionary Housing Payment) or council tax (use Council Tax Hardship Reduction).

## Eligibility

You must be:

- An Aldershire resident (or about to move into Aldershire under a resettlement plan).
- Aged 16 or over.
- In genuine financial crisis with no other resource you can reasonably draw on.

Sanctioned UC claimants, asylum seekers, and migrants with No Recourse to Public Funds are all eligible (we do not apply the qualifying-benefits test).

## How to apply

- Phone: 01432 555 110 (welfare team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/welfare-assistance
- Out of hours, Aldershire Foodbank can issue an emergency food parcel and refer you to us the next working day: 01432 555 990.

## Evidence and decision

We aim to decide within 4 working hours during opening hours, or 24 hours otherwise. Light-touch evidence: bank statement covering the last 14 days, a photo of the meter, a note from a referrer (Citizens Advice, GP, social worker).

## Limits

Maximum 3 awards in any 12-month period. Repeat applications trigger a referral to specialist money advice.
