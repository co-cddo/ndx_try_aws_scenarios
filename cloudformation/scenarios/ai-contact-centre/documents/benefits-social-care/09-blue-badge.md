# Blue Badge applications

The Blue Badge scheme provides parking concessions to people with severe mobility difficulties. Aldershire administers applications under the Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (England) Regulations 2000 (as amended).

## Two routes

### Automatic eligibility (no further assessment)

You qualify automatically if you:

- Receive the higher rate of the mobility component of Disability Living Allowance.
- Receive Personal Independence Payment with 8 points or more on the moving-around descriptor, or 10 points on descriptor E (planning and following journeys).
- Receive a War Pensioner's Mobility Supplement.
- Receive a lump-sum benefit at tariffs 1 to 8 of the Armed Forces Compensation Scheme with a permanent and substantial disability that affects walking.
- Are registered severely sight impaired (blind).

### Discretionary eligibility (assessed by Aldershire)

You may qualify if you:

- Cannot walk, or experience considerable difficulty walking due to a permanent and substantial physical disability.
- Have a non-visible (hidden) disability that means you cannot make a journey without considerable psychological distress or risk of harm to yourself or others (e.g. severe autism, dementia, severe anxiety disorders).
- Are aged under 3 with a condition requiring bulky medical equipment, or that may require quick access to a vehicle for emergency treatment.

## How to apply

- Online (recommended): gov.uk/apply-blue-badge — DWP runs the national portal but the application is decided by Aldershire.
- Phone: 01432 555 130 (Blue Badge team).
- Paper forms available at Civic Centre and any Aldershire library.

## Cost

£10 for a 3-year badge. Lost or stolen badges replaced for £10. Renewal at £10.

## Evidence

- Proof of identity (passport or driving licence).
- Proof of address (council tax or utility bill).
- A passport-style photo (online applicants can upload).
- Proof of qualifying benefit OR medical evidence (consultant letter, OT assessment, prescription list).

## Decision

Automatic applications: 5 working days. Discretionary applications: 12 weeks (an independent mobility assessment may be arranged at no cost). Refusals can be reviewed by a senior officer within 28 days; there is no further statutory appeal but the decision can be challenged by judicial review.
