# Household Support Fund

The Household Support Fund (HSF) is a discretionary local fund passed to upper-tier councils by the Department for Work and Pensions to help vulnerable households with the cost of food, energy, water, and other essentials. The current scheme runs to 31 March 2027.

## Aldershire's 2026/27 allocation

£3.412 million from DWP, supplemented by £400,000 from Aldershire general fund. Of this:

- 60% earmarked for direct support (vouchers, white goods, food parcels).
- 25% for partner-led support (foodbanks, baby banks, school holiday clubs).
- 15% for advice, energy efficiency assessments, and administration.

## Who can apply

- Aldershire residents in financial hardship.
- No qualifying-benefit gateway: applications are means-tested directly on income and outgoings.
- Households with children, pensioners, disabled people, or single parents are prioritised.
- Migrants with No Recourse to Public Funds are eligible.

## What the fund pays for

- Supermarket vouchers (Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury's) up to £200 per household per quarter.
- Energy vouchers (PayPoint or Post Office) up to £100 per household per quarter.
- Water debt relief in partnership with Severn Trent Big Difference Scheme.
- White goods (cooker, fridge, washing machine, bed) for households being resettled.
- School holiday food vouchers (£15 per child per week of school holidays — administered with Aldershire schools).

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/hsf
- Phone: 01432 555 110 (welfare team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- Via a referrer: foodbank, school, GP, social worker, Citizens Advice (recommended for housebound or digitally excluded residents).

## Evidence

Light-touch — last 14 days of bank statements (any account), proof of address. We do not require benefit award letters.

## Decision timescale

5 working days. Vouchers issued by SMS or email within 3 further working days.

## Reference

Section 1 Localism Act 2011 (general power of competence). Funded by DWP Household Support Fund grant determination 2026/27.
