# Short breaks for children with disabilities

Aldershire has a duty under section 25(2) of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008 and the Breaks for Carers of Disabled Children Regulations 2011 to provide short breaks for parents who care for disabled children. The service is published in Aldershire's Short Breaks Statement.

## What is a short break

A short break is any service that gives a parent or other family carer time apart from their regular caring role. Short breaks help the child develop friendships, skills, and confidence, and help the family to cope and stay together. They are not "respite from a difficult child"; the focus is on the child's experience.

## The four-tier offer

### Tier 1 — universal

Any disabled child up to 18 can use:

- Aldershire holiday playscheme (5 weeks across school holidays).
- Aldershire SEND-inclusive leisure-centre membership.
- Aldershire Saturday Club at Brookfield Resource Centre.
- Aldershire Children's Library SEND-friendly storytime.

No assessment needed; phone Aldershire Family Information Service on 01432 555 350.

### Tier 2 — targeted

A small Family Support Plan, no full assessment:

- After-school clubs (specialist staff ratio 1:2 or 1:3).
- Befriending and buddy-up schemes.
- Holiday family days out.
- Weekend group activities.

Apply via the Disabled Children's Team duty desk: 01432 555 380.

### Tier 3 — specialist

Following a section 17 Children Act 1989 assessment:

- Overnight short breaks at the Oakwood short-break unit (registered Ofsted children's home).
- Direct Payments for parents to commission their own short breaks (a regular Personal Assistant, holiday clubs, swimming with a 1:1 worker).
- Specialist play sessions for children with profound and multiple learning disabilities.

### Tier 4 — high-cost specialist

Out-of-area placements where Aldershire cannot meet need locally. Approved through panel only.

## How to access

- Single referral form: aldershire.gov.uk/short-breaks
- Phone: 01432 555 380.
- Schools and SENCOs can refer with parental consent.
- The bot can take initial details and book a callback within 5 working days.

## Charges

Tier 1 and 2 are free. Tier 3 and 4 are part of a children's social care plan and may be subject to a parental contribution under Aldershire's children's social care charging policy (transport and meals only).

## Reference

Children and Young Persons Act 2008 section 25; Breaks for Carers of Disabled Children Regulations 2011; Children Act 1989 section 17 and Schedule 2.
