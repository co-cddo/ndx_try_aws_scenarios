# School clothing grant

Aldershire offers a discretionary school clothing grant of £150 per child per academic year to families on low incomes. The scheme is funded from the Household Support Fund and is reviewed annually each April.

## Eligibility

You can apply if:

- Your child is aged 4 to 16 and in full-time state education at an Aldershire-funded school.
- You or your partner receive a qualifying benefit (the FSM list applies):
  - Universal Credit with earned income under £7,400.
  - Income Support, Income-based JSA, Income-related ESA.
  - Pension Credit Guarantee Credit.
  - Child Tax Credit without Working Tax Credit, income under £16,190.
- You are the parent or legal guardian of the child.

Children placed in independent or out-of-borough schools by Aldershire SEND service are also eligible.

## What it covers

- School uniform (blazer, jumper, polo shirts, trousers, skirts).
- Footwear (school shoes and trainers for PE).
- PE kit (shorts, top, plimsolls, tracksuit).
- A school coat.

The grant is paid as a £150 voucher redeemable at participating retailers (Marks and Spencer, Asda, Sainsbury's, Tesco, and Aldershire School Uniform Shop on the High Street).

## How to apply

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/school-clothing
- Phone: 01432 555 220 (school admissions team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- Postal forms are available from the Civic Centre, AL1 2BC.

## Evidence

- Most recent benefit award letter or Universal Credit assessment statement.
- Proof of address (council tax bill or recent utility bill).
- Child's name, date of birth, and school.

## Application window

The main application window is 1 May to 30 September each year, to support the start of the autumn term. Late applications are accepted until 31 March if funds remain.

## Decisions

Decisions are issued within 14 working days. Vouchers are sent by SMS or email within 5 working days of approval.
