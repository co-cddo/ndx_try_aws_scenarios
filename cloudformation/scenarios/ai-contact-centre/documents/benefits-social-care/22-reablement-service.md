# Reablement service

Aldershire's Reablement Service (Aldershire Pathway Home) provides up to 6 weeks of free, intensive, time-limited home support to help people regain skills and confidence after a hospital admission, illness, or injury. It is free at the point of use under section 14(5)(a) of the Care Act 2014 (which exempts intermediate care and reablement support from charging for the first 6 weeks).

## What we offer

- Personal care (washing, dressing, toileting, medication prompts).
- Help to prepare meals and drinks.
- Walking practice with physiotherapy.
- Stair-climbing and transfer training.
- Help to use a new aid (frame, grab rail, perching stool) installed by Aldershire occupational therapy.
- Confidence-building support to leave the home (shopping, GP appointments, social contact).

## Who is eligible

- Adults aged 18+ recently discharged from hospital, recovering from a fall, illness, or surgery.
- Adults referred by a GP, district nurse, or social worker who have a realistic chance of regaining independence.
- Existing care recipients whose needs may be reduced through reablement.

People with stable, long-term needs unlikely to improve are not normally suitable for reablement; they go straight to a long-term care plan.

## How long

Up to 42 days (6 weeks) free. Outcomes are reviewed weekly. About 65% of Aldershire reablement service users finish with no ongoing care need; the rest move to a long-term plan with a personal budget.

## Cost beyond 6 weeks

If after 6 weeks you still have eligible needs, you move to a long-term care plan. Charging starts on day 43 after a financial assessment.

## How to access

- Hospital: refer through the Aldershire Hospital Discharge Hub at Aldershire General Hospital, 01432 555 580.
- Community: refer through the Adult Social Care Contact Team, 01432 555 500.
- Self-referral: the bot can take initial details and book a phone callback.

## What we cannot do

- Provide medical or nursing care (district nursing covers this).
- Continue indefinitely (the model is time-limited and recovery-focused).
- Replace family or paid carers without an assessment.

## Reference

Care Act 2014 section 14(5); Care and Support (Charging and Assessment of Resources) Regulations 2014, regulation 3.
