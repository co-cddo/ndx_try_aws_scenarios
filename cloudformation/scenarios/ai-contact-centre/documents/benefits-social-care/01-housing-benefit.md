# Housing Benefit

Aldershire District Council administers Housing Benefit (HB) under the Welfare Reform Act 2012 and the Housing Benefit Regulations 2006. The scheme is closed to most working-age claimants, who must claim the Universal Credit Housing Element through DWP instead. Aldershire continues to pay HB to pension-age residents and to working-age residents in supported, temporary, or specified accommodation.

## Eligibility

You can apply if you:

- Pay rent (private landlord, housing association, or supported housing).
- Have low income and savings under £16,000 (no savings limit if you also receive Pension Credit Guarantee Credit).
- Are habitually resident in the UK.
- Live in the property as your main home.

## How to apply

- Online form: aldershire.gov.uk/housing-benefit
- Phone: 01432 555 102 (benefits team, Mon-Fri 9am-5pm).
- In person: Civic Centre, High Street, Aldershire AL1 2BC, Tuesday afternoons and Friday mornings (no appointment needed).
- A paper form can be posted out on request, but online is the fastest route.

## Evidence required

You must provide originals or certified copies of:

- Proof of identity for every adult in the household (passport, driving licence, or birth certificate plus a recent utility bill).
- Most recent two months' bank statements for every account.
- Five payslips (weekly pay) or two payslips (monthly pay) for working applicants.
- Award letters for any other benefits.
- Tenancy agreement and most recent rent statement.
- National Insurance numbers for every adult.

## Decision and payment

Aldershire decides new claims within 28 days of all evidence being received. Backdated awards are possible for up to 1 month (working age) or 3 months (pension age). Payments are made four-weekly in arrears, direct to the tenant or to the landlord on request.

## Changes of circumstances

You must report changes within 1 month. Failure to report can lead to overpayments, which are recovered from ongoing benefit or by invoice under section 75 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a decision, ask for a written statement of reasons within 1 month, then request a revision or appeal to HM Courts and Tribunals Service.
