# Looked-after children and corporate parenting

Aldershire is the corporate parent for around 480 looked-after children at any one time (rate 56 per 10,000, in line with the England average). Corporate parenting is a duty held by the entire council, not just children's services, under sections 1-9 of the Children and Social Work Act 2017.

## What is a looked-after child

A child is looked after if they are:

- Accommodated voluntarily under section 20 Children Act 1989 with parental agreement (no court order).
- Subject to a Care Order under section 31 (the council shares parental responsibility).
- Subject to an Emergency Protection Order under section 44.
- Remanded into local authority care under the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012.
- Placed with the council by a court under a Placement Order under the Adoption and Children Act 2002.

## Where they live

- Foster care (the most common, around 70% of placements) — see separate fostering document.
- Connected persons / kinship care (a relative or friend, around 12%).
- Residential children's home (typically 10-15 year olds with complex needs).
- Independent living (16+ semi-independence pathway).
- Out-of-area secure or specialist placements (rare, complex care needs).

Aldershire runs three council children's homes (Oakwood, Riverside, and The Beeches) plus a contracted framework with Ofsted-rated Good or Outstanding independent providers.

## Statutory tools

- **Care Plan** (regulation 5, Care Planning, Placement and Case Review Regulations 2010).
- **Personal Education Plan (PEP)**: jointly between social worker, virtual school, and school.
- **Health Assessment** within 28 days of placement, then annual (or 6-monthly under-5).
- **Independent Reviewing Officer (IRO)** chairs Looked After Reviews at 28 days, 3 months, and then 6-monthly.
- **Pathway Plan** for over-16s and care leavers.

## Aldershire Virtual School

The Virtual School Head Teacher tracks the educational progress of every looked-after child. Pupil Premium Plus (£2,570 per pupil per year) is targeted on individual interventions through the PEP. Phone 01432 555 410.

## Visiting

Social workers visit at least once every 6 weeks (more frequently in the early weeks of a placement and for younger children).

## Listening to children

Each looked-after child has access to:

- An advocate (commissioned from Coram Voice, 0808 800 5792).
- The IRO between reviews.
- The Children's Rights Director / Children's Commissioner.
- Aldershire's Children in Care Council, "Aldervoice", which feeds into Cabinet quarterly.

## Reference

Children Act 1989; Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (England) Regulations 2010; Children and Social Work Act 2017.
