# Pension Credit

Pension Credit is a means-tested DWP benefit for people over State Pension age (66). It tops up weekly income to a guaranteed minimum and unlocks a wide range of further help. Take-up nationally is around 65%, leaving an estimated 880,000 eligible households missing out. Aldershire actively promotes uptake.

## Two parts

- **Guarantee Credit**: tops up weekly income to £227.10 (single) or £346.60 (couple).
- **Savings Credit**: a small reward for modest retirement provision; only available to those who reached State Pension age before 6 April 2016. Up to £17.30/week (single) or £19.36/week (couple).

There is no upper savings limit, but capital over £10,000 generates tariff income of £1 per £500.

## Eligibility

- You and your partner have reached State Pension age.
- You live in Great Britain.
- Your weekly income is below the guarantee threshold.

Mixed-age couples (one partner under 66) cannot claim Pension Credit; they must claim Universal Credit instead, except where they have been on legacy benefits continuously since before 15 May 2019.

## What it unlocks

A successful Guarantee Credit claim passports you to:

- Free TV Licence (if aged 75+).
- Cold weather payments and Winter Fuel Payment uplift.
- Full Housing Benefit and Council Tax Reduction (no further means test).
- NHS dental treatment, glasses, and travel costs.
- Warm Home Discount (£150 off the electricity bill).
- Help with court fees, debt relief orders, and legal aid.

## How to apply

- Phone: 0800 99 1234 (free, Mon-Fri 8am-6pm).
- Online: gov.uk/pension-credit/apply
- Paper form (PC1) on request.

You can apply up to 4 months before reaching State Pension age. Claims can be backdated up to 3 months automatically.
