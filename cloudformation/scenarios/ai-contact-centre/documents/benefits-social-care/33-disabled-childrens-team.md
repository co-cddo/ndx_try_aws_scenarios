# Disabled Children's Team

Aldershire's Disabled Children's Team (DCT) is a specialist children's social work team for children and young people (0-18) with substantial and permanent disabilities. The team brings together social workers, occupational therapists, family support workers, and a dedicated transitions worker.

## Who qualifies

A child is eligible for DCT support if they have:

- A permanent and substantial disability under section 17(11) Children Act 1989, AND
- Eligible needs requiring specialist social work intervention.

Indicative thresholds:

- Profound and multiple learning disabilities.
- Severe autism with high support needs.
- Sensory impairment with mobility implications.
- Complex medical or palliative needs.
- Life-limiting or life-threatening conditions.

Children with mild to moderate needs are supported through SEND Support, the Family Hub network, or the universal early help offer.

## What we offer

- Specialist Care Act-style needs assessments under section 17 Children Act 1989.
- Direct Payments for parent carers.
- Short breaks (see separate document).
- Single Assessment with health and education partners.
- Dedicated occupational therapy (home adaptations through the Disabled Facilities Grant, see Housing service).
- Equipment for daily living (loaned through the Aldershire Joint Equipment Service).
- Transitions to adult social care (starts at age 14 with a transition plan; transfer at 18).
- Section 17 budget for emergency family support.
- Coordinated planning where the child is also Looked After or has an EHCP.

## Disabled Facilities Grant (DFG)

For home adaptations (ramps, hoists, downstairs bathrooms, wheelchair access). Means-tested for owner-occupiers; not means-tested for children. Maximum grant £30,000. Applications via Aldershire Housing on 01432 555 145.

## Family Fund

Charity grants for low-income families with disabled children (washing machines, sensory toys, family breaks). Aldershire DCT can refer; the family can also self-refer at familyfund.org.uk.

## Council Tax disability reduction

Where a home has been adapted (extra room, wheelchair use, or extra bathroom or kitchen for the disabled person), the council tax is charged at the band below the actual band. Apply on 01432 555 100.

## Reference

Children Act 1989 section 17 and Schedule 2; Children and Families Act 2014 Part 3; Care Act 2014 sections 58-66 (transitions to adult care).
