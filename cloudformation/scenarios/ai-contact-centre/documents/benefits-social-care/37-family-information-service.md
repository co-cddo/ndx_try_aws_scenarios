# Family Information Service and childcare directory

The Aldershire Family Information Service (FIS) is the council's statutory information service for families. It is delivered under section 12 of the Childcare Act 2006, which requires every council to provide information, advice, and assistance about childcare and other services for parents and prospective parents of children up to age 20 (25 if SEND).

## What we publish

- A directory of every Ofsted-registered childcare provider in Aldershire (childminders, day nurseries, pre-schools, after-school and breakfast clubs, holiday playschemes).
- Vacancy information (updated weekly by providers).
- Cost guides.
- Activities and groups (parent-and-toddler, dads' groups, breastfeeding cafes).
- Family support services.
- The Aldershire SEND Local Offer.
- Adult education and family learning courses.

Search at aldershire.gov.uk/fis or call 01432 555 350.

## Childcare entitlements

The FIS helps you understand and apply for:

- **15 hours free for 2-year-olds** of low-income families (means-tested, qualifying benefits).
- **30 hours free for working parents** of children aged 9 months to school age (eligibility through HMRC childcare account).
- **15 hours free for all 3- and 4-year-olds** (universal).
- **Tax-Free Childcare** (top-up of 20% via gov.uk childcare account, max £2,000/year per child).
- **Universal Credit childcare element** (up to 85% of costs, paid in arrears).
- **Holiday Activities and Food Programme** (HAF): free places in school holidays for FSM-eligible children, run by Aldershire Active.

## Other family support

- **Healthy Start**: vouchers for milk, fruit, vegetables, and vitamins for low-income pregnant women and families with under-4s (see separate document).
- **Aldershire Family Hubs**: 5 hubs across the district offering parenting groups, breastfeeding support, paediatric drop-ins, and stay-and-play.
- **Birth-to-Settled-Behaviour** (Triple P) parenting course, free.
- **Aldershire Parenting Talkline**, 01432 555 355, Mon-Fri 9am-7pm.
