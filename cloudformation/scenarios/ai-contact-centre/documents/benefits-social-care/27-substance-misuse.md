# Substance misuse services

Aldershire commissions an integrated substance misuse service for adults and young people, covering alcohol, illegal drugs, and prescription medication dependency. The service is funded from the council's public health grant under the Health and Social Care Act 2012.

## The Aldershire service

The contract is held by **Change Grow Live (CGL) Aldershire**, operating from:

- Adult hub: Hope House, 6 St John's Way, AL1 8RR.
- Young persons' service (under 25): co-located at the Aldershire Youth Hub, 22 Market Square, AL1 2DD.
- Outreach in rural Aldershire (Brookfield, Northlea, Stoneford) on rotating days.

## What is offered

- Free, confidential 1:1 keyworker support.
- Group programmes (recovery, mutual aid, SMART Recovery, AA, NA, CA).
- Structured day care for the most complex needs.
- Community detox (alcohol, opiate substitution with methadone or buprenorphine).
- Inpatient detox referrals (Aldershire Recovery Centre, 14 beds).
- Residential rehabilitation (commissioned out-of-area where Aldershire's residential placement panel agrees, panel meets fortnightly).
- Naloxone distribution and training to reverse opiate overdose.
- Needle exchange.
- Family and friends support groups.

## Who can refer

- Self-referral (recommended): walk in to Hope House Mon-Fri 9am-5pm, or phone 0300 003 7777, or text "ALDER" to 60777.
- GP, hospital, A&E, mental health team, probation, social services, schools.
- Anonymous referrals welcome at the first visit.

## Confidentiality

Substance misuse records are kept separately from the council's social care records and are not shared without consent, except where:

- A child or vulnerable adult is at risk (safeguarding).
- A serious crime is in progress or imminent.
- A court order requires disclosure.

## Crisis support

If someone has overdosed: phone 999 immediately, put them in the recovery position, administer naloxone if you have it. Stay with them until paramedics arrive.

## Drug-related litter

To report a needle in a public place, phone Aldershire Streets on 01432 555 250 — a specialist team responds within 4 hours.

## Reference

Health and Social Care Act 2012; Misuse of Drugs Act 1971; Aldershire Drug and Alcohol Strategy 2024-2027.
