# Cold Weather Payments

A Cold Weather Payment is a £25 payment from DWP for each 7-day period of forecast or recorded average temperature of 0°C or below in your postcode area, between 1 November and 31 March. The payment is automatic for eligible benefit recipients. Aldershire does not administer the scheme but supports residents to ensure their details are correct.

## Eligibility (automatic)

You qualify automatically if, during the cold spell, you receive one of:

- Pension Credit.
- Income Support, Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance, or Income-related ESA AND have a disability or pensioner premium, a child under 5 in the household, or a child receiving disability benefits.
- Universal Credit, AND your award includes a limited capability for work element, a disabled child element, OR you have a child under 5 in the household, AND you do not have earnings in the qualifying assessment period.
- Support for Mortgage Interest, with the same disability/child criteria.

## Aldershire weather station

The DWP-designated weather station for Aldershire postcodes (AL1-AL9, AL12) is **Aldershire Aerodrome** (Met Office ID 03659). Temperature data is published daily on gov.uk/cold-weather-payment.

## How payment is made

- Direct to the bank account where benefit is paid.
- Within 14 working days of the cold spell ending.
- No application needed.

## If you should have been paid but were not

- Phone Pension Credit / DWP local office: Aldershire JCP 0800 169 0190.
- Provide your National Insurance number, address, and the dates of the cold spell.

## Aldershire winter support

- The Aldershire Winter Resilience Plan funds free home insulation top-ups for low-income owner-occupiers.
- Aldershire Library opens as a Warm Space every weekday 9am-7pm and Saturday 9am-5pm from 1 November to 31 March; free hot drinks and a quiet space to read or use a public PC.
- Civic Centre operates Warm Space Wednesdays 12pm-3pm with a free hot meal.
- Aldershire Welfare Assistance issues fuel vouchers for residents in fuel poverty crisis (see separate document).
