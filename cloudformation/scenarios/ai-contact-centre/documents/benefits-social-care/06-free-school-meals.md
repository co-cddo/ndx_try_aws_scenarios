# Free school meals

Aldershire administers free school meals (FSM) for pupils whose parents receive a qualifying benefit, under section 512ZB of the Education Act 1996. FSM is separate from Universal Infant Free School Meals (UIFSM), which is paid automatically to all pupils in Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 regardless of income.

## Eligibility

A child qualifies for FSM if a parent or carer receives one of:

- Universal Credit with annual net earned income under £7,400 (after tax, before benefits).
- Income Support.
- Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance.
- Income-related Employment and Support Allowance.
- Pension Credit Guarantee Credit.
- Child Tax Credit (without Working Tax Credit) with income under £16,190.
- The four-week run-on of Working Tax Credit.
- Support under Part VI of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.

Children with No Recourse to Public Funds (NRPF) may qualify if household income is below £22,700 (£31,200 in London) and the family is supported by Aldershire under section 17 Children Act 1989.

## How to apply

- Online check: aldershire.gov.uk/fsm
- Phone: 01432 555 220 (school admissions team).
- The Eligibility Checking Service runs automatically; a decision is given on screen.

## Evidence

The Eligibility Checking Service confirms entitlement directly with HMRC and DWP. You only need to provide:

- National Insurance number of the parent claiming.
- Date of birth of the parent claiming.
- Child's name, date of birth, and school.

If the automated check fails, send in:

- A recent benefit award letter.
- A copy of your Universal Credit award statement showing earnings for the most recent assessment period.

## Once awarded

The school is notified within 3 working days. The child receives a free meal each school day until the end of the protected period (until age 16 once awarded between June 2022 and the end of the UC managed migration). The school also receives the Pupil Premium grant.

## Free breakfast

Aldershire's local partnership with Magic Breakfast offers free bagels and porridge at 14 Aldershire primary schools. Ask the school directly.
