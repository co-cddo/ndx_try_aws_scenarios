# Drain and gully cleansing

## What we maintain

Aldershire maintains around 41,000 highway drainage gullies (the rectangular grates at the kerb), 350 km of road-side ditches, 4,800 m of culverted watercourses, and around 700 highway soakaways. We do not maintain:

- Foul sewers (Aldermarket Water, 0800 783 4444)
- Private drains and soakaways inside property boundaries (your responsibility)
- Drainage on motorways and trunk roads (National Highways)
- Riparian (riverside) drainage on private land (the landowner)

## Annual cleansing schedule

Each gully on Aldershire's network is on a published cleansing cycle:

| Asset class | Cycle |
| ----------- | ----- |
| Strategic route gullies (A roads, bus routes) | Twice a year |
| Distributor and town-centre gullies | Once a year |
| Residential road gullies | Every 18 months |
| Rural lane gullies | Every 24 months |
| High-flood-risk gullies (named in the surface water management plan) | Quarterly |

Cleansing is delivered by a mechanical jet-vac tanker. The schedule is published on the Council's open data portal at data.aldershire.gov.uk/highways. Crews log each gully visit and any blockage type in the asset management system.

## Reporting a blocked drain

Report a blocked gully or flooded carriageway at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/drainage. Phone 01632 960 525 in working hours; out-of-hours flooding on a strategic route is handled by the 24/7 highways emergency line on 01632 960 512.

Provide:

- Road name and nearest property number, or a What3Words reference
- A photo if it is safe to take one
- Whether water is on the carriageway, partly blocking it, or causing pedestrian inconvenience
- Whether you have seen sewage (a foul-water issue, transferred to Aldermarket Water)

## Response targets

| Issue | Target |
| ----- | ------ |
| Flooding causing road closure or property damage | Make-safe within 2 hours, full clear within 24 hours |
| Standing water on a strategic route | Within 24 hours |
| Standing water on a residential road | Within 5 working days |
| Slow-draining gully (no standing water) | At next scheduled visit |

## Riparian responsibilities

If you own land next to a watercourse you have legal riparian responsibilities to keep it flowing. Council enforcement of section 25 of the Land Drainage Act 1991 is rare but possible where neglect causes flooding. The Drainage Engineer (01632 960 526) advises landowners.

## Sandbags

The Council operates a sandbag scheme at three locations during forecast severe-weather events: Aldermarket Depot, Vale Depot, and Greenway Yard. Listen to local radio or check www.aldershire.gov.uk/floods for activation.
