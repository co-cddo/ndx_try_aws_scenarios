# Personal budgets in EHC plans

## What a personal budget is

A personal budget is the amount of money the council and health partners would otherwise spend on meeting some or all of the provision in your child's Education, Health and Care plan. A personal budget gives you choice and flexibility about how that provision is delivered.

A personal budget is not extra money. It is the same funding that would have been used to commission services directly, allocated to you to spend against the agreed outcomes in the plan.

## What can be included

You can request a personal budget for elements of:

- Section F (special educational provision) - subject to agreement and to the school's role
- Section G (health provision arising from the plan) - through the NHS Aldershire Integrated Care Board
- Section H1 / H2 (social care provision) - through Aldershire Children's Social Care or Adult Social Care for those over 18

Common uses include private speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, music therapy, equine therapy, sensory equipment, and respite care. The provision must deliver outcomes set out in the EHC plan and must be evidenced.

## Three ways to take it

| Method | What happens |
| ------ | ------------ |
| Notional | The council still arranges the support but tells you how much is being spent so you can influence delivery |
| Direct payment | Money is paid into a separate account in your name; you commission and pay the provider; you provide receipts and an annual return |
| Third-party arrangement | The money is paid to a nominated person or organisation (e.g. a charity) who arranges the support on your behalf |

Direct payments require a written agreement, evidence of insurance for any worker you employ directly, and an enhanced DBS check on the worker. The council's Personal Budgets Team supports families through this process; phone 01632 960 484.

## Requesting a budget

You can request a personal budget at the draft plan stage (the 15-day comment window) or at any annual review. The council will respond within 28 days, setting out the amount, the conditions, and how to take it.

The council can refuse a request only on specific statutory grounds (for example, if delivering through a personal budget would cause a disproportionate impact on services for other children). A refusal must be in writing and explain the reasons.

## Reporting and review

Direct payments are reviewed at least annually as part of the EHC plan review. Any underspend at year end can be carried forward by agreement; significant overspends are recovered.

For more information, phone 01632 960 484 or email personal.budgets@aldershire.gov.uk.
