# Pothole reporting

## What counts as a pothole

A pothole is a defect in the road surface deeper than 40 mm and wider than 300 mm at its widest point. Smaller defects are recorded but do not meet the council's intervention threshold for emergency response.

We inspect each report against this threshold. A defect that does not meet the threshold today may be revisited if it deteriorates. We log the report regardless and use it to plan resurfacing programmes.

## How to report

Report a pothole at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/pothole. You will be asked for:

- The road name, the nearest house number or junction, and (where possible) the W3W three-word reference or postcode
- A photo, taken from a safe distance and with a clearly visible reference object such as a 1-pound coin or A4 sheet for scale
- Any further description (e.g. "in the nearside wheel track, just past the speed bump")

You can also phone the Highways Defects line on 01632 960 510 (Monday to Friday 08:00 to 18:00). Out of hours, dangerous defects on the strategic route network are handled by the 24/7 highways emergency line on 01632 960 512.

## Response targets

Aldershire categorises pothole repairs:

| Category | Target | Description |
| -------- | ------ | ----------- |
| Emergency | Within 2 hours | An immediate danger to road users (e.g. exposed reinforcement bar, very deep hole on a 50 mph+ road) |
| Priority 1 | Within 24 hours | Defect on a strategic route (A and B classified roads, bus routes) meeting the intervention threshold |
| Priority 2 | Within 7 days | Defect on a non-strategic road meeting the intervention threshold |
| Priority 3 | Within 28 days | Below threshold but recorded for monitoring |
| Programmed | At next planned inspection | Below threshold, no action |

Emergency repairs are made-safe at the scene (e.g. signed and coned) and a permanent repair scheduled. We do not aim to make every pothole permanent on first visit; first-time-fix runs at around 78 percent.

## Compensation claims

If a pothole has damaged your vehicle, you can submit a Highways Compensation Claim within 28 days. The council has a defence under section 58 of the Highways Act 1980 if our inspection regime has been carried out. We disclose inspection records on request. Phone Insurance Services on 01632 960 145.

## Tracking your report

After reporting you receive a 6-character tracking code (e.g. POT-AB12) by email or text. Track progress at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/track. The bot can also take a tracking number and read back the latest status.
