# Pay-and-display parking: zones, hours, and fees

## On-street pay-and-display

Aldermarket town centre and the principal Vale settlements (Highmere, Lowfold, Brook Green) operate paid on-street parking on shopping streets and around demand generators. Hours of control are typically 08:00 to 18:00 Monday to Saturday, with some zones extending to Sunday or 22:00 (notably around the Aldermarket Theatre district).

Look at the signage at the start of each zone for the exact hours. The bay marking (yellow lines on the kerb, white box markings) and the signs together set the rules.

## Tariffs

| Zone | Tariff |
| ---- | ------ |
| Aldermarket Core (zones 1 to 3) | First 30 mins free, then 1.50 GBP per hour, 4-hour maximum |
| Aldermarket Outer (zones 4 to 7) | 1.00 GBP per hour, 4-hour maximum |
| Vale settlements | 0.80 GBP per hour, 2-hour maximum |
| Hospital approach | 1.20 GBP per hour, no max |
| Theatre district (after 18:00) | Flat 4 GBP from 18:00 to 22:00 |

Sunday tariffs apply in the Aldermarket Core zone only; everywhere else is free on Sundays.

## How to pay

Three options at every machine:

1. Coins (1 GBP, 50p, 20p, 10p; some machines also take 2 GBP)
2. Contactless card (tap and hold for the duration you want)
3. RingGo phone app or 0345 222 8888 (location code printed on the machine)

Receipt by SMS (free of charge) is available with RingGo. Machines do not give change for coin-paid sessions.

## Off-street car parks

Aldershire operates 14 off-street car parks. Tariffs are different from on-street and are clearly displayed at each entrance. Notable:

- Town Hall Multi-Storey: 800 spaces, open 24/7. 2.20 GBP per hour, 18 GBP all day.
- Aldermarket Station East: pay-and-display, no maximum stay, 9.50 GBP all day.
- Vale Square: 80 spaces, 2 hours free with shopping receipt over 5 GBP from a participating shop.
- Greenway Country Park: 4 GBP per visit between Easter and the October half-term, free in winter.

## Season tickets

Off-street season tickets (week, month, quarter, annual) are available for the Town Hall Multi-Storey and Aldermarket Station East at www.aldershire.gov.uk/parking/seasons. The annual ticket pays back at around 110 working days of use.

## Free Sundays scheme

The "Free Sundays" promotion (set out in the 2026 to 2027 budget) makes off-street parking free on Sundays at six town-centre car parks. Town Hall Multi-Storey is included.
