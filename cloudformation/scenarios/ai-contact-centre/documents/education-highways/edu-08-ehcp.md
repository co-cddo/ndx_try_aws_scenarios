# Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCP)

## When to ask for an EHC needs assessment

You can ask for an Education, Health and Care needs assessment if your child or young person aged 0 to 25 may need more support than is normally available through SEN Support. The request can be made by:

- A parent or carer with parental responsibility
- A young person aged 16 to 25 themselves
- A school, college, early years setting, or health professional (with the family's knowledge)

The council must respond to a request within 6 weeks. Phone the SEND team on 01632 960 480 or apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/send/ehc-request.

## The 20-week timetable

The full process from request to issuing a final plan must take no more than 20 weeks, save in narrow exceptional circumstances:

| Week | Stage |
| ---- | ----- |
| 0 | Request received |
| 1 to 6 | Decision whether to assess; written decision issued |
| 6 to 16 | Statutory assessment; advice gathered from education, health, social care, parents, and the young person |
| 16 | Decision whether to issue a plan |
| 16 to 20 | Draft plan shared with parents (15-day window to comment, name a school, request a personal budget) |
| 20 | Final plan issued |

If the council decides not to assess or not to issue a plan, you have a right of appeal to the SEND Tribunal. Mediation through the council's commissioned independent mediator (Vale Mediation Services, 01632 960 489) must be considered before lodging an appeal, except for appeals about placement.

## The plan itself

An EHC plan has 12 sections (A to K, plus an appendix). Sections B (special educational needs), F (provision to meet those needs), and I (placement) are the legally enforceable core. You can ask for a personal budget to deliver some of the support in section F (see personal budgets guidance).

## Annual reviews

Every plan is reviewed annually (or every six months for children under 5). The school chairs the review meeting and circulates paperwork at least two weeks beforehand. Decisions to amend or cease a plan are taken by the SEND panel within 4 weeks of the review.

## Transition reviews

Year 5 and Year 9 reviews are key transition reviews. The Year 9 review must focus on preparation for adulthood, including employment, independent living, friends and community, and good health.

## Help and advocacy

SENDIASS (01632 960 482) gives independent advice. The IPSEA helpline and Contact charity also provide UK-wide guidance. The bot can book a SEND team callback within two working days.
