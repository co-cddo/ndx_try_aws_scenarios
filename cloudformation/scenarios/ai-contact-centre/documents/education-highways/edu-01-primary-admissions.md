# Primary school admissions: September entry

## Who applies through Aldershire

Aldershire District Council coordinates primary school admissions for all 47 maintained primary, infant, and junior schools in the borough and the Vale. Academies and voluntary-aided schools have their own admission authorities but applications still go through the council's coordinated scheme.

If your child was born between 1 September 2021 and 31 August 2022, they are due to start Reception in September 2026. You must apply even if you have an older child already at the school.

## Key dates for the 2026 intake

| Stage | Date |
| ----- | ---- |

| Appeal lodging deadline | 15 May 2026 |

Applications received after 15 January are treated as late and considered only after on-time applications, unless you can evidence exceptional circumstances (for example, a recent move into the area with proof of completion).

## How to apply

Apply online at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/admissions. You will need:

- Your child's full name, date of birth, and address
- Proof of address (council tax bill or tenancy agreement) if you have moved in the last 12 months
- A list of up to six preferences in order; you do not have to fill all six
- Sibling details if a brother or sister is already on roll at one of your preferred schools

Paper forms are available by post on request. Phone the School Admissions Team on 01632 960 410 (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:30) for a paper form or to ask about your application.

## Oversubscription criteria

Where a school is oversubscribed the council applies the published criteria in this order: looked-after children, exceptional medical or social need, siblings on roll at the time of admission, distance from home address measured in a straight line from your front door to the school's main pedestrian gate. Distance is calculated using the council's GIS mapping system; tied distances are decided by random allocation supervised by the Monitoring Officer.

## Children with an EHC plan

Children with an Education, Health and Care plan that names a school are admitted to that school regardless of oversubscription. If you are still in EHCP assessment at the time of application, apply through the normal coordinated scheme and tell the SEND team on 01632 960 480.
