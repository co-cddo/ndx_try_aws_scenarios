# Snow clearance priority routes

## When the snow plan kicks in

Aldershire's Severe Weather Plan is activated when forecast lying snow exceeds 30 mm or when the Met Office issues an Amber or Red warning for snow. The Director of Place is the senior responsible officer; the Highways Manager chairs the silver-cell tactical group.

Snow clearance complements salting (see gritting guidance). When snow is forecast we pre-treat with rock salt and a stickier brine on the strategic network the evening before, and run ploughs through the storm and after.

## Priority order for clearance

Snow clearance follows a strict priority. We clear lower priorities only when higher priorities are passable.

| Priority | Network | Description |
| -------- | ------- | ----------- |
| 1 | Primary route | A and B classified roads, bus routes, access to hospitals, fire, police, ambulance, schools |
| 2 | Secondary route | Connector roads in residential areas |
| 3 | Town-centre footways | Aldermarket High Street, Vale Square, hospital approaches |
| 4 | Estate roads | Residential cul-de-sacs (after multi-day events) |
| 5 | Footways and cycleways | Off-carriageway shared paths |

Most snow events are over within Priority 1 and 2. We aim for Primary routes to be passable with care within 12 hours of snowfall ending.

## Resilient communities

Aldershire encourages communities to look out for each other in severe weather. Voluntary "Snow Wardens" are trained to use snow shovels, salt bins, and to identify vulnerable neighbours. The scheme is run with Aldershire Voluntary Action; phone 01632 960 700.

The council provides public liability insurance for registered snow wardens working on adopted footways. There is no equivalent insurance for unregistered residents but you should not be put off helping a neighbour clear their drive; the often-cited fear of being sued for doing so has very limited basis in law.

## School snow closures

School closures are decided by each school, in consultation with the headteacher's risk assessment. A list is published at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/closures and announced on Aldermarket FM and Vale Radio. The council does not maintain a centralised closure decision; report individual school closures to the school directly.

## Bin collections

Bin collections continue in light snow but routes may be amended. In significant snow we publish a recovery plan on the day at www.aldershire.gov.uk/bins.

## Emergency shelter

In prolonged severe weather the council activates the Severe Weather Emergency Protocol (SWEP) for rough sleepers. The duty Housing Options officer is on 01632 960 999 out-of-hours.

For winter service queries phone 01632 960 530.
