# Salting and gritting routes: winter service

## When we grit

Aldershire's winter service runs from 1 October to 30 April each year. Decisions to treat the network are made by the on-call Winter Duty Officer, in consultation with the council's contracted weather forecaster (MetWeather Highways), based on:

- Forecast road surface temperature falling below 1 degree Celsius
- Forecast precipitation, fog, frost, or ice
- Existing salt residue on the network from previous treatments

A typical winter sees about 75 grit runs. Cold winters can mean 110 or more.

## Priority routes

Aldershire grits a Primary route network of 432 km, around 36 percent of the highway network. The Primary network covers:

- All A and B classified roads
- All bus routes carrying scheduled local bus services
- Access to schools, hospitals, fire stations, ambulance stations, police stations
- Access to railway stations and the bus station
- Designated emergency-services routes (the strategic resilience map)

Primary routes are normally treated as a precaution before forecast freezing. Treatment time from depot leave to last route end is around 2 hours 45 minutes.

A Secondary network of 110 km is treated on prolonged cold spells (more than 48 hours of continuous freezing) or after primary routes are clear. Residential streets are not gritted as routine.

## Pavements and town centres

Aldershire treats around 18 km of priority pavements, principally in the Aldermarket town centre, around the Aldermarket and Vale hospitals, and around principal shopping streets. This is hand-spread by a dedicated pavement crew.

## Salt bins

The council provides 280 yellow salt bins at locations published at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/salt-bins. Salt in these bins is for residents and community use on adopted highways and pavements only, not for use on private drives. We refill bins ahead of the winter season and during prolonged cold spells. To request a refill or report a damaged bin, phone 01632 960 530.

## Snow

For lying snow, ploughs are fitted to the gritting fleet. The same priority ordering applies. School snow days are decided by individual schools; check the school's website or phone the school in the morning.

In severe weather the council may activate the Emergency Plan and request voluntary support through 4x4 Response. The 24/7 highways line is 01632 960 512.

## Forecast and route maps

Route maps and the live status of the gritting fleet are at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/winter. The bot can read the current status (running tonight / not running tonight / running on demand) on request.
