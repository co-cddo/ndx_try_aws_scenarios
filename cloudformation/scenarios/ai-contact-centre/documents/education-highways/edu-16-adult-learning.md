# Adult learning Aldershire

## What we offer

Adult Learning Aldershire is the council's community learning service for adults aged 19 and over. We run around 320 courses a year from three centres (Aldermarket Adult Learning Centre, Vale Community Hub, and Greenway Annexe) and a network of community venues including libraries, leisure centres, and village halls.

Our prospectus is published in late August (autumn term) and early January (spring term). Browse and book at www.aldershire.gov.uk/adultlearning or phone 01632 960 460, weekdays 09:00 to 17:00.

## Free courses: who qualifies

Government-funded courses are free for adults who:

- Earn less than 25,000 GBP a year (gross), or
- Receive a means-tested benefit (Universal Credit, Income Support, Income-related ESA, JSA), or
- Are unemployed and looking for work
- Are aged 19 to 23 and do not yet hold a full Level 2 qualification (English, maths, vocational at Level 2)
- Are aged 19 or over and do not yet hold a full Level 3 qualification (for selected sector courses)

Bring proof of eligibility to enrolment: a benefit letter, payslips, or HMRC tax return.

## Tutors

We employ around 70 sessional tutors, all qualified to at least Level 4 in adult education or equivalent, with subject expertise and an enhanced DBS check. Tutors are recruited twice a year. If you would like to teach with us, phone 01632 960 466 for the tutor pack.

## Accreditation

Most vocational courses lead to a regulated qualification (City and Guilds, NCFE, Pearson). Functional Skills are awarded by Open Awards. Personal development courses are not accredited but issue a certificate of attendance.

## Bursaries and crèche

A small bursary fund covers travel and crèche fees for parents on benefits. Apply at enrolment. Crèche places at Aldermarket Adult Learning Centre are limited; book early.
