# Streetlight outage reporting

## What we maintain

Aldershire is responsible for around 22,400 streetlights, 1,950 illuminated bollards, and 540 illuminated traffic signs across the borough. We do not maintain lights on private estates (these belong to the management company), on motorway and trunk-road slip roads (National Highways), or on rail and canal property (Network Rail or Canal and River Trust).

Each council-maintained lamp column carries a unique 6- or 7-digit asset number on a plate near eye level. Quoting the asset number speeds up your report significantly.

## How to report

Report a fault at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/streetlight or phone the Streetlighting Help Line on 01632 960 515 (Monday to Friday 08:30 to 17:00). Outside these hours the lighting maintenance contractor (Aldermarket Lighting Services) takes urgent calls on 01632 960 516.

You will be asked for:

- The asset number on the column, where visible, or the road and nearest house number
- The fault type (out, flickering, on during the day, leaning, damaged door, exposed wires)
- Whether the area is dark and feels unsafe (which influences priority)

## Response targets

| Fault | Target |
| ----- | ------ |
| Exposed live cabling, leaning column, hit-by-vehicle | Make-safe within 2 hours |
| Single light out | Within 5 working days |
| Group failure (3 or more in a row) | Within 2 working days |
| Lights on during the day | Within 10 working days (low priority) |
| Damaged or open access door | Make-safe within 24 hours, repair within 5 working days |

Where a fault cannot be repaired within target because of cable supply issues, our contractor must notify the council and a temporary supply or replacement column is arranged.

## Energy and dimming policy

Aldershire's streetlights are LED. The dimming profile (where used) is published at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/lighting-policy. On most residential roads lights dim by 50 percent between 00:00 and 05:30; major roads, traffic signal junctions, and town centre areas remain at full output.

The council does not switch off streetlights overnight on any adopted road, in line with our 2022 lighting strategy.

## Christmas lights

Decorative Christmas lights are not part of the streetlighting service. They are installed in November and removed in early January by the Town Centre Management Team (01632 960 240). Faults during the season are reported to that team.

## Hit-by-vehicle damage

If you witness a vehicle hitting a column, please record the registration if safe to do so and report on 01632 960 515. The council recovers repair costs from the driver's insurer.
