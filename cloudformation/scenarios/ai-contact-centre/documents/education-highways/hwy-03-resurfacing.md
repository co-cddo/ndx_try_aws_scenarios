# Road resurfacing programme and consultation

## How we plan resurfacing

Aldershire publishes an annual Highway Maintenance Programme each March covering planned works for the coming financial year (April to March) and indicative schemes for the following two years.

The programme is built from:

- The Council's Pavement Management System (PMS), which scores every section of carriageway from condition surveys carried out twice a year
- Pothole and defect data from the past 24 months
- Bus operator reports on damaged surfaces
- Skid resistance data on the strategic network
- Public reports and councillor casework

We use national TRACS and SCANNER condition data on classified A and B roads, and our own walked condition survey on minor roads.

## What "resurfacing" means

The programme covers a mix of treatments:

| Treatment | When used | Typical lane closure |
| --------- | --------- | -------------------- |
| Inlay (mill and pave) | Mid-life carriageways with rutting and cracking | 1 to 3 days |
| Surface dressing | Sealing surface to extend life by 5-10 years | A few hours, single direction |
| Micro-asphalt | Cosmetic and skid resistance refresh | Half a day |
| Reconstruction | Total failure (rare) | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Patching | Targeted defect repair | A few hours |

Surface dressing schemes (chip seal) are typically delivered between May and August in dry weather; you may notice loose chippings for a week after which a sweeper visit clears.

## Consultation

For schemes affecting traffic movement (closures, parking suspensions, bus diversions), consultation runs for 21 days at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/consultations. For routine resurfacing of existing alignment, we notify residents and businesses by leaflet at least 10 working days before the works.

Letters include:

- Dates of works (with weather caveat)
- Working hours (07:30 to 18:00 weekdays unless emergency)
- Parking suspensions and where to park instead
- Bin collection arrangements
- Emergency contact for the works (24-hour)
