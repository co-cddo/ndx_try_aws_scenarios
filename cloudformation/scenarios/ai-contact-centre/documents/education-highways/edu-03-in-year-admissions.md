# In-year admissions: moving home or arriving from abroad

## When in-year admissions apply

If your child needs a school place outside the September coordinated round (for example because you have just moved into Aldershire, your child is returning from overseas, or you wish to change schools), apply through the in-year admissions process at any point in the school year.

You should still complete an application even if a school has told you it has spaces. The council allocates places to ensure all applications are treated consistently against published criteria.

## How to apply

Apply online at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/in-year. You can list up to three preferred schools. You will need:

- Proof of the child's address in Aldershire (recent utility bill, tenancy agreement, or completion statement). If you have not yet moved you can submit the application but a place will not be confirmed until residence is evidenced.
- The child's most recent school report or attendance record (if previously in education in the UK)
- Passport or birth certificate

Paper forms are accepted by post or by handing in at the Town Hall reception.

## How long it takes

Aldershire commits to making a decision within 15 school days of receiving a complete application. In practice most decisions land in 5 to 10 school days. Schools must admit a child by the start of the next school week once an offer has been accepted; for vulnerable groups (looked-after children, Children Missing Education) the council can direct a school to admit within 10 school days under the Fair Access Protocol.

## If no school in your preference list has a place

The council will offer a place at the nearest school with a vacancy. You retain the right to appeal against a refusal at any preferred school (see school appeals guidance). You may also join the waiting list for any school that turned you down; waiting list ranking follows the school's published oversubscription criteria.

## Children arriving from overseas

There is no charge to apply. Children of refugees, asylum seekers, and those with leave to remain are admitted on the same basis as any other Aldershire resident. The Ethnic Minority Achievement Service (01632 960 425) can support schools and families with English-as-an-additional-language assessments and induction.

## Help

Phone the In-Year Admissions Team on 01632 960 412, Monday to Friday 09:00 to 16:30. Out-of-hours queries are not handled by the AI for in-year cases involving safeguarding; those are routed to the Children's Services duty line on 01632 960 999.
