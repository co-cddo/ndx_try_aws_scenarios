# ESOL classes for non-native English speakers

## What ESOL is

ESOL stands for English for Speakers of Other Languages. The council's ESOL programme helps adults who do not have English as their first language to develop the speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills they need to live, work, and engage with their community in Aldershire.

Classes run at three levels: Pre-Entry / Entry 1 (beginner) up to Level 2 (advanced), with most demand at Entry 1, Entry 2, and Entry 3.

## Who can join

You can join an Aldershire ESOL class if you are:

- Aged 19 or over (16 and 17 with referral)
- Living in Aldershire (any postcode AL1 to AL8)
- Not in full-time school education
- Able to commit to two or three sessions a week

Most learners are funded by the Adult Education Budget, which means free tuition if you are unemployed, on a means-tested benefit, or earning under the threshold (currently 25,000 GBP gross). New arrivals with a UK residence permit, settled status, or refugee status normally qualify; some immigration statuses do not. Bring documents to enrolment and we will advise.

## Where and when

| Centre | Levels available | Times |
| ------ | ---------------- | ----- |
| Aldermarket Adult Learning Centre | Pre-Entry to Level 2 | Mornings, afternoons, evenings |
| Vale Community Hub | Entry 1 and Entry 2 | Mornings only |
| Greenway Annexe | Entry 2 and Entry 3 | Evenings (Tuesday and Thursday) |
| Vale library, Saturday | ESOL with Childcare for parents of school-age children | Saturday mornings, term-time |

The ESOL with Childcare programme on Saturday mornings includes a free crèche for under-5s and a parallel children's reading club for 5- to 11-year-olds, supported by Aldershire Library Service.

## How to enrol

Enrolment days are held twice a year (early September and early January). Walk in to any of the three centres on the published day with:

- Photo ID (passport, BRP, or driving licence)
- Proof of address in Aldershire (utility bill, tenancy)
- Proof of immigration status (if any)
- Proof of any benefits you receive

You will take a short placement assessment with a tutor (about 30 minutes) and be allocated to a class.

If you cannot attend an enrolment day, phone the ESOL Coordinator on 01632 960 462 to book a one-to-one assessment.

## Beyond ESOL

Many learners progress from ESOL into vocational training, work, or higher education. The ESOL team works with the council's employment support service (Aldershire Works) and Aldermarket Job Centre to support routes into employment.

For interpreting at a council meeting or appointment, phone 01632 960 200 and ask for the language line; this is a separate service from ESOL learning.
