# Free school meals: eligibility and how to apply

## Who qualifies

Your child can claim free school meals if you receive any of the following:

- Income Support
- Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance
- Income-related Employment and Support Allowance
- Support under Part VI of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999
- The guarantee element of Pension Credit
- Child Tax Credit (provided you are not entitled to Working Tax Credit and have an annual gross income of no more than 16,190 GBP)
- Working Tax Credit run-on
- Universal Credit, where your household earned income is no more than 7,400 GBP a year (after tax and not counting any benefits you get)

Children in Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 in maintained schools and academies receive Universal Infant Free School Meals regardless of income; you should still apply for the means-tested benefit because it unlocks Pupil Premium funding for your child's school.

## How to apply

Apply online at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/fsm. The form takes about 10 minutes. You will need your National Insurance number or National Asylum Seeker Service reference and your child's school name.

The council runs an automated check against HMRC and DWP records. Most applications are decided within 24 hours. If the automated check fails you may be asked to provide payslips or a benefits award letter.

## Once approved

Approval is sent to the school, which arranges the lunch with no further action needed by you. The benefit lasts for the entire phase of education your child is in (primary or secondary) without the need to reapply, even if your circumstances change.

If your child is a packed-lunch family at a school that requires pre-payment for hot meals, ask the school office about how the benefit is applied; arrangements vary by school.

## Holiday provision

The Holiday Activities and Food (HAF) programme provides free activities and a meal during Easter, summer, and Christmas school holidays for children who have been in receipt of benefits-related FSM at any point in the previous 12 months. Booking opens about three weeks before each holiday at www.aldershire.gov.uk/haf.
