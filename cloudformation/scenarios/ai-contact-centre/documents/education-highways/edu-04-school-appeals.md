# School admission appeals

## Your right to appeal

If your child is refused a place at a school you named on your application, you have a statutory right of appeal to an Independent Appeal Panel. The panel is independent of the council and the school and is constituted under the School Admissions Appeals Code.

You can appeal even if you have accepted a place at another school. Lodging an appeal does not affect your existing offer.

## How to lodge an appeal

Lodge your appeal within 20 school days of the refusal letter. For the September Reception and Year 7 rounds the deadlines are:

| Round | Refusal letter date | Appeal deadline |
| ----- | ------------------- | --------------- |
| Reception 2026 | 16 April 2026 | 15 May 2026 |
| Year 7 2026 | 1 March 2026 | 31 March 2026 |

Appeal forms are at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/appeals or by post on request from the Democratic Services Team. You will be asked to set out your case in writing. You can attach evidence (medical letters, social worker reports, supporting statements). The form must be signed by a parent or person with parental responsibility.

## What happens at the hearing

Hearings are arranged within 40 school days of the deadline for on-time appeals (or within 30 school days of the appeal being lodged, for in-year appeals). They are normally held in person at the Town Hall but can be conducted by video for accessibility reasons.

The panel of three independent members hears the school's case first (why admitting another child would prejudice education at the school), then your case (why your child should be admitted). You can bring a friend, relative, or representative; legal representation is allowed but not usually necessary.

The panel applies a two-stage test:

1. Is the school's prejudice case proven? If not, the appeal is upheld.
2. If prejudice is proven, do your reasons for wanting the school outweigh that prejudice?

Decisions are sent in writing within 5 school days of the hearing.

## Infant class size appeals

For Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 classes, statutory infant class size limits apply (30 children per qualified teacher). Appeals can succeed only if the panel finds the admission decision was unreasonable, the admission criteria were misapplied, or admitting your child would not breach the statutory limit. This is a higher bar than for other year groups.
