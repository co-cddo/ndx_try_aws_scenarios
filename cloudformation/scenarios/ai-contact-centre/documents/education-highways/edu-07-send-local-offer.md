# SEND code of practice and the Aldershire Local Offer

## What the Local Offer is

The Aldershire Local Offer sets out the support available to children and young people aged 0 to 25 with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND), and to their families. It is published at www.aldershire.gov.uk/sendlocaloffer and is co-produced with the Aldershire Parent Carer Forum (APCF).

The Local Offer is a statutory duty under the Children and Families Act 2014 and the SEND Code of Practice (2015). It covers education, health, social care, leisure, transition to adulthood, and the routes for asking for an Education, Health and Care needs assessment.

## How children's needs are met

The Code of Practice sets out a graduated approach: assess, plan, do, review. Most children with SEND have their needs met through SEN Support arranged by their school's Special Educational Needs Coordinator (SENCO). Schools are expected to use up to 6,000 GBP of their notional SEN budget per child before requesting top-up funding from the council.

Where SEN Support is not enough to enable a child to make progress, parents, schools, or young people aged 16 to 25 themselves can request an EHC needs assessment (see EHCP guidance).

## Areas of need

The Code recognises four broad areas of need:

- Communication and interaction (including autism, speech and language)
- Cognition and learning (including specific learning difficulties such as dyslexia, and moderate, severe, or profound learning difficulties)
- Social, emotional and mental health
- Sensory and physical (including hearing and visual impairment)

A child may have needs in more than one area; assessments are holistic.

## Specialist provision in Aldershire

Aldershire commissions resourced provision and special school places at:

- Vale Manor Special School (4 to 19, generic learning difficulties)
- St Christopher's Specialist School (4 to 16, autism and severe communication needs)
- Aldermarket Sensory Resource Base (mainstream secondary with hearing-impaired unit)
- Greenway Centre (alternative provision for permanently excluded pupils)

Out-of-county placements are commissioned where local provision cannot meet need; the SEND Placements Panel meets fortnightly to review proposals.

## Information, advice and support

The SEND Information, Advice and Support Service (SENDIASS) is independent of the council and offers free impartial advice on 01632 960 482, weekdays 09:00 to 16:00. The Aldershire Parent Carer Forum runs coffee mornings at the Town Hall on the second Tuesday of each month.
