# Parking penalty notices: appeal process

## What you have received

A Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) is a civil parking ticket issued under the Traffic Management Act 2004. It is not a criminal fine. PCNs are typically attached to your windscreen by a Civil Enforcement Officer, sent by post if a vehicle is captured by CCTV, or sent to the registered keeper if driven away before a CEO can attach the notice.

The PCN states:

- The contravention code (e.g. 12 - parked in a residents-only bay)
- The amount payable
- The discount window (50 percent off if paid within 14 days; reduced further by 25 percent on some lower-band offences)
- Your right to make representations or formal challenge

## Two-stage challenge process

Aldershire follows the statutory two-stage process:

1. Informal challenge: any time before a Notice to Owner (NTO) is issued. Submit your reasons by email or online at www.aldershire.gov.uk/parking/challenge. We aim to respond within 28 days. If we accept your challenge, the PCN is cancelled. If we reject, the discount window is paused while we consider your challenge and reopened on rejection.

2. Formal representation: after a Notice to Owner is issued (typically 28 to 56 days after the PCN if unpaid). Submit on the form attached to the NTO. Statutory grounds are listed (e.g. you were not the owner, the contravention did not occur, the order was invalid, the penalty exceeds that applicable in the circumstances of the case).

If we reject your formal representation we issue a Notice of Rejection. You then have 28 days to appeal to the Traffic Penalty Tribunal (an independent tribunal at www.trafficpenaltytribunal.gov.uk). Tribunal appeals are free.

## Common grounds we accept

We accept evidence-based challenges. Common reasons your challenge might succeed:

- The signs or markings were inadequate, missing, or contradictory
- A valid permit was on display but obscured (we will ask for permit number to verify)
- Loading or unloading evidence (if loading was permitted at the location)
- A medical emergency, with documentary evidence
- A valid Blue Badge was displayed but not visible to the CEO

We do not generally accept:

- "I was only there a few minutes"
- "There was no warden the last time"
- "I did not see the sign" (unless the sign was genuinely obscured)
- "Other cars were parked the same"

## Discount calculator

Pay within 14 days for the 50 percent discount. Pay between 15 and 28 days at full rate. After day 28, if a Notice to Owner is issued and unpaid, the charge increases by 50 percent (the surcharge). Beyond that, the case can be registered with the County Court and bailiffs instructed.
