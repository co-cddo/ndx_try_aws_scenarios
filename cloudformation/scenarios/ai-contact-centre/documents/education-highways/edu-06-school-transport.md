# Free school transport

## When transport is free

Aldershire provides free school transport where the home-to-school distance exceeds the statutory walking distance and the child attends their nearest school or has been directed there by the council:

| Child's age | Walking distance threshold |
| ----------- | -------------------------- |
| Under 8 | More than 2 miles |
| 8 and over | More than 3 miles |

Distance is measured using the shortest available walking route between your home address and the school gate, including public footpaths and rights of way (see footpaths guidance). Aldershire publishes the route used for each address; you can request a recalculation if you believe a route is not safe in normal weather and daylight.

## Low-income extended entitlement

Children from low-income families (in receipt of free school meals or whose parent receives the maximum Working Tax Credit) have extended entitlement:

- Aged 8 to 11: free transport if the nearest school is more than 2 miles
- Aged 11 to 16: free transport to one of the nearest three suitable schools, where they are between 2 and 6 miles from home, or to the nearest school by religion or belief if 2 to 15 miles

## How to apply

Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/transport once your child has a confirmed school place. You will need the school name, the start date, and proof of address. Decisions are issued within 15 school days. Approved children are sent a transport pass before the start of term.

## Routes and providers

Aldershire contracts a fleet of school buses operated by Vale Coaches (rural routes) and Aldermarket Travel (urban). Some children are assigned a seat on a public service bus; your transport pass acts as the ticket. A handful of children with severe disability are transported by taxi or minibus with passenger assistant; these arrangements are made through the SEND transport team on 01632 960 488.

## Loss of pass

Replacement transport passes cost 12 GBP. Free replacements are issued in the case of theft (with a crime reference), house fire, or transfer between siblings on free routes.

## Refusing or appealing transport

If you choose to send your child to a school further away than your nearest, you must arrange transport yourself. If your application is refused you can appeal in writing within 20 school days. Appeals go to a panel chaired by the Director of Children's Services. Phone 01632 960 415 for forms.
