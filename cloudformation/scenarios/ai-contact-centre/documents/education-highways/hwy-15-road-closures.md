# Road closures and diversions

## Reasons we close roads

Aldershire closes roads under several powers:

- Section 14 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (Temporary Traffic Regulation Order, or TTRO): for works, events, structural concerns
- Section 21 of the Town Police Clauses Act 1847: for community events, processions
- Emergency closure powers: for police-led operations, gas leaks, fires, floods

Most planned closures are for utility works (gas, water, fibre, electric), council resurfacing, or large events.

## Notice and consultation

Planned closures are advertised:

- 14 days minimum notice on lampposts and at the Town Hall noticeboard
- Published at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/closures (live database)
- Listed in www.one.network (the national roadworks database used by satnav providers)
- Notified to bus operators (Aldermarket Travel, Vale Coaches), emergency services, and refuse crews
- Letters to frontagers

Some closures qualify for emergency works without notice (e.g. burst water main). Those are signed at the scene and updated on one.network as soon as practicable.

## Diversions

Every closure has a published signed diversion suitable for all classes of vehicle that would normally use the road. Diversions follow these principles:

- HGV-suitable: avoid weight or height restrictions
- Bus-friendly where possible (with input from operators)
- Properly signed at every junction along the route
- Posted on www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways with the closure record

Where a diversion affects access to a property, frontage access is maintained where possible (you may be asked to park elsewhere or to wait for a brief escort).

## Bus stops and refuse

Bus stops within a closure are suspended and a temporary stop-pole is placed at a nearby diversion location. Refuse collections continue but bins may need to be presented at the closest accessible point; we publish a temporary collection note where this applies.

## Events and street parties

A community can apply for a free road closure for a street party at www.aldershire.gov.uk/streetparty. Submit at least 6 weeks before the date. The street must be a residential street with no through bus or emergency-services route, and the closure must be supported by your immediate neighbours.

## Reporting an unmarked or unsafe closure

If you have come across a closure that is not signed, the diversion is unclear, or signs are damaged, phone 01632 960 580 (24-hour). We will ask for the road, the reason for closure if known, and a description of the signing.

## Appeals

There is no formal appeal against a TTRO; we publish each Order with the statutory grounds and reasoning. Concerns about a particular Order can be raised with the Traffic Engineering Team on 01632 960 545.
