# Parking permits: residents, visitor and business

## Controlled Parking Zones in Aldershire

Aldershire operates 17 Controlled Parking Zones (CPZs) across the borough, mostly in the Aldermarket town centre and around major demand generators (railway station, hospital, college). Each zone has its own hours of control and permit eligibility; check the signs on entry to each zone or the published map at www.aldershire.gov.uk/parking/zones.

## Residents permits

Eligibility:

- Your address is within the published zone boundary
- You are listed as the registered keeper of the vehicle on your V5C, or a long-term hirer with documentation
- You have proof of address less than 3 months old

Pricing (2025 to 2026):

| Permit | Annual | 6-month | Monthly |
| ------ | ------ | ------- | ------- |
| First vehicle | 75 GBP | 42 GBP | 9 GBP |
| Second vehicle | 145 GBP | 80 GBP | 16 GBP |
| Third vehicle | 285 GBP | 155 GBP | 30 GBP |
| Low-emission vehicle (under 100 g/km CO2 or fully electric) | 50 percent of standard | 50 percent of standard | 50 percent of standard |

Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/parking/permits. Permits are virtual (linked to your number plate) and valid from the start date you choose. There is no physical sticker.

## Visitor permits

Visitor permits cover guests, tradespeople, and family carers. Choose between:

- Daily scratchcards (sold in books of 10 for 25 GBP), valid for one calendar day
- Online visitor sessions (paid by hour or day, set up via the resident's permit account)

Households are limited to 100 visitor day-permits a year (50 in zones with the highest pressure, including Zones A and B around the railway station). Carers can apply for an unlimited carer permit on top of the household quota; phone 01632 960 565 with proof from the carer's employer or a benefits letter.

## Business permits

Limited business permits are available in some zones for trades and contractors actively working on a property in the zone. They are not transferable between zones and are issued for fixed periods (1 day to 1 month). Apply with the address of the work, the duration, and proof of trade.

## Disabled badge holders

Disabled Blue Badge holders may park free in any on-street pay-and-display bay in Aldershire and in any disabled bay, displaying a valid Blue Badge. Some private car parks (hospital, supermarkets) operate their own rules.

## Refunds and cancellations

Permits can be cancelled and refunded pro-rata (less a 7 GBP admin fee) if you sell the vehicle or move out of the zone. Apply within 28 days of the change.

## Help

Phone the Permit Office on 01632 960 565, weekdays 09:00 to 17:00. The bot can take a permit application reference number and tell you the status.
