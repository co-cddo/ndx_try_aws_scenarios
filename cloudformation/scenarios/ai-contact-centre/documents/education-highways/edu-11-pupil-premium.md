# Pupil Premium Grant

## What it is

The Pupil Premium Grant is additional government funding paid to schools to raise the attainment of disadvantaged pupils and close the gap with their peers. It is paid to the school, not to families, but parents and carers play a key role in unlocking it by registering for free school meals (FSM).

In 2025 to 2026 the per-pupil rates were:

| Pupil group | Annual rate |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Reception to Year 6 (FSM ever-6) | 1,480 GBP |
| Year 7 to Year 11 (FSM ever-6) | 1,050 GBP |
| Looked-after children (Pupil Premium Plus) | 2,570 GBP |
| Service children (parent in HM Forces) | 350 GBP |

"FSM ever-6" means a pupil who has been eligible for benefits-based free school meals at any point in the previous six years.

## Why registering for FSM matters

Universal Infant Free School Meals (Reception, Year 1, Year 2) do not by themselves trigger Pupil Premium funding. Even if your child is too young to need a means-tested entitlement, registering for FSM if you qualify on income grounds means the school receives Pupil Premium for the next six years.

If your child is in any state-funded school in Aldershire, register at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/fsm or phone 01632 960 350.

## How schools use the funding

Schools have flexibility to use Pupil Premium in any way they believe will close the attainment gap. The council expects schools to publish an annual Pupil Premium strategy on their websites covering:

- An assessment of disadvantaged pupils' needs
- The intended outcomes for the year
- Activities, costs, and the evidence base (typically referencing the Education Endowment Foundation toolkit)
- A review of last year's strategy

Common uses in Aldershire schools include small-group tuition, family liaison workers, pastoral support, breakfast and homework clubs, and subsidised trips. Pupil Premium Plus for looked-after children is managed by Aldershire's Virtual School Head, Tessa Bramley, on 01632 960 491.

## National Tutoring Programme

The National Tutoring Programme allowed schools to deliver subsidised one-to-one and small-group tuition to disadvantaged pupils. Aldershire continues to broker tutoring for looked-after children through the Virtual School after the central programme ended.

## Service Pupil Premium

Children of forces personnel attract a Service Premium for emotional and pastoral support arising from deployment and frequent moves. Tell your school if a parent is currently or has been serving in HM Forces in the last six years; they will register the child to draw the funding.

For school-level questions, contact the school directly. For policy questions, the council's Education Standards Team is on 01632 960 470.
