# Sixth form and post-16 transport

## What the council provides

Aldershire is not under the same statutory duty for transport for 16- to 19-year-olds as for compulsory-age children, but we publish an annual Post-16 Transport Policy Statement and offer subsidised travel passes to qualifying young people in further education or sixth form.

The 2025 to 2026 statement is at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/post16-transport. It is updated each May.

## Subsidised pass

The Aldershire Post-16 Pass costs 380 GBP a year (2025 to 2026 rate) and offers unlimited travel between home and a named place of study, on Vale Coaches and Aldermarket Travel routes, term-time only. It is sold to:

- Year 12 and Year 13 students at any Aldershire sixth form or further education college
- Apprentices aged 16 to 18 attending day-release at a council-approved provider

The pass is delivered as a smartcard. Replacement cards cost 12 GBP.

## Free passes for low-income students

A free pass is issued where:

- The student or family is in receipt of any qualifying benefit listed for free school meals (see FSM guidance), or
- The student is care-experienced (in care or formerly in care) up to age 25, or
- The student has an EHC plan and travels to provision named in the plan

Apply through the SEND transport team for EHCP cases on 01632 960 488; for low-income cases, apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/schools/post16-transport.

## Bursary and 16 to 19 bursary fund

The 16 to 19 Bursary Fund is administered by your sixth form or college, not by the council, and may help with the cost of transport, equipment, books, and trips. Two main types: vulnerable bursary (up to 1,200 GBP a year for specific groups) and discretionary bursary. Speak to your school or college's bursary team.

## Apprentices

Apprentices over 18 are not eligible for the council's subsidised pass. Some employers reimburse travel; ask your apprenticeship coordinator. Aldershire Job Centre Plus runs an apprenticeship travel grant for those leaving Universal Credit; phone 0800 169 0190.

## Independent travel training

Aldershire's SEND team offers independent travel training to young people with EHC plans who could safely travel by bus with support. The eight-week programme reduces reliance on commissioned taxi transport and builds independence. Referral is through the EHCP annual review or directly to 01632 960 487.
