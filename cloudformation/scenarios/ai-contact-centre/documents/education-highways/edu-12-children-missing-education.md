# Children Missing Education (CME)

## What CME means

Children Missing Education are children of compulsory school age (5 to 16) who are not on a school roll and not receiving suitable education otherwise (for example through registered home education or alternative provision).

Aldershire has a statutory duty under section 436A of the Education Act 1996 to identify, as far as possible, all children of compulsory school age in our area who are not receiving a suitable education and to make arrangements for them to do so.

## How children come to our attention

The council's CME team receives referrals from:

- Schools removing a pupil from roll (every off-roll movement is checked against the published common transfer file destinations)
- Health visitors, GPs, and the NHS Aldershire safeguarding team
- Police and social care
- Members of the public, neighbours, and family
- Cross-border alerts from other local authorities

Schools have a legal duty to notify the council within 5 days when a pupil is added to or removed from the admission register, including reasons for removal. The council reconciles the school census with our school-place tracking system fortnightly.

## What we do

When a child is identified as potentially missing education, the CME caseworker will:

1. Check the National Pupil Database, neighbouring authorities, and the elective home education register
2. Visit or write to the family to establish current arrangements
3. If no education is in place, work with the family to identify a suitable school place using the In-Year Admissions Fair Access Protocol
4. If the family is electively home educating, transfer the case to the EHE adviser
5. Record outcomes in the council's CME case management system, retained for 25 years

Where a child cannot be located after reasonable enquiries, the case is escalated to the Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub (MASH) and may be referred to the police as a missing child.

## Off-rolling

Aldershire takes off-rolling (the practice of removing a pupil from the school roll without a formal exclusion or parental withdrawal in their best interest) seriously. Schools where off-rolling patterns emerge are challenged by the local authority School Improvement Adviser and patterns are reported to Ofsted.

## Reporting a concern

If you know a child of school age who is not in education, contact the CME team on 01632 960 423 (Monday to Friday 09:00 to 17:00). For urgent safeguarding concerns out of hours, phone 01632 960 999. The bot will route urgent CME concerns to a human caseworker without delay.
