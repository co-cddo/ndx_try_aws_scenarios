# Junior and secondary school admissions

## When to apply

Aldershire runs two coordinated admission rounds in addition to the Reception round:

- Junior transfer (Year 3 entry) for children moving from a separate infant school to a junior school in September. Apply between 1 November and 15 January in the school year before transfer.
- Secondary transfer (Year 7 entry) for children moving from primary to secondary school. Apply between 1 September and 31 October. National offer day is 1 March.

Most Aldershire children attend an all-through primary so the junior round affects only the Aldermarket north pyramid (Aldermarket Infant feeding into St Mark's Junior and Greenway Junior).

## Selective places: the 11+ test

Aldershire is a non-selective authority and operates no grammar schools. However, the neighbouring Mereborough partnership administers a selective test for two grammar schools whose catchment overlaps the Vale. If you want your child considered for a Mereborough grammar place you must:

1. Register for the test directly with Mereborough Schools Partnership by 30 June. There is no charge.
2. Take the test in early September of the year of application; results are sent to parents in mid-October so they can be used when ranking preferences on the secondary application.
3. List the grammar school as one of your preferences on your Aldershire common application form. Aldershire does not pass on registration to Mereborough automatically.

The test covers verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, English, and maths. Sample papers and the registration form are at www.mereborough.gov.uk/grammar-test.

## Faith and partner-priority schools

Two voluntary-aided secondary schools in Aldershire (St Bede's Catholic and Aldermarket CofE Academy) require a supplementary information form for faith-based criteria. Submit the SIF directly to the school by 31 October. The council's coordinated form is still required; the SIF only governs ranking within the school's faith criterion.

## Late applications and waiting lists

Applications received after the closing date are processed after on-time applications and offered only if places remain. Waiting lists are held until 31 December of the entry year for primary and Year 7 transfer; positions on the list are determined by the published criteria, not date of application.
