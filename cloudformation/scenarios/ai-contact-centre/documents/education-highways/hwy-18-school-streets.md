# School street schemes

## What a School Street is

A School Street is a temporary closure of a road outside a primary school during arrival and departure times, restricting through-traffic for a short period (typically 45 minutes morning and 45 minutes afternoon) on school days only. The aim is to:

- Improve air quality at the school gate
- Reduce congestion and pavement parking around the school
- Encourage walking, scooting, and cycling
- Improve road safety perception for the youngest users

Aldershire has 14 active School Streets at primary schools and is consulting on three more in 2026.

## How they operate

A School Street uses a Traffic Regulation Order made under section 1 or section 9 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984. Closure times are set on the Order; signs (one at each end of the closed length) show the days and times.

Three operating patterns are in use across Aldershire:

| Pattern | How it works |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Volunteer-staffed | Parent volunteers or school staff place removable barriers at the closure points at the published times |
| Council-staffed | A Civil Enforcement Officer attends each session |
| Camera-enforced | ANPR cameras monitor the closure; PCNs are issued to non-permitted vehicles |

Camera-enforced sites operate at three Aldershire schools: Greenway Primary, Vale Edge Junior, and St Mark's Junior.

## Permits and exceptions

Residents and businesses inside the closure (or with the closure as their only vehicular access) can apply for an exemption permit. Apply at www.aldershire.gov.uk/highways/school-streets. Permits are virtual (linked to your number plate) and free.

Other exceptions:

- Emergency vehicles
- Vehicles displaying a valid Blue Badge accessing the school
- Refuse vehicles, post deliveries, and other essential service vehicles
- Vehicles connected with school operations (deliveries, taxis under contract)

Through-traffic and parents dropping off who do not live in the closure are not exempt. Drop-off points are signed nearby (typically a 5- to 8-minute walk) and parents are encouraged to "park and stride".

## Hours of operation

Typical hours are:

- Morning: 08:15 to 09:00
- Afternoon: 14:50 to 15:35

Hours are tailored to each school's staggered start. Closures do not run on weekends, school holidays, training days, or polling days.

## Consultation

Each School Street is consulted on for 21 days before introduction. Comments are collected at www.aldershire.gov.uk/consultations and considered by the Cabinet Member for Highways before the TRO is sealed. Each scheme is reviewed after 12 months.

## Reporting and contact

To report a non-permit vehicle entering a School Street, where the scheme is not camera-enforced, contact the school directly. For policy queries phone the Active Travel Team on 01632 960 600.
