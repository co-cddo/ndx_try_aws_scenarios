# Moving home: closing balance and refunds

When you move home, council tax becomes payable on the new property from the day you move in, and ends on the previous property on the day you move out. We need to know within 21 days of either move.

## Telling us about a move

Use the move-home form at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/move. Complete one form per move (one for moving out, one for moving in if both are in Aldershire).

You will need:
- Your old council tax account reference (if known)
- The date you moved out and the date you moved in
- A forwarding address
- The name of the new occupier of your old property (if you know it) — this helps us close your account quickly
- Names of all adults moving with you

If you are moving from outside Aldershire, you only complete the "moving in" part.

## Closing balance

If you have paid more than you owe to your move date, we automatically issue a refund within 14 working days. Refunds are made direct to the account that paid (the direct debit account, where applicable). If you paid by card or cash, we will write to ask for bank details.

If you owe a balance for the period up to your move date, we issue a closing bill payable in 14 days. You can spread this over the rest of the financial year if needed; ring 01624 800200 to arrange.

## Forwarding address

We send the closing bill or refund letter to your forwarding address. Please give us a UK address where you can receive post for at least 6 months. Failed delivery is a common cause of refunds being delayed.

## Joint movers

If two or more adults were jointly liable, we issue the closing bill in joint names. Either party can pay it, and any refund is split equally unless you tell us otherwise.

## Tenants and landlords

A tenant remains liable up to the end-of-tenancy date as set out in the tenancy agreement, even if the tenant has moved out earlier. After that date, the landlord becomes liable until the next tenant moves in. Provide the tenancy end date when reporting your move.

For complex moves involving probate, divorce, or business assignment, ring 01624 800200, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
