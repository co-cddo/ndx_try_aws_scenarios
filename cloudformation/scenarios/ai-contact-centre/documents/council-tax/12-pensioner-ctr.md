# Pensioner council tax reduction

Pension-age applicants for council tax reduction are protected by national rules and follow a more generous scheme than working-age applicants. The pensioner scheme is set by central government and Aldershire District Council administers it locally.

## Who counts as pension age

You are treated as a pensioner for CTR if you (or your partner if you have one) have reached state pension age. From April 2026 state pension age is 66 years and 9 months and is rising to 67 by 2028. Mixed-age couples (one above and one below state pension age) are treated as working-age.

## Maximum reduction

Pensioner CTR can reduce your bill by up to 100%. There is no minimum payment requirement, unlike the working-age scheme. The scheme follows the rules in the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Prescribed Requirements) (England) Regulations 2012, as amended.

## Pension Credit gateway

If you receive Guarantee Pension Credit, you are likely to qualify for full (100%) CTR automatically. Pension Credit is available even if you have modest savings and is widely under-claimed. We strongly encourage all pensioners to check eligibility at https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit or by calling the Pension Service on 0800 99 1234.

If you receive only Savings Pension Credit, you may still qualify for partial CTR depending on your full income and capital position.

## Capital limits for pensioners

Pensioners have a more generous capital limit:
- Below £10,000: ignored entirely
- £10,000 to £16,000: tariff income of £1 per £500 above £10,000
- Above £16,000: no CTR (unless on Guarantee Pension Credit, in which case capital is ignored)

## How to apply

Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/ctr/pensioner or phone 01624 800215. Aldershire's Pensioner Welfare Officer offers home visits to housebound applicants; ring to arrange. We can also work with Age UK Aldershire (01624 800600) and Citizens Advice Aldershire (01624 800650) for supported claims.
