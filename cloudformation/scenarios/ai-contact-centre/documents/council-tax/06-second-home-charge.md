# Second home charge

A second home is a furnished property that is no one's sole or main residence. From 1 April 2025 Aldershire District Council charges 100% council tax on second homes with no discount, and an additional second-home premium applies from April 2026.

## What counts as a second home

A property is treated as a second home if it is substantially furnished and is not anyone's sole or main residence. Typical examples include:
- A furnished holiday cottage occupied only at weekends or in the summer
- A flat kept for occasional use when working away from home
- A property bought for a family member that is not yet occupied
- A furnished property awaiting sale that is not being lived in

## 2026-27 second home premium

From 1 April 2026, second homes pay an additional 100% premium on top of the standard charge (so a Band D second home pays £3,936 instead of £1,968 per year). This was approved by full council on 27 February 2026 following statutory consultation.

## Exceptions

The premium does not apply to:
- Properties being actively marketed for sale or let at a realistic price (12-month grace from when first marketed)
- Annexes used as part of the main home
- Job-related dwellings (for example, a tied agricultural cottage)
- Caravans on a designated holiday park (these are not dwellings for council tax)
- Properties undergoing major repair or structural alteration (up to 12 months)
- Inherited dwellings (12 months from grant of probate)

## How to register

If you have just bought or inherited a property that is not your main home, complete the second home registration form at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/secondhome. Include proof of your main residence elsewhere (utility bill, voter registration, or driving licence with your main address).

For questions about whether the premium applies in your circumstances, call 01624 800220, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
