# Appeals process: VOA and Valuation Tribunal

Council tax decisions can be challenged through a structured appeals process. Different decisions go to different bodies. This guide sets out which forum hears which type of appeal.

## Banding appeals: Valuation Office Agency (VOA)

If you disagree with your council tax band, your first step is a "proposal" to the VOA (see disputing your band guide). If the VOA refuses, the proposal is automatically referred to the Valuation Tribunal for England.

The Valuation Tribunal is independent of the VOA and the council. It can:
- Confirm the band
- Reduce the band
- Increase the band (rare; happens if the VOA puts a low value)

Hearings are usually held in person at regional centres (the closest to AL1 is in central London) or remotely by video conference. Most appeals are listed within 6 months. There is no fee.

## Liability and discount appeals: ADC then Valuation Tribunal

For decisions made by ADC (whether you are liable, whether a discount applies, the amount of CTR awarded), you must first ask ADC for a reconsideration. Write to: Council Tax Disputes, Aldershire District Council, Civic Centre, AL1 2RR, or email counciltax@aldershire.example.gov.uk within 2 months of the original decision.

If ADC's reconsideration does not resolve the matter (or we do not respond within 2 months), you have a further 2 months to appeal to the Valuation Tribunal at https://www.valuationtribunal.gov.uk.

## Hardship and discretionary decisions

Decisions on the discretionary hardship fund and discretionary section 13A reductions are not appealable to the Valuation Tribunal. The only formal challenge is judicial review in the High Court. We will, however, reconsider any decision on receipt of new evidence within 28 days.

## CTR appeals: ADC then Valuation Tribunal

CTR appeals are heard by the Valuation Tribunal under regulation 11 of the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Detail of Schemes) (England) Regulations 2012. The two-month rule applies as above.
