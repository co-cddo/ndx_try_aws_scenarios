# Property undergoing major repair

If your property is unoccupied because it is being repaired, structurally altered, or is uninhabitable, a discount or exemption may apply. The rules changed in 2013 and the available reductions are now shorter than they once were.

## Class A: Major repair works (now discretionary)

Class A exemption was abolished nationally in April 2013. Aldershire District Council has chosen to grant a 50% local discount for up to 12 months where a property is genuinely uninhabitable due to:
- Major structural repairs (subsidence, fire damage, flood)
- Extensive renovation requiring new electrics, plumbing or roofing
- Demolition and rebuild of significant elements
- Removal of damp-proof course or asbestos abatement

The 12-month period runs from the date the property became uninhabitable, regardless of whether work has yet started.

## Evidence required

Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/uninhabitable. Submit at least two of:
- Photographs showing the condition before and during works
- A surveyor's or structural engineer's report
- A builder's quote or contract showing the scope of work
- Insurance correspondence (for fire or flood)
- Building control or planning application references

A council inspector may visit to verify the property's condition.

## After the 12 months

After 12 months the full 100% charge resumes, even if work is not complete. If the property remains empty for a further 12 months it will then attract the empty homes premium (see empty property guide). If your works are genuinely delayed by force majeure, contact us; we can occasionally extend in exceptional cases.

## Cosmetic works do not qualify

Painting, redecorating, fitting a new kitchen or bathroom, or laying floors does not qualify for the discount. The property must be genuinely uninhabitable, not merely under improvement.
