# Council tax reduction (CTR) for low income

Council tax reduction (CTR) is a means-tested scheme that reduces the council tax bill of households on low incomes. It replaced council tax benefit in April 2013. Around 8,200 Aldershire households currently receive CTR.

## Who can apply

You can apply if you are responsible for paying council tax on the property you live in, and you are on a low income. There is no automatic qualifying benefit; we look at your full circumstances. Typical recipients include:
- People on Universal Credit
- People on income-based Jobseeker's Allowance or Employment and Support Allowance
- People on Pension Credit (see pensioner CTR guide)
- Working households on low earnings

## Working-age scheme: maximum reduction

For working-age applicants the Aldershire scheme provides up to 80% reduction. You always have to pay at least 20% of your council tax (sometimes called a minimum payment). This was set by full council in February 2024 and applies to all working-age claimants, with the exception of war pensioners.

## Income and capital limits

You must have less than £6,000 in capital to qualify for any award. Capital between £6,000 and £16,000 reduces your award using a tariff income of £1 per week for every £250 above £6,000. Above £16,000 you cannot get CTR (this differs for pensioners; see pensioner CTR guide).

Income from work, benefits, occupational pensions and tax credits all count. Some income is disregarded, including child benefit, war disablement pensions, and the first £25 per week of earnings if you are working.

## How to apply

Apply online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/ctr or by phoning 01624 800215. We will ask for evidence of identity, income, capital and household composition. Most claims are decided within 21 working days.

If you are facing court action and your claim is in progress, ring the recovery team on 01624 800210 to put recovery on hold.
