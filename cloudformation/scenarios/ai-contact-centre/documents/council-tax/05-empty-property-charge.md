# Empty property charge

In Aldershire there is no discount for empty unfurnished properties. Council tax is charged at 100% from the day a property becomes empty, even if no one is living there. Long-term empty homes attract additional premiums.

## Standard empty charge

The full council tax charge applies from day one of any property being unoccupied and substantially unfurnished. This includes properties between tenancies, properties on the market for sale, and properties undergoing minor refurbishment.

## Empty home premiums

After 24 months of being empty and substantially unfurnished, the following premiums apply on top of the standard charge:

- After 2 years empty: 100% premium (you pay 200% of the normal charge)
- After 5 years empty: 200% premium (you pay 300%)
- After 10 years empty: 300% premium (you pay 400%)

These premiums were strengthened in April 2024 under the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023. The 2-year threshold was reduced from the previous 24 months under earlier rules.

## Exceptions to the premium

The premium does not apply if the property:
- Is being actively marketed for sale or rent at a realistic price (evidence required)
- Is awaiting grant of probate or letters of administration (12-month grace period)
- Is uninhabitable due to major works (see major works exemption)
- Is an annexe being used as part of the main dwelling
- Forms part of a service member's job-related accommodation

## Reporting an empty property

Tell us when a property becomes empty by completing the change-of-circumstances form at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/move. Include the date the last occupant left, the reason it is empty, and the address where any post should be sent.

For advice on bringing an empty home back into use, the Empty Homes Officer can be reached on 01624 800280.
