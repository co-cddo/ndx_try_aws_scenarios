# Self-contained annexes: separate band, separate bill

A self-contained annexe is treated as a separate dwelling for council tax. The Valuation Office Agency assigns it its own band, and Aldershire District Council issues a separate bill.

## What "self-contained" means

The VOA treats an annexe as self-contained if it has:
- Its own kitchen (cooker connection, sink, food preparation area)
- Its own bathroom (WC, bath or shower)
- Its own front door or lockable internal door
- An ability to be lived in independently

A "granny flat" with all four features above is self-contained. A "guest suite" sharing the main kitchen is not.

## Banding the annexe

The VOA bands the annexe based on its 1991 hypothetical value as if sold separately. Most annexes in Aldershire come in at Band A or B because of their size. The annexe band can be lower than (and is normally never higher than) the main dwelling.

## Discount for family or empty use

A self-contained annexe pays the 50% family annexe discount if occupied by a relative, or if it is unoccupied but used as part of the main household (see family annexes guide). If let to an unrelated third party, the third party becomes the liable person and the discount does not apply.

## Conversion notification

If you convert part of your home into a self-contained annexe, tell us within 21 days at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/move. We will refer the new layout to the VOA, who will band the annexe and update the council tax list. The change is normally effective from the date of completion of the conversion.

You must also obtain any planning permission and building control consent that applies (see https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/planning).

## Practical example

A property in Brookmead (AL5 7TG) is Band E. Its self-contained granny flat (built 2024 with a planning permission) is banded as Band A. Total annual liability: Band E £2,406 + Band A £1,312 = £3,718 (no discount). With the family annexe 50% discount on the annexe (because grandma lives there): £2,406 + £656 = £3,062.
