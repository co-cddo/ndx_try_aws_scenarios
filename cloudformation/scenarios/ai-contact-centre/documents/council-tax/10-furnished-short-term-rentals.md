# Furnished short-term rentals: assessment rules

Properties used for short-term lets through platforms such as Airbnb, Vrbo, or Booking.com may be assessed either for council tax (as a second home) or for non-domestic rates (as a self-catering holiday let). The rules depend on availability and actual letting days.

## The two-stage test

A property is removed from the council tax list and placed on the business rating list only if it meets both:
- Available for commercial letting for at least 140 days in the previous 12 months
- Actually let commercially for at least 70 days in that same period

If only the 140-day availability test is met but actual lets fall below 70 days, the property remains on the council tax list and will be charged as a second home, including the 100% premium from April 2026.

## Mixed-use properties

If you let a room or annexe within your main home as a B&B or short-term rental but the rest of the property is your main residence, the rating treatment depends on the proportion of the property used for letting. We recommend contacting the VOA at https://www.gov.uk/voa for an informal assessment.

## Registration in Aldershire

In Aldershire, all short-term rental hosts must register with ADC under our short-term holiday let registration scheme (Local Government Act 1972 byelaw, adopted October 2025). Register online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/holidaylet. Registration is free.

A copy of the registration certificate must be displayed at the let premises. Failure to register is an offence carrying a fixed penalty of £400.

## Council tax while you decide

Until the VOA confirms a change to the rating list, council tax remains payable. We do not backdate refunds further than the VOA's effective date of change. Continue paying your bill in the meantime to avoid recovery action.
