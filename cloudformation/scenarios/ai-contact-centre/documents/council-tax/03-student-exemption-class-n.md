# Student exemption (Class N)

A property occupied only by full-time students is exempt from council tax under Class N of the Council Tax (Exempt Dwellings) Order 1992. You pay nothing while the exemption is in force.

## What counts as full-time study

For council tax purposes, a "full-time student" is someone who:
- is enrolled on a course at a UK university, college or higher education institution
- is studying for at least one academic year, attending for at least 24 weeks per year
- is normally required to undertake at least 21 hours of study per week

Students aged under 20 on a non-advanced course (for example, A-levels at a sixth-form college) are also covered if the course lasts more than three months and involves at least 12 hours of study per week.

## How to apply

Apply online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/student. You will need to upload a student certificate from each student in the household. Your university registry can issue this; most do so free of charge. The certificate must include your full name, course title, course start and end dates, and the institution's stamp or seal.

If you are studying at the University of Aldershire (AL2 7QQ), certificates can be downloaded directly from the student portal at https://my.uni-aldershire.example.ac.uk/counciltax.

## Mixed households

If a non-student adult lives with you, the property is not exempt but a 25% discount may apply (because the students are disregarded). The non-student becomes the liable person.

## Course gaps and graduation

The exemption ends on the day after your course officially ends, even if you remain in the property afterwards. Tell us within 21 days by calling 01624 800220 or emailing counciltax@aldershire.example.gov.uk.
