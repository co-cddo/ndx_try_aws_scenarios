# Disputing your council tax band

The council tax band of your property is set by the Valuation Office Agency (VOA), not by Aldershire District Council. If you think your band is wrong, you must apply to the VOA. ADC cannot change your band.

## When can you challenge the band

You have a formal right of "proposal" if:
- You have moved into the property in the last 6 months
- The band has been changed by the VOA in the last 6 months
- The character of the property has changed (extension, demolition, conversion)
- A neighbouring use has materially affected value (for example, a new motorway built nearby)
- The Listing Officer has made an error of fact

Outside these circumstances you can still ask the VOA for an "informal review", but the VOA is not obliged to act.

## How to challenge

Online: https://www.gov.uk/challenge-council-tax-band
Phone (VOA): 03000 501 501

You will need:
- Your council tax band
- Your property address and postcode (AL1-AL8 are in the Aldershire valuation list)
- Reasons why the band is wrong
- Comparable properties in your road or area, ideally with their bands and 1991 values

The VOA aims to decide proposals within 6 months. They may inspect the property, ask for floor plans, or request photographs.

## What ADC does while you wait

You must continue to pay your existing bill while the band is being reviewed. If the VOA reduces your band, ADC will issue a refund or credit for the period since the effective date. If the band is increased, you will be billed for the difference.

## Common comparable evidence

Aldershire houses built between 1985 and 1991 in postcodes AL3 and AL4 are well-evidenced in the VOA database, as are 1930s semis in AL1. The Listing Officer can also be reached for procedural questions, although they do not give individual valuation advice in advance.

For council-tax billing questions while a VOA review is open, ring 01624 800200.
