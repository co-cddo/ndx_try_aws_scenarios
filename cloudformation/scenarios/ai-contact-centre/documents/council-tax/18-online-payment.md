# Online and other payment methods

Aldershire District Council accepts council tax payments through a range of channels. Direct debit is the most common, but you can also pay online by card, by bank transfer, in person at a post office, or by cheque.

## Pay online by debit or credit card

Visit https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/pay and enter your 8-digit council tax account reference (shown on your bill). The portal accepts Visa, Mastercard and Maestro debit cards, and Visa and Mastercard credit cards. There is no surcharge for using a credit card.

The site is hosted by Capita's Pay360 platform and is PCI-DSS compliant. We do not see or store your full card number.

## Telephone card payment

Call 01624 800300 and follow the automated prompts. The service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Have your account number and card details ready. The line is fully automated; no advisor is involved with the payment itself, so you can use it outside office hours.

## Bank transfer

Pay direct to: NatWest, Aldershire District Council, sort code 60-50-04, account 12345678. Use your 8-digit council tax account number as the payment reference. Faster Payments arrive same-day; BACS takes 2-3 working days.

## In branch (Post Office or PayPoint)

Take your most recent bill (paper or electronic) to any Post Office or PayPoint outlet. The barcode on the bill is scanned, and you pay with cash or card. Allow 3-5 working days for the payment to reach your account.

## By cheque

Make cheques payable to "Aldershire District Council". Write your council tax account number on the back. Post to: Council Tax Section, Aldershire District Council, Civic Centre, AL1 2RR. Cheques take up to 5 working days to clear.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay

Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted on the website portal but not on the telephone payment line. Use them on a mobile or tablet at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/pay.

For payment problems, call 01624 800200 during office hours, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
