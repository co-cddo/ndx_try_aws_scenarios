# Statement of accounts: requesting a council tax history

You can request a statement of your council tax account showing all transactions (charges, payments, refunds, adjustments) for any specified period. Statements are useful for mortgage applications, immigration cases, separation proceedings, and benefit reviews.

## What a statement shows

A standard statement of accounts shows, for each financial year requested:
- Opening balance brought forward
- Annual charge, with band and effective date
- Any discounts, exemptions or CTR awarded
- Each instalment date and amount
- Each payment received with method
- Closing balance carried forward

We can also produce summary statements showing only the totals per year.

## How to request

Online: https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk → Account → "Request statement". Free, instant download as PDF for the last 3 years; longer periods take up to 5 working days.

Email: counciltax@aldershire.example.gov.uk — provide your full name, address, account reference and the years you need.

Phone: 01624 800200 — we can post or email a paper copy within 5 working days.

## Frequency

There is no limit on how often you can request statements. Most residents do so once or twice a year. We do not charge for paper statements posted out, although heavy or unusual requests may be treated as a freedom of information matter (see FOI guide).

## Statements covering pre-2018

For pre-2018 records (older than 7 years) we may not hold full transaction-level data. We can confirm liability and total payments per year. Older records are kept on microfiche and retrieval takes up to 20 working days.

## Statement for a third party

If you want a statement sent to a third party (mortgage lender, solicitor, immigration adviser), you must provide written authority. Use the consent form at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/consent. We will not send statements directly to third parties without it.

## Tax year and council tax year

Note that the council tax year runs 1 April to 31 March, like the financial year, not the tax year (which is 6 April to 5 April). Statements are produced on the council tax year basis.

For complex requests (probate, fraud investigations, court orders), email tax.records@aldershire.example.gov.uk or call 01624 800200.
