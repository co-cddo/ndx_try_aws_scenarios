# Freedom of Information requests on council tax

Council tax data is held by Aldershire District Council in two categories: personal data about individuals, and aggregate data about the council tax base, collection rates and policies. The Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA) and the UK GDPR govern access to each.

## Personal data: not via FOI

You cannot use FOI to obtain personal data about another resident. Names, addresses, balances, payment history and circumstances are personal data and are exempt from FOI under section 40. We can only release personal data:
- To the data subject themselves (subject access request, free, 30 calendar days)
- With written consent of the data subject
- Where another lawful basis applies (court order, statutory duty)

If you are trying to find out whether someone lives at an address, FOI is not the route. Contact our data protection officer at dpo@aldershire.example.gov.uk for guidance.

## Aggregate data: published or via FOI

Aggregate data is released proactively under our publication scheme at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/foi/publication-scheme. Items routinely published include:
- Council tax base by ward, by band
- Total council tax collection rate (in-year and arrears)
- Number of properties by band, exemption type and discount
- Total amount paid to each enforcement agent firm
- Discretionary hardship fund expenditure
- Equality monitoring summaries

For unpublished aggregate data, submit an FOI at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/foi or by emailing foi@aldershire.example.gov.uk. We respond within 20 working days.

## What we typically refuse

- Lists of premium-payers, debt-defaulters, or those in arrears (section 40)
- Specific recovery cases or court hearings (section 40 and section 31)
- Software source code or commercial data on system suppliers (section 43)
- Internal staff comments and emails about specific cases (section 40 and section 36)

## Cost limit

The "appropriate limit" for FOI is £450 (18 hours of officer time). If your request would exceed it, we will offer to scope it down or refuse the part that exceeds.

## Right of appeal

If you are unhappy with our response, ask for an internal review within 40 working days. After that you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk.

For FOI advice ring 01624 800100 or email foi@aldershire.example.gov.uk.
