# Care leavers: council tax exemption to age 25

Aldershire District Council provides a 100% council tax exemption to all care-experienced young people aged 18 to 25 who are the sole or main resident of the property. This is one of the most generous schemes in the country and was strengthened in 2023 in line with the council's "Aldershire Promise" to care leavers.

## Who qualifies

You qualify if you:
- Were looked after by Aldershire County Council (or any UK local authority) for at least 13 weeks since the age of 14
- Including at least one day after your 16th birthday
- Are aged 18 to 25 years inclusive
- Are the liable person for council tax on the property where you live as your main home

The "looked after" definition follows section 22 of the Children Act 1989. Care leavers from outside Aldershire who move into the district also qualify.

## What is covered

You pay nothing. The exemption applies to:
- Sole occupancy (you live alone)
- Joint tenancies where you and other care leavers are the only liable people
- Households where the only adults are care leavers up to 25 and full-time students

If you live with a non-care-leaver and non-student adult (for example, a partner not from care), they become liable for the full bill but the property is treated as if you are not present, often making them eligible for the 25% single person discount.

## How to claim

Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/careleaver or by emailing care.leavers@aldershire.example.gov.uk. We will need:
- Your full name and address
- Your Aldershire Children's Services contact (or equivalent for other authorities)
- Confirmation from your Personal Adviser or care leaver social worker

The application is reviewed by the council tax team within 14 working days, in liaison with Aldershire County Council Children's Services.

## Other support

Care leavers may also access Aldershire's discretionary hardship fund for non-council-tax debts, and a furniture grant of up to £700 on first tenancy. Speak to your Personal Adviser or call the care leaver helpline on 01624 800450.

For council tax questions, call 01624 800220, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
