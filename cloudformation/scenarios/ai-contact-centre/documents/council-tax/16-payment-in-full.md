# Paying council tax in full (5% prompt-pay discount)

If you pay your full year's council tax in one lump sum by 30 April each year, you receive a 5% prompt-payment discount. This option suits households able to budget annually and is not widely advertised in many councils, but Aldershire has offered it since 2018.

## How the discount works

The full annual charge is shown on your bill. If you pay it in full by 30 April (or the next working day if 30 April is a weekend), we will credit your account with a 5% rebate which is deducted from the next year's bill.

For a Band D property in 2026-27, the standard charge is £1,968. Paying in full by 30 April triggers a £98.40 credit on your 2027-28 bill.

## How to pay in full

You can pay in full by:
- Online card payment at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/pay
- Telephone card payment, 24 hours, on 01624 800300
- Bank transfer (BACS or Faster Payment) to: NatWest, sort code 60-50-04, account 12345678, reference: your 8-digit account number
- Cheque (made payable to "Aldershire District Council") posted to Council Tax Section, AL1 2RR
- In branch at any post office (cash or card; bring your bill so the barcode can be scanned)

## Cooling-off and changes

If you pay in full and then move out of the property part-way through the year, the credit is calculated pro-rata up to the move date and either refunded or transferred to your new Aldershire account. We do not claw back the prompt-pay discount provided you were resident in Aldershire throughout the period covered.

## Other lump-sum options

If you cannot pay the full year but can pay six months in advance, the discount does not apply but you reduce admin and have peace of mind. Speak to the council tax team on 01624 800200 about a tailored arrangement.
