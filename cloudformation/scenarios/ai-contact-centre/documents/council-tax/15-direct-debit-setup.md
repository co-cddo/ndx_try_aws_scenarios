# Setting up direct debit for council tax

Direct debit is the easiest way to pay council tax. Around 76% of Aldershire households now pay this way. Payments are taken automatically each month and you are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee.

## Choice of payment dates

You can pick from four payment dates each month: 1st, 8th, 15th, or 25th. Choose the one that fits best with when you are paid.

## Choice of payment plan

You can choose to pay over either:
- 10 instalments (April to January) — the default option
- 12 instalments (April to March) — better for managing monthly cashflow, slightly smaller monthly amount

Switching from 10 to 12 instalments is free and can be requested at any time. If you switch part-way through the year, the remaining balance is spread across the months left.

## Setting up online

The fastest way is online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/dd. You will need:
- Your council tax account reference (8-digit number on your bill)
- The full name on your bank account
- Your bank sort code and account number
- The date you want payments to be taken

Online setup takes about 5 minutes and the first payment is collected on the next available payment date (allowing at least 10 working days notice).

## Setting up by phone or post

Call 01624 800200, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm), with your bank details to hand. Or download a paper instruction at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/dd-form, sign it, and post it to: Council Tax Section, Aldershire District Council, Civic Centre, AL1 2RR.

## Cancelling or changing

You can cancel a direct debit at any time by contacting your bank. Please also tell us, as cancelling without paying by another method can lead to recovery action. To change your payment date or instalment plan, ring 01624 800200 or update your details on My Aldershire (https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk).
