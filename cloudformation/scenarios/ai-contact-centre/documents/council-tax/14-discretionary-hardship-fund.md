# Discretionary council tax hardship fund

Aldershire District Council operates a discretionary hardship fund for residents who, despite being entitled to council tax reduction (CTR), are still unable to meet their council tax due to exceptional circumstances. The fund sits alongside section 13A(1)(c) of the Local Government Finance Act 1992.

## Who can apply

You must already have claimed CTR (or have a pending claim). The fund is intended to top up CTR where the remaining liability is causing genuine hardship. Typical applicants include:
- Households with high essential outgoings (medical equipment, special diet)
- People recently released from hospital or care
- Those experiencing the bedroom tax or benefit cap
- Sudden loss of income (redundancy, family separation)
- People fleeing domestic abuse and rebuilding finances
- Foster carers placing additional adults in the home

## What the fund can do

The fund can reduce your bill by anything from a one-off payment to a full year's discretionary award. Most awards are between £150 and £600. The fund cannot pay arrears that have already been passed to enforcement agents.

## Application process

Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/hardship. You will need to complete a detailed income and expenditure form and provide:
- Bank statements for the last 2 months
- Recent payslips, benefit letters, or self-employed accounts
- Evidence of unusual expenditure (medical receipts, special equipment, court orders)
- A short statement explaining the cause of hardship

## Decision and appeal

A decision is made within 21 working days. If we cannot decide within that time we will write to explain. If we refuse, the only formal challenge is judicial review, but we will reconsider if you provide new evidence.

The hardship fund has a finite annual budget. Awards in the final quarter (January to March) may be smaller as the fund nears exhaustion. For urgent help, call the welfare team on 01624 800240.
