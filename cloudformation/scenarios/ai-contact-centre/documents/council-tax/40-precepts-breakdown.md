# Council tax precepts: where your money goes

Aldershire District Council collects council tax on behalf of itself and three other "precepting authorities". This guide breaks down the 2026-27 Band D charge so you can see exactly which body receives each pound.

## 2026-27 Band D total: £1,968 (unparished area)

The £1,968 Band D charge in an unparished area is made up of:

| Authority | Annual Band D | Percentage |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Aldershire County Council | £1,438.40 | 73.1% |
| Aldershire District Council | £236.16 | 12.0% |
| Aldershire Constabulary | £214.32 | 10.9% |
| Aldershire Fire and Rescue | £79.12 | 4.0% |
| **Total** | **£1,968.00** | **100%** |

In parished areas, an additional precept is added (see "Parish precepts" below).

## What each precept funds

**Aldershire County Council (£1,438.40):** adult social care, schools, public health, highways and transport, libraries, child protection. The county precept includes the 2% Adult Social Care precept, ringfenced for ASC services. Total ASC precept in 2026-27 is £155 of the £1,438.40.

**Aldershire District Council (£236.16):** waste collection, planning, housing, environmental health, leisure centres, parks, environmental enforcement, council tax administration, elections.

**Aldershire Constabulary (£214.32):** policing services, including community policing, criminal investigation, custody, and emergency response. Set by the Police and Crime Commissioner.

**Aldershire Fire and Rescue (£79.12):** fire stations, response vehicles, prevention work, and major incident planning. Set by the combined fire authority.

## Parish precepts

If you live in a parished area (about 60% of Aldershire properties), an additional parish precept is added. 2026-27 parish Band D precepts range from £18 (Maple Bottom) to £198 (Ashvale-with-Bewton). The bill on the doormat shows the relevant parish line separately.

A complete list of parish Band D precepts is published at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/parishes. The parish precept funds local services such as village halls, parish playgrounds, parish councils' open spaces, and consultative responses on local planning applications.

## Year-on-year change

The 2026-27 increases compared with 2025-26:
- ADC: +2.99% (the maximum permitted without referendum)
- County (general + ASC): +4.99% (+2.99% general, +2.0% ASC)
- Constabulary: +£14 cash flat-rate
- Fire: +2.99%

The blended increase for a typical Band D property is 4.7%.

For detailed budget reports see https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/budgets.
