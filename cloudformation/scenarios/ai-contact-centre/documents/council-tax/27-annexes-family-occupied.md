# Annexes occupied by family members (50% discount)

A self-contained annexe occupied by a relative of the main household qualifies for a 50% council tax discount. This is the "family annexe" reduction introduced by the Council Tax (Reductions for Annexes) (England) Regulations 2013.

## What counts as a family annexe

To qualify, all of the following must be true:
- The annexe is structurally part of, or in the curtilage of, the main dwelling
- The annexe is self-contained (own kitchen and bathroom)
- The occupier is a relative of someone living in the main dwelling
- The annexe is the relative's main home

"Relative" is defined widely: spouse, civil partner, parent, child, grandchild, sibling, aunt, uncle, nephew, niece, in-laws, and step-relatives all qualify.

## What you pay

The annexe receives its own separate council tax band (set by the VOA) and bill. The 50% reduction is applied to that bill. So a Band B annexe attracting a £1,531 bill will pay £765.50 per year.

The main dwelling pays its own bill in full, unaffected.

## Empty annexe used by main household

If the annexe is empty but used as part of the main dwelling (a home gym, an office, occasional guest room), it may still qualify for the 50% discount under regulation 4 of the same instrument. This requires the annexe not to be let separately, not to be a holiday let, and not to be substantially furnished as a separate dwelling.

## How to apply

Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/annexe. We will need:
- Floor plans showing the annexe layout
- Proof the occupier is a relative (birth certificate, marriage certificate)
- Confirmation of main residence (driving licence, voter roll)

Approvals are typically issued within 21 working days. The discount is backdated to the start of the qualifying period.
