# Council tax bands and 2026-27 annual charges

Aldershire District Council (ADC) sets and collects council tax for properties across the district, covering postcodes AL1 to AL8. Your band is determined by the Valuation Office Agency (VOA) based on the open-market value of your property as at 1 April 1991.

## 2026-27 charges by band

The figures below are the total annual amount payable for a Band D property in an unparished area. Properties in parished areas pay an additional parish precept. Charges include precepts for Aldershire County Council, Aldershire Constabulary, and Aldershire Fire and Rescue.

- Band A: under £40,000 (1991 value), £1,312 per year
- Band B: £40,001 to £52,000, £1,531
- Band C: £52,001 to £68,000, £1,750
- Band D: £68,001 to £88,000, £1,968
- Band E: £88,001 to £120,000, £2,406
- Band F: £120,001 to £160,000, £2,843
- Band G: £160,001 to £320,000, £3,281
- Band H: over £320,000, £3,937

## Where the money goes

For every £1 of council tax collected in Aldershire:
- 73p funds Aldershire County Council services (adult social care, schools, highways)
- 12p funds Aldershire District Council services (waste collection, planning, housing, environmental health)
- 11p funds Aldershire Constabulary
- 4p funds Aldershire Fire and Rescue Service

A detailed breakdown is available in the leaflet sent with your annual bill, and online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/precepts.

## How to find your band

Look at your most recent council tax bill, or check the VOA's online register at https://www.gov.uk/council-tax-bands. If you believe your band is wrong, see our guide on disputing your band.

For questions about your bill, call the council tax team on 01624 800200, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
