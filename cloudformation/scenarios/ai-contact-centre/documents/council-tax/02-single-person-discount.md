# Single person discount (25%)

If you are the only adult (aged 18 or over) living in your property as your main home, you can claim a 25% discount on your council tax bill. This is the most common council tax discount and around 12,400 households in Aldershire currently receive it.

## Who counts as an adult for council tax

When working out the number of adults in a property, certain people are "disregarded" and not counted. These include full-time students, apprentices on low pay, live-in carers (not caring for a partner), people in prison, and people who are severely mentally impaired (with a doctor's certificate). See our guide on disregards for the full list.

If you live alone, or if everyone else in your property is disregarded, you may be entitled to the 25% single person discount.

## How to claim

Apply online at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/discount. You will need:
- Your council tax account reference (8-digit number on your bill)
- The date you became the only adult resident
- Names and dates of birth of any other adults who used to live there, and where they have moved to

The form takes about ten minutes. Most claims are processed within 14 working days, and a revised bill is issued automatically.

## When the discount must stop

You must tell us within 21 days if another adult moves into your property as their main home. This includes a partner, a returning adult child, or a lodger. Failure to do so may result in a £70 civil penalty under section 14C of the Local Government Finance Act 1992, plus repayment of any discount wrongly claimed.

If you are unsure whether someone counts as a resident (for example, a partner who works away during the week), call the discounts team on 01624 800220, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
