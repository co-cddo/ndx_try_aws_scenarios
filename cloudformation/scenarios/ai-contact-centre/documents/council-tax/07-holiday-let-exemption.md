# Holiday let assessment (commercial)

Furnished holiday lets in Aldershire that meet HMRC and VOA tests are assessed for non-domestic (business) rates rather than council tax. This guide explains the criteria and how the council co-ordinates with the VOA.

## The 70-day commercial test

To be treated as a self-catering holiday let for non-domestic rating, a property must meet all of the following in the previous 12 months:
- Be available for short-term commercial letting for at least 140 days
- Have actually been let commercially for at least 70 days
- Be furnished and equipped to a standard suitable for short-stay guests

If both tests are met, the VOA will move the property from the council tax list to the rating list and you will receive a business rates bill instead. Many small holiday lets qualify for 100% small business rate relief, paying nothing.

## Evidence we may ask for

ADC works with the VOA to confirm holiday let status and may request:
- Booking diaries showing dates let
- Receipts and invoices to guests
- Listings on commercial booking sites (Airbnb, Booking.com, Sykes Cottages, etc.)
- Bank statements showing rental income
- Marketing materials such as websites or brochures

## What if the tests are not met

If your property is furnished and intermittently let but does not reach 70 days commercially in a year, it remains on the council tax list as a second home. From April 2026 it will attract the second-home premium (see second home guide).

## Application process

The VOA decides whether a property is a holiday let; we cannot make this decision for you. Apply at https://www.gov.uk/voa/business-rates. Once the VOA confirms the change, ADC will issue a closing council tax bill and the rating list will be updated.

For council tax billing questions while a VOA decision is pending, call 01624 800200.
