# Changing the address details on your council tax account

Sometimes you need to change the address details we hold for council tax — for example after a roadname change, a postcode update, or where the postal address has been amended by Royal Mail. This is different from moving home (see the moving in/out guide).

## Roadname or postcode changes

Royal Mail and the Local Land and Property Gazetteer (LLPG) team at Aldershire District Council manage road naming and addressing. When a road is renumbered, renamed, or split, the LLPG team automatically updates the council tax record. You will receive a confirmation letter and a revised bill (no balance changes; only the address layout).

If you discover an error in your address (wrong house number, missing flat number) please tell us. Apply at https://www.aldershire.example.gov.uk/counciltax/address.

## Forwarding address (correspondence only)

If you want bills sent to a correspondence address (different from the property address), use the same form. Common cases include:
- Landlords managing a portfolio
- Property owners working overseas
- Adult children handling a parent's affairs (with consent)

Provide written consent from the named liable person if you are not them.

## Proof of identity

For any address change we ask for two forms of evidence to prevent fraud:
- Photo ID (driving licence, passport, national identity card)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, tenancy agreement)

If you are acting on behalf of someone, a power of attorney, deputyship order, or letter of authority is also needed.

## Same person, multiple Aldershire properties

If you own multiple properties in Aldershire, we can link them under a single login on My Aldershire. Each property has its own 8-digit reference and its own bill, but you can manage them from one dashboard. Set this up at https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk by adding additional accounts.

## Name changes (marriage, deed poll)

To change a name on the bill (for example, after marriage or deed poll), email counciltax@aldershire.example.gov.uk with the supporting certificate. We update the record within 5 working days. Joint liability is unaffected by a name change.
