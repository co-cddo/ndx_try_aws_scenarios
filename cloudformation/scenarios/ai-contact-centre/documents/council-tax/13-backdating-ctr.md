# Backdating council tax reduction

A claim for council tax reduction normally starts from the Monday after we receive it. In some circumstances we can backdate the start date so you receive support from an earlier point. The rules differ for working-age and pension-age claimants.

## Working-age backdating

For working-age claimants, CTR can be backdated for up to 6 months from the date your written request reaches us, provided you can show "good cause" for the delay in claiming. Good cause is judged on the facts of your case but typically includes:
- Serious illness, hospital admission or bereavement during the period
- Mental health difficulties that prevented you understanding the entitlement
- Misleading or incorrect advice from another agency (Jobcentre, DWP, housing officer)
- Domestic violence or coercive control
- Periods in custody followed by release without funds

You must apply in writing setting out why you did not claim sooner, and provide supporting evidence (medical letter, agency correspondence, etc.).

## Pension-age backdating

For pensioners the backdating rules are more generous. CTR can be backdated for up to 3 months automatically, with no need to show good cause. To get a longer backdate (up to 6 months), submit a written request explaining your circumstances.

## How to request a backdate

Either tick the backdating section on the standard CTR form, or write separately to the council tax team at counciltax@aldershire.example.gov.uk or by post to: Council Tax Section, Aldershire District Council, Civic Centre, AL1 2RR. Include:
- Your full name, address and council tax account number
- The date from which you want the backdate to apply
- Your reason for the delay (working-age) or simply request the maximum backdate (pensioner)
- Any supporting evidence

## Decision and appeal

We aim to decide backdate requests within 28 days. If we refuse, you can ask for a reconsideration, then appeal to the Valuation Tribunal (see appeals guide). The recovery team will be told if your claim is under review.

For help completing a backdate request, ring 01624 800215, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
