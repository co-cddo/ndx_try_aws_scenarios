# Major works exemption: uninhabitable properties

Aldershire District Council operates a discretionary 50% local discount for properties that are genuinely uninhabitable due to major works. This replaces the former Class A national exemption (abolished in April 2013) and is described in detail in our Property Under Repair guide.

## Definition of "uninhabitable"

A property is uninhabitable for this discount if at least two of the following are true:
- No working kitchen (no working cooker, sink, or food preparation area)
- No working bathroom (no working WC, bath or shower, or hot water)
- No safe access (structural problems, scaffolding throughout, no front door)
- No working heating or insulation in winter months
- Major electrical or plumbing works in progress, with services off

Cosmetic refurbishment does not qualify, however thorough.

## Length of discount

The discount runs for up to 12 months from the date the property became uninhabitable, regardless of when works actually start or finish. After 12 months the full charge resumes.

## Council inspection

Officers normally visit the property to verify uninhabitability. Inspections take place within 10 working days of application, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm). The inspector takes photographs and may ask to see your builder's quote, structural survey, and insurance correspondence.

## Insurance and force majeure

Where the uninhabitability is caused by an insured event (fire, flood, subsidence) and the insurer is involved, the 12-month period can be extended in exceptional cases. Provide insurer correspondence with your application. Examples in Aldershire have included:
- Cellar Group fire of January 2024 (AL2 9KL): 18-month extension granted
- 2025 floods at Brookside (AL6 3PN): 14-month extensions for 11 properties

## Interaction with empty homes premium

The discount pauses the 24-month empty homes clock. So if works finish in month 10, the property is treated as empty from month 10 onwards, and the empty homes premium does not start until 24 further months have passed.
