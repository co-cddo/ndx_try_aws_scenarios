# Mobile homes and caravans: council tax assessment

Mobile homes (also called "park homes") and caravans used as a permanent residence are treated as dwellings for council tax. Caravans on holiday parks may not be. This guide explains the rules.

## Park homes (residential parks)

A park home on a residential park (such as Aldershire Lakes Park, AL4 5LP) is a permanent dwelling. The Valuation Office Agency bands it like any other home — usually Band A in Aldershire because of its modest 1991 value.

The occupier (the home owner) is the liable person. The site fees paid to the park owner are separate; they cover ground rent and communal services and do not include council tax.

About 280 park homes in Aldershire are on the council tax list, mostly in AL4 and AL6.

## Touring caravans on residential pitches

A touring caravan that has become someone's main home is also a dwelling for council tax. The VOA may band it. The situation is unusual but does occur on a small number of farm sites and informal pitches.

If you live in a touring caravan as your main residence, contact ADC at 01624 800200 to ensure you are correctly registered.

## Caravans on holiday parks

A caravan on a designated holiday park (Aldershire Lakes' tourer field, Brookside Holiday Park, etc.) is not a dwelling for council tax. Holiday park caravans are subject to non-domestic (business) rates instead, paid by the park operator and recovered through pitch fees.

You cannot use a holiday-park caravan as a main home — both planning permission and the site licence prohibit it. Doing so risks losing the pitch and back-billing for council tax.

## Mobile homes used as second homes

A residential park home used as a second home pays council tax in the same way as any other second home (see second home guide). From April 2026 the second-home premium applies.

## Park-specific issues

For park homes, the Mobile Homes Act 2013 protects security of tenure. Council tax bills are sent to the home address and not the park's main office. If the park changes hands the council tax accounts are unaffected.

Call 01624 800200 to check the status of any specific pitch or park.
