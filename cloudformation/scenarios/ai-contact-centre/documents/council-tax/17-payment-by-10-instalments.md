# Paying council tax by 10 instalments

The default council tax payment plan is 10 monthly instalments running from April to January. This has been the statutory default since 1992 (Council Tax (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 1992).

## How the 10 instalments work

Your annual bill is divided into 10 broadly equal payments:
- The first instalment is due on the date shown on your bill (usually 1 April or 1 May)
- The remaining nine instalments fall due on the same day of each subsequent month, finishing in January
- February and March are payment-free months

For a Band D property charged £1,968 per year, each monthly payment is £196.80.

## Switching to 12 instalments

You can ask to spread your bill over 12 months instead of 10. This makes each monthly payment smaller (£164 instead of £196.80 for a Band D), and runs from April through to March. You can switch at any point in the year, although the most common time is at billing in March/April.

To switch, log into My Aldershire at https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk or ring 01624 800200. The change takes effect from the next month's payment.

## Missed instalment: what happens

If you miss an instalment, we send a "first reminder" notice 14 days after the due date. You then have 7 days to pay the missed amount. If paid, your normal instalment plan continues. If not, recovery action begins (see recovery guide).

A second missed instalment in the same year leads directly to a "final notice" demanding the whole year's balance immediately. There is no further reminder.

## Tip: synchronise with payday

Many missed instalments happen because the council tax due date does not match payday. Direct debit lets you choose between the 1st, 8th, 15th, or 25th of each month. If you pay by standing order, set up the standing order on your payday for the closest available council tax due date.

For help arranging payments, ring 01624 800200.
