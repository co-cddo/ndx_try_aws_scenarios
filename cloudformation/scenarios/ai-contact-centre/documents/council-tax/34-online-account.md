# My Aldershire: online council tax account

My Aldershire is the council's secure online portal at https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk. You can view bills, set up direct debit, change personal details, apply for discounts and check your balance 24/7.

## Registering

Visit https://my.aldershire.example.gov.uk and click "Register". You will need:
- Your council tax account reference (8-digit number on your bill)
- Your name as it appears on the bill
- Your postcode
- A working email address

Verification takes 2 minutes. After signing in for the first time, set up two-factor authentication using either an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy) or SMS to a UK mobile.

## What you can do online

- View current and past bills
- Set up, change or cancel direct debit
- Switch between 10-month and 12-month payment plans
- Apply for single person discount, student exemption and other discounts
- Apply for council tax reduction (CTR)
- Tell us about a change of address
- Tell us about a change of household members
- Make one-off card payments
- Download e-statements

## Going paperless (e-billing)

You can switch to paperless e-billing from your account dashboard. We email you a PDF of your bill instead of posting it. Around 41% of Aldershire accounts are now paperless.

## Forgotten password and login problems

Click "Forgotten password" on the sign-in page. We email a one-time link valid for 30 minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder. The email comes from no-reply@aldershire.example.gov.uk.

If you cannot reset your own password (for example, you no longer have access to the registered email), call 01624 800200 to arrange manual identity verification. Allow 5 working days.

## Account security

We will never call or email asking for your full password, full bank details or login codes. If you receive such a call or email, ignore it and report to action.fraud@cityoflondon.police.uk or call us on 01624 800200 to verify.

For technical issues with the portal, email digital@aldershire.example.gov.uk during Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm).
