# Recovery of council tax arrears

If you fall behind with your council tax payments, ADC follows a structured recovery process set out in the Council Tax (Administration and Enforcement) Regulations 1992. We always prefer to agree a payment plan rather than escalate to court.

## Stage 1: First reminder

Sent 14 days after a missed instalment. You have 7 days to bring the account up to date. If you pay, your instalment plan continues unchanged. Around 38% of arrears cases are resolved at this stage.

## Stage 2: Second reminder

If you miss a second instalment in the same year, we send a second reminder. After this point, missing a third instalment leads directly to a final notice — there are no more reminders in that year.

## Stage 3: Final notice

The final notice removes your right to pay by instalments. The whole outstanding balance for the year is now due in 7 days.

## Stage 4: Court summons

If you do not pay or contact us, we apply to the magistrates' court for a liability order. The court summons adds £75 in costs to your bill. You will receive a copy of the summons by post, with a hearing date at Aldershire Magistrates' Court (AL1 5DD). You can attend the hearing or pay before the date to stop costs.

## Stage 5: Liability order

If granted, the liability order gives the council legal powers to recover the debt:
- Attachment of earnings (deduction from wages, fixed scale)
- Deduction from benefits (Universal Credit, Income Support, ESA, JSA, Pension Credit)
- Referral to enforcement agents (bailiffs) under the Tribunals Courts and Enforcement Act 2007
- Charging order against the property (for debts over £1,000)
- Bankruptcy petition (for debts over £5,000) — see bankruptcy guide

## We will pause if you contact us

At every stage, contacting the recovery team to agree a payment arrangement will pause further action. Ring 01624 800210 (recovery team direct line, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm (Wednesday 10am to 5pm)). Even after a liability order, an arrangement is usually possible.
