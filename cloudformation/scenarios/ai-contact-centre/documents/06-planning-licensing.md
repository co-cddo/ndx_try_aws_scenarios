# Planning, building control, licensing

Aldershire is the Local Planning Authority for the district. Planning enquiries are a high-volume contact-centre topic.

## Planning permission

You generally need planning permission for: new buildings, major extensions, changes of use, demolitions, and most external alterations to listed buildings or in conservation areas.

You generally do not need planning permission for: small extensions within Permitted Development limits, internal alterations to non-listed buildings, replacing windows like-for-like, and most garden sheds.

The bot does not give planning-permission decisions. It signposts to:

- The online Planning Portal for permission checks and applications.
- The Pre-application advice service (chargeable, optional).
- The Duty Planner phone line (Tuesdays and Thursdays, 14:00 to 17:00).

## Tracking an application

Provide the application reference (format: APP/AL/YYYY/NNNN). The bot can look up the status (validated, awaiting consultation, pending decision, decided) in the bot's read-only mirror of the Planning back-office system. Decisions, conditions, and the decision notice are not read by the bot; the resident is signposted to the Planning Portal for the full record.

## Building control

Building Control is a separate function from Planning. It covers structural compliance with the Building Regulations: foundations, electrical work, drainage, fire safety. Any building work that affects the structure or services of a property requires Building Control approval and inspection.

The bot can take an initial enquiry and route to a Building Control officer. It does not give Regulations advice.

## Licensing

Aldershire issues licences for: alcohol sales, late-night refreshment, regulated entertainment, gambling premises, taxis, street trading, animal welfare (boarding kennels, breeding), and skin piercing.

Each licence type has its own application form, fee, and consultation process. The bot signposts; it does not process applications.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not make planning decisions, give legal opinions on planning law, or interpret application conditions. Bedrock Guardrails block hallucinated planning or licensing advice.
