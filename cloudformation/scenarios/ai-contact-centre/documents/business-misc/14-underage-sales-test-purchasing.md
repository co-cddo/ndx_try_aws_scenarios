# Underage sales and test purchasing

Aldershire trading standards conducts test purchasing across the district to enforce age-restricted sales legislation. Test purchasing is the use of accompanied young volunteers to attempt to buy age-restricted products, with full safeguards under the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 codes of practice.

## Age-restricted products in scope

- Tobacco and tobacco-related products including vaping devices and nicotine-inhaling products (Children and Young Persons (Protection from Tobacco) Act 1991, Tobacco and Related Products Regulations 2016)
- Alcohol (Licensing Act 2003)
- Knives and bladed articles (Criminal Justice Act 1988)
- Lottery and scratchcards (Gambling Act 2005)
- Fireworks (Fireworks Regulations 2004)
- Solvents (Intoxicating Substances (Supply) Act 1985)
- Spray paints (Anti-Social Behaviour Act 2003)
- Energy drinks containing more than 150mg/L caffeine (voluntary code, several major retailers)
- Pets (Animal Welfare Act 2006)

## How test purchasing is conducted

Trading standards officers work with vetted volunteers aged 13-17. Each operation is briefed in advance, and:

- Volunteers wear ordinary clothes, no make-up that ages them, no jewellery beyond what they would normally wear
- Volunteers carry identification proving their actual age
- An adult officer accompanies (often staying outside in eyeshot)
- Audio is recorded for evidence
- Where a sale takes place, the seller is informed afterwards by an officer in uniform-equivalent role

If a sale is refused, the officer thanks the staff member; the council may write to the business commending compliance.

## Outcomes of failed test purchases

If a sale is made:

- First-time low-severity: written warning, advice on Challenge 25 protocols, follow-up visit
- Repeat or high-severity (knives, fireworks): formal caution, fixed penalty notice, or prosecution
- For licensed premises (alcohol): possible review of premises licence under the Licensing Act 2003

## Challenge 25

Aldershire encourages all retailers to operate "Challenge 25" — staff request photo ID from anyone who appears under 25 attempting to buy any age-restricted product. Free Challenge 25 till stickers, training materials, and a refusals book template are available from trading standards.

## Reporting underage sales you have seen

If you have seen a shop or pub repeatedly serving under-age customers, report to Citizens Advice (0808 223 1133) or directly to Aldershire trading standards (01632 960 280, tradingstandards@aldershire.gov.uk). All reports are confidential.
