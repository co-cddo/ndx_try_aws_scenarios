# Hardship business rate relief

Where a ratepayer would suffer hardship if Aldershire District Council did not reduce or remit their business rates, the council can grant discretionary hardship relief under section 49 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988.

## Who can apply

Any ratepayer of a non-domestic property in Aldershire can apply, subject to the test of hardship and the public interest test.

The relief is rare and discretionary. It is intended to bridge a temporary, unforeseeable financial difficulty — not to subsidise a business model that is structurally unviable. Examples of cases the Discretionary Relief Panel has considered include:

- A high street retailer affected by a 6-month closure of the adjacent road for utilities works
- A community pub temporarily closed for fire damage repairs not covered by insurance
- A new sole-trader business hit by an exceptional one-off liability (e.g. boiler failure in winter)

## What you need to demonstrate

The Panel considers:

- Hardship: that paying the rates would cause hardship over and above ordinary trading difficulty. We ask for last 12 months' management accounts, last filed accounts, current cash flow forecast, and evidence of the trigger event.
- Public interest: that granting relief is in the interest of council taxpayers (loss of an essential service to the community, loss of significant local employment, loss of a heritage asset). We weigh the cost of relief against the benefit.

The Panel may consult the ward councillor and the Aldershire Business Hub before deciding.

## What you can get

- Up to 100% relief on the bill for a fixed period (typically 3 to 12 months)
- Partial relief in combination with a payment plan

The 25% of the relief that falls on the council's General Fund (75% being recovered through the rates retention scheme) is the practical limit on what we can afford to grant. We publish the budget for hardship relief in the annual financial settlement.

## How to apply

- Complete "Hardship Relief Application" at aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/hardship
- Phone the business rates team on 01632 960 220 to discuss first
- Email: businessrates@aldershire.gov.uk

Decisions are usually made within 28 days. Where the Panel needs to meet, decisions take up to 8 weeks.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot grant hardship relief, advise on whether you would qualify, or comment on the merits of an application. It can email the application form and book a callback with a business rates officer.
