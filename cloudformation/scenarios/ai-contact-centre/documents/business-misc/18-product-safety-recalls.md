# Product safety and recalls

Product safety in the UK is enforced under the General Product Safety Regulations 2005, plus sector-specific regulations covering electrical equipment, toys, cosmetics, lighters, furniture and a wide range of other categories. Aldershire trading standards is the primary local enforcement body for products sold by Aldershire-based retailers.

## What we do

- Investigate complaints about unsafe products
- Sample products for testing (the Public Analyst at Aldershire County Laboratory holds UKAS accreditation for many product types)
- Issue prohibition notices, suspension notices, and recall notices under Part 4 of the Regulations
- Prosecute manufacturers, importers and retailers for putting unsafe products on the market

## OPSS

The Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) is the national body. They maintain the Product Safety Database and the official "Product Recall" page at gov.uk/product-recalls-faulty-goods. Trading standards services across the country feed into OPSS intelligence.

## Recent recalls relevant to Aldershire

Examples in the last 12 months that we have publicised through our website (aldershire.gov.uk/news/product-recalls):

- A counterfeit USB-C charger recalled for fire risk after sample failures at Aldermarket market
- A children's electric ride-on car recalled by the importer following multiple battery overheating reports
- A range of cheap halogen heaters that exceeded surface temperature limits and presented a burn hazard
- Several brands of imported decorative candles with leaded wicks (banned content)
- A model of cot mattress with non-compliant fire retardancy

## How to report an unsafe product

If a product is currently posing immediate danger (fire, electric shock, choking risk to a child):

- Make safe (unplug, remove from reach)
- Stop using it
- Report to Aldershire trading standards: 01632 960 280
- Or to OPSS via gov.uk/report-dangerous-product

You will be asked: product name, model number, where bought, when, what happened, photos if possible. Do not throw the product away — we may need it as evidence.

## Get recall notifications

Sign up for OPSS recall alerts at gov.uk/product-recalls-faulty-goods. Aldershire trading standards also posts weekly recall summaries to our X / Twitter account @AldershireDC and Facebook page.
