# Aldershire on social media — official accounts

Aldershire District Council uses social media for service updates, news, public consultation and rapid emergency communication. Official accounts are run by the council's Communications Team and follow the guidance in the LGA's "Social Media — Guidance for Local Authorities" framework and our own Social Media Policy.

## Official accounts

- X (Twitter): @AldershireDC — main news, service updates, traffic and bin alerts
- Facebook: facebook.com/AldershireDistrictCouncil — community news, longer pieces
- LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/aldershire-district-council — recruitment, business engagement
- YouTube: youtube.com/@AldershireDC — webcasts of council meetings, time-lapse and explainer content
- Instagram: @aldershire_dc — parks, civic events, photography
- Threads: @aldershire_dc — mirror of selected X content

We do not currently have an official TikTok or Bluesky presence; this is reviewed annually by the Communications Manager.

## Service-specific accounts

- @AldershireWaste (X) — bin collection day updates, freezing weather and missed-collection alerts
- @AldershireParks (X) — parks news, events, ranger-led walks
- @AldermarketMus (X / Instagram) — Aldermarket Museum exhibitions and events
- @AldermarketTH (X) — Town Hall events, weddings, civic photo opportunities

The full list of official accounts is at aldershire.gov.uk/social-media.

## What we post

- Service updates (bin collection changes around bank holidays, public holidays affecting our offices)
- Council news (press releases, decisions of public interest)
- Public consultation invitations
- Job vacancies
- Emergency information (severe weather, public health, evacuations)
- Events at council venues (Town Hall, Corn Exchange, libraries)
- "Did you know" engagement content highlighting council services

## Response times

We monitor messages on X and Facebook during office hours (Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00). We aim to respond within 4 working hours. Out-of-hours emergencies should always go to the 24/7 emergency line.

We do not handle individual service requests through social media — they are routed to the appropriate team. For most cases, the right routes are:

- Phone: 01632 960 200
- Online: aldershire.gov.uk
- This bot

## Beware of unofficial accounts

There are several unofficial accounts using "Aldershire" in the handle. Trust only those listed at aldershire.gov.uk/social-media. We will never ask for personal information (NI number, bank details, full date of birth) by direct message. If in doubt, phone us.

## Following our councillors

Many councillors have personal social media accounts. These are their own and not council-controlled. The councillors' code of conduct (under the Localism Act 2011) governs their conduct online; complaints about a councillor's online conduct go to the Monitoring Officer at monitoringofficer@aldershire.gov.uk.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not post on the council's behalf. It can email the social media directory and route media enquiries to the press office.
