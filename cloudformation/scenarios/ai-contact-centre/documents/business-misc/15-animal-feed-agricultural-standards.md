# Animal feed and agricultural standards

Aldershire trading standards enforces a body of agricultural legislation across the rural Aldershire Vale, where some 320 working farms produce dairy, beef, lamb, cereals and small-scale market garden produce.

## Animal feed

Under the Animal Feed (Composition, Marketing and Use) (England) Regulations 2015, feed manufacturers, packers and retailers must:

- Register feed business establishments with us
- Comply with composition standards (no banned protein sources, declared mineral content)
- Apply correct labelling — feed type, intended species, ingredients, additives, batch number, manufacture date, durability date
- Maintain traceability records for two years

We carry out scheduled and intelligence-led inspections. Feed sampling is performed annually for Aldershire mills; samples go to the Public Analyst at Aldershire County Laboratory. Failures most commonly relate to mislabelling rather than composition, but each year a small number of microbial or contaminant failures trigger product withdrawal.

## Agricultural produce standards

We enforce produce labelling standards under the Agricultural Produce (Grading and Marking) Act 1928 and successor regulations. Inspections check:

- Egg labelling (free range / barn / caged classification, traceability code, packaging date)
- Fruit and vegetable marketing standards
- Beef labelling under retained Regulation (EC) No 1760/2000 (origin, slaughterhouse, cutting plant)

## Animal health and welfare

Animal welfare on farms is led by the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA), but trading standards has supporting roles in:

- Movement records (cattle passports, sheep flock registers)
- Welfare in transit (Welfare of Animals (Transport) (England) Order 2006) — particularly at Aldermarket Livestock Market on Tuesdays
- Pet shop licensing (delegated to environmental health, see separate guidance)

## Plant health

Notifiable plant pests and diseases must be reported to the APHA. Aldershire trading standards supports inspections at the two licensed Aldershire plant nurseries.

## Reporting concerns

- Animal welfare emergency at a farm: APHA on 03000 200 301
- Animal feed concern: Aldershire trading standards 01632 960 280
- Plant health: APHA Plant Health and Seeds Inspectorate
- Cattle / sheep movement records: Aldershire trading standards 01632 960 280

The bot cannot inspect a farm, sample feed, or advise on movement records. It can take a complaint and route it to trading standards or the appropriate national agency.
