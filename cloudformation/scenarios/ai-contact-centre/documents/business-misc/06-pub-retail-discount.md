# Retail, hospitality and leisure discount

Central government has run a relief scheme for retail, hospitality and leisure businesses since the COVID period. The current iteration is the Retail, Hospitality and Leisure Business Rates Relief Scheme 2024-25 (RHLR), administered locally by Aldershire District Council under section 47 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988.

## The scheme

For 2024-25:

- 75% relief on business rates bills for eligible occupied retail, hospitality and leisure properties
- Cash cap: a maximum of £110,000 per business (across all of the business's properties in England)
- Subsidy control: subject to the Minimal Financial Assistance limits under the Subsidy Control Act 2022 (£315,000 over three years across all subsidies, including this one)

## Who is eligible

The scheme covers properties that are wholly or mainly used as one of:

- Shops (newsagents, butchers, hardware, chemists, charity shops, post offices, etc.)
- Cafes, restaurants, pubs, takeaways
- Hairdressers, nail bars, barbers, beauty salons, dry cleaners
- Cinemas, theatres, museums, art galleries
- Hotels, guest houses, self-catering accommodation
- Sports clubs, gyms, swimming pools, ten-pin bowling, soft play
- Wedding venues, bingo halls, amusement arcades

Excluded: financial services (banks, building societies, betting shops, payday lenders, pawnbrokers), professional services (solicitors, accountants, estate agents), medical services (vets, dentists, doctors, osteopaths), post-office sorting offices, casinos.

## How to apply

The discount is applied automatically to qualifying accounts. If you believe your property qualifies but the discount is missing from your bill, contact us:

- Phone: 01632 960 220
- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/rhlr
- Email: businessrates@aldershire.gov.uk

If you do not wish to receive the relief (for example, because you would breach the £110,000 cash cap or exceed the subsidy threshold), you can refuse it. We will issue a revised bill.

## Past schemes

For 2023-24 the relief was 75% with a £110,000 cap. For 2022-23 the relief was 50% with a £110,000 cap. We hold records back to 2019-20 (Expanded Retail Discount).
