# Appealing your business rates — the VOA process

Aldershire District Council does not set the rateable value (RV) of your property. The Valuation Office Agency (VOA), an executive agency of HMRC, sets RVs and maintains the rating list. If you believe your RV is wrong, you appeal to the VOA, not to the council.

## The Check, Challenge, Appeal (CCA) process

CCA replaced the old "appeal" system in 2017. It has three stages and is run online via gov.uk/correct-your-business-rates:

### Check

You confirm or correct the facts about your property held by the VOA — floor area, age, layout, services. You will need:

- A Government Gateway account
- Your property's billing authority reference (top right of your bill from us)
- Plans, photos, lease details

The VOA must respond within 12 months. If they accept your factual changes, the RV may be adjusted automatically.

### Challenge

If you disagree with the RV after Check is closed, you submit a Challenge within 4 months. You must give a reason — for example:

- The RV is too high compared to comparable properties
- A material change in circumstances has reduced the value (long-term road works, demolition of nearby anchor tenant, fire damage)
- The property should be deleted from the list (it is no longer non-domestic)

The VOA must decide within 18 months. They may agree, partly agree, or reject.

### Appeal

If you disagree with the Challenge decision, you have 4 months to appeal to the Valuation Tribunal for England (independent of VOA and council). The Tribunal hears the case and issues a binding decision. There is a fee (£150 for small businesses, £300 otherwise) which is refunded if you win.

## What about the bill while you appeal?

You must continue to pay your business rates as billed during a CCA process. If you win, the council will refund the overpayment with interest from the effective date of the change.

## When the council can help

We cannot advise on the merits of an appeal, but we can:

- Confirm the property's current RV and the multiplier we apply
- Provide your billing authority reference and copies of past bills
- Apply the revised RV to your account once the VOA notifies us of a change

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot lodge a Check, Challenge or Appeal — those go directly to the VOA via Government Gateway. It can email a fact sheet, find your billing authority reference, and route urgent billing queries to a human officer.
