# Charity rate relief

Registered charities and Community Amateur Sports Clubs (CASCs) occupying non-domestic property wholly or mainly for charitable purposes are entitled to mandatory rate relief of 80% under section 43(6) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988.

## Mandatory 80% relief

The 80% relief is mandatory: Aldershire District Council must grant it where the eligibility tests are met. The property must be:

- Occupied by a charity registered with the Charity Commission, an exempt charity, or a registered CASC
- Used wholly or mainly for the charitable purposes of the charity (or, where charitable shops, used for selling goods donated to the charity)

Examples that typically qualify: charity shops, scout huts, community centres run by charitable trusts, hospices, museums run by charitable trusts, Aldershire Hospice's three retail premises in Aldermarket.

## Discretionary top-up to 100%

Aldershire District Council operates a discretionary top-up scheme under section 47 of the 1988 Act. We can award a further 20% relief to bring the bill to zero, subject to:

- The charity demonstrating local benefit to Aldershire residents
- Trustees confirming the property is not let to a non-charitable subtenant
- The charity not having reserves above three years' running costs (we ask for the latest annual return filed with the Charity Commission)

The Discretionary Relief Panel (a committee of three councillors plus the s151 Officer) considers applications quarterly. Decisions are notified in writing within 14 days.

## How to apply

- Mandatory 80%: complete the "Charity Rate Relief — Mandatory" form at aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/charity-relief
- Discretionary 20%: complete the "Charity Rate Relief — Discretionary Top-up" form on the same page
- Phone: 01632 960 220 to discuss before applying

You will be asked for the charity registration number, latest accounts, and a description of how the premises are used.

## CASCs

Community Amateur Sports Clubs registered with HMRC are entitled to the same 80% mandatory relief. Aldershire's discretionary top-up applies on the same basis. Clubs in scope include the Aldermarket Bowling Club, Aldershire Vale Cricket Club, and Aldermarket Athletics Club.
