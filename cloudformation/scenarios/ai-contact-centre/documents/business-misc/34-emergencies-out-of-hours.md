# Council emergencies — 24/7 emergency line

Aldershire District Council operates a 24/7 emergency line for issues that cannot wait until the next working day. The line is for genuine emergencies in the council's area of responsibility; it is not a general enquiry line and is not the police, ambulance or fire service.

## In a life-threatening emergency

Always 999 first.

- Police: 999 (or 101 non-urgent)
- Fire: 999
- Ambulance: 999
- NHS: 111
- Aldershire Constabulary domestic abuse hotline: 0808 200 0247 (24/7)
- National Domestic Abuse Helpline: 0808 2000 247 (24/7)
- Samaritans: 116 123

Once any immediate danger is being handled, the council emergency line can be called for the council's part of the response.

## When to call the council emergency line

Call us out of hours (evenings, weekends, bank holidays) for:

- Homelessness with nowhere safe to stay tonight (statutory duty under the Housing Act 1996)
- Urgent housing repairs in council-owned tenancies (gas leak, no heating in winter for vulnerable resident, severe water leak)
- Child protection or adult safeguarding concerns where the County's Out-of-Hours team is needed (we route through to the joint OOH service)
- Fly-tipping that is dangerous (e.g. blocking the highway, asbestos, hazardous chemicals — police 999 first if highways risk)
- Anti-social behaviour at council venues (Town Hall, leisure centres, parks) outside opening hours
- Serious environmental health risks (sewage flood, food premises with imminent risk to public health)
- Civil contingencies — formal council activation under the Civil Contingencies Act 2004 (severe weather, flood, major incident)

## The emergency number

01632 960 999 (24/7)

This routes to a contracted out-of-hours call handling service, with on-call duty officers from the council's housing, environmental health, safeguarding and emergency planning teams. They have authority to commit council resources in the moment.

The same number rings during office hours for completeness, but normal Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 calls are better made on 01632 960 200 (the main switchboard).

## What we will not do out of hours

- Take routine service requests (bin missed, street light out, planning enquiry — these wait for the next working day)
- Process payments (council tax, business rates, fines)
- Discuss live planning applications, complaints, FOIs

The duty officer will record your details and pass them to the relevant team for the next working day.

## Civil emergencies

Aldershire District Council is a Category 1 responder under the Civil Contingencies Act 2004. In a major emergency we coordinate with Aldershire Constabulary, the Aldermarket Hospital Foundation Trust, the Environment Agency, and Aldershire Resilience Partnership. Public information is published at aldershire.gov.uk/emergencies and on the @AldershireDC X account.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot is not a routing line for life-threatening emergencies. If your situation is urgent, hang up and dial 999 or the council emergency line directly. The bot will offer those numbers prominently when it detects safeguarding language.
