# Subject Access Requests (SARs)

A Subject Access Request (SAR) is a request by an individual for a copy of the personal data Aldershire District Council holds about them. SARs are governed by the UK GDPR (Article 15) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Your rights

You can ask us:

- For confirmation that we are processing your personal data
- For a copy of that personal data
- For information about why we have it, where we got it from, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how to exercise your other data rights

This is your right; we cannot charge for the first request (unless it is "manifestly unfounded or excessive"), and we must respond within one calendar month, extendable by two further months for complex requests.

## How to make a request

- Online form: aldershire.gov.uk/sar
- Email: dpo@aldershire.gov.uk
- Letter: Data Protection Officer, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH

You can request verbally — but we strongly encourage written requests so the scope is clear.

## What we will ask for

- Your full name and any other names we may hold you under (maiden name, previous address)
- Your date of birth
- Proof of identity (passport, driving licence, utility bill in the last 3 months) — we will not accept the request without ID, to protect your data
- The information you want — any reasonable narrowing helps us respond faster (date range, specific service area)

If you are asking on behalf of someone else, we need their written consent and your ID. For under-12s, parental requests are accepted from those with parental responsibility. Over-12s usually exercise their own rights.

## What you will receive

Within 30 calendar days (extended where necessary) we will provide:

- A copy of the personal data we hold about you, in a portable format (PDF / CSV)
- Information on our purposes, recipients, retention periods
- Confirmation of your right to rectification, erasure, restriction, objection
- Information on your right to complain to the ICO

We may redact third-party personal data, legally privileged material, or material that would prejudice live investigations. We tell you what types of redactions we have applied.

## Other rights under UK GDPR

- Right to rectification — correcting inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — limited in public-task processing
- Right to restriction — pause processing while a dispute is resolved
- Right to data portability — for consent / contract-basis processing
- Right to object — including against direct marketing (absolute) and against profiling

## Complaining

If you are dissatisfied, complain first to our Data Protection Officer (dpo@aldershire.gov.uk). If still dissatisfied, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not retrieve personal data files. It can route a SAR to the DPO with your basic details (you will then need to provide ID separately).
