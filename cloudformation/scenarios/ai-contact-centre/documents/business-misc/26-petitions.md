# Petitions to the council

Aldershire District Council operates a petitions scheme under section 11 of the Local Government Act 2000 and our own Constitution. Petitions are a way for residents to bring matters to the council's attention and, depending on the size of the petition, trigger different forms of council response.

## Who can sign

Any person who lives, works, or studies in Aldershire can sign a petition addressed to the council. Children may sign with parental awareness. We do not require an age check; we do require a name, postcode, and signature (electronic signatures count for online petitions).

## Petitions thresholds

- Any petition with at least 50 valid signatures: receives a written response from the relevant Cabinet Member or Service Director within 20 working days
- 500 signatures: triggers consideration at the next meeting of the relevant Scrutiny Committee
- 1,500 signatures: triggers a debate at Full Council
- 3,000 signatures: triggers the right to call senior officers (Director or above) to give evidence at Scrutiny

These thresholds apply to petitions about matters within the council's powers. Out-of-scope subjects include policing, schools, social care, and NHS — we forward those to the appropriate body and acknowledge to the petitioners.

## How to submit a petition

- Online petition tool: aldershire.gov.uk/petitions (preferred — collects signatures with postcode validation, is auditable)
- Paper petition: deliver to Democratic Services, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH
- Email scanned paper sheets to democraticservices@aldershire.gov.uk

Each paper page must show the petition wording at the top. The first signatory is treated as the lead petitioner unless another is nominated.

## What we will not accept

- Anonymous petitions
- Petitions making unfounded allegations against named individuals
- Petitions duplicating one resolved in the last 6 months
- Petitions that are vexatious, racist, sexist, otherwise discriminatory, or in breach of the law
- Repeated submissions on the same subject by the same lead petitioner

We tell the lead petitioner promptly if a petition is rejected, and explain why, with a route to challenge the rejection.

## Recent petitions

In the last municipal year, Aldershire received 24 petitions. Examples:

- 2,140 signatures opposing a proposed reduction in evening opening hours at Aldermarket Library — debated at Full Council, decision changed
- 880 signatures asking for a zebra crossing at Vale Road school gates — referred to Aldershire County Council (highways authority) with a council letter of support
- 612 signatures opposing planning application 23/0547 — considered as objections within the planning process, not the petitions process
- 56 signatures calling for an extra dog-fouling bin in Mill Hill Park — actioned, bin installed within 6 weeks
