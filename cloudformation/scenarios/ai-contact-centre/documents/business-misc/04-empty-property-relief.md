# Empty property rate relief

When a non-domestic property becomes unoccupied, Aldershire District Council applies an automatic exemption for a fixed period. This is set in primary legislation under the Non-Domestic Rating (Unoccupied Property) (England) Regulations 2008.

## Standard exemption periods

- Most properties: 3 months full exemption from the date the property becomes empty
- Industrial and warehouse premises: 6 months full exemption
- Listed buildings: full exemption while empty (no time limit)
- Properties with rateable value below £2,900: full exemption while empty
- Properties owned by charities, where the next use will be wholly or mainly charitable: full exemption while empty
- Properties owned by CASCs, where the next use will be wholly or mainly that of a CASC: full exemption while empty

After the exemption period ends, full business rates become due, including the standard multiplier (no SBRR on unoccupied premises).

## What "empty" means

A property is treated as unoccupied from the day after the previous occupier vacates and removes their stock and trade fixtures. Token occupation (leaving a chair and a phone in the unit) does not avoid empty rates and may be challenged as anti-avoidance under the Levelling Up and Regeneration Act 2023.

## The 6-week reset rule

If a property is reoccupied for at least 6 continuous weeks (post 1 April 2024 the rules were revised to 13 weeks under recent regulations — check the current position) and then emptied again, the 3 (or 6) month exemption restarts. This rule has been used legitimately by landlords arranging short occupations between long voids; see also anti-avoidance below.

## Anti-avoidance

The council reviews suspected sham occupations. Indicators include occupations of short duration with no genuine trading activity, no utilities consumption, no signage, and no presence on the electoral / business directory. Where avoidance is identified, we issue revised bills and may pursue penalties under the Local Government Finance Act 1992 (as amended).

## How to claim

- Tell us when your property becomes empty: aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/empty-property
- Phone: 01632 960 220

You will be asked for the date of vacation, forwarding contact details, and (where applicable) evidence of the previous tenant's departure (lease end, email correspondence).

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot apply or remove empty property relief. It can record the date the property became empty against your account and arrange a callback from a business rates officer.
