# Press office — media enquiries

Aldershire District Council's Communications Team handles media enquiries from journalists, broadcasters, and online publishers covering the district. Routine media engagement is part of our duty of openness under the Localism Act 2011 and our standing as a public authority under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

## Who we work with

- Aldermarket Gazette (weekly print, daily online)
- Vale Echo (weekly print)
- BBC Radio Aldershire (local radio, regional news)
- Aldershire Live (online-only news)
- ITV Anglia and BBC Look East (regional TV coverage)
- National press, where Aldershire stories are picked up
- Trade and specialist press (e.g. Local Government Chronicle, Building, Public Finance)
- Independent bloggers and citizen journalists

## How to contact us

- Press office direct: 01632 960 210
- Out-of-hours press line: 07700 900 010 (mobile, monitored evenings and weekends for genuine media needs)
- Email: press@aldershire.gov.uk

We aim to acknowledge media enquiries within 1 working hour and respond substantively within 4 working hours. Late-breaking enquiries (deadline within the hour) will be flagged in the email subject; we will respond as fast as we can or explain why we cannot.

## What we will provide

- Confirmed facts about council decisions, services, expenditure
- Quotes from a named spokesperson (Cabinet Member, Service Director, Chief Executive)
- Briefing background on policy issues
- Photographs from our library (royalty-free for editorial use, subject to credit)
- Access to council venues for filming where reasonable

## What we will not do

- Speculate about decisions not yet made
- Comment on individual cases (planning applications, benefit claims, council tax accounts) where data protection or legal privilege applies
- Provide quotes attributed to fictitious or third-party spokespeople
- Arrange interviews with elected members in their party-political role (those go through party press offices)
- Override decisions of independent bodies (e.g. the Standards Committee, Planning Committee)

## Press releases

Press releases are issued via:

- Email distribution list (journalists can subscribe at aldershire.gov.uk/press/subscribe)
- aldershire.gov.uk/news
- @AldershireDC X account, with key links and quotes

Embargoes are honoured. We will tell you about embargoed releases in advance and ask for confirmation that you accept the embargo.

## Filming and photography on council property

Most council property is open for filming on the same basis as any other public space, subject to safeguarding (e.g. requesting parents' permission when filming children at events). Some sites need permission and a small location fee:

- Town Hall and Council Chamber: contact mayors.office@aldershire.gov.uk
- Aldermarket Museum and parks: contact press@aldershire.gov.uk
- Council leisure centres: contact the operator (Aldermarket Active CIC)

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not respond to media enquiries. It will tell journalists to contact press@aldershire.gov.uk directly during the working day, or use the out-of-hours line for genuine media-deadline situations.
