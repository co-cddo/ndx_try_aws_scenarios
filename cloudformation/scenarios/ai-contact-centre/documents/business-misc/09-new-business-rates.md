# New business — getting onto the business rates list

When a business takes occupation of non-domestic premises in Aldershire, several things happen. This guide walks through the rate-setting process for a new occupier.

## You must tell us when you start trading

By law (Local Government Finance Act 1988, schedule 9, paragraph 5) the new occupier must notify the billing authority — that is, Aldershire District Council — within 21 days of becoming responsible for the property.

Tell us by:

- Online form: aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/new-occupier
- Phone: 01632 960 220
- Email: businessrates@aldershire.gov.uk

You will need: trading name, registered company number (if a limited company) or VAT number, the date you took occupation, the property address, the previous occupier's name (if known), and a billing contact email.

## Completion notices for new buildings

If the property is a brand-new building or a substantial conversion (e.g. office to residential reverted to office), it may not yet be on the rating list. The council issues a completion notice under schedule 4A of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 specifying the day from which the property is deemed to be complete. We notify the VOA, who add the property to the list and assign a rateable value.

The completion notice can be challenged within 4 weeks of issue if you believe the property is not in fact complete (e.g. essential services not yet connected). Appeals go to the Valuation Tribunal.

## What you will receive

Within 4-8 weeks of notifying us, you should receive:

1. A welcome letter explaining your account
2. Your business rates account reference (which you should quote in all correspondence)
3. The first bill, pro-rated from the date of occupation
4. Information on Small Business Rate Relief if your RV is under £15,000
5. Information on the Retail, Hospitality and Leisure discount if applicable

## Aldershire Business Hub introduction

We offer all new occupiers a free introductory call from the Aldershire Business Hub (01632 960 270, businesshub@aldershire.gov.uk). They can advise on:

- Available reliefs
- Local business networks
- Skills and apprenticeship support
- Tendering for council contracts
- Workspace grants for commercial improvement (subject to budget)

You can opt out of this call when notifying us of new occupation.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot register your new occupation, issue a completion notice, or set up a direct debit on the new account. It can take your details, prefill the new-occupier form, and book a callback from the business rates team.
