# Consumer Credit Act enforcement

Aldershire trading standards has duties under the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (as amended by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 and subsequent legislation) to investigate certain credit-related offences and unfair practices. Most consumer credit regulation now sits with the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), but trading standards retains a role in:

- Unauthorised credit broking
- Misleading credit advertising
- Misleading hire-purchase, conditional sale, or rent-to-own marketing
- Unauthorised debt collection practices that fall outside the FCA's remit
- Illegal money lending (loan sharks) — referrals to the Illegal Money Lending Team for England

## What we see in Aldershire

The most common consumer credit complaints reaching us are:

- Used-car dealers selling on hire purchase without explaining the agreement properly, or applying excessive admin/early-settlement fees
- Doorstep credit sellers who are no longer authorised post-FCA migration
- Online "buy now pay later" providers operating without the required disclosures
- Aggressive in-house debt collection by retailers (rather than authorised debt collection agencies)
- Loan sharks operating in cash from house addresses

## Loan sharks (illegal money lending)

Loan sharks lend cash without FCA authorisation, often charging extortionate interest, and use threats or violence for repayment. They thrive in communities under financial pressure.

Report to the England Illegal Money Lending Team:

- 24/7 hotline: 0300 555 2222
- Text: LOANSHARK to 60003
- stoploansharks.co.uk

Reports can be anonymous. Victims are not prosecuted for borrowing from a loan shark, and may have their unlawful debt written off.

## Misleading credit advertising

Adverts for credit products must show the representative APR, total amount repayable, and various other prescribed information. Misleading credit ads (e.g. "0% APR" with hidden charges) can be reported to:

- Aldershire trading standards: 01632 960 280
- Citizens Advice: 0808 223 1133
- The Advertising Standards Authority: asa.org.uk

## What we cannot deal with

We do not handle individual disputes about regulated FCA matters (most consumer credit). For these:

- Free advice: Citizens Advice 0808 223 1133, MoneyHelper 0800 138 7777
- Complaints about FCA-authorised firms: The Financial Ombudsman Service, financial-ombudsman.org.uk, 0800 023 4567
