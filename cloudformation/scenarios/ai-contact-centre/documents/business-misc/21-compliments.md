# Compliments — telling us what is going well

Aldershire District Council captures compliments alongside complaints. They tell us what is working, recognise individual staff, and inform our annual Customer Insight report to the Cabinet.

## What we count as a compliment

Any positive feedback about a council service, member of staff, contractor, or volunteer that goes beyond a routine "thank you" recorded at the moment of the interaction. Examples we have logged this year:

- A bin loader who returned a wallet found in a recycling bin to its owner
- A Council Tax officer who walked a recently bereaved customer through three options for managing the deceased's account, in person, taking 90 minutes
- A planning officer who proactively spotted that an applicant's neighbour had not been consulted because of a postcode error
- An environmental health team that resolved a noisy-neighbour case from initial report to closure within 14 days

## How to leave a compliment

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/compliments
- Phone: 01632 960 200
- Email: customerservices@aldershire.gov.uk
- In person at the Council Reception
- Through a councillor, who will pass it to the Customer Services team

You can name the staff member or team, or simply describe the service. We will not use your details without consent if your compliment is published in our annual report.

## What happens with a compliment

- Logged in our Customer Insight system
- Forwarded to the named staff member's line manager (almost always read out at the next team meeting)
- Considered for the quarterly "Aldermarket Star" staff recognition scheme
- Aggregated quarterly for the Customer Insight report to Cabinet
- Sometimes shared, with consent, on the council's social media accounts

## Why this matters

Compliments are not vanity. They tell us what good service looks like in practice, in our own words rather than the abstract language of the corporate plan. They help defend services that are under budget pressure. They balance the picture we get from complaints, which by nature concentrates on what has gone wrong.

Every quarter the Customer Services team reports the ratio of compliments to complaints to the Audit and Performance Committee. In Q3 2024-25 the ratio was 1.4 compliments to every 1 complaint, against an LGA benchmark median of 0.6.

## Compliments to councillors

Compliments to elected members are forwarded to the member, with copies to the Group Leader and the Monitoring Officer (where service-related), at the member's request.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot will happily take a compliment and route it. It cannot promise that a specific named staff member will read it personally, but they almost always do.
