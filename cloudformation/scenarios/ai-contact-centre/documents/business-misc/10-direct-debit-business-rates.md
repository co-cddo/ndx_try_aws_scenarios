# Paying your business rates by Direct Debit

Direct Debit is the cheapest, most reliable way to pay business rates. Aldershire District Council collects more than 78% of business rates by Direct Debit and we encourage all ratepayers to use it.

## Instalments — 10 or 12

By default, we spread your annual bill over 10 monthly instalments (April to January). You can request 12 monthly instalments (April to March) by contacting us before the financial year starts.

Why some choose 10: the cash flow advantage of two payment-free months at the start of the new financial year.

Why some choose 12: smoother monthly cash flow, smaller individual payments.

Both options collect the same total amount.

## Choice of payment dates

Direct Debit can be taken on:

- The 1st of the month
- The 15th of the month
- The last working day of the month

Pick the one that aligns with your business income cycle.

## How to set up a Direct Debit

You will need:

- Your business rates account reference (top right of your bill)
- The name and address shown on the bill
- Your bank account number and sort code
- Authority to set up Direct Debits on the account (some company accounts require dual authorisation)

Set up by:

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/direct-debit (recommended, instant)
- Phone: 01632 960 220 (single ratepayers only — dual-authorised accounts must be set up online or by paper mandate)
- Paper mandate: download from the website, complete and post to Business Rates Team, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH

## Direct Debit Guarantee

Direct Debits are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee. If the council takes the wrong amount, you are entitled to an immediate refund from your bank. We will then re-collect the correct amount.

We notify you in writing of any changes to the amount or date at least 10 working days in advance.

## Cancelling a Direct Debit

You can cancel at any time via your bank or building society. Please tell us as well so we can issue revised payment arrangements; if your account simply lapses to "unpaid" we will follow our standard recovery process (reminder, summons, liability order).

## Other payment options

If Direct Debit is not for you, we accept payment by online card, BACS (using account 60-83-71, account number 76341295, your account reference as the payment reference), telephone card payment, or cash/cheque at the Town Hall reception.

The bot cannot set up a Direct Debit (which requires bank authentication) or change your payment date. It can email the mandate form, take a callback request, or transfer you to a business rates officer.
