# Council meetings, agendas, and webcasts

Most decisions of Aldershire District Council are taken in public meetings. Meetings, agendas, papers and minutes are published in line with the Local Government Act 1972 (as amended), the Openness of Local Government Bodies Regulations 2014, and our own Constitution.

## Types of meeting

- Full Council: 7 times a year, the formal meeting of all 51 elected councillors. Sets the budget, council tax, the Constitution, and major plans.
- Cabinet: monthly. The Leader and 7 Cabinet Members take key executive decisions on policy and resource allocation.
- Scrutiny Committees: People (Resources & Communities), Place, and Audit & Performance. Hold the Cabinet to account, undertake themed inquiries, review pre-decision items.
- Planning Committee: every 4 weeks. Determines major and contentious planning applications.
- Licensing Committee: as required.
- Standards Committee: investigates councillor conduct complaints.
- Outside Bodies and Partnership meetings: variable.

The full schedule for the municipal year is published at aldershire.gov.uk/council-meetings/calendar.

## Agendas and papers

Agendas and most papers are published 5 clear working days before the meeting. Papers may be marked "exempt" or "confidential" and excluded from the public version where they meet a category in Schedule 12A of the Local Government Act 1972 (e.g. commercial confidentiality, legal privilege, individual personal data).

If you cannot find a paper you expected, or wish to challenge an exemption claim, contact Democratic Services on 01632 960 230 or democraticservices@aldershire.gov.uk.

## Webcasts

All Full Council, Cabinet, Scrutiny, Planning Committee and Licensing Committee meetings are webcast live and archived for 6 months at aldershire.gov.uk/webcasts. Older recordings are kept on the council's YouTube channel indefinitely. Subtitles are auto-generated; we make formal British Sign Language interpretation available on request 5 working days before a meeting.

## Attending in person

Members of the public are welcome to attend any non-exempt meeting in person at the Town Hall, Aldermarket Square. The Council Chamber accommodates 60 in the public gallery. No prior booking required. Reasonable adjustments (loop, BSL, easy-read agenda) are available on request.

## Speaking at meetings

You can:

- Ask a public question at Full Council or Cabinet (deadline noon, 5 working days before)
- Speak at the Planning Committee on a specific application (3-minute slot, request by noon, 2 working days before)
- Submit a petition (see separate article)

Procedures and forms at aldershire.gov.uk/council-meetings/get-involved.

## Minutes

Approved minutes are published within 5 working days of approval at the next meeting of the same body. Draft minutes (unconfirmed) are published within 10 working days of the meeting.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not take questions for full Council or arrange BSL interpretation. It can email a meeting reminder, send the agenda link, and route procedural enquiries to Democratic Services.
