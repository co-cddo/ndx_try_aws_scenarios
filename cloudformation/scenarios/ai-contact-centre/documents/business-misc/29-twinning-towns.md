# Aldershire's twin towns

Aldershire District Council maintains formal twinning arrangements with two European towns. Twinning links date back to the post-war period and continue through citizen exchanges, school links, business connections, and civic visits.

## Cherbourg, Normandy, France

Aldermarket has been twinned with Cherbourg since 1956. The link grew from contact between the towns' local newspapers in the years after the war and was formalised at a civic ceremony in the Aldermarket Corn Exchange.

Active strands:

- Annual schools exchange between Aldermarket Comprehensive School and Lycee Jean-Francois Millet
- Aldermarket Bowling Club annual fixture against the Cherbourg petanque club (alternating venue)
- Aldermarket Operatic Society biennial collaboration with the Cherbourg Choeur Saint-Sauveur
- Civic visits in alternate years (Mayor and small delegation; modest budget)
- Wreath-laying at the Cherbourg memorial each June by the Aldermarket British Legion branch

## Lubeck-Travemunde, Germany

The link with Lubeck-Travemunde dates from 1972, established through trade contacts at the Aldermarket Maritime Festival when freight links revived after the war. The connection is largely commercial and educational.

Active strands:

- Aldershire Vale Cricket Club's "Hanseatic Cup" against a Lubeck-Travemunde cricket XI (the latter playing on a matting wicket; an annual source of polite Anglo-German diplomacy)
- Sixth-form economics exchange between Aldermarket Sixth Form College and Hansa-Schule Lubeck
- Aldershire Business Hub small-business exchange — typically 10 SMEs from each side meeting annually for 3-day trade-link visits
- Civic dinner each November at the Lubeck Rathaus, with a return visit each May

## Twinning Association

The Aldershire Twinning Association is a voluntary body, separate from the council, that organises citizen-level exchanges. They are always recruiting; particular need for hosts willing to accommodate visitors during exchange weeks. Find them at aldershiretwinning.org.uk.

## Funding

Civic twinning costs are met from a small budget within the Mayor's Office (around £6,000 a year). All visits are non-political, paid for by the host where appropriate, and modest in scope. Expenses are published in the council's transparency data.

## Cherbourg-Octeville note

Cherbourg merged with neighbouring Octeville in 2000 to form Cherbourg-Octeville, and in 2016 was further consolidated into Cherbourg-en-Cotentin. The twinning arrangement carried forward to the new commune. Visits and signage refer pragmatically to "Cherbourg".
