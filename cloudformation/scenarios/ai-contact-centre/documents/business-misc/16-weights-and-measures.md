# Weights and measures — petrol pumps, scales, and measured sales

Aldershire trading standards enforces the Weights and Measures Act 1985 and associated regulations. Inspectors authorised under section 72 of the Act verify that retailers are measuring goods accurately, so the public are paying for what they actually receive.

## Petrol pumps and forecourts

Every fuel pump on every Aldershire forecourt is verified at installation and re-verified at intervals (typically 2-5 years depending on pump type) by an Inspector of Weights and Measures. Verified pumps carry a sticker with the inspection date.

We test each pump by drawing 5 or 10 litres into a calibrated proving measure and checking against the pump display. Tolerance is +/- 0.5% (or +/- 25ml on a 5-litre draw, whichever is larger). Failures trigger immediate sealing of the pump until repair and re-verification.

We also inspect:

- Display of unit price (pence per litre) prominently and clearly
- Consistency between pump display and till receipt
- Sale of "loose" fuel (pre-paid amount) versus "free flow"
- AdBlue and forecourt LPG dispensers

In 2024 we performed 142 forecourt visits across Aldershire's 31 fuel retailers. Three pumps were found out of tolerance (2 over-reading by 0.6%, 1 under-reading by 0.7%); all were repaired within 48 hours.

## Retail scales

Butchers, fishmongers, greengrocers, delicatessens and market stalls weighing food at point of sale must use approved-class scales (typically Class III). Inspections check:

- Type approval mark on the scale
- Verification mark in date
- Tare function operating correctly
- Pricing computation matches advertised unit price
- Bagged sales (e.g. potatoes) match the declared weight

## Pre-packed goods

We sample pre-packed goods (sliced bread, tea, cereal, sugar, drinks) for compliance with the average quantity system under the Weights and Measures (Packaged Goods) Regulations 2006. Three rules apply: (1) the average must be at or above the nominal quantity, (2) no more than 2.5% may be more than one tolerable negative error (TNE) below, and (3) none may be below twice the TNE.

## Beer pulls and short measure

Pubs serving draught beer or cider in pints or half-pints must give a full measure. We respond to "short measure" complaints and conduct test pours.
