# Freedom of Information requests

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA), anyone in the world can ask Aldershire District Council for recorded information that we hold. We must respond within 20 working days, either by providing the information, refusing it under a specified exemption, or asking for clarification.

## What counts as an FOI request

To count as a valid FOI request under section 8 of the Act, the request must:

- Be in writing (email or letter — phone requests are recorded as service requests instead)
- State the requester's real name (pseudonyms invalidate the request, though you can ask us to keep your details confidential when responding)
- Provide a return address (postal or email)
- Describe the information sought clearly enough for us to identify it

You do not need to mention the FOIA — we will treat any sufficiently clear request for recorded information as an FOI by default.

## How to ask

- Online form: aldershire.gov.uk/foi
- Email: foi@aldershire.gov.uk
- Letter: Information Governance Officer, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH

## What we publish anyway

Many requests cover information we already publish. Before submitting an FOI, browse:

- aldershire.gov.uk/about-us — corporate, financial, structural
- aldershire.gov.uk/transparency — spending over £500, contracts register, senior salaries, councillor allowances
- aldershire.gov.uk/data — published datasets

We update transparency data monthly under the Local Government Transparency Code 2015.

## Our response

Within 20 working days we will: send the information, send some of the information and explain the rest is exempt, or fully refuse and explain why. Common exemptions:

- Section 21: information already accessible (we will tell you where)
- Section 22: information intended for future publication
- Section 31: prejudice to law enforcement
- Section 36: prejudice to effective conduct of public affairs (rare)
- Section 40: personal data (we cannot share information about identifiable third parties)
- Section 41: confidential information provided by a third party
- Section 43: commercial interests

## Costs

There is no charge for most FOI responses. Where compliance would exceed the "appropriate limit" (£450 / 18 hours of work) under the Fees Regulations, we may refuse or quote a fee.

## Internal review and the ICO

If dissatisfied with our response, you can ask for an internal review within 40 working days. We will reply within a further 20 working days. If still dissatisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot does not handle FOIs directly. It can take basic details and email them to the FOI team, who will then respond within 20 working days from receipt.
