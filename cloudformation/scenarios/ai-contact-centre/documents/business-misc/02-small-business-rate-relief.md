# Small Business Rate Relief (SBRR)

Small Business Rate Relief (SBRR) reduces or eliminates the business rates bill for occupiers of small premises. Aldershire District Council administers SBRR under section 43 of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 and the Non-Domestic Rating (Small Business Rate Relief) Regulations.

## What you get

If you occupy a single non-domestic property in England with a rateable value (RV):

- Under £12,000: 100% relief — you pay nothing
- £12,000 to £15,000: tapered relief, decreasing from 100% at £12,000 to 0% at £15,000
- £15,001 to £50,999: no SBRR, but the small business multiplier (49.9p for 2024-25) is applied automatically

Properties with RV over £51,000 fall onto the standard multiplier (54.6p).

## Multiple properties

You can still get SBRR if you occupy more than one property, provided:

- All other properties each have an RV under £2,900, AND
- The total RV of all your properties is under £20,000 (£28,000 in Greater London)

You only get the relief on the main property, but the small business multiplier still applies to the others.

## How to apply

SBRR is not automatic — you must apply. Aldershire makes this straightforward:

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/business-rates/sbrr
- Phone: 01632 960 220
- Form: download "SBRR application" from the website, post to Business Rates Team, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH

You will need your business rates account reference (top right of your bill) and the trading name and address of the property.

## Backdating

We can backdate SBRR for up to six years subject to evidence of continuous occupation and that you have not held more than one ineligible property. Contact us if you think you have been overpaying.

## Telling us about changes

You must tell us within 21 days if you take on another property, your circumstances change, or you cease to occupy the property. Failure to notify can result in a recovered bill plus interest.
