# Complaints procedure — Stage 1, Stage 2, LGSCO

Aldershire District Council operates a two-stage corporate complaints procedure, with a final route of escalation to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman (LGSCO). The procedure is published in full at aldershire.gov.uk/complaints and complies with LGSCO good practice guidance.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about the quality, standard or delivery of a council service, the action of staff or contractors, or our failure to meet a published service standard. It is not:

- A first-time service request (e.g. "please collect my bin which has been missed") — log this with the service team
- A request for information (use the FOI procedure)
- A challenge to a council decision under a separate statutory appeal route (e.g. planning, council tax, benefits — these have their own appeal procedures)

If you are unsure, contact us and we will route your concern correctly.

## Stage 1 — service-level investigation

You raise a complaint:

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/complaints
- Phone: 01632 960 200
- Email: complaints@aldershire.gov.uk
- Letter: Customer Services, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AS1 1TH

We acknowledge within 3 working days. The relevant service manager investigates and replies within 15 working days. If we cannot reply within 15 working days (complex case requiring third-party involvement) we tell you and give a revised timescale.

The Stage 1 response will: summarise your complaint, explain what we found, say whether the complaint is upheld, partly upheld, or not upheld, set out any actions we will take, and tell you how to escalate to Stage 2 if dissatisfied.

## Stage 2 — independent review

If you remain dissatisfied with the Stage 1 outcome, ask for a Stage 2 review within 28 days. The case is reviewed by a Service Director (or the Monitoring Officer for complaints involving conduct). They may:

- Uphold the Stage 1 finding
- Substitute a different finding
- Order remedial action (e.g. a goodwill payment, a service improvement)

Stage 2 responses are issued within 25 working days.

## Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman (LGSCO)

If you remain dissatisfied after Stage 2, you can complain to the LGSCO. The LGSCO is an independent body that investigates complaints about councils:

- 0300 061 0614
- lgo.org.uk
- The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman, PO Box 4771, Coventry, CV4 0EH

The LGSCO normally requires you to have completed our internal procedure first. They have power to recommend financial remedies and service improvements; their recommendations are not legally binding but in practice almost always implemented.

## Equality Act 2010

If you need a reasonable adjustment to access the complaints procedure (e.g. British Sign Language interpreter, easy-read format, a carer to make the complaint on your behalf), tell us when you complain and we will arrange.

## What this AI cannot do

The bot cannot investigate or resolve complaints. It can take the details, log a Stage 1 complaint, and email you a confirmation reference.
