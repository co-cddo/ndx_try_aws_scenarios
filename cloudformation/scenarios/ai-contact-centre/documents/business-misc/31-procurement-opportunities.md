# Procurement opportunities — selling to the council

Aldershire District Council buys around £52m of goods, services and works each year. We follow the Public Contracts Regulations 2015 (and from October 2024, the Procurement Act 2023) and our own Contract Procedure Rules, which require open and fair competition.

## Where opportunities are advertised

- Find a Tender Service (FTS): for high-value contracts (over the threshold for goods/services / works)
- Contracts Finder: for contracts above £30,000 (including below-threshold) in the public sector
- Aldershire's e-procurement portal: aldershire.gov.uk/procurement (free registration, all open opportunities)

The portal is the simplest way to track opportunities — you register your interest by category (e.g. construction, ICT, professional services) and receive automatic alerts.

## Thresholds and processes

Approximate values for 2024:

- Under £5,000: at least one quote, sourced by the service team
- £5,000 to £29,999: 3 written quotes, evaluated against published criteria
- £30,000 to FTS threshold: open quotation via the portal, advertised on Contracts Finder
- Above FTS threshold: full open or restricted procedure, advertised on FTS and Contracts Finder, regulated tender timescales

The thresholds for FTS publication are set by central government and updated periodically. The current values are at gov.uk/transparency/find-a-tender.

## What we buy

Major spend categories:

- Waste services contract (currently with Allied Waste; 7-year contract from 2022)
- ICT and software (multi-supplier framework via NHS Shared Business Services or Crown Commercial Service)
- Highways grounds-maintenance
- Building works on council assets (Town Hall, depots, leisure centres)
- Professional services (legal, audit, surveying, design)
- Temporary accommodation (homelessness duties)

The full Contracts Register, listing every contract over £5,000 with title, supplier, value, duration and end date, is published quarterly under the Local Government Transparency Code 2015 at aldershire.gov.uk/transparency/contracts.

## Social value

Under the Public Services (Social Value) Act 2012 and our own Social Value Policy, every tender above £30,000 includes a 10% social value weighting. We expect bidders to demonstrate local employment, apprenticeships, environmental impact, support for VCSE sector, and reduction of carbon.

## Aldershire Pound

We track the proportion of council spend that is retained in the Aldershire local economy ("Aldershire Pound"). The current rate is 41% for 2023-24, against a target of 50% by 2027.
