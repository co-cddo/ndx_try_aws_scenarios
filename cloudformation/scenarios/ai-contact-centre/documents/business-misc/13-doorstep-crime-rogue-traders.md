# Doorstep crime and rogue traders

Doorstep crime — sometimes called rogue trading or distraction burglary — is a high-priority concern for Aldershire trading standards and the Aldershire Constabulary. Older and vulnerable residents are disproportionately targeted.

## What it looks like

Typical patterns reported in Aldershire over the last 12 months:

- Cold-callers offering "roof inspections", "moss treatment", or "tarmac driveways" door-to-door, then doing shoddy or fictional work for inflated prices
- Tree surgeons demanding immediate cash payment after an unsolicited "free quote" turned into work being done before consent was given
- Bogus utility-meter readers gaining entry to scope a property for distraction theft
- "Charity collectors" with a clipboard and no ID, harvesting financial details
- Pressure-selling of solar panels, conservatories, or insulation with finance agreements signed at the doorstep

## Your rights

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, you have a 14-day cooling-off period for almost all goods and services bought at the doorstep. The trader must give you written notice of this right; if they do not, the cooling-off period extends to 12 months.

You are not obliged to pay for unrequested work. You should not give cash to anyone arriving on your doorstep without prior arrangement.

## How to report

- Police 999 if you feel threatened, the trader is at your door now, or you suspect a distraction burglary in progress
- Police 101 for non-urgent reports
- Trading standards via Citizens Advice consumer helpline 0808 223 1133
- Aldershire trading standards direct: 01632 960 280
- Anonymously: Crimestoppers 0800 555 111

Report even if you did not part with money — intelligence builds the picture and protects others.

## No Cold Calling Zones

Aldershire trading standards has worked with parish councils to designate "No Cold Calling Zones" in specific streets, particularly in retirement-heavy areas (parts of Vale Road, Mill Hill, Ridge Park). Signs at each end of the zone advise legitimate cold callers to leave; persistent doorstep traders in these zones can be prosecuted under the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008.

## Buy with Confidence

Aldershire participates in the national "Buy with Confidence" scheme. Members are vetted local traders who have agreed to consumer-friendly terms. Find an approved trader at buywithconfidence.gov.uk.
