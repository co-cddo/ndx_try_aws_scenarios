# The Mayor of Aldershire — civic engagements and the Mayor's Charity

Aldershire District Council elects a Mayor and Deputy Mayor each May at the Annual General Meeting of Council. The role is largely ceremonial; it is the civic figurehead of the district, separate from the political Leader of the Council.

## What the Mayor does

- Chairs the formal meetings of Full Council
- Represents the council at civic occasions (Remembrance Sunday, Armed Forces Day, the Aldermarket Christmas Lights, the Vale Show)
- Hosts visiting dignitaries (the Lord Lieutenant, twin-town delegations, foreign consular visits)
- Champions the Mayor's Charity each year
- Signs Citizens' Awards (long service, exceptional contribution)
- Welcomes new citizens at the British citizenship ceremonies held at the Town Hall

The Mayor is not a political role. By convention, the Mayor steps back from party politics for the year of office and chairs Council impartially.

## Inviting the Mayor to your event

We welcome invitations from local groups, charities, schools and community events. Submit at least 4 weeks before the event:

- Online: aldershire.gov.uk/mayor/invite
- Email: mayors.office@aldershire.gov.uk
- Phone: 01632 960 240

Please include: event name, date, time, venue, description, organising body, expected attendance, any role the Mayor would be asked to play (cut a ribbon, present an award, give a short address).

The Mayor's Office runs a tight calendar (around 350 engagements a year), so early notice helps. The Deputy Mayor can attend where the Mayor is unavailable.

## Mayor's Charity

Each Mayor selects a charity (or up to three) at the start of their year. Charities are usually local Aldershire organisations or local branches of national charities. Mayoral fundraising for 2024-25 supports:

- Aldershire Hospice (palliative care)
- The Vale Foodbank
- Aldermarket Mental Health Together (a coalition of local mental-health peer-support groups)

Donations to the Mayor's Charity Fund are welcomed at aldershire.gov.uk/mayor/charity. Cheques payable to "Aldershire Mayor's Charity Trust" can be sent to the Mayor's Office, Town Hall.

## Citizens' Awards

The Mayor presents annual Citizens' Awards in May for outstanding voluntary contribution to the district. Nominations open in February at aldershire.gov.uk/mayor/citizens-awards. Past recipients include Aldermarket scout leaders of 30+ years, lifelong volunteers at the Vale Hospice charity shops, and the lollipop person at Mill Hill Primary School (29 years' service).
