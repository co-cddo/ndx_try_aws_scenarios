# Business support and grants — Aldershire Business Hub

The Aldershire Business Hub is the council's economic development team, working with the Aldershire Local Enterprise Partnership and central government's Department for Business and Trade to support businesses across the district.

## What the Hub does

- Free 1:1 advice for new and existing businesses
- Sign-posting to start-up loans, grants, and equity finance
- Support for tendering for public-sector contracts (council, NHS, central government)
- Skills brokerage with Aldershire College and the apprenticeship levy transfer scheme
- Workspace grants for commercial improvement, subject to annual budget allocation
- Networking events: monthly breakfast at the Corn Exchange (first Wednesday, 7:30am, free)
- Quarterly "Aldermarket High Street" forum bringing together retailers, the BID, and the council

## Current grant schemes (subject to availability)

### High Street Vibrancy Grant

Capital grants of up to £5,000 for shopfront improvements in designated high-street zones (Aldermarket High Street, Vale Road, Mill Lane, Ridge Road). Match-funding required (50%). Open March-October each year, decisions monthly.

### Net Zero Business Grant

Capital grants of up to £10,000 for energy efficiency measures (heat pumps, insulation, LED lighting, EV charging for fleet). Funded through the Public Sector Decarbonisation Scheme partnership.

### Rural Business Grant

For businesses outside Aldermarket and the larger villages. Up to £15,000 for productivity improvements, IT, marketing, premises adaptations.

### Start-Up Bursary

A £500 bursary for first-year businesses to cover initial professional fees (legal, accounting). Eligibility: trading less than 12 months, registered address in Aldershire, sole trader or limited company.

## State aid / subsidy control

All grants are awarded subject to the Subsidy Control Act 2022 and the £315,000 Minimal Financial Assistance threshold. We will ask you to declare past subsidies received over the previous three years.
