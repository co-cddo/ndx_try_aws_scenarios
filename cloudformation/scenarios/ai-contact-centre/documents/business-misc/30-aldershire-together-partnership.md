# Aldershire Together — partnership working across the district

Aldershire Together is the strategic partnership board for the district. It brings together the council, public services, voluntary sector, business and resident representatives to set shared priorities for the wellbeing of Aldershire residents. It operates under section 4 of the Local Government Act 2000 and the Localism Act 2011.

## Who is on the board

- Aldershire District Council (Leader, Chief Executive)
- Aldershire County Council (Lead Member for Communities, Chief Executive)
- Aldershire Constabulary (Chief Superintendent for the Aldermarket Basic Command Unit)
- Aldershire Integrated Care Board (NHS — primary care lead)
- Aldermarket Hospital Foundation Trust (CEO or deputy)
- Aldershire College and Aldershire University (Chairs)
- Aldershire Voluntary Action (umbrella for the local VCSE sector)
- Aldermarket Business Improvement District
- Two ward councillors elected annually by Full Council
- Three resident representatives recruited through open application

The board is chaired by the Council Leader; vice-chaired by the County's Lead Member.

## What it does

- Sets the Aldershire Together Plan, a 4-yearly strategic document with shared priorities (current plan: 2023-2027)
- Coordinates partnership delivery on:
  - Health inequalities (under section 116B of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007)
  - Community safety (under sections 5-7 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 — the partnership is the formal Community Safety Partnership)
  - Children and Families
  - Skills and economic development
- Holds joint accountability for the use of pooled funds (around £4.2m in 2024-25)
- Convenes thematic sub-groups: Mental Health; Domestic Abuse; Children Missing Education; Cost of Living

## Current priorities

Aldershire Together Plan 2023-2027 has 4 strategic themes:

1. A healthier Aldershire — closing health inequalities, focused on Mill ward and Vale East ward
2. A safer Aldershire — community safety, ASB, hate-crime reduction
3. A skilled Aldershire — apprenticeships, adult learning, and digital inclusion
4. A greener Aldershire — biodiversity, decarbonisation, active travel

The plan is published at aldershire.gov.uk/aldershire-together.

## Public meetings

Aldershire Together meets quarterly in public at the Town Hall. Agendas, papers and minutes follow the same publication standards as council meetings (5 clear days notice). Webcasts are available.

The next meeting is published at aldershire.gov.uk/aldershire-together/calendar.

- Become a resident representative — recruitment opens January each year
- Attend a public board meeting and ask a public question (deadline noon, 5 working days before)
