# LGBTQ+ safe-space services

Aldershire is home to people of every sexual orientation and gender identity, and the council's services are inclusive in design and practice. LGBTQ+ residents may also benefit from services that are explicitly designed for them, particularly around safety, mental wellbeing, and community connection.

## Aldershire LGBT+ Network

The Aldershire LGBT+ Network is a council-funded community organisation supporting LGBTQ+ residents of all ages.

- Phone: **01632 960 920**.
- Email: **info@aldershire-lgbt-network.example.org**.
- Drop-in: Tuesdays and Thursdays 4-7pm at Riverside Community Centre, Mill Road, Aldershire AL1 2RR.

Activities and services:

- Weekly social meets; specialist groups for under-25s, over-50s, parents of LGBTQ+ children, trans and non-binary residents, and asylum seekers.
- One-to-one support sessions on housing, harassment, work issues, and family conflict.
- Counselling (LGBTQ+ informed) at affordable sliding-scale fees, free for under-25s and means-tested adults.
- Annual Aldershire Pride event in July, alongside Pride month activities throughout June.

## Hate crime and personal safety

LGBTQ+ residents are over-represented in hate crime victims nationally and locally. Aldershire's joint Hate Crime Action Plan with Aldershire Constabulary (covered separately) includes:

- Specific outreach to underreporting groups including LGBTQ+ residents.
- The LGBT+ Network as a designated **third-party reporting centre**: residents can report hate crime there if they prefer not to go to the police directly. Reports are forwarded to Aldershire Constabulary with the resident's consent.
- A target-hardening grant programme up to £500 for households experiencing repeat homophobic, biphobic, or transphobic incidents.

## Galop helpline

For specialist anti-violence support: **Galop** national helpline **0800 999 5428** (Monday-Thursday 10am-8.30pm; Friday 10am-4.30pm). Galop covers domestic abuse, sexual violence, hate crime, conversion therapy, and "honour-based" abuse.

## Domestic abuse in same-sex relationships

LGBTQ+ residents experiencing domestic abuse can access the standard pathway through ADAS (**01632 960 511**, 24-hour helpline **01632 960 999**) and through Galop. Refuge and emergency accommodation through ADAS includes provision for trans women.

## Trans-specific support

- **TranzWiki**: national network of trans support groups, including local listings for Aldershire and surrounding districts.
- **Mermaids** (under-20s and families): **0808 801 0400**.
- **Aldershire NHS Gender Identity Service Liaison Worker**: based at Aldershire General Hospital, supports navigating the pathway to NHS Gender Identity Clinics. Refer through your GP.

## Conversion practices

So-called conversion practices (attempts to change someone's sexual orientation or gender identity) are recognised by Aldershire Council, NHS, and Aldershire Constabulary as a form of abuse. Disclosures are routed to safeguarding (adult or child as appropriate) and to Galop. Conversion therapy is opposed in policy across all UK government departments and is subject to ongoing legislative work.

## Workplace and service-provider inclusion

The council is a Stonewall Diversity Champion and ranked in the top 100 employers in the most recent Workplace Equality Index. Internal training in inclusive practice is mandatory for front-line staff, including the contact centre. The contact centre's bot is configured to use neutral language and to follow the resident's lead on pronouns and form of address.

## Reporting concerns

- **Hate crime in progress**: 999.
- **Non-emergency hate crime**: 101 or LGBT+ Network on 01632 960 920.
- **Domestic abuse**: ADAS 24-hour helpline 01632 960 999.
- **Concerns about a young person**: Aldershire MASH 01632 960 800 or Mermaids 0808 801 0400.
- **General council services**: 01632 960 100.
