# Hate crime: reporting and support

A hate crime is any criminal offence that is perceived by the victim or any other person to be motivated by hostility or prejudice based on a person's actual or perceived race, religion, sexual orientation, transgender identity, or disability. The College of Policing's perception-based definition is the standard used by Aldershire Constabulary and partner agencies.

A hate **incident** is non-criminal conduct meeting the same definition. We record and respond to both.

## How to report

- **999** in an emergency.
- **101** for non-urgent reports to Aldershire Constabulary.
- **True Vision** at **report-it.org.uk**: a national third-party reporting site.
- **Stop Hate UK** helpline on **0800 138 1625** (24/7, confidential).
- **Aldershire Council Community Safety** team on **01632 960 142** (Monday to Friday).
- **Aldershire Hate Crime Reporting Centres**: a network of 12 community venues across the district where staff are trained to take and forward reports. List at **aldershire.example.gov.uk/community-safety/hate-crime-centres**.

## What happens after a report

For criminal hate crimes:

1. Aldershire Constabulary investigate. The CPS may apply for an enhanced sentence on conviction under sections 145-146 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003.
2. The council's Community Safety team is informed (subject to consent in third-party cases) and offers practical and emotional support to the victim.
3. We co-ordinate with the housing provider if the victim wants to consider a managed move and with the Aldershire Sanctuary scheme if home security needs upgrading.

For non-criminal hate incidents the council leads the response, working with partners as appropriate.

## Aldershire Hate Crime Action Plan

The council and Aldershire Constabulary publish a joint Hate Crime Action Plan. The 2024-2027 plan focuses on:

- Increased reporting in under-reporting communities (we work with the Aldershire Multi-Faith Forum, the Aldershire LGBT+ Network, and the Disability Equality Group).
- Faster victim contact (target: 24 hours from report to first contact, currently met in 86% of cases).
- Schools-based prevention (every secondary school in the district participates in the annual hate-crime awareness fortnight).
- Reducing repeat victimisation through a dedicated repeat-victim case manager.

## Support services

- **Victim Support (Aldershire)**: free, confidential support for victims of any crime. **0808 168 9111** (24/7) or **victimsupport.org.uk**.
- **Galop**: LGBT+ specific anti-violence charity. **0800 999 5428**.
- **Tell MAMA**: Muslim hate crime support. **0800 456 1226**.
- **CST Community Security Trust**: Jewish community support. **0800 032 3263**.
- **Disability Rights UK helpline**: **0330 995 0400**.

## What you can expect from us

- A non-judgmental response. Your perception that the conduct was hate-motivated is recorded as such.
- Confidentiality. We do not contact the perpetrator or share your details without your consent.
- Practical support: home security checks, target-hardening grants up to £500 for high-risk cases, casework with your housing provider.
- Independent advocacy through Aldershire Advocacy CIC if you would like a third party to support you through the process.
