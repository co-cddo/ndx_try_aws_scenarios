# Carer support: assessments and breaks

Anyone who provides unpaid care to a family member or friend who could not manage without help is a carer in the meaning of the Care Act 2014. There are around 19,000 unpaid carers in the Aldershire district. Carers are entitled to council support in their own right, separately from the support given to the cared-for person.

## Statutory entitlements (Care Act 2014, section 10)

A carer has a statutory right to a **Carer's Assessment** if they appear to have needs for support that have arisen, or may arise, as a result of caring. The threshold is low: any carer can request and is entitled to receive an assessment.

The assessment covers:

- The impact of caring on the carer's wellbeing.
- The carer's wishes about whether and how much to care.
- Whether the carer wants and is able to work, study, train, or take part in social activities.
- The carer's own physical, mental, and emotional health.

If the carer has eligible needs (Care and Support (Eligibility Criteria) Regulations 2014), the council must provide support. Eligibility threshold is met if:

- The need arises because of providing care, **and**
- The need has, or is likely to have, a significant impact on the carer's wellbeing, **and**
- As a consequence, the carer is unable to achieve specified outcomes (looking after themselves, maintaining family relationships, working, etc.).

## How to request a Carer's Assessment

- Phone **01632 960 410** and ask for a Carer's Assessment.
- Online at **aldershire.example.gov.uk/social-care/carers**.
- Through the cared-for person's social worker.
- Through your GP, who can refer.

The assessment is free and there is no charge for any support that follows.

## Support that may be offered

- **Carer's Personal Budget**: a direct payment to spend on what helps you sustain caring. Typical uses: gym membership, taxi to medical appointments, garden help, technology to make caring easier.
- **Replacement care (respite)**: short stays in a care home for the cared-for person, or a paid worker coming into the home, so the carer has a break. Aldershire's Replacement Care Service offers up to **10 nights per year** for eligible carers, free of charge.
- **Emergency Carer Card**: a card in your wallet that, if you were taken ill, alerts emergency services that someone depends on you. Free, register on **01632 960 410**.
- **Carer's Annual Health Check**: free at most Aldershire GP practices, covering blood pressure, mental wellbeing, and flu/Covid vaccinations.
- **Information, advice, and advocacy** through Aldershire Carers Hub (commissioned partner).

## Aldershire Carers Hub

The Aldershire Carers Hub is a council-commissioned charity supporting carers across the district. Services include:

- Drop-in advice five days a week at the Aldershire Community Centre.
- Carers' peer support groups (12 across the district, including specialist groups for dementia carers, parent carers of children with disabilities, and male carers).
- A monthly newsletter, training programme, and trips programme.
- Support to navigate benefits (Carer's Allowance, Attendance Allowance for the cared-for, Carer's Premium on Universal Credit).

Phone **01632 960 580** or visit **aldershirecarershub.example.org**.

## Carer's Allowance

Carer's Allowance is a UK-wide benefit administered by DWP. The basic rate (April 2026) is **£81.90 per week** for carers providing 35+ hours of care per week to someone in receipt of a qualifying disability benefit. The claimant must earn no more than £151 per week. Apply at **gov.uk/carers-allowance** or phone the Carer's Allowance Unit on **0800 731 0297**.

## Crisis support

If your caring situation is at crisis point: phone **Adult Social Care** on **01632 960 850**. We can arrange emergency replacement care, hospital admission of the cared-for person where appropriate, or other immediate support.

## Hospital discharge

When the cared-for person is being discharged from hospital, the hospital social work team must consult you as a carer (Care Act 2014, section 60). You can request a Carer's Assessment as part of discharge planning if your caring role is changing.

## Legal framework

Care Act 2014 (sections 10, 20, 60); Care and Support (Eligibility Criteria) Regulations 2014; Aldershire Carers Strategy 2023-2028.
