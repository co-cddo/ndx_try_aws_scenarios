# Forced marriage: protection and support

A forced marriage is one where one or both spouses do not (or, in the case of some adults with learning disabilities or mental capacity issues, cannot) consent to the marriage and duress is involved. Forced marriage is a **criminal offence** in England and Wales under the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (section 121).

It is distinct from an arranged marriage, where families are involved in choosing a partner but both spouses freely consent.

## How Aldershire Council responds

If a person discloses to a council officer that they are at risk of forced marriage or have been forced into a marriage, the officer:

1. Listens, takes the disclosure seriously, and does not contact the family or the alleged perpetrators.
2. Assesses immediate risk: if there is risk of imminent travel abroad, this is treated as urgent.
3. Refers to **Aldershire Children's Services** (under-18s) or **Adult Social Care** (over-18s with care and support needs).
4. Refers to **Aldershire Constabulary** Public Protection Unit.
5. Contacts the **Forced Marriage Unit (FMU)** for specialist advice on **020 7008 0151** (Monday to Friday 9am-5pm) or, out of hours, the FCDO consular emergency line **020 7008 5000**.

## Forced Marriage Protection Orders

A Forced Marriage Protection Order (FMPO) is a civil order made by the family court under Part 4A of the Family Law Act 1996 (inserted by the Forced Marriage (Civil Protection) Act 2007). The order can include any prohibition or requirement the court considers necessary, for example:

- Prohibiting family members from removing the person from the UK.
- Requiring the surrender of the person's passport.
- Prohibiting threats, intimidation, or violence.
- Specifying who the person can have contact with.

Applications can be made by the person themselves, by a relevant third party (the council is a relevant third party for those at risk of forced marriage), or by a person with the leave of the court. The council's Legal Services team handles applications on referral.

Breach of an FMPO is a **criminal offence** punishable by up to 5 years' imprisonment.

## Forced Marriage Unit (FMU)

The FMU is a joint Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) and Home Office unit that supports victims and professionals. It can:

- Provide advice on cases.
- Support overseas rescues from forced marriage situations.
- Coordinate with British embassies and high commissions.
- Issue practical safety advice (the "spoon trick" hidden item, embassy contact details on small cards).

Contact: **020 7008 0151** or **fmu@fcdo.gov.uk**. In an overseas emergency: **020 7008 5000** (24/7).

## "Honour-based" abuse and related concerns

Forced marriage often sits alongside other forms of so-called honour-based abuse, including controlling behaviour, threats, and (rarely but seriously) family violence. We respond to these as integrated safeguarding cases. Aldershire's MARAC handles high-risk cases.

## Statutory and policy framework

Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (section 121); Forced Marriage Act 2007; Multi-agency statutory guidance for dealing with forced marriage (HMG, 2023); Aldershire Multi-Agency Honour-Based Abuse Protocol (2024).
