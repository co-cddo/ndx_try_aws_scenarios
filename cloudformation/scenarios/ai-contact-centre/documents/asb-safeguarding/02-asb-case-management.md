# ASB case management and investigation timescales

Once a report is triaged, the Aldershire Community Safety team opens a case and assigns it to a named officer. Case progress is tracked in our case management system and reviewed weekly by the Community Safety Manager.

## Investigation timescales

We work to published timescales so reporters know what to expect:

- **Acknowledgement**: within 24 hours on working days, 72 hours otherwise.
- **First contact with the reporter**: within 5 working days for Tier 2, within 24 hours for Tier 3.
- **Initial assessment complete**: within 10 working days for Tier 2, within 5 working days for Tier 3.
- **Action plan agreed with the reporter**: within 15 working days of opening the case.
- **Case review meetings**: every 4 weeks while the case is active.
- **Case closure**: when the agreed outcomes have been delivered or sustained for 8 weeks without further incident.

## What an investigation involves

The officer will typically:

1. Interview the reporter and any witnesses, in person or by phone.
2. Visit the location to assess the conduct and physical environment.
3. Liaise with partner agencies: Aldershire Constabulary neighbourhood team, the perpetrator's landlord, schools, youth services, mental health services.
4. Issue an incident diary so the reporter can log subsequent incidents in a format that meets the standard of evidence required by the courts.
5. Decide on the proportionate intervention: warning letter, Acceptable Behaviour Contract, Community Protection Notice, civil injunction, closure order, referral for criminal prosecution.

## Information sharing

We share information with partner agencies under the Aldershire ASB Information Sharing Agreement, which sets out the lawful basis (Crime and Disorder Act 1998, section 115; Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, sections 105 and 106), the categories of data shared, and the safeguards. The agreement is reviewed every two years and is published at **aldershire.example.gov.uk/community-safety/data-sharing**.

## Updating the reporter

The case officer provides the reporter with an update at every case review meeting (every 4 weeks). If you have not heard from your officer, phone **01632 960 142** and ask for an update by case reference number. We will not share information about the perpetrator's circumstances or any sanctions imposed without the perpetrator's consent, but we will tell you whether action has been taken.
