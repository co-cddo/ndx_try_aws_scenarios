# Reporting anti-social behaviour

Aldershire's Community Safety team takes reports of anti-social behaviour (ASB) from residents, businesses, and visitors to the district. Reports are triaged within 24 hours on weekdays and within 72 hours over weekends and bank holidays.

## How to report

- Phone the Community Safety line on **01632 960 142** (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm).
- Out of hours: Aldershire Constabulary on **101** for non-urgent matters, **999** in an emergency.
- Online form at **aldershire.example.gov.uk/asb/report** (available 24/7, suitable for written submissions and uploading evidence such as photographs or video clips).
- In writing to Community Safety Team, Aldershire District Council, Civic Centre, High Street, Aldershire AL1 1CC.
- Anonymous reports via Crimestoppers on **0800 555 111**.

## What to include

For us to investigate effectively, please tell us:

- The address or location of the behaviour.
- The date, time, and duration of incidents.
- A description of what happened and who was involved (if known).
- The impact on you, your household, and the wider community.
- Whether you have reported it elsewhere (police, housing provider, environmental health).

## Severity tiers

We grade every report against a published severity matrix:

- **Tier 1 (low)**: isolated incidents, minor noise, untidy garden, dog fouling. Logged for pattern monitoring; no immediate intervention.
- **Tier 2 (medium)**: repeat incidents from the same address, persistent low-level harassment, drug paraphernalia in public places. Officer assigned within 5 working days.
- **Tier 3 (high)**: threats, intimidation, suspected criminality, hate-motivated conduct, vulnerable victim. Officer assigned within 24 hours; referral to Aldershire Constabulary's neighbourhood team.

## Confidentiality

Reports are confidential. We do not share the reporter's identity with the alleged perpetrator unless we are required to do so by a court, by a statutory disclosure scheme, or where the reporter has given written consent. The lawful basis for our processing is the public task in section 17 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 and our statutory ASB functions under the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014.

## What we cannot help with

We are not the right route for one-off neighbour disagreements, civil boundary disputes, parking on private land, or matters where there is no nuisance or harm to the community. For those, see our mediation and civil-route pages or contact your housing provider.
