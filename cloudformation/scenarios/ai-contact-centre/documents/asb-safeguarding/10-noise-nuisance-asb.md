# Noise nuisance as anti-social behaviour

Noise complaints come to Aldershire through two routes: the **environmental health team** (statutory nuisance) and the **community safety team** (anti-social behaviour). Both routes are valid, but they have different evidence requirements, different tools, and different expected outcomes. Choosing the right route helps us get the right outcome for you.

## Environmental health route

The environmental health team handles **statutory nuisance** under Part 3 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990. They use this route when:

- The noise is **predictable and recurring** (e.g. an extractor fan running at unsocial hours, a regular barking dog, music from a venue).
- The noise can be measured or witnessed in a structured way.
- The likely outcome is a noise abatement notice (section 80) on the source.

Diary sheets, witnessed incident reports, and sometimes professionally calibrated noise monitoring equipment in the complainant's home are typical evidence. Phone the environmental health line on **01632 960 175** to start this route.

## Community safety / ASB route

The community safety team handles noise as **anti-social behaviour** when:

- The noise is **part of a wider pattern** of conduct from the same address or person (parties, shouting, intimidation, drug-related visitor traffic).
- The noise is **unpredictable** and a structured environmental health investigation is impractical.
- There is a **vulnerability** dimension: the perpetrator or the victim has additional needs that warrant a multi-agency response.
- The intervention is more likely to be a **CPN, ABC, or injunction** against a person than an abatement notice on a source.

The intervention is about behaviour, not decibels. Phone **01632 960 142** to start this route.

## How we decide

When you call, the call handler asks a small number of questions and routes you accordingly. If you describe a single neighbour having loud parties two or three nights a week, we will probably go environmental health first. If you describe a flat with constant visitor traffic, shouting, and noise at all hours, we will route to community safety.

Sometimes both teams work the case jointly. We have a joint noise & ASB protocol that allows us to share information and avoid duplicating investigations, signed by both heads of service in 2023.

## Out-of-hours noise response

For active, unreasonable noise after 10pm and at weekends, the **out-of-hours noise team** can attend (subject to availability) and witness the noise. Call **01632 960 175** and select the out-of-hours option. Coverage is Friday and Saturday nights from 9pm to 3am, and bank holidays.

## What you can do yourself

For one-off events, talking to your neighbour is usually the most effective first step (see our neighbour-disputes-mediation page). For repeat issues, keeping a diary of dates, times, and impact is the single most useful piece of evidence you can give us, regardless of which route we use.

## Penalties

Breach of an environmental health abatement notice is a criminal offence with a maximum fine of £5,000 for domestic premises (£20,000 for industrial). CPN breaches in the ASB route are described separately.
