# Conservation areas

A conservation area is a designated area of special architectural or historic interest, the character or appearance of which it is desirable to preserve or enhance. Aldershire has 11 designated conservation areas, including:

- Aldermarket Old Town (designated 1969, extended 2008)
- Bramley Heath Village (1972)
- Mill Lane (1985)
- Hartshorn Riverside (1992)
- Wendlebury Conservation Area (2003)

A full list with maps is at planning.aldershire.gov.uk/conservation-areas.

## What needs consent within

Within a conservation area, you need permission for many things that would otherwise be permitted development:

- Demolition of any building over 115 cubic metres requires Conservation Area Consent
- Removal of trees with a stem diameter over 75mm (measured 1.5m above ground) requires 6 weeks' notice to the council
- Cladding, render, pebbledash, or stone facing of houses requires planning permission
- Side extensions to houses require planning permission
- Roof extensions and dormers in many positions require permission
- Satellite dishes on chimneys or front-facing walls require permission
- Solar panels visible from a highway may require permission

## Article 4 directions

Aldershire has issued Article 4 directions in three conservation areas (Aldermarket Old Town, Mill Lane, Bramley Heath) which remove additional Permitted Development rights, including:

- Replacement of original windows
- Painting of previously unpainted brick or stone
- Removal of historic chimney stacks
- Front garden hardstanding

If your property is in an Article 4 area, you will need full planning permission for these works. Fee is the standard householder fee of GBP 258.

## How character is assessed

Aldershire publishes a Conservation Area Appraisal for each area, describing:

- Historical development
- Key views and townscape features
- Important buildings (listed and unlisted)
- Boundary justification
- Management proposals and threats to character

These are statutory documents under section 71 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990.
