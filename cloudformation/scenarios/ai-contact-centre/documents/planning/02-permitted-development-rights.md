# Permitted development rights

Permitted Development (PD) rights let you carry out specific building works without a planning application. Rights are set out in the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) (England) Order 2015 (the GPDO).

## Common householder PD allowances

Schedule 2, Part 1 of the GPDO covers houses (not flats):

- Class A: rear extensions up to 4m deep (detached) or 3m (other), max eaves height 3m within 2m of boundary
- Class B: roof extensions up to 50m3 added volume (40m3 for terraced), no front-facing dormers
- Class C: re-roofing and rooflights flush with the slope
- Class D: porches up to 3 square metres, max 3m high, more than 2m from any highway
- Class E: outbuildings (sheds, garages, summerhouses) covering up to 50% of garden area
- Class F: hard surfaces in front gardens (porous, or drainage to a soakaway)

## Where PD does NOT apply

PD rights are restricted or removed in:

- Conservation areas (e.g. Aldermarket Old Town and Bramley Heath)
- National Parks, AONBs, World Heritage Sites
- Listed buildings (any external alteration needs Listed Building Consent)
- Article 4 areas (the council has formally withdrawn PD rights, e.g. Mill Lane Article 4 zone)
- Where a previous condition has removed PD rights from the property

Flats and maisonettes have very limited PD rights. Most external works to flats need full planning permission.

## Lawful Development Certificate

If you want certainty before starting work, apply for a Lawful Development Certificate (Proposed). Fee is GBP 129 for householders. Aldershire's Planning team determines within 8 weeks. See the Lawful Development Certificate document in this folder.

## Prior approval

Some PD classes require "prior approval" (a notification process, not full permission). Examples: larger rear extensions (Class A part-prior-approval procedure), changes of use under Class MA (commercial to residential). Application fee for prior approval is typically GBP 120.
