# Major development fees

Major development fees are set nationally and uplifted by 25% from 6 December 2023. These figures apply to applications submitted on or after that date.

## Outline applications (per 0.1 hectare)

| Site area | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 2.5 hectares | GBP 578 per 0.1 ha |
| Over 2.5 hectares | GBP 14,455 + GBP 173 per 0.1 ha over 2.5 ha (capped at GBP 173,300) |

## Full applications - dwellings

| Number of dwellings | Fee |
|---|---|
| 1 to 50 dwellings | GBP 578 per dwelling |
| 51+ dwellings | GBP 28,890 + GBP 173 per dwelling over 50 (capped at GBP 405,000) |

## Full applications - non-residential floorspace

| New floorspace | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 40 sqm | GBP 293 |
| 40 to 75 sqm | GBP 578 |
| 75 to 3,750 sqm | GBP 578 per 75 sqm |
| Over 3,750 sqm | GBP 28,890 + GBP 173 per 75 sqm over 3,750 (capped at GBP 405,000) |

## Other major application fees

- Reserved Matters: same scale as the original outline (per dwelling or per hectare)
- Section 73 (variation of condition) on a major: GBP 578
- Discharge of conditions on majors: GBP 145 per request
- Non-material amendments: GBP 234
- Major Lawful Development Certificate: half of the equivalent planning application fee

## Mineral extraction

- Up to 15 hectares: GBP 257 per 0.1 ha
- Over 15 hectares: GBP 38,505 + GBP 124 per 0.1 ha over 15 ha (capped)

## Wind turbines and solar farms

- Up to 5 turbines or 5 hectares of solar: GBP 578 per turbine or 0.1 ha
- More: tapered scale

## Commencement and discharge fees

After permission, expect to pay further fees for:

- Discharge of conditions: GBP 145 per request (covering one or many conditions)
- Section 73 variations: GBP 578 each
- Non-material amendments under section 96A: GBP 234 each

## Surcharges

- Retrospective applications (works already done): same fee as if applied for in advance, but no free go provision
- Applications without all required documents: not validated until complete; the fee clock does not start

## Major application thresholds

A "major" application in fee terms is:

- 10+ dwellings, OR
- 1,000+ sqm new floorspace, OR
- 1+ hectare site area

These thresholds are different from EIA thresholds; check the relevant regulations separately.
