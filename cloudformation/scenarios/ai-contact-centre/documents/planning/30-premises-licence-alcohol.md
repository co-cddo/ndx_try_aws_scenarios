# Premises licence (alcohol and entertainment)

Selling alcohol, providing late-night refreshment, or hosting regulated entertainment requires a Premises Licence under the Licensing Act 2003. Aldershire is the licensing authority for the district.

## Licensable activities

Under section 1 of the 2003 Act:

- Sale of alcohol by retail
- Supply of alcohol by or on behalf of a club
- Provision of regulated entertainment (live music, recorded music, performance of a play, exhibition of film, indoor sporting event, boxing or wrestling, dance performance)
- Provision of late-night refreshment (hot food or drink between 23:00 and 05:00)

## Application requirements

You must submit:

- Application form (see lic.aldershire.gov.uk/forms)
- Operating Schedule describing how the four licensing objectives will be met:
  - Prevention of crime and disorder
  - Public safety
  - Prevention of public nuisance
  - Protection of children from harm
- Plan of the premises showing scale, exits, kitchens, bars
- Designated Premises Supervisor (DPS) consent form (a personal licence holder must be named)
- Disclosure of relevant convictions
- Application fee
- Statement of intent for sale of alcohol (on or off-premises)
- Public notice (printed in local paper)
- Site notices displayed for 28 days

## Fee bands

Based on the property's non-domestic rateable value:

| Band | Rateable value | New application | Annual fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Up to GBP 4,300 | GBP 100 | GBP 70 |
| B | GBP 4,301 to GBP 33,000 | GBP 190 | GBP 180 |
| C | GBP 33,001 to GBP 87,000 | GBP 315 | GBP 295 |
| D | GBP 87,001 to GBP 125,000 | GBP 450 | GBP 320 |
| E | GBP 125,001 and over | GBP 635 | GBP 350 |

Multipliers apply for premises used "exclusively or primarily for the supply of alcohol for consumption on the premises" in Bands D and E.

## Consultation

The application is sent to "responsible authorities":

- Aldershire Licensing team
- Aldershire Constabulary
- Public Health (Aldershire ICB)
- Environmental Health
- Trading Standards (County)
- Fire Authority
- Aldershire Children's Safeguarding (County)
- Planning
- Home Office Immigration

A 28-day consultation period runs. Responsible authorities and "interested parties" (residents within the vicinity) can make representations.

## Hearings

If valid representations are received, the Licensing Sub-Committee holds a hearing. Three councillors. Held in public at the Town Hall. Decision within 5 working days.

## Conditions

The licence carries:

- Mandatory conditions (set by national regulations: Challenge 25, no irresponsible promotions, free water available, etc.)
- Conditions volunteered in the Operating Schedule
- Conditions imposed by the Sub-Committee in response to representations
