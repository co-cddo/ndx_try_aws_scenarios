# Pre-application advice

Before submitting a formal planning application, you can ask Aldershire for written pre-application advice. This is a chargeable, optional service that helps you understand the issues, the council's policies, and the likely outcome.

## Why use it

- Reduces the risk of refusal
- Identifies issues early when the design can still change
- Cheaper than a refusal followed by a fresh application
- Statutory consultees (Highways, Environment Agency) can be involved at this stage for major schemes
- Mandatory for most major applications under Aldershire's Local Validation List

## Service tiers

### Tier 1: Householder

GBP 96 + VAT. One officer site visit. One written response (typically 4 to 6 pages). Response within 21 working days.

### Tier 2: Minor (1 to 9 dwellings, up to 999 sqm commercial)

GBP 348 + VAT. One meeting at Aldermarket Town Hall or by Teams. One written response. Response within 28 working days.

### Tier 3: Major (10 to 49 dwellings, 1,000 to 4,999 sqm commercial)

GBP 1,176 + VAT. One meeting, written response, follow-up meeting on revised proposals (within 6 months). Response within 35 working days.

### Tier 4: Strategic (50+ dwellings, 5,000+ sqm commercial)

GBP 2,940 + VAT for the initial pack. Bespoke Project Officer assigned. Multiple meetings. Statutory consultees engaged. Service Level Agreement signed.

### Listed Buildings

Tier 1 fee. Conservation Officer always involved. May refer to Historic England for Grade I and Grade II*.

## What you submit

- Site location plan
- Description of the proposal
- Indicative layout / sketches
- Photographs
- Any technical reports already commissioned (heritage statement, transport assessment)
- Specific questions you want answered

## What you get back

- A written officer opinion on the proposal's compliance with the Local Plan
- Identified issues (highways, flood risk, ecology, design)
- Policy references
- Pointers to validation requirements
- An indication of likely officer recommendation (NOT a decision)

The advice is not legally binding. Material changes between pre-application and application can change the answer. New evidence (e.g. ecology surveys) can shift the recommendation.

## Confidentiality

Pre-application advice is treated as confidential between the applicant and the council until a planning application is submitted. After submission, the advice may be referred to in the officer's report and is disclosable under Environmental Information Regulations 2004.
