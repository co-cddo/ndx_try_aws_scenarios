# Discharge of conditions

When planning permission is granted, it usually carries conditions. Some conditions are "pre-commencement" (must be met before works start), some are "during construction", some are "occupational" (before first occupation). Each must be formally discharged by Aldershire before the development can lawfully proceed past that point.

## Why this matters

Starting works in breach of a pre-commencement condition can render the entire permission unlawful. The Greyfort case (2011) established that a development started in breach of a true pre-commencement condition is itself unauthorised, even though the planning permission exists. Enforcement immunity may not run.

## Common conditions

- Construction Management Plan (parking, deliveries, site hours)
- Materials samples (often a panel built on site for officer inspection)
- Landscaping scheme
- Surface water drainage scheme
- Foul drainage scheme
- Contamination investigation and remediation
- Archaeology (watching brief or excavation)
- Ecological mitigation (e.g. great crested newt licence)
- Boundary treatment
- External lighting
- Noise management plan

## How to apply

Submit a "Discharge of Conditions" application via the Planning Portal. List each condition by number and submit the supporting information for each.

You can submit one application covering multiple conditions, or one per condition. One combined application is usually cheaper.

## Fees

GBP 86 per request for a householder permission. GBP 145 per request for any other type of permission. The fee is the same whether you cover one condition or many in a single submission.

## Determination

Statutory period: 8 weeks. If Aldershire has not determined within 12 weeks (or another time agreed in writing), you can apply for "deemed discharge" under section 74A of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (some conditions excluded, e.g. those relating to listed buildings or ecology).

## Common rejection reasons

- Insufficient detail (e.g. landscaping plan without species list)
- Not addressing the wording of the condition
- Missing supporting reports (contamination, ecology) when explicitly required
- Late submission (after the development has started, where the condition was pre-commencement)

## What discharge does not do

A discharge does not vary the condition. If you want to vary a condition, use a section 73 (variation) application or section 96A (non-material amendment). Different routes, different fees.
