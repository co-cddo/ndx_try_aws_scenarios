# Animal welfare licensing

A range of activities involving animals require licensing under the Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (England) Regulations 2018. Aldershire is the licensing authority for the district.

## Licensable activities

Five categories under the 2018 Regulations:

1. Selling animals as pets (commercial pet shops)
2. Providing or arranging for the provision of boarding for cats or dogs (kennels, catteries, home boarding, day care)
3. Hiring out horses (riding schools, trekking centres)
4. Breeding dogs (3 or more litters in any 12-month period, OR commercial scale)
5. Keeping or training animals for exhibition (e.g. travelling petting farms, animal acts)

Other animal activity licences:

- Dangerous Wild Animals Act 1976: for keeping listed species (e.g. some primates, big cats, certain reptiles)
- Performing Animals (Regulation) Act 1925: for trainers / exhibitors
- Zoo Licensing Act 1981: for zoos and similar (Aldershire has none)

## Star ratings

Most licences carry a 1 to 5 star rating, calculated from a risk-weighted score on welfare standards. Higher ratings:

- Allow the licence to be issued for longer (up to 3 years)
- Reduce the frequency of inspections
- Reduce the fee proportionally

A 5-star licence can be valid for 3 years; a 1-star for 1 year.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be a "suitable person":

- No relevant unspent convictions (Animal Welfare Act 2006, Protection of Animals Acts)
- No history of disqualification by a court from keeping animals
- Hold appropriate qualifications or experience
- Have suitable premises and facilities
- Comply with the published statutory guidance for the activity

## Aldershire's vet inspections

All applications include a veterinary inspection (cost included in the fee for major activities). Inspections are unannounced for renewals. Aldershire works with a panel of approved vets, and joint inspections with the Animal Welfare Officer.

## Application timeline

- Submit application with fee, plans, and supporting documents
- Vet inspection arranged (typically 4 to 8 weeks)
- Decision within 12 weeks of valid application
- Licence issued, displayed at the premises

## Conditions

Statutory conditions are set by the regulations and the published guidance for each activity. Local conditions can be added on welfare grounds.

## Penalties

Operating an unlicensed activity: unlimited fine on indictment, up to 5 years' imprisonment, possible disqualification from keeping animals (Animal Welfare Act 2006, sections 9 to 13).
