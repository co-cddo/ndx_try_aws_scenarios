# Householder planning application

A householder application is needed for works to a single dwelling that fall outside Permitted Development. Typical examples include rear extensions over the volume threshold, two-storey side extensions, dormer windows on the front roof slope, and outbuildings that exceed permitted limits.

## When you need one

You need a householder application for:

- Single-storey rear extensions deeper than 4 metres on a detached house, or 3 metres on any other type
- Two-storey extensions
- Front extensions of any kind
- Roof extensions that increase the existing roof volume by more than 50 cubic metres (40 for terraced)
- Outbuildings closer than 2 metres to the boundary that exceed 2.5 metres in height
- Boundary walls fronting the highway over 1 metre high

## Fees

The householder application fee from 1 December 2023 is GBP 258. This covers most extensions and alterations. If you submit a second related application for the same property within 12 months of a refusal, the fee is waived under the "free go" provisions of the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Applications) Regulations 2012.

## How to apply

Apply through the Planning Portal (planningportal.co.uk). Aldershire receives the application electronically within one working day. You will need:

- Site location plan at 1:1250 (red line boundary)
- Block plan at 1:500
- Existing and proposed elevations and floor plans at 1:50 or 1:100
- Design and access statement (only required for listed buildings or conservation areas)

## Validation and decision

Once received, the Planning team has 5 working days to validate. Invalid applications are paused until missing information is supplied. The statutory determination period is 8 weeks for householder applications. Aldershire publishes the decision on the public planning register at planning.aldershire.gov.uk.
