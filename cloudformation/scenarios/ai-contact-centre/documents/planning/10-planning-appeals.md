# Planning appeals

If Aldershire refuses your planning application, attaches conditions you disagree with, or fails to determine within the statutory period, you can appeal to the Planning Inspectorate (an executive agency of the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government).

## Who can appeal

Only the applicant can appeal. Third parties (objectors) cannot appeal a grant of permission. They can only seek judicial review in the High Court (3 months, costly, narrow grounds).

## Time limits

- Householder and minor commercial: 12 weeks from decision date
- Other applications: 6 months from decision date
- Non-determination appeals: 6 months from the date the council should have decided

Late appeals are not accepted. Diary the date as soon as you receive the decision notice.

## Appeal procedures

The Inspectorate will allocate one of three procedures:

- Written representations: the most common, used for householder and most minor cases. Determined on the papers. Typical timeline 18 to 24 weeks.
- Hearing: more formal but not adversarial. The Inspector chairs a discussion. Typical timeline 26 to 32 weeks.
- Public Inquiry: formal, evidence on oath, advocates and witnesses. Reserved for major or complex cases. Typical timeline 36 to 52 weeks.

## How to appeal

Use the Appeals Casework Portal at acp.planninginspectorate.gov.uk. You need:

- Full grounds of appeal
- Statement of case
- Documents originally submitted to the council
- The decision notice and officer report
- Any supporting expert reports

There is no fee for the appeal itself. Costs in your own time and any consultants.

## Costs awards

Either party can apply for costs against the other if behaviour was unreasonable. Examples: a local authority refusing without proper reasons, or an appellant withdrawing late. Awards can include preparation costs and consultancy fees.

## Outcomes

The Inspector's decision is binding. Possible outcomes:

- Allowed (permission granted, sometimes with different conditions)
- Dismissed (refusal upheld)
- Split decision (allowed for some elements, dismissed for others)

Decisions are published at acp.planninginspectorate.gov.uk and are searchable by appeal reference (format APP/X/YY/MMNNNNNN).

## Aldershire's appeals

Aldershire's appeal performance is published quarterly at planning.aldershire.gov.uk/performance. Approximately 28% of appealed refusals are allowed, broadly in line with the national average.
