# Premises licence: renewal, transfer and variation

Once granted, a Premises Licence is open-ended (it does not expire) but it can be transferred, varied, or surrendered. Annual fees must be paid. The Licensing Act 2003 governs all of these processes.

## Annual fee

Premises Licences carry an annual fee, payable on the anniversary of the grant. Failure to pay leads to suspension under section 55A. Aldershire issues reminders 21 days before the due date.

Fees by Band:

| Band | Annual fee |
|---|---|
| A | GBP 70 |
| B | GBP 180 |
| C | GBP 295 |
| D | GBP 320 |
| E | GBP 350 |

Pay online at lic.aldershire.gov.uk/payment, or by phone on 01394 555 0280.

## Transfer of licence

If a Premises Licence is sold or assigned to a new operator, a Transfer Application is required (section 42). The new licence holder applies on Form LIC-T1. Fee is GBP 23.

The application must include:

- Consent of the existing holder (or proof of why this is impossible, e.g. insolvency)
- Disclosure of relevant convictions for the new holder
- Notice to the police (section 42(6)) - 14-day police representation window

The transfer is effective from the date specified, usually the day after the application is "given". You can apply for "Interim Effect" to take possession immediately.

## Designated Premises Supervisor (DPS) change

The DPS is the named personal licence holder responsible for day-to-day operation. To change the DPS, file Form DPS1 with the police service and Aldershire. Fee is GBP 23. Police have 14 days to object.

The new DPS must hold a valid Personal Licence. Their consent (Form DPS Consent) must be filed.

## Variation of licence

To change conditions, hours, layout, or licensable activities, you need a Variation. Two routes:

### Minor variation

For changes that cannot adversely affect the licensing objectives (e.g. moving a non-public bar, a small extension to a kitchen, removing redundant conditions). Fee is GBP 89. 10-working-day consultation. Decided by the Licensing Manager. No hearing.

### Full variation

For substantive changes (longer hours, new licensable activities, change in primary use). Treated like a new application. Fee equals the new-application fee for the relevant Band. Full 28-day consultation. May require a Sub-Committee hearing if representations are received.

## Surrender

To surrender a licence, complete Form LIC-S. No fee. The surrender takes effect immediately. Licence cannot be reinstated; a fresh application would be needed.

## Lapse and reinstatement

If the licence holder dies, becomes mentally incapable, or insolvent, the licence "lapses" but can be reinstated within 28 days by an interim authority notice (section 47). This protects the business through transition.

## Review of licence

Any responsible authority or interested party can apply for a review (Form LIC-R) if a licensing objective is being undermined. The Sub-Committee can: do nothing; modify conditions; exclude licensable activities; remove the DPS; suspend; or revoke. Reviews are public hearings.

## Personal Licence

Distinct document. See separate Personal Licence document. A Personal Licence is needed by every DPS and by individuals authorising alcohol sales.
