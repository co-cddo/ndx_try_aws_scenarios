# Public consultation on planning applications

When Aldershire receives a valid planning application, it consults the public and statutory bodies. The procedural rules are in the Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015.

## Methods of consultation

Aldershire uses three methods:

### Neighbour notification letters

Sent to the occupiers of properties immediately adjoining the application site (or land within 20 metres of the boundary). Sent within 5 working days of validation.

### Site notices

A yellow weatherproof notice is fixed close to the site at least 7 days before the consultation deadline. Required for:

- All major applications
- Listed Building Consent
- Conservation Area applications
- Departures from the Local Plan
- EIA development

For non-major applications outside protected areas, neighbour notification is sufficient.

### Press notice

Published in the Aldershire Gazette (Thursdays). Required for the same categories as site notices, plus advertisements affecting amenity. The cost is recouped from the application fee where applicable.

## Consultation period

The standard period is 21 days from the date of the latest of (a) the neighbour letter, (b) site notice posting, (c) press notice publication. Comments received after the deadline are still considered if the decision has not yet been made.

## How to comment

- Online via planning.aldershire.gov.uk/consultations
- By post to: Planning, Aldershire District Council, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square, AL1 1AA
- By email: planning.comments@aldershire.gov.uk

Comments must be in writing. Anonymous comments are not considered. The author's name and address are published online (signature is redacted).

## Material considerations

Only "material planning considerations" can affect the decision. Examples:

- Loss of light or overshadowing
- Loss of privacy / overlooking
- Visual impact on the area or street scene
- Highway safety and parking
- Noise and disturbance
- Impact on protected species or trees
- Compliance with the Local Plan
- Heritage impact

Examples of NON-material matters (will not affect the decision):

- Loss of property value
- Boundary disputes
- Private right of way (separate civil matter)
- Personal circumstances of the developer or applicant
- Loss of a private view
- Construction noise (covered separately by Environmental Health)

## Speaking at Committee

If your comment is going to Planning Committee, you can register to speak (see Planning Committee document for procedure). Three minutes per side; pre-registration required.

## Re-consultation

If revised plans are submitted that materially change the proposal, Aldershire will re-consult for a further 14 days.
