# Tree Preservation Orders (TPO)

A Tree Preservation Order is a statutory protection on individual trees, groups of trees, or woodlands. It is made by Aldershire Council under section 198 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990.

## What a TPO does

A TPO makes it an offence to cut down, top, lop, uproot, wilfully damage, or wilfully destroy a protected tree without the council's written consent. Penalties:

- Magistrates' Court: unlimited fine
- Crown Court: unlimited fine, with the value of timber and the wider environmental harm taken into account

Aldershire has approximately 1,840 individual TPOs in force, plus 23 area orders covering woodlands.

## Checking if a tree is protected

Three things to check:

1. Is the tree subject to a TPO? Search at planning.aldershire.gov.uk/tpo-map
2. Is the tree in a conservation area? Trees with a stem diameter over 75mm in conservation areas are automatically protected (different procedure, see below)
3. Is the tree subject to a planning condition (e.g. on a recent housing site)?

## Applying to fell or work on a TPO tree

Use form TPO/01, available at planning.aldershire.gov.uk/forms. There is no fee. The application must include:

- Address and tree location plan
- Species and approximate age
- Description of proposed works (fell, crown reduction, deadwood removal)
- Reason for the works
- Photographs

Determination period is 8 weeks. Aldershire's Tree Officer, Mike Aldridge, will normally visit the tree before deciding.

## Conservation area trees (no TPO)

Different procedure. Notify the council 6 weeks in advance using form CA/Tree. The council can either:

- Allow the works to proceed (silence after 6 weeks counts as consent)
- Make an emergency TPO to prevent the works

## Dangerous trees

Section 198(6) of the 1990 Act allows works to a TPO tree without consent if the tree is "dead, dying, or has become dangerous". You must give 5 days' written notice unless there is an immediate emergency. Take photographs before works. Replant a replacement where the tree was felled.
