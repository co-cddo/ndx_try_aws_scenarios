# Householder application fees - current schedule

Fees for planning applications are set nationally by the Town and Country Planning (Fees for Applications, Deemed Applications, Requests and Site Visits) (England) Regulations 2012. Most fees were uplifted by 25% on 6 December 2023.

## Householder fees (England, from December 2023)

| Application type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Householder planning application | GBP 258 |
| Householder Lawful Development Certificate (Existing) | GBP 258 |
| Householder Lawful Development Certificate (Proposed) | GBP 129 |
| Householder prior approval (Class A larger extension) | GBP 120 |
| Discharge of Conditions (householder permissions) | GBP 86 per request |
| Householder Listed Building Consent | No fee |
| Non-material amendment | GBP 34 |
| Variation of condition (s73) | GBP 258 |
| Removal of condition | GBP 258 |
| Tree works (TPO trees) | No fee |
| Conservation area tree notification | No fee |
| Hedgerow Removal Notice | No fee |

## Free go provision

If your householder application is refused or withdrawn, you can submit a like-for-like resubmission within 12 months of the refusal date and pay no fee. Conditions:

- Same applicant
- Same site
- The proposal must be of "the same character or description" as the original

The free go applies once only. If you withdraw the resubmission, the free go is gone.

## Multi-purpose applications

If you submit a single application covering multiple development categories (e.g. a house extension AND a new outbuilding), Aldershire calculates the fee based on the highest-rated component. There is no compounding.

## Payment

Fees are payable on submission, otherwise the application is invalid. Acceptable payment methods:

- Online via Planning Portal (BACS or card)
- Card payment by phone: 01394 555 0200
- BACS to Aldershire's Planning bank account (request details from the Validation team)

## VAT

Planning fees are not subject to VAT.

## Refunds

Fees are partially refundable in narrow circumstances:

- Application not validated within 26 weeks: refund of fee
- Withdrawn before validation: full refund minus admin fee of GBP 35
- Withdrawn after validation but before consultation: 50% refund

Refunds take 4 to 6 weeks. Apply in writing to the Validation team.

## Reduced and zero fees

- Disabled access works to a dwelling: no fee
- Listed Building Consent: no fee
- Works necessary to comply with an enforcement notice: no fee (but only with prior agreement)
