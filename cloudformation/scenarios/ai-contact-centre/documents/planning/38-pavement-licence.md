# Pavement licence (outdoor seating)

A pavement licence permits a business serving food or drink to place removable furniture on the public highway adjacent to its premises. The framework was introduced by the Business and Planning Act 2020 (originally as a temporary Covid measure) and made permanent by the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023, with permanent provisions taking effect from 31 March 2024.

## What can be placed

Permitted furniture (must be removable):

- Tables and chairs
- Counters or stalls for serving food or drink
- Heaters and umbrellas
- Barriers (subject to highway authority requirements)
- Planters used as boundary markers

Furniture must be removed and the highway returned to its standard condition outside the licensed period.

## Where it applies

The licence is for the part of the public highway directly adjacent to the front of the premises. It does NOT cover:

- Parts of the carriageway used by motor vehicles
- Cycle lanes
- Cycle tracks
- Designated bus stops or taxi ranks

## Aldershire requirements

Aldershire has adopted local conditions, including:

- Minimum 1.5 metre clear pavement width must be maintained for pedestrians (preferred 2.0 metres)
- 2.0 metre clear width on classified routes (e.g. Mill Street, High Street, Bridge Lane)
- Tactile paving must remain unobstructed for visually impaired users
- Disabled access must be retained
- Smoke-free outdoor area requirements (under review by Department of Health)
- No amplified entertainment (a separate Premises Licence variation is needed)
- No obstruction of fire hydrants, manholes, or street furniture access
- Removable barriers required where furniture abuts the carriageway

## Application

Form PVL1, lodged at lic.aldershire.gov.uk/pavement.

Documents required:

- Site plan showing the layout of furniture, dimensions, distances to kerb and frontage
- Photograph of the proposed area
- Public liability insurance (minimum GBP 5 million)
- Risk assessment
- Method statement for set-up and removal

## Fees

- New application: GBP 500
- Renewal: GBP 350
- Variation: GBP 200

Licences are issued for up to 2 years.

## Consultation and decision

Aldershire publishes the application on its register and on a notice at the site for 14 days. Representations can be made on highway safety, accessibility, and amenity grounds. Determination within 28 days. Silence is deemed consent (the licence is granted by default).

## Conditions

Mandatory conditions in the Business and Planning Act 2020:

- Smoke-free seating area (see government guidance)
- Compliance with highway authority requirements

Local conditions can be added to address site-specific concerns.

## Refusal grounds

The council can refuse if:

- Conditions cannot be set to make the proposal acceptable
- The proposal would obstruct access to disabled persons
- The proposal would cause harm to amenity or highway safety
- The applicant has held licences revoked in the previous 12 months

## Enforcement

Failure to comply with conditions: the council can issue a "remedial notice" (4 hours to remedy). Repeated breach: licence revoked. Operating without a licence: notice to cease, removal of furniture by the council with cost recovery.
