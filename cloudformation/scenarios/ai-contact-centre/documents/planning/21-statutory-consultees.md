# Statutory consultees

For certain types of planning application, Aldershire is legally required to consult specified bodies. Schedule 4 of the Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015 lists the consultees and the trigger criteria.

## Highway authorities

- Aldershire County Council Highways: consulted on all proposals affecting roads in the district except trunk roads
- National Highways (formerly Highways England): consulted on proposals within 67 metres of the A14 and the A1(M)

Consultation period: 21 days. Highways' response forms a key part of the assessment of access, parking, and traffic impact.

## Environment Agency (EA)

Consulted on:

- Development in Flood Zone 2 or 3 (as shown on EA flood maps)
- Development of 1 hectare or more in any flood zone
- Development that may affect groundwater, contamination, or water quality
- Major industrial development requiring an environmental permit

The EA can object on flood risk grounds, which can be a "holding" or a "substantive" objection. Substantive objections are difficult to overcome.

## Historic England (HE)

Consulted on:

- Listed building consent applications affecting Grade I and Grade II* buildings (statutory)
- Demolition or substantial alteration to scheduled monuments
- Development affecting registered parks and gardens, registered battlefields, or World Heritage Sites
- Major development in conservation areas of national significance

For Grade II buildings, consultation is at the discretion of the Conservation Officer.

## Natural England (NE)

Consulted on:

- Development affecting Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSIs)
- Development affecting Special Areas of Conservation, Special Protection Areas (Habitats Regulations Assessment)
- Development that may affect protected species (great crested newts, bats, badgers, dormice, reptiles)
- Major housing within 5 km of the Stodmarsh and Bramley Heath Common SPAs (recreational disturbance)

## Lead Local Flood Authority

Aldershire County Council acts as LLFA for surface water flooding. Consulted on:

- Major development (10+ dwellings or 1,000+ sqm)
- Drainage strategy review

## Other consultees

- Coal Authority: development in coal mining reporting areas (parts of the western district)
- HSE: development near major hazard pipelines or sites
- NHS Aldershire ICB: major housing developments (s106 healthcare contributions)
- Police: design out crime advice on major schemes
- Sport England: development on or near playing fields (statutory)
- Local Wildlife Trust: ecology advice (non-statutory)
- Aldermarket Civic Society: heritage advice (non-statutory)

## Parish councils

Aldershire's 47 parish councils are consulted on every planning application within their boundary. This is a non-statutory but well-established practice. They have 21 days to respond.

## How responses are weighted

The Planning Inspectorate has confirmed that statutory consultee responses carry significant weight, particularly where they object on technical grounds. Officers must explain in their report why any departure from a statutory consultee's view is justified.
