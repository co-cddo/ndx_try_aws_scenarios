# Sex establishment licensing (SEV)

Sex establishments are licensed under Schedule 3 to the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 (as amended by section 27 of the Policing and Crime Act 2009). Aldershire adopted the discretionary Schedule 3 powers in 2012, and the regime now covers sex shops, sex cinemas, and sexual entertainment venues (SEVs).

## What is a sex establishment

- Sex shop: a premises used for the retail sale of sex articles to a "significant degree"
- Sex cinema: a premises used for screening film with a sexual nature in a way deemed primary
- Sexual Entertainment Venue (SEV): a premises providing live sexual entertainment for the financial gain of the organiser, occurring on more than 11 occasions per year

## Aldershire's policy

Aldershire's adopted Sex Establishment Policy (revised 2024) sets:

- Maximum number of SEVs in the district: 1 (currently zero in operation)
- Maximum number of sex shops: 2 (currently zero in operation)
- Locations considered "inappropriate":
  - Within 200 metres of a school, place of worship, community centre, or play area
  - In residential areas
  - Adjacent to other sensitive uses

The policy is published at lic.aldershire.gov.uk/sex-establishment.

## Application

Apply on Form SE1, with:

- Plan of the premises showing layout, security, signage
- Operating Schedule including age verification, security staffing, alcohol arrangements (if licensed)
- Disclosure of convictions for the applicant and named officers
- Proof of right to occupy the premises
- Site notice (28 days) and press notice (in the Aldershire Gazette)
- Application fee

## Fees

- New application: GBP 4,375
- Renewal: GBP 2,950
- Transfer: GBP 1,200
- Variation: GBP 1,800

Fees are based on the cost of administering the regime; they reflect the cost of consultation, hearing time, and inspection.

## Determination

Statutory consultation period: 28 days. Representations can be made by anyone. The application is determined by the Licensing Sub-Committee in public.

Grounds for refusal include:

- Applicant unfit
- Inappropriate location (per the published policy)
- Number of premises in the area equal to or exceeds the policy maximum
- The grant would adversely affect a school, place of worship, or other sensitive use

Refusals are appealable to Magistrates' Court within 21 days.

## Conditions

The Sub-Committee can attach conditions covering:

- Operating hours (typically 11:00 to 23:00 for sex shops; later for SEVs by exception)
- Window displays (no visibility from the street)
- Age verification (Challenge 25, signage)
- Security and CCTV
- Staff training

## Renewal

Annually. Applications must be made at least 28 days before expiry. Late applications can be considered but the licence does not extend beyond expiry.

## Enforcement

Operating without a licence (or in breach of conditions) is an offence under paragraph 20 of Schedule 3:

- Maximum fine: GBP 20,000

Aldershire's Trading Standards and Licensing teams undertake test purchases and unannounced inspections.
