# Gambling Act licences

Gambling premises are regulated under the Gambling Act 2005. The Gambling Commission licenses operators (Operating Licence) and persons (Personal Management Licence). Aldershire is responsible for premises licences and certain permits.

## What Aldershire licenses

### Premises Licences (section 159)

For premises where gambling takes place, including:

- Bingo premises
- Adult gaming centres (slot machine premises for over-18s)
- Family entertainment centres (low-stake amusements for all ages)
- Betting premises (off-track bookmakers)
- Tracks (where dog or horse racing takes place)

### Permits

For lower-risk gambling activities:

- Club gaming permits (for snooker clubs, sports clubs)
- Club machine permits
- Family entertainment centre gaming machine permits
- Prize gaming permits
- Licensed Premises Gaming Machine Permits (for pubs, restaurants with up to 2 machines)

## Eligibility

The applicant must already hold:

- An Operating Licence from the Gambling Commission (premises licences only)
- A Personal Management Licence from the Gambling Commission (where required)

Aldershire's role is local: it focuses on premises suitability, location, layout, and conditions.

## Three licensing objectives

Section 1 of the Gambling Act 2005:

- Preventing gambling from being a source of crime or disorder, being associated with crime or disorder, or being used to support crime
- Ensuring that gambling is conducted in a fair and open way
- Protecting children and other vulnerable persons from being harmed or exploited by gambling

These differ from the Licensing Act 2003 objectives (no public nuisance; no public safety).

## Application

Form GA1, lodged at lic.aldershire.gov.uk/gambling, with:

- Operating Licence reference
- Plan of premises (scale, layout, machine positions)
- Operating schedule addressing the three objectives
- Public notice (Aldershire Gazette)
- Site notices (28 days)
- Application fee

## Fees (set by Aldershire under section 212)

| Activity | New | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Bingo premises | GBP 1,750 | GBP 600 |
| Adult gaming centre | GBP 1,750 | GBP 750 |
| Family entertainment centre | GBP 1,500 | GBP 600 |
| Betting premises | GBP 1,500 | GBP 500 |
| Tracks | GBP 2,500 | GBP 750 |
| Club gaming permit | GBP 200 | GBP 50 |
| LPGMP (pubs) | GBP 50 | GBP 50 |

Variation, transfer, and reinstatement fees are also published in the Council's Gambling Act fees schedule.

## Statement of Principles

Section 349 requires Aldershire to publish a Statement of Principles every 3 years. The current Statement (2025-2028) sets:

- Aldershire's approach to its three objectives
- Local areas of concern (e.g. proximity to schools and youth services)
- Approach to vulnerable persons and self-exclusion schemes (Multi-Operator Self-Exclusion Scheme - MOSES, BACTA SENSE, GAMSTOP)

The Statement is published at lic.aldershire.gov.uk/gambling-policy.

## Sub-Committee hearings

Where representations are received from responsible authorities or interested parties (residents, businesses, schools within close proximity), the application is heard by the Licensing Sub-Committee. Three councillors. In public.

## Penalties

Operating without the necessary premises licence: fine on summary conviction up to GBP 50,000 or up to 51 weeks' imprisonment.
