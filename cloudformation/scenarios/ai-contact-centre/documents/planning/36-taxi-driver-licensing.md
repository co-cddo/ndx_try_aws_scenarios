# Hackney carriage and private hire driver licensing

Aldershire issues two types of driver licence: hackney carriage (taxi) and private hire vehicle (PHV / minicab). The legal framework is the Town Police Clauses Act 1847, the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976, and the Road Traffic Acts.

## Hackney carriage vs private hire

- Hackney carriage: can ply for hire (be hailed in the street, wait at a rank). Roof-mounted "TAXI" sign. Metered fares. Aldershire has 142 hackney carriage plates in issue (capped under section 16 of the 1985 Transport Act).
- Private hire (PHV): pre-booked only via a licensed Private Hire Operator. Cannot ply for hire. No roof sign. Fares set by operator. No cap on numbers.

The driver licence is similar for both, with some differences in vehicle and operator licensing.

## Eligibility - Driver Licence

You must:

- Be 18 or over (Aldershire policy: 21+)
- Have held a UK or EU driving licence for at least 12 months
- Have lived in the UK for at least 36 months (where checks are practical)
- Pass a DBS Enhanced check (reviewed against the council's "Fit and Proper Person" Policy)
- Pass a Group 2 medical examination (DVLA standards)
- Pass the council's Knowledge Test (Aldermarket area, key landmarks, basic geography)
- Pass a topographical / safeguarding awareness assessment
- Have right to work in the UK
- Speak English to B1 level (CEFR) or above

## Disqualifying offences

Aldershire's policy follows the IoL "Suitability Guidance":

- Sexual offences: lifetime exclusion in most cases
- Violence: 10 years from end of sentence
- Drug supply: lifetime exclusion
- Drug possession: 10 years
- Drink-driving: 7 years
- Other dishonesty: 5 years (varies by severity)

The council considers each case individually but its policy is that fit-and-proper-person status is not granted lightly.

## Application process

1. Submit application form, photographs, and documents
2. Pay fee (Driver Licence: GBP 295 for 3 years)
3. DBS application (GBP 38)
4. Medical (your own GP or council-approved doctor: typically GBP 90 to GBP 120)
5. Knowledge Test (GBP 50, three attempts maximum within 6 months)
6. Decision typically 8 to 12 weeks

## Vehicle licence

A separately licensed vehicle is required. Aldershire's vehicle policy:

- Maximum age 8 years from first registration (10 years for wheelchair-accessible)
- Annual MOT and council inspection
- Carbon emission standards (Euro 6 minimum from 2024)
- Fully comprehensive insurance
- Distinctive livery for hackney carriages (yellow door panels)
- Vehicle Licence fee: GBP 240 per year

## Operator licence

Required for any business taking PHV bookings (not for hackney carriage drivers operating individually). Fee: GBP 365 for 5 years.

## Penalties

- Driving a private hire vehicle without a licence: fine up to GBP 1,000, possible disqualification
- Plying for hire in a private hire vehicle: serious offence; insurance void
- Failure to display badge: fixed penalty
