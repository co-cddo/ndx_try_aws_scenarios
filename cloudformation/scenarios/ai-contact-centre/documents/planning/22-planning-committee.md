# Planning Committee

Aldershire Planning Committee determines planning applications that fall outside officer delegation (see separate document on Delegated Decisions). It is a quasi-judicial committee operating under the council's constitution.

## Membership

11 elected members, balanced to reflect political composition:

- 6 Conservative
- 3 Labour
- 1 Liberal Democrat
- 1 Green

The Chair is elected annually at Annual Council in May. Substitutes can attend if a regular member is unavailable, provided they have completed planning training within the last 12 months.

## Meeting schedule

Monthly, on the second Wednesday at 18:30. Meetings held in the Council Chamber, Town Hall, Aldermarket Square. 2025 dates published at planning.aldershire.gov.uk/committee.

Special meetings can be called for major or time-critical applications.

## Public attendance

The public is welcome. Seating for 60 in the Chamber. Live streaming on the council's YouTube channel and on Microsoft Teams. An audio recording is uploaded the next working day.

## Public speaking

Three minutes per side per item:

- Up to 3 objectors (sharing the 3 minutes)
- Up to 3 supporters (sharing the 3 minutes)
- The applicant or agent (3 minutes)
- The relevant ward councillor (3 minutes, can be in addition)
- The parish council (3 minutes)

Register by 17:00 on the working day before the meeting. Online form: planning.aldershire.gov.uk/committee-speak. Or call Democratic Services on 01394 555 0290.

## What the Committee can do

For each application:

- Approve as recommended
- Refuse as recommended
- Approve against officer recommendation (with stated reasons)
- Refuse against officer recommendation (with stated reasons that must be tenable on appeal)
- Defer for site visit, further information, or further negotiation

## Cooling-off period

If members are minded to refuse against officer advice, the Constitution requires a cooling-off period (a fortnight) before the decision is taken, to allow officers to advise on the appeal risk. This protects the council from cost awards.

## Site visits

The Committee can resolve to visit a site before deciding. Site visits are conducted by the Chair, the relevant ward members, and senior planning officers. They are strictly for observation, not for receiving representations.

## Conflicts of interest

Members must declare any disclosable pecuniary interest, predetermination, or bias. They must not predetermine - they may have a "predisposition" but must come to the meeting with an open mind. Failure to follow this can render a decision void.

## Decisions

Decisions are taken by simple majority of those present and voting. The Chair has a casting vote. Decisions are notified within 5 working days and published on the council's website.
