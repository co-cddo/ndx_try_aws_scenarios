# Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL)

The Community Infrastructure Levy is a charge on most new development used to fund infrastructure that supports growth: schools, transport improvements, open space, healthcare facilities. Aldershire adopted its CIL Charging Schedule on 1 April 2018, with revisions in 2022.

## Who pays

Liability falls on the landowner at the moment development is "commenced". The "commencement" trigger is defined in CIL Regulation 7. Liability transfers automatically unless a specific notice (Form 2: Assumption of Liability) is filed.

## Who is exempt

- Self-build housing (must be lived in by the applicant for at least 3 years)
- Self-build extensions and annexes
- Affordable housing
- Charitable development for charitable use
- Buildings under 100 sqm (except creation of an additional dwelling)
- Most agricultural buildings (limits apply)

To claim self-build exemption, file Form 7 (Part 1) before commencement and Form 7 (Part 2) within 6 months of completion. Late filings cause loss of exemption and full CIL becomes payable.

## Aldershire CIL rates (2024)

- Residential (Aldermarket urban): GBP 130 per sqm
- Residential (Bramley Heath corridor): GBP 95 per sqm
- Residential (Aldershire Vale rural): GBP 60 per sqm
- Retail (over 280 sqm): GBP 75 per sqm
- All other uses: GBP 0 (zero-rated)

Rates are indexed annually using the BCIS All-in Tender Price Index.

## How it is calculated

CIL = chargeable rate x net additional gross internal area x BCIS index

Net additional area = new floorspace minus the internal area of any building demolished or converted (if it has been in lawful use for at least 6 of the last 36 months).

## Liability and payment

The council issues a Liability Notice with the planning permission. After commencement, the council issues a Demand Notice. CIL is payable in instalments under Aldershire's Instalment Policy:

- Total under GBP 50,000: full payment within 60 days of commencement
- GBP 50,000 - GBP 250,000: 50% within 60 days, 50% within 12 months
- Over GBP 250,000: 25% within 60 days, 25% within 6 months, 50% within 18 months

## Penalties

Late payment surcharges are punitive:

- Within 30 days late: 5% of unpaid amount or GBP 200 (whichever greater)
- After 6 months: a further 5%
- After 12 months: a further 5%
- Failure to file commencement notice: GBP 2,500 surcharge

## Spending

CIL Regulation 59A requires Aldershire to publish an Infrastructure Funding Statement annually. The 2023-24 statement shows GBP 4.2m collected, GBP 3.6m spent on schools, junction improvements, parks. 15% is passed to parishes (25% where a Neighbourhood Plan is adopted).
