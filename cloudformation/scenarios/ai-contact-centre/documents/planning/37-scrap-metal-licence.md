# Scrap metal dealer licence

Anyone who carries on business as a scrap metal dealer must hold a licence under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013. Aldershire is the licensing authority for scrap metal dealing in the district.

## Two types of licence

### Site Licence

Required for premises where scrap metal is received, stored, or processed. Multiple sites can be covered under one licence; each site must be specified. Aldershire has 7 active site licences (as of 2024).

### Collector's Licence

Required for itinerant collectors who buy scrap metal door-to-door, from skip waste, or by similar means. Restricts operation to within Aldershire's district. To collect across council boundaries, separate licences are needed in each authority. Aldershire has 23 active collector's licences.

## Definition of scrap metal

Section 21 of the 2013 Act defines scrap metal as "any old, waste or discarded metal, including any metal product, article or assembly of metal whose value falls below the cost of producing or refurbishing it" - and includes parts of vehicles.

Common items: car batteries, copper cabling, lead flashing, white goods, aluminium drinks cans, end-of-life vehicles.

## Eligibility

The applicant (and Site Manager) must be a "suitable person". Aldershire considers:

- Convictions for relevant offences (including theft, handling stolen goods, fraud, driving offences)
- Compliance with previous licences
- Compliance with environmental permits (Environment Agency consultation)
- Confiscation orders and unspent enforcement actions

The council can impose conditions on licences and, in extreme cases, can revoke them.

## How to apply

Submit Form SMD1 with:

- Application form including site details and named site manager(s)
- Identity check on all named persons (passport, driving licence)
- Disclosure of convictions
- DBS Basic Disclosure
- Environment Agency permit references (where applicable)
- Trading Standards consultation outcome
- Application fee

## Mandatory conditions

The 2013 Act sets mandatory conditions:

- No purchase of scrap metal in cash (electronic transfer or crossed cheque only)
- Verify and record the seller's identity (driving licence, passport, council tax bill)
- Keep records of all transactions for 3 years
- Mark or paint each scrap metal load to allow tracking
- No sale to unlicensed buyers

Cash transactions are an offence; the supplier of the metal also commits an offence by accepting cash.

## Consultation and decision

Applications are consulted on:

- Aldershire Constabulary
- Environment Agency
- Aldershire Trading Standards (County)

The standard consultation period is 28 days. Determination within 12 weeks of valid receipt.

## Inspection and enforcement

Aldershire and the police carry out joint operations to inspect site records. Common offences:

- Operating without a licence: unlimited fine
- Cash transactions: fine up to GBP 5,000
- Failing to maintain records: fine up to GBP 2,500
- Permitting cash payment as the seller: fine up to GBP 1,000
